# The Speedy’s boats: the two boats of the pirate brig

*Two boats slung at the brig’s davits, one lost under the capsized hull and the other smashed on the rocks at the mouth of the Mercy; neither is saved.*

Keywords: canot, canots, embarcations, boats, davits, porte-embarcations, ligne de sonde, Speedy, pirates

- **What it is.**
  - The brig has boats hung from davits, hauled up and lowered on tackles; the first takes seven men, one at the helm, four at the oars and two crouched forward (III.3 ¶23). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: On halait sur les palans des porte-embarcations
    - quote: Sept hommes y descendirent
  - The same boat takes about a dozen when it goes back to the islet (III.3 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: se jetèrent dans l’embarcation au nombre d’une douzaine
  - A second boat takes eight men (III.3 ¶43). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Un second canot fut également lancé à la mer dans lequel huit hommes prirent place
  - A convict in its bow sounds the channel with a lead line, to learn the channel the Mercy has dug; some thirty pirates in the brig's shrouds follow the boat and take bearings for the brig's landing (III.3 ¶26). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: tenait une ligne de sonde à la main
    - quote: le chenal creusé par le courant de la Mercy
    - quote: Une trentaine de pirates, dispersés dans les haubans, ne perdaient pas un des mouvements du canot et relevaient certains amers
  - The second boat is broken on the reefs at the mouth of the Mercy and is quite unusable (III.3 ¶47; III.4 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: s’y brisa à l’embouchure de la Mercy
    - quote: absolument hors d’usage
  - The first disappears in the sinking of the brig, probably crushed by her, and does not come up again (III.4 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: l’autre avait disparu dans l’engloutissement du brick
    - quote: sans doute écrasée par lui, n’avait pas reparu
  - The colonists regret that they could not be saved (III.4 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Il était regrettable que les deux embarcations du brick n’eussent pu être sauvées
  - A man of the crew of the boat that broke on the rocks at the mouth of the Mercy is the one who flees from the corral by the south buttress of Mont Franklin (III.7 ¶53). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: C’était l’un de ceux dont le canot s’était brisé sur les roches, à l’embouchure de la Mercy
- **Appears in:** III.3, III.4, III.7
