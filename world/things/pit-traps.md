# The pit traps: the covered pits set round the plateau and the forest edge

*Simple pits roofed with branches and grass, baited at the bottom and dug where prints were thickest; they caught foxes, boars and peccaries.*

Keywords: trappes, traps, fosses, pits, snare, hunting, game, appât, bait, culpeux, peccary

- **What it is.**
  - Pencroff proposes to set traps as soon as he has leisure, expecting great good from them (I.20 ¶2). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: se proposait d’établir des trappes dont il attendait le plus grand bien
  - Harbert, Pencroff and the reporter set traps on the Grande-Vue plateau and at the edge of the forest (I.22 ¶6). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des trappes sur le plateau de Grande-Vue et aux abords de la forêt
  - The traps are extremely simple: pits dug in the ground, a roofing of branches and grass hiding the mouth, and some bait at the bottom (I.22 ¶7). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des fosses creusées dans le sol, au-dessus un plafonnage de branches et d’herbes
    - quote: au fond quelque appât
  - They are dug where numerous prints show frequent passage of quadrupeds, not at random (I.22 ¶7). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des empreintes plus nombreuses indiquaient de fréquentes passées de quadrupèdes
  - They are visited every day (I.22 ¶7). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Tous les jours, elles étaient visitées
  - The first catches are culpeux, three times in the first days; the dead foxes then serve as bait (I.22 ¶7, ¶12). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: à trois reprises, pendant les premiers jours
    - quote: amorcées avec ces cadavres de renards
  - The snares give more profit than the traps (see [the snares](snares.md)) (I.22 ¶13). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les collets donnèrent plus de profit que les trappes
  - In the second week of August the traps yield boars, more useful than the foxes (I.22 ¶14). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des animaux autres que des culpeux, et plus utiles
  - After the snowstorm the traps, buried under snow, are found whole and empty, with many prints round them (see [the claw prints](claw-prints.md)) (I.22 ¶31). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: retrouvèrent les trappes parfaitement intactes
    - quote: Aucun animal n’y était tombé
  - On 24 October Pencroff finds in one a female peccary and her two young (I.22 ¶62). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une femelle de pécari et ses deux petits
- **Appears in:** I.20, I.22
