# Ulysse Grant: Federal general besieging Richmond

*The Union general under whom Cyrus Smith began the war, whose siege holds Smith and the others prisoner.*

Keywords: Grant, general, Union, Federal, Richmond, siege, Sécession

- **What it is.**
  - He is the general whose troops besiege Richmond (I.2 ¶1). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: assiégée par les troupes du général Ulysse Grant
  - He tried in vain, in February 1865, by coups de main to seize Richmond (I.2 ¶3). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dans un de ces coups de main que tenta, mais inutilement, le général Grant pour s’emparer de Richmond
  - He is the general who answered "Je ne compte jamais mes morts !" and is called "le terrible Grant" (I.2 ¶5). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Je ne compte jamais mes morts !
  - Cyrus Smith began under him in the Illinois volunteers (I.2 ¶5). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Après avoir commencé sous Ulysse Grant dans les volontaires de l’Illinois
- **On the page.**
  - He continues energetic operations; the victory at Petersburg was very dearly contested and his forces, joined with Butler's, still get no result before Richmond (I.2 ¶17). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: La victoire de Petersburg lui avait été très-chèrement disputée
  - On 5 April Richmond fell into his hands, the secessionist revolt was put down and the cause of American unity triumphed (I.2 n1). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Richmond tombait aux mains de Grant
