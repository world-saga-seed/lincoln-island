# The sugar maple: the maple whose sap gives sugar

*The "acer saccharinum", a maple of the middle zones that the island has in great number; tapped for its sap.*

Keywords: érable, acer saccharinum, maple, sugar maple, sap, liqueur, incisions, acérinées

- **What it is.**
  - It is the "acer saccharinum", a kind of maple of the family acerinées, thriving in all the middle zones, of which the island has a great number (I.20 ¶31). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: l’« acer saccharinum », sorte d’érable de la famille des acérinées
    - quote: dont l’île possédait un grand nombre
  - The colonists draw its liquor by deep incisions (I.22 ¶43). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: en faisant à cet arbre des incisions profondes
- **Leads to.**
  - Its sap gives the colonists their sugar (see [the maple sugar](maple-sugar.md)) (I.20 ¶31). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ils avaient même fabriqué du sucre, sans cannes ni betteraves
- **Appears in:** I.20, I.22
