# The tent: the shelter of the colonists by the mouth of the Mercy

*The tent under which the six colonists camp near the Mercy’s mouth after leaving Granite-house, and where the final explosion finds them.*

Keywords: tente, camp, Mercy, embouchure, abri, shelter

- **What it is.**
  - The colonists camp under a tent near the mouth of the Mercy, having abandoned Granite-house and not wishing to shelter in the Chimneys (III.19 ¶60). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ils campaient sous une tente, près de l’embouchure de la Mercy
    - quote: ils n’avaient même pas voulu chercher abri dans les Cheminées
  - The six are gathered under their tent when the explosion hurls them into the sea (III.20 ¶4). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: réunis alors sous leur tente, ils avaient été précipités à la mer
- **Appears in:** III.19, III.20
