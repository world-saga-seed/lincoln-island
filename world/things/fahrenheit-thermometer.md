# The Fahrenheit thermometer: the thermometer of the chest

*One Fahrenheit thermometer in the chest, by which the winter cold is read in degrees Fahrenheit.*

Keywords: thermomètre, Fahrenheit, temperature, instrument, chest, froid, cold, observations thermométriques

- **What it is.**
  - The chest holds one Fahrenheit thermometer among its instruments (II.2 ¶53). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: 1 thermomètre de Fahrenheit
  - The temperature of the winter is given in degrees Fahrenheit, the thermometric observations of the three winter months averaging no more than eight degrees, which the text turns into 13.33 centigrade below zero (III.14 ¶49). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: la moyenne des observations thermométriques ne donna pas plus de huit degrés Fahrenheit (13°,33 centig au-dessous de zéro)
- **Appears in:** II.2, III.14
