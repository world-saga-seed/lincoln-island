## Open and flags
- Seam with II.8: II.8 ends in the evening talks of summer (the 24 March anniversary "avant peu"); ¶1 here opens in the first week of March. The chapters overlap in time; II.8 ¶45 and ¶48 run to the end of summer and to late March, while II.9 returns to early March. Card neither as an error. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- Direction of Tabor: ¶91 gives Tabor at `deux degrés et demi plus à l’ouest et deux degrés plus au sud que l’île Lincoln`, and ¶99 gives the distance as `cent cinquante milles environ dans le nord-est`. West and south of Lincoln does not fit north-east from it. Both are the text's. *(Drafted.)*
- Tabor's coordinates against Lincoln's: Lincoln `150° 30′` W and `34° 57′` S (ln5); Tabor `153°` and `37° 11′` (¶91): the differences are 2° 30′ and 2° 14′. The text says "two degrees". *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- The error margin: ¶75 says any error must not exceed five degrees; ln4 gives the first reading as a band 150° to 155° and 30° to 35° (five degrees wide), and ¶83 says the error had not exceeded five degrees. *(Drafted.)*
- Length of the storm: ¶5 says eight days from about 2 March (¶2), and ¶16 ends it "about 9 March"; ¶1 puts the change in the first week. *(Drafted.)*
- ¶13: `Harbert had already grown two inches in a year`, said in March of the island's first year, a little short of a year since the landing (II.8 ¶48 gives 24 March as the anniversary). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- The date 1 April 1866 is a Sunday and Easter in the text; the text states it and does not give the year. *(Drafted.)*
