## Open and flags

- **Eight miles or six.** The chapter summary (¶0) says "À huit mille du premier campement". ¶81 says "à six milles environ des Cheminées" at six o'clock, and the cave is five minutes' walk inland (¶85). The text does not give eight. I.5 ¶42 has Nab and Spilett going "plus de huit milles" on the first day. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: à six milles environ des Cheminées
- **The islet.** ¶77: "l’îlot, absolument invisible alors", with the definite article, as if the islet were already known. Chapter 00700's span was not given the earlier chapters; whether an islet is introduced before I.7 is for the reader of 00100 to 00300 to say. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: elles devaient passer par-dessus l’îlot, absolument invisible alors
- **Moon and tide.** ¶71 "La lune, nouvelle alors" and ¶77 "une marée de syzygie" agree. The date of the new moon is not given elsewhere in my chapters. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: La lune, nouvelle alors, et, par conséquent, en conjonction avec le soleil
- **Hours of the tide.** ¶27: "la mer, qui baissait alors" at about 8 p.m.; ¶77: "La mer commençait à monter" at about 2:30 a.m.; I.4 ¶63: reflux at 1 p.m. on the 25th. I have not worked the tide table; card neither. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: La mer commençait à monter
- **Distance of the loss from the coast.** ¶10: "à douze cents pieds au plus du rivage"; I.5 ¶42: "à quelques centaines de pieds de la côte"; I.6 ¶8: "à quelques centaines de pas d’un rivage". Three readings, not one figure. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: à douze cents pieds au plus du rivage
- **Dinner and supper.** ¶21 "préparer le dîner", ¶30 "souper", ¶70 "restes du souper": same meal. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Pencroff s’occupa aussitôt de préparer le dîner.
- **The tide table and the wind.** The wind is from the south-east and also they walk north with it at their backs (¶72); the coast is "exposée à l’est" (¶32). *(Drafted.)*
