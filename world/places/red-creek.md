# The Red Creek: a stream near whose mouth coal lies open

*A stream of the island: a clear, deep creek between high banks of red earth running a mile and a half to the lake; coal lies in the open near its mouth.*

Keywords: Creek-Rouge, brook, stream mouth, coal seam, creek, ruisseau, terre rouge, oxyde de fer, red earth, banks, iron oxide
