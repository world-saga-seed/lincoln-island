# Baie Washington: the large indentation of the south coast

*The wide bay of the island's south, named after Washington on 30 March together with the Union Bay and Mont Franklin.*

Keywords: baie Washington, Washington Bay, south, échancrure, bay, south coast

- **What it is.**
  - Smith proposed the name baie Washington for the large indentation of the south (I.11 ¶63). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: à cette large échancrure du sud, celui de baie Washington
  - It was named at once, with the Union Bay and Mont Franklin (I.11 ¶65). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: on nomma baie de l’Union, baie Washington et mont Franklin
  - Seen from the angle at the end of the promontory, the coast runs north-east to form the bay (II.5 ¶27). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: pour suivre la direction nord-est en formant la baie Washington
  - From there the whole south shore can be taken in at a glance: first a broad, very smooth, flat beach edged at the back by a line of trees; then an irregular shore that throws sharp points out to sea; at last some blackish rocks heaped in picturesque disorder at cap Griffe (II.5 ¶27). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une large grève très-unie et très-plate, bordée d’une lisière d’arbres en arrière-plan
    - quote: projetait des pointes aiguës en mer
    - quote: quelques roches noirâtres s’accumulaient dans un pittoresque désordre pour finir au cap Griffe
  - It is called "l’immense baie" (II.5 ¶27). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le fond de l’immense baie
  - Sandbanks run far out from its shore, and beyond them reefs; Pencroff calls them "bad waters" (II.5 ¶29). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Des bancs de sable, qui se prolongent au large, et plus loin, des écueils
  - Pencroff says the sands swallow everything thrown on them, so that the hull of a ship of several hundred tons disappears entirely in a few days, leaving only timber on the reefs (II.5 ¶31, ¶33). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: engloutissent tout ce qui s’y jette
    - quote: la coque d’un navire de plusieurs centaines de tonneaux y disparaisse entièrement
  - The foot of the bay is reached at one o'clock, twenty miles from the end of the promontory (II.5 ¶37). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ils avaient franchi une distance de vingt milles
  - From the foot of the bay to cap Griffe the coast is irregular, oddly jagged and covered by a long line of reefs that follows the sandbanks and that the tide will uncover; the beach is narrow between the line of reefs and the edge of the forest (II.5 ¶39). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une côte irrégulière, bizarrement déchiquetée
    - quote: une longue ligne de ces écueils qui succédaient aux bancs de sable
    - quote: la grève était peu spacieuse et resserrée entre la lisière des récifs et celle de la forêt
  - The granite wall rises higher and higher along it, and from below only the green crowns of the trees on top can be seen (II.5 ¶40). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: La muraille de granit tendait aussi à s’exhausser de plus en plus
  - Edible shellfish abound on its beach, but they could be worked profitably only when a crossing of the Mercy is made and the means of transport improved (II.5 ¶41). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les coquillages comestibles abondaient sur cette plage
    - quote: lorsqu’une communication aurait été établie entre les deux rives de la Mercy
  - The shore shows no trace of any landing, old or recent, no wreck, no camp remains, no ashes of a fire, no footprint, and nothing connected with the supposed wreck (II.5 ¶26, ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la moindre trace d’un débarquement ancien ou récent
    - quote: rien de ce qui avait rapport au naufrage présumé n’apparaissait sur ce littoral
  - Its heights, near Port Ballon, give a view of the sea to the south-west beyond cap Griffe (III.1 ¶57). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: pour l’observer, il serait alors nécessaire de gagner les hauteurs de la baie Washington, près de Port-Ballon
  - Its vast shore, from the cap de la Griffe to the promontoire du Reptile, has been explored only imperfectly (III.11 ¶25). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le vaste littoral de la baie Washington depuis le cap Griffe jusqu’au promontoire du Reptile
