# The compresses: linen pads soaked in cold water for Harbert’s wounds

*Compresses of linen laid on both of Harbert’s wounds and kept wet; all the linen of the corral house is used up.*

Keywords: compresses, toile, linge, pansements, blessure, charpie, Harbert

- **What it is.**
  - Linen compresses are laid on both wounds and kept constantly soaked with cold water (see [the cold water](cold-water.md)) (III.7 ¶44). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Des compresses de toile furent appliquées sur les deux blessures du pauvre Harbert
    - quote: durent être constamment imbibées d’eau froide
  - Pencroff keeps them permanently damp (III.7 ¶88). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Pencroff maintenait ses compresses dans un état permanent d’humidité
  - Covered with compresses and lint, the wounds are bound neither too tight nor too loose, and all the linen of the house is sacrificed (III.8 ¶10). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: n’étaient serrées ni trop ni trop peu
    - quote: Tout le linge de l’habitation avait été sacrifié
- **Appears in:** III.7, III.8
