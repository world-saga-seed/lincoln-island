# Kingfishers: the fishers on the lake rock

*Kingfishers that live in common on a rock in Lake Grant and dive for fish with a shrill cry.*

Keywords: martin-pêcheur, kingfisher, lac Grant, lake, rocher, rock, pêche, fishing, plongeon, dive

- **What it is.**
  - Several pairs live in common on the rock that rises from the lake a few hundred feet from the south shore (I.12 ¶68). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Là, vivaient en commun plusieurs couples de martins-pêcheurs
  - They perch on a stone, grave and motionless, watching for fish, then dart, dive with a shrill cry, and reappear with the prey in the beak (I.12 ¶68). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: guettant les poissons au passage, puis, s’élançant, plongeant en faisant entendre un cri aigu, et reparaissant, la proie au bec
- **Appears in:** I.12
