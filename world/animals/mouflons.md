# Mouflons: the wild sheep of the mountain slopes

*Large wild sheep with horns bent back, woolly under silky tawny hair, that stand and stare at the colonists as if they had never seen men.*

Keywords: mouflon, mouton, sheep, bighorn, horns, wool, toison, gigot, côtelette, mountain, never seen men

- **What it is.**
  - Half a dozen big animals seen at fifty paces on the mountain's upper slope, with strong horns curved back and flattened toward the point (I.10 ¶35). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: aux fortes cornes courbées en arrière et aplaties vers la pointe
  - They have a woolly fleece hidden under long silky tawny hair (I.10 ¶35). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: à la toison laineuse, cachée sous de longs poils soyeux de couleur fauve
  - Not ordinary sheep, but a species common in the mountain regions of temperate zones, which Harbert names mouflons (I.10 ¶36). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: à laquelle Harbert donna le nom de mouflons
  - Pencroff holds them for sheep because they have legs and chops (I.10 ¶39). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Eh bien, ce sont des moutons !
  - They stand motionless among the basalt debris and stare as if they saw bipeds for the first time; then, suddenly afraid, they bound off over the rocks (I.10 ¶40). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: comme s’ils voyaient pour la première fois des bipèdes humains
  - Their presence was noted on the exploration of the mountain; a serious hunt is planned for the fine season, and their wool is to make clothes (I.20 ¶4). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dont la présence avait été signalée
    - quote: une fois la laine récoltée
  - Since mouflons are not lacking, Cyrus Smith means to raise a flock for the colony's needs, for wool or skins (I.22 ¶59). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: puisque les mouflons ne manquaient pas
    - quote: un troupeau qui serait élevé pour les besoins de la colonie
- **Appears in:** I.10, I.20, I.22
