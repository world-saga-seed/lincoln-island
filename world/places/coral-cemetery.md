# The coral cemetery: the burial place of the Nautilus crew, in the Pacific

*Where the dead companions of Nemo rest, on the floor of the Pacific.*

Keywords: graveyard, burial, crew, dead, sea floor

- **What it is.**
  - It is a cemetery of coral on the bottom of the Pacific, where the companions who died went to rest (III.16 ¶59). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dans leur cimetière de corail, au fond du Pacifique
- **On the page.**
  - After the deaths the Nautilus was empty and Nemo alone (III.16 ¶59). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Le vide se fit dans le Nautilus
- **Appears in:** III.16
- **Open:**
  - The text gives no position or number of graves.
    - quote: leur cimetière de corail
