# Kangaroos: the bounding game of the lake forest

*Grey long-furred kangaroos that clear the thickets in thirty-foot leaps and outrun men and dog, of a kind the reporter says is good stewed.*

Keywords: kangourou, kangaroo, bond, leap, gibier, game, venaison, venison, broche, étuvée, Australia

- **What it is.**
  - A troop of quadrupeds hopping and bounding, in leaps of thirty feet, like "mammifères volants", passing over the thickets as if from tree to tree like squirrels (I.12 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: faisant des sauts de trente pieds, véritables mammifères volants
  - Harbert names them kangaroos (I.12 ¶47). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Des kangourous !
  - Stewed, the reporter says, kangaroo is worth the best venison (I.12 ¶49). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: cela vaut la meilleure venaison
  - They are elastic game that rebounds like a ball; men and dog on their trail lose them in the underwood after five minutes (I.12 ¶50). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ce gibier élastique, qui rebondissait comme une balle
  - They are giants with long grey fur, the species hardest to take; the black, red and rock kangaroos and kangaroo-rats are easier, and about a dozen species are counted (I.12 ¶57). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: C’étaient des géants à longue fourrure grise
- **Appears in:** I.12
