## Open and flags

- Evening hour. The summary says "Five in the evening", the text says "It was nearly six o'clock then" (¶14). Both are quoted in the timeline; neither is carded. *(Drafted.)*
- Islet length. ¶22 and ¶23 give a mile and a half south, then "deux milles environ", from the promontory's base, while ¶25 says "no more than two miles in length". The figures are not obviously in conflict (the walk includes the promontory), but are not reconciled. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- Direction. ¶14 says they walked north along "the east coast"; ¶22 says the turn back took them south. The narrator says: "they were walking south, and that was going the opposite way". *(Drafted.)*
- Stars. ¶36 puts "la Croix du Sud" "at the south pole of the world"; Smith is said to be unable to observe the northern stars "in the northern part of the new continent". Left as printed. *(Drafted.)*
- Nab drifts (¶49: "He was drifting at extreme speed"): he lands "several thousand feet" from the point opposite his start; the direction of the drift is not given. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- Plate caption pl1 repeats a line of ¶20 and adds nothing else. *(Drafted.)*
