# The sounding line: the long cord with an iron block that Pencroff pays out in the Shark-gulf

*A long cord with an iron block, serving Pencroff as a sounding line, about fifty fathoms long, which touches no bottom in the Shark-gulf.*

Keywords: ligne de sonde, sonde, corde, bloc de fer, brasses, profondeur, Bonadventure, fathom

- **What it is.**
  - It is a long cord kept aboard the Bonadventure, serving as a sounding line, with an iron block tied to it (II.20 ¶18). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une longue corde qui lui servait de ligne de sonde
    - quote: à laquelle était attaché un bloc de fer
  - It measures about fifty fathoms, and in the Shark-gulf it runs out to its end without touching bottom (see [the Shark-gulf](../places/golfe-du-requin.md)) (II.20 ¶18). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Cette ligne mesurait environ cinquante brasses
    - quote: elle se déroula jusqu’au bout sans heurter le sol
- **Appears in:** II.20
