# The corral: the palisaded pasture at the Red Creek's sources, for the island's wool-bearing animals

*A meadow at the foot of Mont Franklin's southern croup, enclosed by a round palisade and sheds, built in January and February to hold mouflons and wild goats; the colonists' sheepfold.*

Keywords: corral, enclos, palissade, mouflons, sheepfold, pen, hangars, wool, laine, Red Creek, sources
