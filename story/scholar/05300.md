## a

After: The 14th of February, the eve of the departure, was a Sunday.

The colonists kept the days of the week from their first April on the island, and their weekdays agree with one another: the 5th of April, 1865, a Wednesday; Easter on the 16th; Pentecost, a Sunday, on the 4th of June; a Friday on the 30th of June; Sunday the 29th of October; Easter again on the 1st of April, 1866; and the 29th of November, 1867, a Friday, by Nab’s note in chapter LI. From that Friday to this day is seventy-seven days, eleven weeks, which makes the 14th of February a Friday as well. The date is fixed, for the departure had been set for the 15th. I take it that this day was a Friday.
