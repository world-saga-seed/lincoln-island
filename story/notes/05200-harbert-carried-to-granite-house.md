## Open and flags
- **Dates of the second fit.** ¶42 says the first fit did not return "that night" nor "le jour suivant"; ¶43 says they awaited the next day "ce lendemain"; ¶52 then dates the day and night of anxiety "7 décembre", and ¶53 puts the second fit "Vers le milieu de la journée". Counting from the first fit on 6 December, the day after is the 7th, so the text does not say plainly whether the second fit falls on the 7th or the 8th. Both sides:
  - quote: elle ne revint pas davantage le jour suivant
  - quote: cette journée du 7 décembre
  - quote: La journée du lendemain, 8 décembre
  - Card neither version. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- **A correction behind "7 décembre".** ¶52's "décembre" is Wikisource's correction of the print's "novembre" (`texts/README.md`; page 508 of the print reads "7 novembre"). The reading, "décembre"; the print's, "novembre". A citation resting on the date should say so. *(Drafted.)*
- **The ¶10 count.** From 11 November to the 29th is 18 days by plain subtraction; the text says "dix-neuf jours". Quote:
  - quote: Il y avait donc dix-neuf jours
  - *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- **The five hours.** The first and second fits are each said to last five hours (¶34, ¶54). *(Drafted.)*
