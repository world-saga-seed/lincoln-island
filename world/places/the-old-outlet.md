# The old outlet: the passage in the granite by which the lake’s overflow ran to the sea

*The lake’s drain, a hole at its south point that the blast of 21 May left bare: a winding, widening passage of black granite older than the island’s waters, running down to the great cavern and the well.*

Keywords: déversoir, ancien déversoir, outlet, overflow, conduit, boyau, couloir, passage, tunnel, spillway, stair, descent, torches, trop-plein
