# The gum trees: the gigantic gum trees of the lower Red Creek

*Gigantic gum trees that arch over the lower Red Creek.*

Keywords: gommiers, gum tree, Red Creek, gigantic, trees

- **What it is.**
  - The Red Creek runs under the vault of casuarinas, banksias and gigantic gum trees (I.12 ¶66). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: sous la voûte des casuarinas, des banksias et des gommiers gigantesques
  - It is among the trees of the forest cut through on 31 October, most of which were already known round the lake and the Grande-Vue plateau, generally of medium size because their number has hindered their growth (II.4 ¶5). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des gommiers
    - quote: généralement de taille médiocre, car leur nombre avait nui à leur développement
  - Clumps of green gum trees stand on Tabor, among other tall trees of the kind growing on Lincoln (II.13 ¶47). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des bouquets de gommiers verdoyants
  - Five or six magnificent gum trees shelter the Tabor hut (see [the Tabor dwelling](../places/the-tabor-dwelling.md)) (II.14 ¶21). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: cinq ou six magnifiques gommiers
  - It is one of the known kinds that follow one another beyond sight in the forest of the Far-West (III.11 ¶42). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: Déodars, douglas, casuarinas, banksias, gommiers, dragonniers et autres essences déjà reconnues, se succédaient au delà des limites du regard
- **Appears in:** I.12, II.4, II.13, II.14, III.11
