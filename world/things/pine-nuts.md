# The pine nuts: the edible almonds of a pine found in the forest

*An excellent almond from a pine that Harbert finds in the forest, eaten ripe by Pencroff and Nab as the first vegetable food.*

Keywords: pin pignon, pin pigeon, amandes, almonds, nuts, pine kernels, edible fruit, dessert

- **What it is.**
  - It is the almond of a tree with edible fruit, esteemed in the temperate regions of America and Europe (I.9 ¶97). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: qui produit une amande excellente, très-estimée dans les régions tempérées de l’Amérique et de l’Europe
  - The almonds were in a perfect state of ripeness (I.9 ¶97). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ces amandes étaient dans un parfait état de maturité
  - Pencroff takes them as dessert, with algae for bread and raw mussels for meat (I.9 ¶98). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des amandes pour dessert
  - The nuts, with the sargasses, completed the supper at the Chimneys (I.10 ¶15). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Les sargasses et les amandes de pin pignon complétèrent ce repas
  - A few dozen of them with the capybara's remains made the supper on the mountain (I.10 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: quelques douzaines d’amandes de pin pignon formèrent les éléments du souper
  - The stock of game and almonds was entirely used up at the plateau breakfast (I.12 ¶13). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la réserve de gibier et d’amandes fut totalement épuisée
  - Nab keeps his harvests of pine nuts, with rhizomes and dragon-tree roots, in special baskets; the supply is renewed after the storm, and the nuts are among the dishes of the 24 October dinner (I.22 ¶28, ¶37, ¶74). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ses récoltes de rhizomes, d’amandes de pin-pignon et de racines de dragonnier
    - quote: des amandes de pignon
