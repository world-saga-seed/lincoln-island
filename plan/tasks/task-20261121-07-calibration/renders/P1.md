# P1: II.19 ¶2

¶2

What was passing at that time in their own land? The image of their country was ever present before their eyes, that country rent by civil war at the moment when they had left it, and which the rebellion of the South was perhaps still drenching with blood! It was a great grief to them, and they often talked together of these things, without ever doubting, however, that the cause of the North must triumph, for the honour of the American Confederation.
