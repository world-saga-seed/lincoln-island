# 04 Card the existing work

*Basic.*

**When:** after setup; before any new outlining.

**Uses:** [card-existing](../../plan/workflows/card-existing.md) (the named people, places and things, carded with their quotes, then `tools/cards.py build`), [scene-outlines](../../plan/workflows/scene-outlines.md) (prompt D: existing prose taken apart into numbered beats) and [character-sheet](../../plan/workflows/character-sheet.md) (Basic); as Refinements, [habits](../../plan/workflows/habits.md), [capture-notes](../../plan/workflows/capture-notes.md) (outside notes), [proposals](../../plan/workflows/proposals.md) and [daily-life-study](../../plan/workflows/daily-life-study.md) (where the setting is thin), [distill](../../plan/workflows/distill.md), [card-audit](../../plan/workflows/card-audit.md).

**What happens:** facts go onto marked cards and sheets, and each existing scene becomes a numbered outline; the questions go to the writer in batches ([kit/method/cards.md](../method/cards.md)).

**Outputs:** `world/`, `story/notes/characters/`, chapter outlines and notes, the voice sheet. The kit's example (in the kit's repository, `example/world/` and `example/story/notes/characters/`) has cards and a character sheet.

**Done when:** every named thing in the manuscripts has a card or is a crowd figure; every fact is marked; every existing scene has an outline; links and cards at 0.
