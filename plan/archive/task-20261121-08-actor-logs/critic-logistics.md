# Critic 1: logistics and physics (report as returned)

*A fresh agent, read-only, no git. Saved by the main session from the agent's final message. It audited the logs as they stood at bb92c68, before R1121-62: findings 4, 5, 12 and 14 are on the light-boat net, now replaced, and are struck in the interview as stale (rank b).*

Totals: 10 findings of rank (a), 6 of rank (c). Every quote was checked with grep -F.

1. (a) **Sight lines.** The household could see the two on the bar. In the hour before the squall the windows command the bay, and the shutters stand open for light: there is no glass, and no candles until after 5 June. Quotes: "Toute la baie de l’Union se développait magnifiquement devant eux" (texts/fr-1875/19.md); "Si nous traitions un peu la question de l’éclairage" (texts/fr-1875/20.md); "à l’embouchure de la Mercy, entre l’îlot et la muraille de granit" (world/places/mercy-river.md); "The pretext is the lines themselves." (outline 01950); "Indoors through the squall, shutters closed. From the windows no one sees the bar." (household log). Fix: the squall's rain comes ahead of its wind and blots out the bar, the shutters are drawn, and the log says who is at which window. Alternative: the bar lies out of the windows' line, round the salient at the Mercy's mouth.
2. (a) **The Chimneys shelter.** The Chimneys are where the text fears the tides breaking in, and where Cyrus takes precautions. Quotes: "They wait in the Chimneys, now the forge." (outline 01950); "il était à craindre que les grandes marées, poussées par les vents du large, n’y fissent encore irruption" and "Cyrus Smith prit même quelques précautions" (texts/fr-1875/20.md); "They shelter in the Chimneys, by my pick." (Pencroff and Harbert log). Fix: they shelter under the granite wall at the foot of the ladder, or climb home. Alternative: the Chimneys, with Cyrus away, and the reason.
3. (a) **A lost day.** The 2 June low water is mid-morning, and the next falls after dark (sunset near 5 pm). "Home, 2 June, evening" leaves the day unaccounted for. Quotes: "### 1. Pencroff sees it (1 June, morning low water)" and "### 6. Home, wet (2 June, evening)" (outline 01950). Fix: home at midday or in the afternoon. Alternative: a reason to wait, such as watching for the float until the light goes.
4. (a) **The net's place and its keeper.** A long row, a short net in front of the windows. Stale: replaced by R1121-62.
5. (a) **The barnacles' age.** A net set in late May is too new to be thick with barnacles. Stale: R1121-62 makes the fouling years old.
6. (a) **"Their knives."** In June 1865 there are two knives, the halves of Top's collar, and the forging list names none. A knife could also cut the lashings. Quotes: "They have their knives." (outline 01950); "Voilà deux couteaux, Pencroff !" (world/people/cyrus-smith.md); "ciseaux de charpentier, puis, des fers de pioche, de pelle, de pic, des marteaux, des clous, etc." (texts/fr-1875/15.md). Fix: Pencroff has his knife, one of the two, and the lashings lie under the sand on the hidden side. Alternative: no knife, only hands and the stone.
7. (a) **The ford at a neap.** On 25 March, near the springs, the channel left a gut up to five feet deep that Harbert swam; at a neap it is deeper, and the June water is cold. Quotes: "dont la profondeur ne dépassait pas cinq pieds. Harbert, pour qui l’eau eût été trop haute, nageait comme un poisson" and "ils les mirent en paquet sur leur tête" (texts/fr-1875/03.md); "They ford the channel to the bar, as the whole party fords it three days later" (outline 01950). Fix: the bar lies on the coast side of the gut, reached dry-shod over the bared sands, and "ford" goes. Alternative: keep the ford, the two stripped, Harbert swimming.
8. (a) **The sighting range.** The channel is half a mile wide, and the float has no size. Quotes: "On the bar a round thing, half buried, catches his eye, the wrong shape for a stone." (outline 01950); "il en était séparé par un canal large d’un demi-mille environ" (world/places/ilot-du-salut.md). Fix: give it a size (about a foot across) and put the bar near the beach. Alternative: the wet glass catches the low sun.
9. (a) **Top.** Where he is on 1 to 3 June is open, and a dog on the bar would draw the house's eye. Quote: "Where he is on 1 to 3 June is not settled (gap pass)." (household log). Fix: Top stays with Cyrus in the house.
10. (a) **The 02000 seam line.** ¶15's "Demain" dates the talk to the eve of the seal hunt. Quotes: "The talk of ¶5 to ¶18 has no date, so the insert's three days fit before ¶19's" (outline 02000); "Demain, en organisant une chasse aux phoques." (texts/fr-1875/20.md). Fix: reword it as dated by "Demain" (¶15) to the eve of 5 June; the insert still fits.
11. (c) "before the two set out" should be "before Pencroff sets out" (household log).
12. (c) Nemo at the crypt's bar near a neap. Stale: R1121-62.
13. (c) Nemo's well climbs cannot be nightly; make it "on some nights". Met by the rebuilt log ("At times").
14. (c) Nemo's search in sight of the windows. Stale: R1121-62 (no search).
15. (c) **The pretext must fit in the hour.** If it is no lie, they lift the lines on the way out, and the joke implies they did. Fix: write that into the log.
16. (c) **The citation for the hooks.** I.15 ¶51 names nails and "etc.", not hooks. Fix: cite "(I.15 ¶51, 'etc.'; named at II.8 ¶15)".
