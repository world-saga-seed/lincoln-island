# The salt: salt left in the rock hollows by evaporation

*Salt deposited in the hollows of the rocks by evaporation, found by Harbert; the only mineral product that enters the colonists’ food.*

Keywords: sel, rocks, evaporation, seasoning, mineral, alimentation

- **What it is.**
  - Harbert finds salt deposited in the hollows of the rocks by evaporation, a mineral substance that came very opportunely to the first breakfast of pigeon eggs and rock mussels (I.6 ¶10). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: du sel déposé dans le creux des roches par évaporation
    - quote: vint fort à propos
  - The colonists have salt in abundance, the only mineral product that enters food (I.20 ¶31). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le sel, le seul des produits minéraux qui entre dans l’alimentation
  - Sea salt is chloride of sodium, from which Smith extracts soda and chlorine (see [the soda](soda.md)) (III.14 ¶48). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: De ce chlorure de sodium, qui n’est autre chose que le sel marin
- **Leads to.**
  - Nab uses his time salting or smoking meats, which gives him excellent preserves (I.20 ¶2). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: saler ou à fumer des viandes
- **Appears in:** I.6, I.20, III.14
