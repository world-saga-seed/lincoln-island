# The world's rules

*What holds everywhere in the book. Every new card and every beat of the insert is checked against these: does this make sense here? Each rule cites its ruling and the French it rests on.*

0. **The world is as known in 1874 to 1875.** Nothing later goes in, even where it is true. *(Settled R1121-39.)*
1. **Verne's French is the canon** (Hetzel, 1875). Where White changes a fact, that is White, not the world. White reaches the page only in his forms of the names and his spelling ([R1121-20, R1121-21](../story/voice/white.md)). *(Settled R1121-40.)*
2. **The record is a retelling.** Verne's slips stay as printed. New writing follows the values settled on the ledger, and a discord is ruled when a draft leans on it (R1121-07). The discords are listed in [the timeline](../story/calendar/timeline.md#across-the-chapters). *(Settled R1121-41.)*
3. **Anything strange is Captain Nemo's or nature's.** There is no third hidden hand. His list is not the whole of it: H4, H5 and H17 ([the register](../plan/reread-hooks.md)) stay off it and stay open (R1121-11). The insert adds no act of his. *(Settled R1121-42.)*
   - quote: Ainsi s’expliquaient tant d’incidents qui devaient paraître surnaturels (III.16 ¶65)
   - quote: je n’ai jamais allumé de feu dans la nuit du 19 au 20 octobre (II.19 ¶88)
4. **Nemo's means and limits.** The Nautilus cannot leave the crypt. He goes out by the light boat and in the diving suit. He acts to save, supply, inform or defend the colonists. He is never seen whole until III.15. He hears everything said in Granite-house. See [Captain Nemo](people/captain-nemo.md) and [the crypt](places/crypte-dakkar.md). *(Settled R1121-43.)*
   - quote: il ne pouvait plus franchir l’entrée de la crypte (III.16 ¶63)
   - quote: assez d’eau pour qu’une légère embarcation pût passer la barre (III.16 ¶63)
   - quote: Revêtu de son scaphandre, il se promenait sous les eaux (III.16 ¶62)
   - quote: il ne voulut point être vu (III.16 ¶64)
   - quote: il entendait les colons raconter le passé, étudier le présent et l’avenir (III.16 ¶64)
5. **The colonists have only what they brought or have made by that date.** See [the tool chain](things/tool-chain.md). *(Settled R1121-44.)*
6. **From outside, only four things reach the island:** the harpoon in the whale (II.10), the bottle, the Speedy and the Duncan. A thing of Nemo's is no exception, even when the colonists read it as one. *(Settled R1121-45.)*
   - quote: Un navire du Vineyard ! (II.10 ¶62)
7. **Providence is how they speak.** Characters and narrator alike use it of what happens. The cause on the page is always work, science, Nemo, or the plain chance Verne names. Providence never acts. *(Settled R1121-46.)*
   - quote: Cependant la Providence devait, en cette circonstance, venir directement en aide aux colons (I.20 ¶33)
   - quote: quand le hasard le fit assister à la chute du ballon (III.16 ¶62)
8. **The seasons are southern.** June is the first month of winter. *(Settled R1121-47.)*
   - quote: La saison d’hiver commença véritablement avec ce mois de juin (I.20 ¶1)
9. **Moon and tide follow the text's own 1865 phases:** new on 27 March with a spring tide (I.7), full about 10 April (I.13), full about 2 November (II.3 ¶9). So early June falls near the first quarter, at neap tides. See [the moon](things/moon.md). *(Settled R1121-48.)*
   - quote: la lune devait être pleine trois jours après (II.3 ¶9)
10. **Who is on the island when.** In June 1865: the five colonists and Top. Jup comes in November 1865 (II.6), Ayrton in October 1866 (II.15). *(Settled R1121-49.)*
11. **The clue register is kept.** Nothing new explains an open thread. No line removes a clue. New clues go on [the register](../plan/reread-hooks.md). *(Settled R1121-50.)*
12. **Units are Verne's:** feet, miles, degrees Fahrenheit, in White's forms on the page. *(Settled R1121-51.)*
