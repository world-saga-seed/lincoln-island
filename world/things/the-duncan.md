# The Duncan: Lord Glenarvan’s steam yacht

*A pleasure steam yacht belonging to the Scottish laird Lord Glenarvan, commanded by Captain John Mangles with a crew of fifteen, which carries the search for Captain Grant and leaves Ayrton on Tabor.*

Keywords: Duncan, yacht, vapeur, Glenarvan, Mangles, Glasgow, Tabor, caronades, Ayrton, Grant, navire écossais

- **What it is.**
  - She is a pleasure steam yacht belonging to the Scottish laird Lord Glenarvan (II.17 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un yacht de plaisance à vapeur, le Duncan, appartenant au laird écossais, Lord Glenarvan
  - She is commanded by Captain John Mangles and manned by a crew of fifteen (II.17 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: commandé par le capitaine John Mangles et monté par un équipage de quinze hommes
  - She is fitted out at Glasgow for a distant campaign in which the Lord’s family and Captain Grant’s children take part (II.17 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Le yacht le Duncan fut équipé pour une campagne lointaine
    - quote: quittant Glasgow
  - Her second is Tom Austin, who commands her while Glenarvan crosses Australia and takes her to New Zealand on the letter’s order (II.17 ¶57, ¶66). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le Duncan, aux ordres du second, Tom Austin
    - quote: le second conduisait le navire
  - She carries caronades, whose firing Ayrton hears on 3 March (1855) (II.17 ¶68). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les caronades du Duncan qui faisaient feu
  - She leaves Ayrton on Tabor Island and on the same call finds Captain Grant and his two men there (II.17 ¶76). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ce fut là aussi que, par un vrai miracle, on retrouva le capitaine Grant et ses deux hommes
  - Smith counts her coming back among the colonists’ chances of salvation, since Glenarvan has promised Ayrton to return for him; the colonists call her "le yacht écossais" and "le navire écossais" (II.19 ¶17, ¶26). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ce retour du navire écossais
    - quote: Mais si le yacht écossais venait d’ici là
  - Hetzel’s note says the events of the tale are drawn from "Les Enfants du capitaine Grant" (II.17 n1). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Les Enfants du capitaine Grant
  - Ayrton knows her for a steam yacht, which would show smoke; she too is rigged as a brig, so that a funnel between her two masts is the sign that tells her from the ship in sight (III.1 ¶51, ¶63). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le Duncan est un yacht à vapeur
    - quote: si une cheminée s’élevait entre les deux mâts
    - quote: Le yacht écossais était, lui aussi, gréé en brick
  - She comes toward the rock in a straight line under forced steam, and the colonists are taken into a steamer’s cabin (III.20 ¶16, ¶20). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: en droite ligne, en forçant sa vapeur

  - She is Lord Glenarvan’s yacht, now commanded by Robert Grant, sent to Tabor to seek Ayrton and bring him home after twelve years of expiation (III.20 ¶26). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le yacht de lord Glenarvan, alors commandé par Robert, le fils du capitaine Grant
    - quote: pour y chercher Ayrton et le rapatrier après douze ans d’expiation
  - After leaving Tabor without Ayrton she makes a hundred miles to the north-east to look for him and his companions at Lincoln, led by the notice; the colonists land in America fifteen days after the rescue (III.20 ¶28, ¶29, ¶50). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: faire route à cent milles de là dans le nord-est
