# The firs: the tall firs of the forest

*Admirable firs a hundred and fifty feet high among the conifers, from whose young trunks sticks are torn.*

Keywords: sapins, fir, conifer, trees, tall trees, sticks, wood

- **What it is.**
  - There are admirable firs measuring a hundred and fifty feet in height (I.6 ¶27). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des sapins admirables, mesurant cent cinquante pieds de hauteur
  - Three sticks were torn from the trunk of a young fir (see [the clubs](clubs.md)) (I.9 ¶86). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: après avoir arraché trois bâtons au tronc d’un jeune sapin
  - A broad clump of firs stands halfway up the mountain beside a small brook that falls in cascade (I.10 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: au pied d’un large bouquet de sapins, près d’un petit ruisseau qui s’en allait en cascade
  - Fir, which abounds on the island, is chosen for the Bonadventure: a wood a little "fendif", easy to work, which stands water as well as elm (see [the elms](elms.md)) (II.10 ¶4). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: On se décida pour le sapin
    - quote: bois un peu « fendif », suivant l’expression des charpentiers, mais facile à travailler
  - A young straight fir without knots makes the boat’s mast (II.12 ¶84). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un jeune sapin, bien droit, sans nœuds
- **Appears in:** I.6, I.9, I.10, II.10, II.12
