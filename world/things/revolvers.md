# The revolvers: the colonists’ pistols, origin not given

*Revolvers carried by Smith into the well and by the colonists against the culpeos, with no source named.*

Keywords: revolver, revolvers, pistolet, armes à feu, firearms, culpeux, puits, balles

- **What it is.**
  - Smith takes a revolver, with a lantern and a cutlass at his belt, down into the well (II.11 ¶83). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ayant allumé une lanterne, pris un revolver et passé un coutelas à sa ceinture
  - The colonists arm themselves with axes, carbines and revolvers to meet the raid of culpeos (II.12 ¶37). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: s’armant de haches, de carabines et de revolvers
  - Their first revolver shots, flashing in the dark, drive back the first assailants (II.12 ¶38). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: leurs premiers coups de revolver, lançant de rapides éclairs dans l’obscurité, firent reculer les premiers assaillants
  - Thanks to their firearms the colonists almost always kept the assailants at a distance (II.12 ¶54). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: grâce à leurs armes à feu, presque toujours ils avaient pu tenir les assaillants à distance
- **Appears in:** II.11, II.12
- **Open:**
  - Where the revolvers came from is not said in these chapters; the weapons of the chest, as listed, name carbines, cutlasses and boarding sabres but no revolver (see [the carbines](carbines.md)) (II.2 ¶53). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: 2 carabines à inflammation centrale. | 5 coutelas
