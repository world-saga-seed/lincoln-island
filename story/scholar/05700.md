## a

After: At a quarter past nine a vivid flash showed them the palisaded enclosure,

The colonists left Granite House at twelve minutes past nine, and at forty-seven minutes past they still had two miles to go. They cannot have been at the corral at a quarter past. They were on the western shore a few minutes before ten. The flash came, then, between forty-seven minutes past nine and ten, and the quarter past nine cannot stand. I find nothing that gives the minute.
