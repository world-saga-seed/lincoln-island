# The nitric acid: distilled from the saltpetre by the sulphuric acid

*Nitric acid (acide azotique) got on 20 May by distilling the island’s saltpetre with sulphuric acid; the colonists hold it afterwards for nitro-glycerine and guncotton.*

Keywords: acide azotique, nitric acid, acid, distillation, saltpetre, nitro-glycerine, guncotton, fulminate

- **What it is.**
  - Spilett lists nitric acid and mercury among what the fulminate for guns would need (I.14 ¶41). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: du mercure et de l’acide azotique pour le fulminate
  - Smith lacked it: he could not make saltpetre from potash by nitric acid, since this was the very acid he wanted to get in the end (I.17 ¶48). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: l’acide azotique lui manquait
  - He got it easily: the saltpetre, attacked by sulphuric acid, gave it by distillation (I.17 ¶55). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le salpêtre, attaqué par l’acide sulfurique, lui donna précisément cet acide par distillation
  - Having it at his disposal, Smith could make a guncotton-like primer for the nitro-glycerine (I.17 ¶70). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: puisqu’il avait de l’acide azotique à sa disposition
  - Smith counts it, with saltpetre, sulphur and charcoal, among what the colonists have for making guncotton or powder (I.18 ¶9). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: puisque nous avons l’acide azotique, le salpêtre, le soufre et le charbon
  - For the pyroxyle Smith has nitric acid made easily from the natural saltpetre attacked by sulphuric acid; he has only ordinary nitric acid, not fuming, and mixes it with concentrated sulphuric acid to get the same result (see [the pyroxyle](pyroxyle.md)) (II.8 ¶10, ¶13). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: avait déjà pu facilement produire de l’acide azotique, en attaquant le salpêtre que lui fournissait la nature
    - quote: n’avait à sa disposition que de l’acide azotique ordinaire
  - For the battery Smith has nitric acid at his disposal, and fills a number of glass flasks with it (see [the telegraph battery](the-telegraph-battery.md)) (II.18 ¶41, ¶43). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: acide azotique et potasse, tout cela était à sa disposition
    - quote: remplis d’acide azotique
- **Appears in:** I.14, I.17, I.18, II.8, II.18
