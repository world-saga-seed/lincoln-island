# The Far-West road: the way cut with axes through the Far-West to the Reptile promontory

*The road the colonists cut with the axe from the Port-Ballon bridge through the Far-West forest toward the end of the Serpentine peninsula, begun in February and named "route du Far-West".*

Keywords: route, road, Far-West, hache, axe, setlers, promontoire du Reptile, Port-Ballon, chemin, trouée

- **What it is.**
  - The colonists decide to cut through the woods to the Reptile promontory with the axe, and so to make the first line of a road linking Granite-house to the end of the peninsula, sixteen or seventeen miles long (III.11 ¶29). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ils chemineraient à la hache et jetteraient ainsi le premier tracé d’une route
    - quote: sur une longueur de seize à dix-sept milles
  - It leaves the Port-Ballon road on the left at the end of the Mercy bridge and plunges into the great woods of the Far-West (III.11 ¶40). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: laissant cette route sur leur gauche, commencèrent à s’enfoncer sous le couvert de ces immenses bois qui formaient la région du Far-West
  - For the first two miles the trees are widely spaced and the cart passes freely (III.11 ¶41). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Pendant les deux premiers milles, les arbres, largement espacés, permirent au chariot de circuler librement
  - About six miles from Granite-house passage turns difficult, and trees have to be felled to cross thickets (III.11 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: à six milles environ de Granite-house, la circulation devint assez difficile
  - The colonists, true "setlers", spare the great fine trees and sacrifice the small ones, so that the road runs far from straight with many bends (III.11 ¶52). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: épargnaient les grands et beaux arbres
    - quote: ils sacrifiaient les petits
    - quote: la route prenait une direction peu rectiligne et s’allongeait de nombreux détours
  - Smith reckons a camp at fourteen miles from Granite-house, and not more than five miles from the Reptile promontory (III.11 ¶64). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: à quatorze milles de Granite-house
    - quote: pas être à plus de cinq milles du promontoire du Reptile
  - Made later, from the clearing opened on the last excursion, into a practicable road, it takes the name "route du Far-West" (III.14 ¶15). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: On profita de la trouée déjà faite lors de la dernière excursion pour ouvrir une route praticable, qui prit le nom de route du Far-West
  - It is whimsically laid out, the choice of timber partly fixing its line, but it eases access to a notable part of the Serpentine peninsula (III.14 ¶15). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: elle était capricieusement tracée, et ce fut un peu le choix des bois qui en détermina le tracé
    - quote: elle facilita l’accès d’une notable portion de la presqu’île Serpentine
  - Along it the felled oaks and elms are hauled to the Chimneys for the new ship (see [the schooner](the-schooner.md)) (III.14 ¶15). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les arbres furent transportés aux Cheminées
- **Appears in:** III.11, III.14
