*Several boxes of percussion caps from the shelf of the Tabor hut, brought back to Lincoln.*

Keywords: amorces, caps, primers, boîtes, boxes, percussion, Tabor

- **What it is.**

  - At Lincoln the packets of caps are very well received, with the barrels of powder and lead (II.15 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
