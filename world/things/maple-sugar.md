# The maple sugar: sugar refined from the maple’s sap

*Sugar made from the sap of the sugar maple, first used as a syrup, then boiled in great earthen vessels and cast in clay moulds into reddish, almost transparent loaves and tablets.*

Keywords: sucre, sucre d’érable, sugar, syrup, sirop, raffinage, refining, maple, moulds, tablettes

- **What it is.**
  - The colonists have made sugar without canes or beets, from the liquor of the sugar maple (I.20 ¶31). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: fabriqué du sucre, sans cannes ni betteraves
  - It is the only sugar at their disposal; they use the sap as it stands for cooking, and it whitens and takes a syrupy consistency as it ages (I.22 ¶43). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: d’autre sucre que cette substance liquide
    - quote: tendait à blanchir et à prendre une consistance sirupeuse
  - To crystallize it, it needs only purifying by an extremely easy operation, not a complicated refinery (I.22 ¶48). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: pour cristalliser cette liqueur, il suffisait de l’épurer
  - The result is a sugar a little russet in colour, almost transparent and of perfect taste, in loaves and tablets (I.22 ¶49). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: de couleur un peu rousse, mais presque transparent et d’un goût parfait
    - quote: formait des pains et des tablettes
- **Made from.**
  - The sap is set on the fire in great earthen vessels and simply evaporated; a scum rises, and Nab stirs it with a wooden spatula to hasten evaporation and keep it from a burnt taste (I.22 ¶48). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dans de grands vases de terre
    - quote: remuer avec une spatule de bois
  - After some hours of boiling it becomes a thick syrup poured into clay moulds, made beforehand in the kitchen furnace, of various shapes, and next day it has set (I.22 ¶49). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Après quelques heures d’ébullition
    - quote: des moules d’argile, préalablement fabriqués dans le fourneau même de la cuisine
- **Appears in:** I.20, I.22
