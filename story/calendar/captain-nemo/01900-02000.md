## The old net

- **How it was lost:** the crew laid it as a set net off the Union Bay shore, in the Nautilus's ordinary work, while she still sailed. It fouled in the bar and they could not get it back. It has been coming apart there ever since. Lincoln's port was one of her occasional ports of call. *(Settled R1121-62, R1121-63.)*
  - quote: un des ports sous-marins qui lui servaient quelquefois de points de relâche (III.16 ¶60)
- **When:** before the crew died and he came here alone, at least six years before 1865. Its year is left in Verne's discord (III.16 ¶58 against ¶62, n1) and is never dated on the page. *(Settled R1121-62.)*
  - quote: ses compagnons moururent et allèrent reposer dans leur cimetière de corail (III.16 ¶59)
- **What he knows of it:** that a net was lost in that bar years ago. *(Proposal.)* Nothing that bears on 1865: he does not know that the squalls have uncovered it, nor that anyone has seen it.
