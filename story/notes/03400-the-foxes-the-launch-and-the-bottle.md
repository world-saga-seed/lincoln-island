## Open and flags

- **Day of the first bed.** ¶62 says Jup rose "Dix jours après avoir pris le lit, le 21 août", which puts the taking to bed on 11 August; but the fight ended with dawn after a night that began on the 11th (¶15, ¶47), so Jup was found on the morning of the 12th. Card neither. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Dix jours après avoir pris le lit, le 21 août, maître Jup se leva.
  - quote: dans la nuit du 11 août
- **Distance of the bottle from the coast.** ¶123 puts the boat at "un demi-mille de la côte" and ¶130 has the bottle seized "à quelques encâblures de la côte". *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: On n’était plus qu’à un demi-mille de la côte
  - quote: à quelques encâblures de la côte
- **The language of the document.** ln3 prints the note in French, and II.13 ¶6 says it is "écrit en langue anglaise". Card neither. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Naufragé… île Tabor : 153° O. long. — 37° 11′ lat. S.
  - at: II.12 ln3
- **The flag's stars.** ¶12 says "trente-sept étoiles représentant les trente-sept états" and adds the 38th. The count is the text's. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: aux trente-sept étoiles représentant les trente-sept états de l’union
- **The five and the seven.** ¶100 says "ces cinq enfants" and ¶116–¶118 count five colonists, then six and seven with Jup and Top. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: ces cinq enfants qui lui sont tombés du ciel
- **The launch day and the bottle day.** The launch (¶87) and the trial sail with the bottle are on the same day, 10 October, as ¶90 says "le jour même". II.13 ¶17 dates the next day as 11 October. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: l’essai en allait être fait, le jour même
- **Tobacco reaches Jup.** Pencroff's pipe passes to Jup (¶70); nothing says how Jup learned to light it (¶73 says he lights it "à un charbon ardent"). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- **Wikisource corrections.** None of the three that change a fact falls here. I was not given the places of the other five. *(Drafted.)*
