# Polynesia: the archipelagos east of the Pacific the long-haul ships frequent

*The Polynesian archipelagos, whose shipping routes Tabor lies off, whose trade the Speedy’s cargo is made for, and which the escaped convicts roam.*

Keywords: Polynésie, polynésiens, archipels, Polynesian, archipelagos, routes, cabotage, Pacific

- **What it is.**
  - Long-haul ships of the Polynesian archipelagos, New Zealand and the American coast follow routes that lie clear of the islet of Tabor (III.1 ¶12). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les longs courriers des archipels polynésiens, de la Nouvelle-Zélande et de la côte américaine
  - Convicts who escape from Norfolk by seizing ships run about the Polynesian archipelagos (III.2 ¶48). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ils courent alors les archipels polynésiens
  - Its trade is the "grand cabotage", for which ships load utensils, manufactured goods and tools (III.4 ¶59). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le grand cabotage de la Polynésie
- **Appears in:** III.1, III.2, III.4, III.14
