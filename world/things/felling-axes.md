# The felling axes: the two woodcutter’s axes of the chest

*Two woodcutter’s axes, taken on the first exploration to cut a way through the thick forest.*

Keywords: haches de bûcheron, felling axes, axes, tools, forest, chest

- **What it is.**
  - There are two woodcutter’s axes in the chest (II.2 ¶53). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: 2 haches de bûcheron
  - Both are taken on the exploration to clear a way through the thick forest (II.3 ¶5). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les deux haches de bûcheron, qui devaient servir à frayer une route dans l’épaisse forêt
  - The way through the Far-West is expected to need the axe in hand (II.4 ¶1). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: marcher la hache à la main
- **Appears in:** II.2, II.3, II.4
