# The electric bullets: Nemo's secret weapon

*Electric bullets whose secret Nemo kept, used in his underwater hunts, and which struck the convicts.*

Keywords: balles électriques, ammunition, firearm, weapon, convicts

- **What it is.**
  - Nemo kept the secret of them (III.16 ¶65). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ces balles électriques dont il avait le secret
  - He used them in his underwater hunts (III.16 ¶65). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: qu’il employait dans ses chasses sous-marines
- **On the page.**
  - With them he struck down the convicts (III.16 ¶65). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: frappa les convicts de ces balles électriques
- **Appears in:** III.13, III.16
- **Open:**
  - *L'Île* does not say what the bullets are made of or how they are fired; *Vingt mille* does, carded on [Captain Nemo](../people/captain-nemo.md) (VML I.15 ¶81, ¶83). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dont il avait le secret
