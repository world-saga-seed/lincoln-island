# The warren: the natural rabbit warren at the lake’s south-west

*A slightly damp meadow of willows and aromatic herbs, found by Harbert, with burrows holding hundreds of rabbits: the colonists’ inexhaustible larder.*

Keywords: garenne, rabbits, lapins, burrows, terriers, meadow, prairie, herbs, thyme, game, warren

- **What it is.**
  - Harbert finds a natural warren toward the south-west angle of the "lagon": a slightly damp meadow covered with willows and with aromatic herbs (I.19 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une garenne naturelle, sorte de prairie légèrement humide, recouverte de saules et d’herbes aromatiques
    - quote: vers l’angle sud-ouest du lagon
  - The herbs are thyme, wild thyme, basil and savory, all of the scented labiate family that rabbits love (I.19 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: thym, serpolet, basilic, sarriette
    - quote: de la famille des labiées
  - It produces useful plants in abundance, and a naturalist would find many specimens of plant life there (I.19 ¶31). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: elle produisait en abondance des plantes utiles
  - The true site of the warren, found later the same day, is ground pierced like a skimmer with burrows (I.19 ¶35). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Le sol y était perforé comme une écumoire
  - Hundreds of small rabbit-like animals bolt from the burrows so fast that Top could not catch them (I.19 ¶40). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des centaines de petits animaux, semblables à des lapins
    - quote: Top lui-même n’aurait pu les gagner de vitesse
  - The rabbits are fairly like their European kin and are commonly known as "American rabbits" (I.19 ¶41). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des lapins assez semblables à leurs congénères d’Europe
    - quote: lapins d’Amérique
  - The rabbits are delicious and a precious resource for the colony, which seemed likely to be inexhaustible (I.19 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ils étaient délicieux
    - quote: semblait devoir être inépuisable
  - Not a day passed without the warren giving its share of rodents to Pencroff’s snares (I.20 ¶2). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il n’était pas de jour que la garenne ne fournît son contingent de rongeurs
  - The monardes from which the colonists make their tea were brought from the warren (see [the Oswego tea](../things/oswego-tea.md)) (I.20 ¶31). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les monardes rapportées de la garenne
  - Rabbits from the warren were taken in the snares almost every day, so that the table was always rabbit (I.22 ¶13). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: quelque lapin de la garenne se laissât prendre
    - quote: C’était toujours du lapin
  - It gives its usual share of game, enough to feed the whole colony (II.1 ¶36). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la garenne donnait-elle son contingent accoutumé, qui eût pu nourrir toute la colonie
- **Appears in:** I.19, I.20, I.22, II.1
- **Open:**
  - Which water the "lagon" is: I.19 ¶30 puts the warren toward "l’angle sud-ouest du lagon", and Lake Grant is called "lac" elsewhere. Which water is meant is not said. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: vers l’angle sud-ouest du lagon
