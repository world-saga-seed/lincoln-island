# The Speedy’s cargo: the goods of the pirate brig, salvaged by the colonists

*The varied cargo of a Polynesian coaster, taken by Bob Harvey with the brig, carried in chests and casks, and hauled ashore piece by piece between 18 and 21 October to enrich Granite-house.*

Keywords: cargaison, cargo, caisses, chests, barils, casks, colis, sauvetage, salvage, Speedy, richesses, assortiment, cabotage

- **What it is.**
  - When Bob Harvey took the brig she was laden with arms, munitions, utensils and tools of all sorts, meant for one of the Sandwich Islands (III.2 ¶44). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: chargé d’armes, de munitions, d’ustensiles et outils de toutes sortes, destinés à l’une des Sandwich
  - Smith thinks of her cargo as a fortune: a ship is a complete little world, and the colony’s stock will be much increased; on a large scale it will be the equivalent of the chest found at the point of the Wreck (see [the chest](the-chest.md)) (III.4 ¶23). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un navire est comme un petit monde au complet
    - quote: « en grand, » l’équivalent de la caisse trouvée à la pointe de l’Épave
  - The chests lie thick on the half-broken deck and, having been in the water for a very limited time, may not be spoiled (III.4 ¶57). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Des caisses de toutes sortes l’encombraient
    - quote: elles n’avaient séjourné dans l’eau que pendant un temps très-limité
  - The cargo is very varied, an assortment of utensils, manufactured goods and tools of the kind that ships doing the great coasting trade of Polynesia load, just what the colony needs (III.4 ¶59). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une cargaison très-variée, un assortiment d’articles de toutes sortes, ustensiles, produits manufacturés, outils
    - quote: tels que chargent les bâtiments qui font le grand cabotage de la Polynésie
  - It is not made of heavy bales but of simple packages, easy to move (III.4 ¶60). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ce n’étaient point de lourds ballots
    - quote: de simples colis
  - It is taken out through the opening in the hull with a tackle, hauled into the pirogue and carried to the beach, with no sorting at first (III.4 ¶58). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un palan qui servait à hisser les barils et les caisses
    - quote: On prenait tout, indistinctement
  - Most of the cases hold ready-made clothes, enough to dress a whole colony, with linen and shoes (see [the clothing](the-speedys-clothing.md)) (III.4 ¶69). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: La plupart contenaient des vêtements confectionnés
  - It also holds casks of tafia, hogsheads of tobacco, firearms and blades, bales of cotton, farm implements, carpenters’, joiners’ and smiths’ tools, and chests of seeds of every kind that the short stay in the water has not harmed (see [the tafia](tafia.md), [the tobacco](tobacco.md), [the arms](the-speedys-arms.md), [the cotton](cotton-bales.md), [the farm tools](the-speedys-farm-tools.md), [the trade tools](the-speedys-trade-tools.md), [the seeds](the-speedys-seeds.md)) (III.4 ¶71). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des barils de tafia, des boucauts de tabac, des armes à feu et des armes blanches, des balles de coton
    - quote: des instruments de labourage, des outils de charpentier, de menuisier, de forgeron
    - quote: des caisses de graines de toute espèce
  - The colonists, who have since tooled themselves, will still find use for it all (III.4 ¶71). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ces richesses trouveraient leur emploi
  - There is room in the stores of Granite-house, but on the first day there is no time to put it all away, and the packages are piled near the Chimneys under watch (III.4 ¶72, ¶73). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: La place ne manquait pas dans les magasins de Granite-house
    - quote: les caisses et colis entassés auprès des Cheminées
  - On 19, 20 and 21 October everything of value or use in cargo or rigging is saved: the hold is unloaded at low water and the goods stored at high water (III.4 ¶75). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: À mer basse, on déménageait la cale
    - quote: À mer haute, on emmagasinait les objets sauvés
  - The colony’s arsenal gains as much as its stores and offices (III.4 ¶76). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: l’arsenal de la colonie avait non moins gagné au sauvetage que les offices et les magasins de Granite-house
  - The brig’s destruction is a new source of riches: utensils and tools of every kind, arms and ammunition, clothes and instruments crowd the stores of Granite-house (III.14 ¶48). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: L’incident du brick détruit avait été une nouvelle source de richesses
    - quote: ustensiles et outils de toutes sortes, armes et munitions, vêtements et instruments
