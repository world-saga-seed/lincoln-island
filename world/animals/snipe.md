# Snipe: the bécassines of the marsh and lake

*Small marsh game birds found in bands in the south-east marsh and on the frozen lake, shot with arrows.*

Keywords: bécassine, snipe, marais, marsh, gibier, game, oiseau, bird, flèche

- **What it is.**
  - Snipe live in bands over the marsh waters, little afraid of men and easy to approach (I.21 ¶54). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: bécassines y vivaient par bandes
    - quote: ces volatiles peu craintifs se laissaient facilement approcher
  - Snipe flutter by thousands over the frozen lake, among ducks, pintails and guillemots (I.22 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: canards et bécassines, pilets et guillemots
    - quote: Il y en avait des milliers
  - Snipe fall to the arrows of Harbert and Gédéon Spilett (I.22 ¶57). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: bécassines, enfin gibier de poil ou de plume, tombaient sous leurs flèches
- **Appears in:** I.21, I.22
