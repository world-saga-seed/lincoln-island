# The flat knot: the knot on the Bonadventure’s anchor cable that is not Pencroff’s

*A flat knot in the rope that holds the Bonadventure’s cable to the bitt, found on 10 November.*

Keywords: nœud plat, noeud, corde, bitte, câble, Bonadventure, Pencroff

- **What it is.**
  - A rope made fast the anchor cable to the bitt itself, to keep it from slipping (III.6 ¶29). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une corde qui amarrait le câble sur la bitte même, pour l’empêcher de déraper
  - Pencroff says it is a flat knot and not his; he would swear to it (III.6 ¶28, ¶31). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ce n’est pas moi qui ai fait ce nœud
    - quote: Ceci est un nœud plat
  - Pencroff holds that a sailor’s hand does not err: it keeps the habit of its own knot (III.6 ¶33). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: On a ça dans la main, naturellement, et la main ne se trompe pas
- **Appears in:** III.6
