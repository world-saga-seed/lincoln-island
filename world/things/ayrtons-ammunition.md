# Ayrton’s ammunition: the munitions carried to the corral for him

*The arms and munitions put in the corral house for Ayrton, which are gone when he disappears.*

Keywords: munitions, Ayrton, corral, poudre, balles, arms

- **What it is.**
  - Arms, munitions and tools are carried to the corral when the house is made ready for Ayrton (II.17 ¶28). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des armes, des munitions, des outils furent transportés au corral
  - The munitions Ayrton is supplied with are gone from the corral, and gone with him (III.7 ¶54). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les munitions, dont Ayrton était approvisionné, avaient disparu avec lui
- **Appears in:** II.17, III.7
