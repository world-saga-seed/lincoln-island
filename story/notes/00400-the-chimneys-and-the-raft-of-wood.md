## Open and flags

- **"Landing place".** ¶8 says the cleft is "a few hundred paces below the place where they had landed"; I.5 ¶26 says they landed "the day before, some two hundred paces from the Chimneys". The first "débarqués" could be the crossing of the channel on the 25th (I.3 ¶60) and the second the balloon landing on the 24th. The text does not say which. Card neither. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: à quelques centaines de pas au-dessous de l’endroit où ils étaient débarqués
- **Plateau crest versus the mountain's distance.** ¶47 gives the snow-capped mountain "at a distance of six or seven miles", and forest "up to two miles from the coast". No clash within the chapter; for later distances. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: s’étendaient de vastes masses boisées
- **Two hours.** ¶64: "avant deux heures, il arrivait à l’embouchure"; ¶63 gives one o'clock as the start. Whether "avant deux heures" means before two o'clock or within two hours is not clear. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: avant deux heures, il arrivait à l’embouchure
- **Himalayan cedar in the Pacific.** ¶29 calls the deodars "very numerous in the Himalayan zone"; I.6 ¶27 repeats "déodars". Not a discord; noted for the botany. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Le jeune naturaliste reconnut plus particulièrement des « déodars »
- **Spelling.** The print has "très-dur", "très-adhérents" with the hyphen; kept as is in quotes. *(Drafted.)*
