# Bustards: the outardes among the game

*Big game birds shot with arrows, of the houbara kind, a fine pair of which is kept in the poultry-yard.*

Keywords: outarde, bustard, houbara, mantelet, gibier, game, flèche, basse-cour

- **What it is.**
  - Bustards are among the game, with agoutis, kangaroos, capybaras, pigeons, wild ducks and snipe, that fall to the arrows of Harbert and Gédéon Spilett (I.22 ¶57). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: outardes, canards sauvages
  - By the end of the summer the poultry-yard holds a fine pair of bustards of the "houbara" kind, marked by a sort of mantle of feathers (II.8 ¶45). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un beau couple d’outardes, qui appartenaient à l’espèce « houbara »
    - quote: caractérisée par une sorte de mantelet de plumes
- **Appears in:** I.22, II.8
