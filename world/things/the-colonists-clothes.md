# The colonists’ clothes: the garments they wore when the balloon threw them down

*The only clothes the colonists own, warm and strong but wearing out: the open question of the first winter.*

Keywords: vêtements, habits, clothes, clothing, chaussures, shoes, laine, wool, peaux, skins, winter, cold

- **What it is.**
  - The question of clothes and shoes is called a grave one (I.19 ¶21). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: La question des vêtements et des chaussures, — question grave assurément
  - The colonists have no clothes but those they wore when the balloon threw them on the island (I.20 ¶3). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Les colons n’avaient d’autres habits que ceux qu’ils portaient, quand le ballon les jeta sur l’île
  - The clothes are warm and strong and kept in perfect cleanliness, but would soon need replacing (I.20 ¶3). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ces habits étaient chauds et solides
    - quote: tout cela demanderait bientôt à être remplacé
  - Smith’s ingenuity is at a loss; the first winter must be borne without too much complaint (I.20 ¶4). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: l’ingéniosité de Cyrus Smith fut en défaut
  - The plan is to hunt the mouflons in the fine season and, once the wool is gathered, make warm, solid stuffs (I.20 ¶4). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une fois la laine récoltée, l’ingénieur saurait bien fabriquer de chaudes et solides étoffes
  - The seal skins from the islet are to be made into strong shoes (see [the sealskins](sealskins.md)) (I.20 ¶21). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: cette peau devant servir à la fabrication de solides chaussures
  - In the intense cold they are said to be insufficiently clad (I.22 ¶1). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des gens insuffisamment vêtus
  - The clothes have lasted the winter but will not last to the next; skins of carnivores or wool of ruminants must be got at any price (I.22 ¶59). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: avaient passé l’hiver, mais ils ne pourraient pas durer jusqu’à l’hiver prochain
    - quote: Peaux de carnassiers ou laine de ruminants
- **Appears in:** I.19, I.20, I.22
