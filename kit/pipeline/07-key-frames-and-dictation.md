# 07 Key frames and dictation

*Refinement. Add this when a chapter is graded thin or none.*

**When:** for every chapter graded thin or none, in story order, with the writer at the keyboard.

**Uses:** [dictation](../../plan/workflows/dictation.md), then [ripple](../../plan/workflows/ripple.md); the idea is in [kit/method/key-frames.md](../method/key-frames.md).

**What happens:** Claude writes a brief, the writer dictates the scene, and the dictation is carded ([dictation](../../plan/workflows/dictation.md)); after a run of them, a ripple scan.

**Outputs:** new key frames, carded. The kit's example (in the kit's repository, `example/story/notes/`) has the key frames of its arc and a brief for chapter 00700.

**Done when:** no chapter in the horizon is graded none; the ripple scan has run.
