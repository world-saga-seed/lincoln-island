# The beacon fire: the glow on Lincoln that guides the Bonadventure home

*A bright fire seen twenty miles off to the north-east about two in the morning, lit evidently by Smith, which gives Pencroff the bearing of Lincoln.*

Keywords: feu, fire, lueur, glow, signal, phare, beacon, nuit, night, Lincoln, retour, return

- **What it is.**
  - About two in the morning Pencroff, at the helm, sees a bright glow twenty miles to the north-east, and cries "Un feu !" (II.14 ¶105, ¶106, ¶107). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Vers deux heures du matin
    - quote: une vive lueur apparaissait à vingt milles dans le nord-est
  - The narrator says it is evidently lit by Smith, and shows the route to follow (II.14 ¶107). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: cette lueur, évidemment allumée par Cyrus Smith, montrait la route à suivre
  - It shines above the horizon like a star of the first magnitude (II.14 ¶108). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: comme une étoile de première grandeur
  - The colonists of the Bonadventure believe that Smith lit it on the Granite-house plateau in the night of 19 to 20 October to guide them in; without it they would have passed to leeward of the island (II.19 ¶83). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la précaution que vous avez prise d’allumer un feu dans la nuit du 19 au 20 octobre, sur le plateau de Granite-house
    - quote: nous aurions passé sous le vent de l’île
  - Smith says in private that he never lit a fire that night, neither on the plateau of Granite-house nor in any other part of the island (II.19 ¶88). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: je n’ai jamais allumé de feu dans la nuit du 19 au 20 octobre
  - Spilett, Harbert and Pencroff all saw it, and it served to find the island’s situation on a dark night (II.20 ¶3). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ce feu, il l’avait bien réellement vu
    - quote: Ce feu leur avait servi à reconnaître la situation de l’île pendant cette nuit sombre
  - Spilett holds it was certainly a fire lit by the hand of man, not a partial eruption or a meteor, as Smith suggests (II.20 ¶35, ¶36). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: N’était-ce pas une éruption partielle du volcan, un météore quelconque
    - quote: c’était certainement un feu allumé de main d’homme
  - It shone like a star of the first magnitude, very bright, projecting a kind of electric sheet, and was placed on the heights of Granite-house; it was not a star, for the sky was covered with big clouds and a star would not have been so low on the horizon (II.20 ¶59, ¶61, ¶62, ¶63). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ce feu brillait comme une étoile de première grandeur
    - quote: il projetait comme une nappe électrique
    - quote: il était certainement placé sur les hauteurs de Granite-house
    - quote: le ciel était couvert de gros nuages
  - Smith says that in that night neither he nor Nab lit a fire on the coast and that they did not leave Granite-house (II.20 ¶64, ¶66). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ni Nab, ni moi, nous n’avons allumé un feu sur la côte
    - quote: Nous n’avons pas quitté Granite-house
  - It makes the colonists admit a mystery, an inexplicable influence evidently favourable to them (II.20 ¶68). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Une influence inexplicable, évidemment favorable aux colons
- **Appears in:** II.14, II.15, II.19, II.20
- **Open:**
  - The fire is seen on the night of 18 to 19 October; the next chapter has the boat land at seven in the morning "the next day, 20 October, after four days’ voyage"; card neither (II.14 ¶102; II.15 ¶1). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: La nuit du 18 au 19 fut obscure et froide
    - quote: Le lendemain, — 20 octobre, — à sept heures du matin, après quatre jours de voyage
  - The date of the fire: the earlier chapters give the night of 18 to 19 October for the sighting (II.14 ¶102), and II.19 ¶83, ¶88 and II.20 ¶64 give the night of 19 to 20 October; card neither (II.19 ¶83; II.20 ¶64). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dans la nuit du 19 au 20 octobre
    - quote: pendant cette nuit du 19 au 20 octobre
