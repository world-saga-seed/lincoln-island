# The kitchen garden: the plateau's vegetable beds, in small squares

*The vegetable garden of the Grande-Vue plateau, well kept, watered and defended against birds, divided into small squares of lettuces, vitelottes, sorrel, radishes, horseradish and other crucifers.*

Keywords: potager, garden, vegetables, légumes, carrés, laitues, vitelottes, oseille, raves, raifort

- **What it is.**
  - It is well kept, well watered and well defended against the birds, and divided into small squares where grow lettuces, vitelottes, sorrel, radishes, horseradish and other crucifers (II.8 ¶43). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Le potager, maintenant bien entretenu, bien arrosé, bien défendu contre les oiseaux, était divisé en petits carrés
  - The earth of the plateau is prodigiously fertile, and good harvests are expected (II.8 ¶43). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: La terre, sur ce plateau, était prodigieusement féconde
  - The unknown works in it with the tilling tools (II.16 ¶7). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: L’inconnu avait commencé à se servir des outils de labourage, et il travaillait au potager
  - The seeds Harbert gathered on Tabor are carefully sown, and the plateau becomes a vast kitchen garden, well laid out and well kept (see [the Tabor seeds](../things/tabor-seeds.md)) (II.16 ¶79). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Le plateau formait alors un vaste potager, bien dessiné, bien entretenu
  - As the vegetables multiply, the simple squares are enlarged until they tend to become true fields and to replace the meadows (II.16 ¶79). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il avait fallu agrandir les simples carrés, qui tendaient à devenir de véritables champs et à remplacer les prairies
  - It is better to turn the Grande-Vue plateau into a kitchen garden, defended by its deep belt of creeks, and to carry the meadows, which need no protection from quadrumana and quadrupeds, outside it (II.16 ¶79). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Mieux valait, d’ailleurs, transformer en potager le plateau de Grande-Vue, défendu par sa profonde ceinture de creeks
    - quote: reporter en dehors les prairies qui n’avaient pas besoin d’être protégées contre les déprédations des quadrumanes et des quadrupèdes
- **Appears in:** II.8, II.16
