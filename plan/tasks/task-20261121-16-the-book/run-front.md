# About This Book

This book records how the added chapter of *Lincoln Island*, this project’s edition of Verne’s *Mysterious Island*, was made, and sets it beside what it was measured against. The novel is published separately.

Three chapters are given here, each at every stage the run made or used:

- **XIX** and **XX**, Verne’s chapters on either side of the insertion;
- **XIX bis**, “The Float in the Bar”, the chapter inserted, which has no French original.

For each chapter, in this order:

- **White’s translation (1876)**, as he printed it, where he has the chapter;
- **the Opus control** and **the Gemini control**, the baseline: two AI models, Claude Opus and Gemini, each given the same prompt, a few lines as someone who knows the book would write them, with Verne’s French where there is one and White’s neighbouring chapters pasted in whole, and no cards, no ledger and no checks;
- **version 1**, the first finished version by the World Saga Seed method, drafted from the cards (the planning notes for the chapter) and the ledger (the author’s recorded decisions), and revised after its checks for facts, voice and omissions;
- for XIX bis only, two more stages: **after the human’s read-aloud**, when the human read version 1 aloud and the chapter was revised from what was read, and **after the character check**, when each new turn the read-aloud added was tested against what the colonists would do;
- **the final**, as it stands in the novel.

After the chapters come the three chapters’ cards, as they now stand; the ledger, every ruling, one row each, in the order it was made; the blind scores, taken by the human, who put each version to Gemini cold, without saying which was which; and the human’s read-aloud changes, as the human sent them to the author.
