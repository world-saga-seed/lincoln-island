# The veranda: the creeper-covered shelter Nab built on the plateau's edge

*A veranda covered with climbing plants that Nab raised with his own hands on the edge of the Grande-Vue plateau, where the colonists sit in the summer evenings.*

Keywords: vérandah, veranda, Nab, plateau, evening, creepers, plantes grimpantes

- **What it is.**
  - It is a kind of veranda covered with climbing plants, raised by Nab with his own hands on the edge of the Grande-Vue plateau, where the colonists sit on summer evenings (II.8 ¶47). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: sous une sorte de vérandah couverte de plantes grimpantes, que Nab avait élevée de ses propres mains
    - quote: sur la lisière du plateau de Grande-Vue
  - The colonists gather under it after dinner, at the plateau’s edge, to watch night rise on the horizon (II.9 ¶69). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: étaient réunis sous la vérandah, à la lisière du plateau de Grande-Vue
- **Appears in:** II.8, II.9
