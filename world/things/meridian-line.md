# The meridian line: a north-south line fixed with two trees

*A fixed line of two trees lined up with the sun at noon, fixed on 2 April to give the north.*

Keywords: méridienne, north, orientation, noon, bearing, sun line, compass point

- **What it is.**
  - It is an invariable meridian line for later operations (I.13 ¶35). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une méridienne invariable pour ses opérations ultérieures
  - It is fixed by two trees lined up with the sun (I.13 ¶35). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: en mettant l’un par l’autre avec le soleil deux arbres qui devaient lui servir de repères
- **Made from.**
  - It was made on 2 April (I.13 ¶33). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ce fut dans la journée du 2 avril que Cyrus Smith s’occupa de fixer l’orientation de l’île
  - The point of the sky the sun occupies at noon is the north (I.13 ¶34). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le point du ciel qu’il occuperait à ce moment serait le nord
  - The refraction of the sun was taken into account (I.13 ¶34). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: en tenant compte de la réfraction
- **Leads to.**
  - It fixes the orientation of the island (I.13 ¶33). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: fixer l’orientation de l’île
- **Appears in:** I.13
- **Open:**
  - The text and the editor's note disagree on the length of the night on 1 to 2 April. The text gives twelve hours twenty-four minutes between sunset and sunrise; the note gives sunrise at 5 h 33 and sunset at 6 h 17 in the evening. Neither is carded, nor the six hours twelve minutes to noon that follows from the first.
    - quote: douze heures vingt-quatre minutes s’étaient écoulées
    - quote: le soleil se levait à 5 h. 33 m. du matin, et se couchait à 6 h. 17 m. du soir
