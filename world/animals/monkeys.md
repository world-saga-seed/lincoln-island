# Monkeys: the unafraid bands of the western forest

*Vigorous, agile herbivorous quadrumanes in bands in the forest beyond the Mercy, that watch men without fear, as if they had never seen them.*

Keywords: singe, singes, monkey, quadrumane, quadrumanes, bandes, herbivores, gibier, Far-West, frères dégénérés

- **What it is.**
  - Pencroff, if his eyes did not deceive him, sees bands of monkeys running under the thickets of the Mercy's upper forest (II.3 ¶58). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: si les yeux du marin ne le trompèrent pas, il crut apercevoir des bandes de singes qui couraient sous les taillis
  - Two or three stop and look at the colonists without showing any terror, as if seeing men for the first time and not yet having learned to fear them (II.3 ¶58). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: sans manifester aucune terreur
    - quote: comme si, voyant des hommes pour la première fois, ils n’avaient pas encore appris à les redouter
  - They are vigorous and extremely agile, and might be redoubtable if provoked (II.3 ¶58). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ces singes, vigoureux, doués d’une extrême agilité, pouvaient être redoutables
  - The narrator says they are exclusively herbivorous and excellent game (II.3 ¶59). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ces animaux, qui sont uniquement herbivores, forment un gibier excellent
  - They are called "quadrumanes" (II.3 ¶58; II.4 ¶7). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ces quadrumanes
    - quote: ces agiles et robustes quadrumanes
  - They are numerous, show lively astonishment at the sight of the men, whose look is new to them, and bound from branch to branch; they show no hostile disposition (II.4 ¶7). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des bandes de singes qui semblaient marquer le plus vif étonnement à la vue de ces hommes
    - quote: Ces singes étaient nombreux
    - quote: ils ne manifestèrent aucune disposition hostile
- **Appears in:** II.3, II.4
