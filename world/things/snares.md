# The snares: loops of plant fibre set at the warren

*Snares (collets) of woody fibre, later of curry-jonc, made by Pencroff and set at the warren’s burrows; they brought more game than the traps.*

Keywords: collets, snares, nooses, loops, rabbit, hunting, fibre, warren, garenne

- **What it is.**
  - At the first visit to the warren the colonists have no snares, nor the means of making them (I.19 ¶40). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: pas de collets, ni de quoi en fabriquer
  - Pencroff has made snares of woody fibres, and not a day passes without the warren giving its share of rodents (see [the warren](../places/the-warren.md)) (I.20 ¶2). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Il avait fabriqué des collets de fibres ligneuses
  - The hunters of the marsh trip carry snares with spears, bows and arrows (I.21 ¶8). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: armés d’épieux, de collets, d’arcs et de flèches
  - He made them also of curry-jonc fibres (see [the curry-jonc](curry-jonc.md)), and they gave more profit than the traps; rarely a day passed without a rabbit from the warren caught in one (I.22 ¶13). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des collets en employant les fibres du curry-jonc
    - quote: les collets donnèrent plus de profit que les trappes
- **Appears in:** I.19, I.20, I.21, I.22
