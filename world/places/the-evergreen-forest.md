# The evergreen forest: the conifer forest upstream of the river mouth

*A forest of deodars and pines, green in autumn, with dead wood heaped under it, beginning at the first sharp bend of the river.*

Keywords: forêt, conifers, woodland, deodar, pine, dead wood, riverbank, tall grass

- **What it is.**
  - It begins at the river's sharp bend, a quarter of an hour's walk up the left bank from the mouth (I.4 ¶29). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: À partir de ce point, son cours se poursuivait à travers une forêt d’arbres magnifiques
  - Its trees are conifers and kept their green despite the late season (I.4 ¶29). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ils appartenaient à cette famille des conifères
  - Among them are deodars, giving off an agreeable aroma, and clumps of pines (I.4 ¶29). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: répandaient un agréable arôme
  - Dry branches in the tall grass crackle underfoot like fireworks (I.4 ¶29). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: qui crépitaient comme des pièces d’artifice
  - Enormous quantities of dead wood lie at the trees' feet, so that there is no need to lop them (I.4 ¶32). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: d’énormes quantités de bois mort gisaient à leurs pieds
  - It reaches from the mountain's first slopes to two miles from the coast (I.4 ¶47). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: jusqu’à deux milles de la côte
- **On the page.**
  - A mass of tall trees was visible from the islet to the right of the cut face (I.3 ¶53). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la masse confuse de grands arbres
  - The grass on the bank had probably never been trodden by a man (I.4 ¶38). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le pied d’un homme ne s’était, probablement, jamais hasardé
- **Appears in:** I.3, I.4
- **Open:**
  - I.4 does not name this forest. I.13 ¶36 names the wood of the Jacamar as the part of the forest on the left bank of the Mercy, and I.4 ¶28 puts this forest on the left bank of the river at the Chimneys; the card does not join them.
    - quote: ils commencèrent à remonter la rive gauche de la rivière
