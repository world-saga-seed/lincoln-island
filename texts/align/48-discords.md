### 1. cut: the sun at the Tropic of Capricorn (III.6 ¶2; White ¶2)
- The French says the sun was reaching the Tropic of Capricorn and giving the longest days; White keeps the longest days and drops the sun's position. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Le soleil arrivait sur le tropique du Capricorne et donnait les plus longs jours de l’année.
  - white: They were now at the longest days in the year

### 2. changed: Ayrton leaves at dawn, not in twilight about nine (III.6 ¶7; White ¶4)
- The French has Ayrton set out on the 9th at dawn; White has him drive off in the twilight, about 9 o'clock. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Ayrton partit donc le 9 dès l’aube
  - white: So Ayrton drove off in the twilight, about 9 o’clock

### 3. cut: the hour of departure (III.6 ¶15; White ¶9)
- The French says the three left Granite-house about three o'clock; White gives only the afternoon. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: vers trois heures, tous trois quittèrent Granite-house
  - white: So off the three went in the afternoon of November 10.

### 4. cut: why the fugitives were not found (III.6 ¶17; White ¶10)
- The French gives the reason: the convicts, not yet knowing the colonists' number or defences, had probably gone to the least accessible parts of the island; White leaves only that no trace was found. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: n’étant pas encore fixés sur le nombre des colons et sur les moyens de défense dont ils disposaient
  - white: but they found no trace of the fugitives.

### 5. changed: "on top of or inside" becomes "above or below" (III.6 ¶18; White ¶10)
- The French says Port-Ballon cannot be found from sea or land unless one is on top of it or inside it; White says it can be seen only from directly above or below. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: à moins d’être dessus ou dedans
