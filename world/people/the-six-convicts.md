# The six convicts: the Speedy's survivors landed on the right bank

*Six men of the second boat, wrecked at the Mercy's mouth, who reach land armed while the brig is sunk with the rest.*

Keywords: six convicts, survivors, Speedy, pointe de l’Épave, Mercy, right bank, rive droite, évadés, armés
