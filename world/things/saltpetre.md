# The saltpetre: potassium nitrate, in layers on the island

*Nitrate of potash, of which the island holds several layers; it makes the tinder more inflammable.*

Keywords: nitrate de potasse, salpêtre, potassium nitrate, niter, mineral, gunpowder ingredient

- **What it is.**
  - The island holds several layers of it (I.13 ¶56). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dont l’île possédait plusieurs couches
  - It is nothing other than saltpetre (I.13 ¶56). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: qui n’est autre chose que du salpêtre
  - Smith needs potassium nitrate, better known as nitre salt or saltpetre, for his later preparation (I.17 ¶47). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: plus connu sous le nom de sel de nitre ou de salpêtre
  - Harbert found a bed of it in the north of the island, at the foot of Mont Franklin; it only had to be purified (I.17 ¶48). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Harbert en découvrit un gisement dans le nord de l’île, au pied du mont Franklin
    - quote: il n’y eut plus qu’à purifier ce sel
  - Attacked by sulphuric acid and distilled, it gives nitric acid (see [the nitric acid](nitric-acid.md)) (I.17 ¶55). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le salpêtre, attaqué par l’acide sulfurique
- **On the page.**
  - The engineer used it to impregnate the dried armoise (see [the tinder](tinder.md)) (I.13 ¶56). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: l’ingénieur l’eut imprégnée de ce nitrate de potasse
- **Appears in:** I.13, I.17
- **Open:**
  - The place of these layers is not stated.
    - quote: plusieurs couches
