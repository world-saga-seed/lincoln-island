# The west coast: a shore of forest, with no cliff, reef or beach

*The island’s west shore as the colonists first see it on 31 October: the forest itself is the coast, planted on a granite base above the highest seas, with the Falls-river dropping into a small cove.*

Keywords: côte occidentale, west shore, littoral, forest coast, lisière, Pacific, falaise, sea, trees, swell

- **What it is.**
  - Smith estimates the west coast to lie at least five miles from where the pirogue then is (II.3 ¶31). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: distante, suivant son estime, de cinq milles au moins
  - It contrasts with the east coast: no granite wall, no reef offshore, not even a sand beach (II.4 ¶34). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Plus de muraille de granit, aucun écueil au large, pas même une grève de sable
  - The forest forms the shore, and its last trees, beaten by the waves, lean over the water (II.4 ¶34). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: La forêt formait le littoral
    - quote: se penchaient sur les eaux
  - The bank is raised above the level of the highest seas, on a base of granite, and the trees stand as firmly as those inland (II.4 ¶34). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: La berge était surélevée de manière à dominer le niveau des plus grandes mers
    - quote: supporté par une base de granit
  - The Falls-river drops into the sea here through a small cove (see [the Falls-river](riviere-de-la-chute.md)) (II.4 ¶35). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: tombaient d’une hauteur de plus de quarante pieds
  - North of the Falls-river the forest edge runs about two miles; then the trees thin and picturesque heights follow an almost straight line running north and south (II.4 ¶36). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: se prolongeait sur un espace de deux milles environ
    - quote: qui courait nord et sud
  - Between the Falls-river and the promontoire du Reptile it is only wooded masses, the trees upright or leaning, their roots bathed by the long swell (II.4 ¶36). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ce n’était que masses boisées
    - quote: dont la longue ondulation de la mer venait baigner les racines
  - This stretch, unlike the arid and wild northern one, offers refuges that castaways might use (II.4 ¶36). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: cette partie du littoral offrait des refuges que l’autre, aride et sauvage, eût évidemment refusés à des naufragés
  - The distance from the mouth of the Falls-river to the promontoire du Reptile is about twelve miles; on a passable beach it would take four hours, but the trees to turn, the brushwood to cut and the lianas to break doubled the time (II.4 ¶39). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: douze milles environ
    - quote: les arbres à tourner, les broussailles à couper, les lianes à rompre
  - It shows nothing of a recent wreck, though the sea could have carried everything off (II.4 ¶40). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il n’y avait rien qui témoignât d’un naufrage récent sur ce littoral
  - Game with fur no longer shows on the strip, but birds swarm there, and there is hardly a tree without a nest (II.4 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le gibier de poil ne se montrait plus sur cette lisière
    - quote: Pas un arbre qui n’eût un nid
  - Smith had believed the west shore might offer refuge to a ship in distress or on a regular course; since it offers no landfall, the south shore must be searched (II.5 ¶5). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le rivage occidental pouvait offrir refuge, soit à un bâtiment en détresse
    - quote: ce littoral ne présentait aucun atterrage
- **Appears in:** II.3, II.4, II.5
