# The carob trees: new trees of the Far-West, their pods eaten by the onaggas

*Carob trees found by Harbert on 16 February in the Far-West forest, not seen before on the island, whose long pods the onaggas crop and whose pulp is sweet and excellent.*

Keywords: caroubiers, carob, gousses, pods, pulpe, onaggas, Harbert, essences nouvelles, new species

- **What it is.**
  - Harbert finds on 16 February new kinds of trees, whose presence had not yet been noted on the island, among them carob trees (III.11 ¶51, ¶53). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Harbert découvrit des essences nouvelles, dont la présence n’avait pas encore été signalée dans l’île
    - quote: des caroubiers
  - The onaggas crop their long pods greedily, and the pods give sweet pulps of excellent taste (III.11 ¶53). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dont les onaggas broutèrent avec avidité les longues gousses
    - quote: fournirent des pulpes sucrées d’un goût excellent
