# Large beasts: the unseen fauves of the slopes and forest

*Beasts of prey, not yet seen, whose tracks mark the mountain's lower slopes and whom the colonists expect in the forest.*

Keywords: fauve, bête, beast, prey, empreintes, tracks, griffes, claws, dangereux, dangerous, jaguar

- **What it is.**
  - Tracks showing the recent passage of large animals, "fauves ou autres", are found on the mountain's lower slopes (I.10 ¶27). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des empreintes qui indiquaient le passage récent de grands animaux, fauves ou autres
  - The animals that frequent the heights are of the sure-footed, supple-backed kind, chamois or izard; traces are many (I.10 ¶33). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Les animaux qui fréquentaient ces hauteurs — et les traces ne manquaient pas
  - Certainly some dangerous animals live in the thick forests of the island (I.12 ¶16). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: quelques animaux dangereux habitaient ces épaisses forêts de l’île
  - On the march through the Jacamar Wood wild boars are seen, but it does not seem that redoubtable fauves are to be met (I.15 ¶38). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il ne semblait pas que l’on dût rencontrer de fauves redoutables
  - Cyrus Smith fears that dangerous animals abound on the island, if no man lives there (I.16 ¶7). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: je crains que les animaux dangereux n’y abondent
  - On the north rim of the lake no fauve shows itself, and it is probable that such animals haunt rather the thick forests of the south (I.16 ¶29). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: aucun fauve ne se montra
    - quote: ces animaux fréquentaient plutôt les épaisses forêts du sud
- **Appears in:** I.10, I.12, I.15, I.16
