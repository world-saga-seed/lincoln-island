# 10 Read

*Basic.*

**When:** once a volume is refined, before it is accepted.

**Uses:** [reader-tests](../workflows/reader-tests.md) (the blind probe, Basic; the reread test and the closed-book quiz, Refinements); as Refinements, [series-critic](../workflows/series-critic.md), [interest-check](../workflows/interest-check.md), [ereader](../workflows/ereader.md), [voice-render](../workflows/voice-render.md) (optional).

**What happens:** blind readers test the prose against the spec, failures route back ([reader-tests](../workflows/reader-tests.md#how-failures-route-back)), and the writer reads.

**Outputs:** the scored reader-test tables, the merged interest check, the trims with their seams checked, the book, the filed comments. Examples: [the probe](../example/reports/probe-00100-00400.md), [the interest table](../example/reports/interest-merged.md).

**Done when:** every reshoot and trim the writer picked is done or scheduled; the writer's comments are filed.

**Watch for:** ranking the book by model scores; cutting a clue to lift one.
