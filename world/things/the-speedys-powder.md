# The Speedy’s powder: twenty copper-lined barrels from the stern

*The brig’s powder, kept in the stern powder-room; some twenty barrels lined with copper come out of the wreck unharmed and save Smith his own powder.*

Keywords: poudre, powder, barils, barrels, cuivre, copper, soute, magazine, Speedy

- **What it is.**
  - Ayrton reckons that a ship in the pirates’ trade can hardly lack powder, and sees barrels of it in the room (III.2 ¶62, ¶71). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: La poudre ne devait pas manquer à un navire qui faisait un pareil métier
    - quote: ces tonneaux de poudre
  - Smith expects that powder, which is usually enclosed in metal casings, will not have suffered from the water, and some barrels may be saved; it proves so (III.4 ¶61, ¶62). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: est ordinairement enfermée dans des enveloppes de métal
    - quote: Ce fut, en effet, ce qui était arrivé
  - About twenty barrels, lined inside with copper, are found among the projectiles and taken out with care (III.4 ¶62). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une vingtaine de barils, dont l’intérieur était garni de cuivre
    - quote: qui furent extraits avec précaution
  - Smith had meant to blow up the hull to gather the wreckage on the coast, but a gale and heavy sea let him spare his powder (III.4 ¶77). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: lui permirent d’économiser sa poudre
- **Appears in:** III.2, III.4
