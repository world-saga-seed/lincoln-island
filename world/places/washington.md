# Washington: the capital of the Union, whose meridian the colonists keep time by

*The American capital: its meridian is nearly Richmond's and the colonists' reference for the longitude; Lincoln dies there.*

Keywords: Washington, capital, Union, meridian, longitude, 77° 3′ 11″, Greenwich, time, Lincoln

- **What it is.**
  - A frightful crime would be committed there sixteen days after 30 March, and Lincoln fall to a fanatic's bullet on Good Friday (I.11 ¶92). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un crime effroyable serait commis à Washington
  - Richmond lies on a meridian that is about Washington's (I.12 ¶9). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: méridien qui est à peu près celui de Washington
  - On 16 April, when true time and mean time coincide, the hour shown by Spilett's watch would be the true hour at Washington (I.14 ¶70). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: l’heure donnée par Gédéon Spilett serait l’heure vraie qu’il serait alors à Washington
  - It is five hours of time from the island: noon on the island is five in the evening at Washington (I.14 ¶74). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il était midi à l’île Lincoln, quand il était déjà cinq heures du soir à Washington
  - Washington lies at 77° 3′ 11″, say seventy-seven degrees from the Greenwich meridian (I.14 ¶75). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Washington est par 77° 3′ 11″
- **Appears in:** I.11, I.12, I.14
