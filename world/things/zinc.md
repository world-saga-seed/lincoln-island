# The zinc: the metal of the chest’s lining, spent on the telegraph battery

*The zinc casing of the chest, evidently soldered with care to keep out damp, which Smith uses in 1867 for his battery.*

Keywords: zinc, enveloppe, caisse, doublure, pile, métal, soudé, battery

- **What it is.**
  - A second casing of zinc lines the inside of the chest, evidently so that the contents should be safe from damp in all circumstances (see [the chest](the-chest.md)) (II.2 ¶45). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Une seconde enveloppe en zinc doublait l’intérieur de la caisse
  - Smith says it could not be better used than for the battery (II.18 ¶40). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la caisse trouvée à la pointe de l’Épave était doublée d’une enveloppe de ce métal, qui ne pouvait pas être mieux utilisée que dans cette circonstance
  - In the battery it is cut into plates, one dipped in nitric acid and the other in the potash solution (see [the battery](the-telegraph-battery.md)) (II.18 ¶44). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Cyrus Smith prit ensuite deux lames de zinc
- **Appears in:** II.2, II.18
