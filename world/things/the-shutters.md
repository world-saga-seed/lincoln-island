# The shutters: the thick shutters that close the facade

*Thick shutters of the facade’s windows, shutting out wind and rain, which Smith can disguise at need.*

Keywords: volets, shutters, windows, facade, wind, rain, closure, winter

- **What it is.**
  - Smith intends to close the openings with thick shutters that let neither wind nor rain through and that he could disguise at need (I.19 ¶13). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des volets épais, qui ne laisseraient passer ni le vent, ni la pluie
    - quote: qu’il pourrait dissimuler au besoin
  - The thick shutters close the windows of the facade (I.19 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: D’épais volets permettaient de fermer les fenêtres de la façade
  - In the cold of August every opening of the facade is stopped hermetically, leaving only the strict passage to renew the air (I.22 ¶41). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: obstruer hermétiquement toutes les ouvertures de la façade
    - quote: le strict passage au renouvellement de l’air
  - On 3 November the windows' shutters are shut, seen through curtains of foliage; the apes later push them back (II.6 ¶21, ¶43). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les fenêtres, dont les volets étaient clos
    - quote: dont ils avaient repoussé les volets
- **In XIX bis.** *(The inserted chapter, 01950; R1121-132.)*
  - On 2 June the shutters are kept closed from first light against the rain on an east-north-east wind (XIX bis ¶21). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: from first light the thick shutters were kept closed
- **Appears in:** I.19, I.22, II.6, XIX bis
