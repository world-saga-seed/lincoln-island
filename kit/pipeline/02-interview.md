# 02 Interview

*Basic.*

**When:** after the intake; one or two sessions.

**Uses:** [kit/interview/README.md](../interview/README.md) (the rules) and [kit/interview/questions.md](../interview/questions.md) (the script).

**What happens:** first, `plan/settled/ledger/` and `plan/state.md` are made from the templates ([the first act](../interview/README.md#the-first-act)); then questions by section, each answer a ruling on the ledger, each section read back ([the rules](../interview/README.md#the-rules-of-the-interview)).

**Outputs:** `plan/interview.md`; ledger rows; the first lines of `CLAUDE.md`'s "About the writer", the voice sheet and the arc outline. The kit's example (in the kit's repository, `example/interview-record.md`) has one.

**Done when:** every section was read back and agreed; open questions are held in `plan/state.md`.
