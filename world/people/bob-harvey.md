# Bob Harvey: Ayrton's old accomplice, chief of the Speedy's pirates

*A former master mariner turned convict and sea-robber, who seized the brig Speedy off Norfolk Island and commands her convict crew.*

Keywords: Bob Harvey, Harvey, convict, pirate, chief, Speedy, Norfolk, capitaine au long cours, Ayrton
