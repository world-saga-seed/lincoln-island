## a

After: they delayed the departure a week, that is, till November 20.

The guns were tried on the 8th (chapter XLVII), and Ayrton left for the corral on the 9th. From either day to the 20th is twelve or eleven days, not a week, nor the nine days of the next paragraph. The 20th was never reached, for the colonists set out on the 11th, when Ayrton did not come back. I find nothing to say whether the date or the delay is wrong, and leave it open.
