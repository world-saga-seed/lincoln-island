# Timeline fragment i: the French II.6 to II.9 (02800 to 03100)

Dates are 1865 for II.6 and II.7, 1866 for II.8 and II.9 where the text or the count of months gives the year; the text states "l’année 1866" once (II.8 ¶6).

## 02800 (II.6)

- 1865-11-01 to 1865-11-02, night: the colonists, finding the ladder gone, sleep badly in the Chimneys (II.6 ¶17). *(My reading, to confirm.)* The date rests on II.7 ¶9 and ¶11 (the ladder "renvoyée hier" on the morning of 3 November).
  - quote: les colons, malgré leur lassitude, dormirent bien sur le sable des Cheminées
- Seven months on the island, at the time of the ladder's loss (II.6 ¶12). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: depuis sept mois qu’ils habitaient l’île
- 1865-11-02, before 5 a.m.: the windows show through their foliage curtains at dawn (II.6 ¶21). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: avant cinq heures, les fenêtres
- 1865-11-02, about two hours after the arrow and the first shots: breakfast time; a half-hour hunt (II.6 ¶55–¶58). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: Deux heures se passèrent
  - quote: Au bout d’une demi-heure
- 1865-11-02, about two hours after breakfast: nothing changed (II.6 ¶60). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: Deux heures plus tard
- 1865-11-02, after noon: the colonists leave the Chimneys for the overflow; the apes flee; the ladder comes down; Jup is taken (II.6 ¶68). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: Il était déjà plus de midi

## 02900 (II.7)

- 1865-11-02, "la veille" of the 3rd: Port Ballon "discovered" (II.7 ¶14). *(Drafted.)* Flagged: on 2 November II.6 has the colonists at Granite-house and the Chimneys all day; both lines stand.
  - quote: découvert la veille sur la côte méridionale

- Late December 1865: the onaggas first harnessed; the envelope and rigging brought home; back at 8 p.m. (II.7 ¶61–¶64). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: ce fut vers la fin de décembre qu’on essaya pour la première fois les onaggas

## 03000 (II.8)

- 1866-01-01 to 1866-01-07, the first week of January: linen made (II.8 ¶1). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: La première semaine de janvier fut consacrée à la confection du linge
- January 1866: the year 1866 begins with persistent heat and good hunting (II.8 ¶6). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Avec le début de l’année 1866
- January 1866 (undated): salmon run in the Mercy (II.8 ¶19). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Des saumons vinrent par bandes s’aventurer dans la Mercy
- Late January 1866: corral works begun (II.8 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Ce fut vers la fin de janvier que les colons entreprirent de grands travaux
- Late January to about 20 February 1866: the corral takes "trois semaines" (II.8 ¶35). *(My reading, to confirm.)* Flagged: the battue on 7 February is dated before three weeks from the end of January run out; both lines stand.
  - quote: ne demanda pas moins de trois semaines
- 1866-02-07: the battue at the foot of Mount Franklin; about thirty mouflons and ten goats penned (II.8 ¶36, ¶39). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Cette opération se fit le 7 février, par une belle journée d’été
- 1866-02-08: the colonists visit the corral the day after the battue (II.8 ¶41). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: le lendemain, ils n’en retournèrent pas moins visiter le corral
- February 1866: no event of importance (II.8 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Pendant ce mois de février, il ne se passa aucun événement de quelque importance
- Late summer 1866 (February to March): bustards, shovellers and Mozambique cocks in the poultry-yard (II.8 ¶45). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Vers la fin de l’été
- Eleven months since contact; the anniversary of the landing due "le 24 mars" (II.8 ¶48). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Voilà onze mois que toute communication entre eux et le reste des humains avait été interrompue
  - quote: le 24 mars, arrivait l’anniversaire de ce jour où le ballon les jeta sur cette côte inconnue
- 1865-03-24 (the anniversary's date): the balloon throws them on the coast (II.8 ¶48). *(Drafted.)* Flagged for the writer against Part I's own date of the landing; this fragment does not decide it.
  - quote: le 24 mars, arrivait l’anniversaire de ce jour où le ballon les jeta sur cette côte inconnue

## 03100 (II.9)

- 1866-03-02: thunder and hail; Pencroff covers his wheat (II.9 ¶2–¶4). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: le 2, le tonnerre gronda avec une extrême violence
- 1866-03-02 to about 1866-03-10: eight days of bad weather (II.9 ¶5). *(Drafted.)*

- October 1866 (planned): the boat to put to sea (II.9 ¶103). *(Drafted.)*

- More than a year on the island by April 1866 (II.9 ¶67). *(Drafted.)*
