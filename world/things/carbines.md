# The carbines: the two central-fire carbines of the chest

*Two central-fire carbines, one taken on the first exploration with some cartridges.*

Keywords: carabines, carbines, inflammation centrale, central fire, cartouches, cartridges, arms, chest, Spilett

- **What it is.**
  - There are two carbines with central-fire ignition in the chest (II.2 ¶53). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: 2 carabines à inflammation centrale
  - One of the carbines is taken on the exploration, with some cartridges (II.3 ¶6). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: on prit aussi une des carabines et quelques cartouches
  - Spilett stands before the jaguar with a carbine at his shoulder; the card reads it as the chest carbine (II.4 ¶65). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: la carabine à l’épaule

    - quote: deux carabines et quatre fusils
