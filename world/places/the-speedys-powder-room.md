# The Speedy’s powder-room: the magazine in the stern of the brig

*A locked compartment behind a bulkhead at the stern, under the poop, where Ayrton breaks the padlock; the part of the hull holding it suffers least when she sinks.*

Keywords: soute aux poudres, powder-room, magazine, dunette, cadenas, padlock, cloison, Speedy, arrière, stern

- **What it is.**
  - The powder-room, as always, is at the stern of a ship, under the poop (III.2 ¶62, ¶64). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la soute aux poudres, qui est toujours située à l’arrière d’un bâtiment
    - quote: sous la dunette du brick, où devait être la soute
  - It lies behind the bulkhead that closes the after compartment, in a door that has a padlock (III.2 ¶65, ¶66). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la cloison fermant le compartiment d’arrière
    - quote: il s’agissait de briser un cadenas
  - It holds barrels of powder (see [the powder](../things/the-speedys-powder.md)) (III.2 ¶71). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ces tonneaux de poudre
  - Bob Harvey shuts its door again after Ayrton’s attempt (III.2 ¶74). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Bob Harvey avait refermé la porte de la soute
  - After the wreck the colonists reach it at the stern, once under the poop, as Ayrton had told them (III.4 ¶61). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dans cette partie que surmontait autrefois la dunette
    - quote: suivant l’indication d’Ayrton
  - The part of the hull in which it lies has suffered least, so the destruction cannot be put down to an explosion (III.4 ¶62). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: La portion de la coque dans laquelle se trouvait située la soute était précisément celle qui avait le moins souffert
- **Appears in:** III.2, III.4
