# The iron-shod spears: sticks tipped with a sharp iron

*Sticks set with a sharp iron, with the bows and arrows the colonists’ only arms on 6 May; carried down the old outlet after the blast.*

Keywords: épieux, épieu ferré, spears, bâtons emmanchés d’un fer aigu, iron-pointed sticks, weapons, arms, pikes

- **What it is.**
  - On 6 May the colonists’ only arms are bows, arrows and sticks mounted with a sharp iron (I.16 ¶29). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Arcs, flèches, bâtons emmanchés d’un fer aigu, c’étaient là leurs seules armes
  - Before the descent after the blast they fetch picks, iron-shod spears and fibre ropes from the Chimneys (I.18 ¶4). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des épieux ferrés
  - Their iron points are read as made of the iron forged between 20 April and 5 May; the text does not say so (I.16 ¶29). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: bâtons emmanchés d’un fer aigu
  - The iron-shod spears, the bow and the arrows do wonders in the hunters’ hands (I.19 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les épieux ferrés, l’arc et les flèches des chasseurs faisaient merveille
  - With the spears the hunters easily kill half a dozen seals on 5 June (I.20 ¶21). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: armés de leurs épieux ferrés, en tuèrent aisément une demi-douzaine
- **Appears in:** I.16, I.18, I.19, I.20, I.21
