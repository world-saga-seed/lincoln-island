# The corral lantern: the lit lantern on the table of the corral house

*A lantern burning on the table of the corral house on the night of 19 February, seen as a faint trembling light through the window; who lit it is not said.*

Keywords: fanal, lantern, lumière, light, table, corral house, lueur, fenêtre

- **What it is.**
  - Harbert sees a light, and the colonists see a faint glow tremble through the panes of the window facing them (III.12 ¶66, ¶68, ¶71). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Une lumière !
    - quote: ils virent trembloter une faible lueur
  - On the table of the house’s one room burns a lit lantern (see [the corral house](../places/the-corral-house.md)) (III.12 ¶79). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Sur la table brillait un fanal allumé
  - A lantern is kept at the corral, Smith says (III.15 ¶48). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Nous en trouverons un au corral
  - Nab strikes a light and lights it, and carries it into every corner of the room, then takes it with him (III.15 ¶58, ¶72). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Un coup de briquet fut donné par Nab, et, un instant après, le fanal était allumé
    - quote: Nab prit le fanal allumé
- **Appears in:** III.12, III.15
- **Open:**
  - Who lit it, and when, is not said (III.12 ¶79). *(Drafted.)*
