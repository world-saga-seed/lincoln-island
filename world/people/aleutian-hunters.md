# The Aleutian hunters: the trappers of Russian America whose whalebone engine Cyrus Smith borrows

*Hunters of the Aleutians, in Russian America, who kill wolves and foxes with whalebone strips bent and frozen in ice.*

Keywords: Aléoutiens, Aleutian, Aleut, chasseurs, hunters, pêcheurs, Amérique russe, Russian America, fanons, whalebone, baleen, piège, trap, glace

- **What it is.**
  - They are hunters of Russian America who frequently use the whalebone engine that Cyrus Smith says is not of his invention (II.10 ¶75). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Cet engin n’est pas de mon invention
    - quote: il est fréquemment employé par les chasseurs aléoutiens dans l’Amérique russe
  - Their method, as Cyrus Smith gives it, is to bend the baleens in the frost, coat them with ice that keeps their curve, hide them under a layer of fat and sow them on the snow; when a starving animal swallows one, the heat of its stomach melts the ice and the baleen, unbending, pierces it with its sharp ends (II.10 ¶75). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: je les recourberai, je les arroserai d’eau
    - quote: une couche de graisse
    - quote: la chaleur de son estomac fera fondre la glace
    - quote: le fanon, se détendant, le percera de ses bouts aiguisés
  - The narrator calls the invention one 'renouvelée des pêcheurs aléoutiens' when the colonists' traps succeed (II.11 ¶29). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: cette invention, renouvelée des pêcheurs aléoutiens
- **Appears in:** II.10, II.11
- **Open:**
  - The engine is credited to 'chasseurs aléoutiens' in Cyrus Smith's mouth (II.10 ¶75) and to 'pêcheurs aléoutiens' in the narrator's (II.11 ¶29) (II.10 ¶75; II.11 ¶29). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: chasseurs aléoutiens
    - quote: pêcheurs aléoutiens
