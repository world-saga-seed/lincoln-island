# The branch hut: the shelter of branches at the ore, built 20 April

*A hut of branches bound with lianas and plastered with clay, at the forest’s edge at the foot of the Franklin spurs.*

Keywords: hutte, branch hut, camp, branchages, forge camp, lianes, clay, shelter

- **What it is.** A made thing, a hut. Build if needed. *(Drafted.)*
- **Appears in:** I.15 ¶31, ¶40
