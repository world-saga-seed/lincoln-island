# The boat, the whale and the tobacco (notes)

## Notes for drafting

- **Where it sits.** The French II.10, April to 31 May of the second year (year given in the next chapter, II.11 ¶9, "l’hiver de 1866-67"). It follows II.9 ¶103, the decision to build a boat to put to sea "vers le mois d’octobre prochain"; ¶1 takes it up directly: "il voulait visiter l’île Tabor". *(Drafted.)* The year of this chapter's dates is my reading from II.11 ¶9. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- **Cast.**
  - Pencroff (¶1): wants to visit Tabor; once served on a whaler (¶66); worked some years in a Brooklyn yard (¶7). *(Drafted.)*
  - Cyrus Smith, "l’ingénieur" (¶2; named ¶5): plans the boat; invents the baleen traps. *(Drafted.)*
  - Gédéon Spilett, "le reporter" (¶5): finds the tobacco. *(Drafted.)*
  - Harbert (¶5; "le jeune garçon" ¶22): with Spilett when the tobacco is found; names it. *(Drafted.)*
  - Nab (¶5) and "maître Jup" (¶5): household work; Nab's cry that the whale is ashore (¶47). Top does not appear in this chapter. *(Drafted.)*
  - Dieffenbach, naturalist, cited for the whale's milk (¶65); Aleut hunters of Russian America, source of the trap (¶75); the crew of the Maria-Stella, not seen (¶62). *(Drafted.)*
- **Places.**
  - Tabor island (¶1), the aim of the boat. *(Drafted.)*
  - The Chimneys and the cliff: the boat-yard in the recess between them (¶6). *(Drafted.)*
  - The Far-West forest, its south-west and a clearing (¶19, ¶21). *(Drafted.)*
  - Granite House (¶19), the plateau of Prospect Heights (¶44). *(Drafted.)*
  - Union Bay, Cape Mandible, Cape Claw, the islet (¶44). *(Drafted.)*
  - Wreck Point, three miles from Granite House, the Mercy bridge and the Mercy's right bank (¶49). *(Drafted.)*
  - The "Whale-Field", between New Zealand and South America (¶40); Vineyard, "Port de l’état de New York" (¶62, n2). *(Drafted.)*
- **Things.**
  - The boat: 35 feet of keel, 9 of beam, at most 6 of draught, decked, two hatches and cabins, sloop-rigged, carvel-built, fir (¶3–¶4). *(Drafted.)*
  - The wheat: five bushels from the second harvest, 130,000 grains to the bushel, 650,000 grains (¶10–¶11); the third field (¶18). *(Drafted.)*
  - Koala skins, tanned with sulphuric acid (¶19). *(Drafted.)*
  - Tobacco: leaves dried, chopped and roasted on hot stones (¶33); the filled pipe (¶90). *(Drafted.)*
  - The harpoon with the Maria-Stella inscription (¶61, ln3). *(Drafted.)*
  - The whale: 80 feet, perhaps 150,000 pounds (¶51); tongue 6,000 lb of oil, lower lip 4,000 (¶67); 800 baleen blades with teeth six feet long (¶67); milk like cow's milk (¶65). *(Drafted.)*
  - Baleen traps: a dozen blades cut in six (¶69). *(Drafted.)*
  - Gunfire used to drive off birds of prey (¶64); rifles loaded with ball (¶19); the pocket compass (¶19). *(Drafted.)*
- **Time.**
  - "Aussitôt les arbres choisis ... Huit jours après" the keel is laid (¶6). The fine season is "six mois" off (¶5). *(Drafted.)*
  - 15 April: second wheat harvest (¶9). Last fortnight of April: three koalas killed (¶19). 30 April: tobacco found (¶21). *(Drafted.)*
  - 1 May: the whale is a topic; it had been seen "depuis quelques jours" (¶34–¶35). 3 May: stranded (¶47). Cutting up: three days (¶66). Baleen traps made after (¶69). *(Drafted.)*
  - "vers la fin du mois" the boat is half planked (¶81). 31 May: the pipe (¶82). *(Drafted.)*
  - The chapter covers about six weeks, from mid-April to 31 May, with the boat-building running through it. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- **Clues.**
  - The harpoon of the Maria-Stella in a dead whale (¶54, ¶61–¶62). Pencroff gives an innocent route (¶58); Spilett "que l’affirmation de Pencroff ne satisfaisait pas" (¶59). The whale arrives dead and ashore (¶47, ¶54). *(Drafted.)*
  - "Mais ce que les colons ne pouvaient faire, le hasard le fit pour eux" (¶47): the stranding is put down to chance. *(Drafted.)*
  - Tobacco found in the Far-West where "elle pousse très-abondamment" (¶25). *(Drafted.)*
  - Spilett, once, in ¶59 doubts Pencroff's explanation; Smith says "Cela est parfaitement possible" (¶60). *(Drafted.)*
- **Length.** 101 numbered paragraphs (¶1–¶101), plus the summary ¶0. 3,317 words in ¶1–¶101; 51 more in ¶0. *(Drafted.)*

## Open and flags

- **Tobacco: one month or two.** The tobacco is found on 30 April and Pencroff gets his pipe on 31 May, but the preparation is said to take two months. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: C’était le 30 avril.
  - quote: Cela demanda deux mois
  - quote: le 31 mai, il devait éprouver une des plus grandes joies de sa vie
  - Card neither version.
- **The whale's stay.** ¶35 says the whale had been seen "depuis quelques jours" on 1 May, and ¶44 that it did not leave; ¶47 gives 3 May for the stranding. These are consistent; noted so a draft keeps the sequence 1 May, 3 May. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: Depuis quelques jours, on avait pu observer en mer
- **The yield figures.** The text gives five bushels, 130,000 grains to the bushel, 650,000 grains, and a promised four thousand bushels from the third harvest. The figures are carried as printed and not checked. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: nous aurons quatre mille boisseaux
- **Fruit of the first harvest.** "la deuxième récolte de blé" says there was a first, not in this chapter. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- **Wikisource corrections.** None of the three that change a fact falls in this chapter. I was not given where the other five fall. No reading in this chapter is known to rest on one. *(Drafted.)*
- **Whaling firsts.** The text states that Pencroff once served on a whaler (¶66); the earlier chapters were not given to me, so I do not know whether this is new. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: Pencroff avait autrefois servi sur un navire baleinier
- **Seam with II.9 ¶103.** The first beat follows from it; no discord. *(Drafted.)*
