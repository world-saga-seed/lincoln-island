# The sorrel: the common sorrel Harbert brought, good against scurvy

*Common sorrel, an anti-scorbutic plant, grown in the plateau garden.*

Keywords: oseille, sorrel, antiscorbutique, potager, garden

- **What it is.**
  - It is a common sorrel, among the wild plants Harbert brings and transplants to the Grande-Vue plateau; its antiscorbutic properties are not to be disdained (II.8 ¶43). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une oseille commune, dont les propriétés anti-scorbutiques n’étaient point à dédaigner
  - Sorrel grows in the small squares of the kitchen garden (see [the kitchen garden](../places/the-kitchen-garden.md)) (II.8 ¶43). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: laitues, vitelottes, oseille, raves, raifort
  - Harbert recognises sorrel in the old Tabor plots, whose seed he means to take to Lincoln (see [the Tabor plots](../places/tabor-vegetable-plots.md); [the Tabor seeds](tabor-seeds.md)) (II.13 ¶80). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: de l’oseille
- **Appears in:** II.8, II.13
