# The frigate rammed by the Nautilus: the warship Nemo sank

*The frigate that pursued the Nautilus in the north of the Atlantic and was sunk by its ram a few days before the three men fled.*

Keywords: frégate, frigate, vaisseau, warship, éperon, ram, représailles, sunk, Atlantic, English, bélier

- **What it is.**
  - A grave incident was witnessed by the three men, which the professor must have told in his book, and whose echo must have been terrible (III.16 ¶70). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un grave incident dont les trois étrangers jetés à son bord avaient été témoins
  - A few days before the escape the Nautilus, pursued by a frigate in the north of the Atlantic, threw itself like a ram on the frigate and sank it without mercy (III.16 ¶71). *(Drafted.)*

    - In the earlier book the pursuer is a great ironclad two-decker with a spur and two funnels, which fires on the Nautilus, whose flag is not hoisted and whose nationality Nemo says shall remain a secret (VML II.21 ¶15, ¶17, ¶22, ¶50). *(Drafted.)*

- **Appears in:** III.16, VML II.20, VML II.21
- **Open:**
  - Whether a frigate or a two-decker, English or unnamed, in a narrow bay or in the open sea: III.16 ¶71 and ¶73 say a frigate, English, in "une baie étroite et peu profonde"; the earlier book has a "deux-ponts cuirassé" of unnamed nationality, met at sea, Nemo letting it pursue him from the evening before until the morning of 2 June at about 47° 24′ north and 17° 28′ west (III.16 ¶71, ¶73; VML II.21 ¶17, ¶50, ¶55, ¶72; II.20 ¶52). Carded neither. *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une frégate anglaise
    - quote: dans une baie étroite et peu profonde
    - quote: un deux-ponts cuirassé
    - quote: Sa nationalité, du moins, restera un secret pour vous
    - quote: par 47° 24′ de latitude et 17° 28′ de longitude
  - The days between: III.16 ¶71 says "quelques jours avant la fuite"; the earlier book has the Nautilus wander "quinze ou vingt jours" after the sinking, and gives the sinking as "ce terrible jour du 2 juin" (III.16 ¶71; VML II.22 ¶9; II.21 ¶72). Carded neither. See [Captain Nemo](../people/captain-nemo.md) for the dates. *(Drafted.)*
