# The armoise: mugwort, a downy herb that gives tinder

*A downy plant of the armoise genus, found on 15 April; dried, it is the colonists' tinder.*

Keywords: artemisia, mugwort, artemise, absinthe, herb, wormwood, tinder plant

- **What it is.**
  - It belongs to the genus armoise, whose chief species include absinthe, citronella, tarragon and gepi (I.13 ¶51). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une certaine plante appartenant au genre armoise, qui compte parmi ses principales espèces l’absinthe, la citronnelle, l’estragon, le gépi
  - It is covered with long silky hairs, and its leaves with a cottony down (I.13 ¶53). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: revêtue de poils soyeux et longs, dont les feuilles étaient recouvertes d’un duvet cotonneux
  - Smith named it the artemise, Chinese armoise for the learned (I.13 ¶55). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: c’est l’artemise, l’armoise chinoise pour les savants
- **On the page.**
  - Smith pulled up several tufts and offered them to the sailor (I.13 ¶51). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: en arracha plusieurs touffes
  - Pencroff asked whether it was tobacco; Smith said no (I.13 ¶55). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Non, répondit Cyrus Smith
- **Appears in:** I.13
