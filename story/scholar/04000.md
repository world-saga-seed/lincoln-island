## a

After: A few days later, and it was the 26th of March.

The colonists were thrown on the island on the 24th of March, 1865: chapter II says so, and chapter I brings them down on the afternoon of that day. Chapter XXX looks forward to the first anniversary on the 24th. The 26th here agrees with neither, and the third anniversary, in chapter LV, is put on the 25th. I take the 24th, which the account of the day itself gives.
