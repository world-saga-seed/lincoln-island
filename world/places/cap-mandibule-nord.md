# Cap Mandibule-Nord: the northern of the two capes of the Shark-gulf

*The northern jaw of the Shark-gulf, one of the two capes that close the Union Bay on the north-east.*

Keywords: cap Mandibule-Nord, Mandible-cape, north, jaw, north-east

- **What it is.**
  - The Union Bay is closed on the north-east by two capes with a narrow gulf between them (I.11 ¶22). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Au nord-est, deux autres caps fermaient la baie
  - It was named cap Mandibule-Nord when Spilett remarked that there are two capes and Pencroff gave them the names Nord and Sud; Spilett wrote them down (I.11 ¶71). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le cap Mandibule-Nord et le cap Mandibule-Sud
  - The two Mandibule capes close the horizon on the north from the windows of Granite-house (I.19 ¶48). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les deux caps Mandibule fermaient au nord
- **Appears in:** I.11, I.19
