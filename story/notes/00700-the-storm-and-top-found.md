## Open and flags

- **Eight miles or six.** The chapter summary (¶0) says "Eight miles from the first camp". ¶81 says "about six miles from the Chimneys" at six o'clock, and the cave is five minutes' walk inland (¶85). The text does not give eight. I.5 ¶42 has Nab and Spilett going "more than eight miles" on the first day. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: à six milles environ des Cheminées
- **The islet.** ¶77: "the islet, quite invisible then", with the definite article, as if the islet were already known. Chapter 00700's span was not given the earlier chapters; whether an islet is introduced before I.7 is for the reader of 00100 to 00300 to say. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: elles devaient passer par-dessus l’îlot, absolument invisible alors
- **Moon and tide.** ¶71 "The moon, new then" and ¶77 "a spring tide" agree. The date of the new moon is not given elsewhere in my chapters. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: La lune, nouvelle alors, et, par conséquent, en conjonction avec le soleil
- **Hours of the tide.** ¶27: "the sea, which was then ebbing" at about 8 p.m.; ¶77: "The sea was beginning to rise" at about 2:30 a.m.; I.4 ¶63: reflux at 1 p.m. on the 25th. I have not worked the tide table; card neither. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: La mer commençait à monter
- **Distance of the loss from the coast.** ¶10: "twelve hundred feet from the shore at most"; I.5 ¶42: "a few hundred feet from the coast"; I.6 ¶8: "a few hundred paces from a shore". Three readings, not one figure. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: à douze cents pieds au plus du rivage
- **Dinner and supper.** ¶21 "prepare dinner", ¶30 "souper", ¶70 "leftovers of supper": same meal. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Pencroff s’occupa aussitôt de préparer le dîner.
- **The tide table and the wind.** The wind is from the south-east and also they walk north with it at their backs (¶72); the coast is "exposed to the east" (¶32). *(Drafted.)*
