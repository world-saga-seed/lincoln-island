# The peninsula and the open corral (notes)

## Notes for drafting
- **Where it sits.** Follows III.11 ¶65 (the Reptile-End reached, no trace of the convicts' retreat). It runs from 18 February 1868 to the night of 19 February at the corral. *(Drafted.)*
- **Cast.**
  - Cyrus Smith (¶4), Pencroff (¶5), Gédéon Spilett (¶7), Harbert (¶12), Nab (¶54). *(Drafted.)*
  - Top and Jup (¶16). *(Drafted.)*
  - Ayrton, found in the corral house (¶82). *(Drafted.)*
  - The convicts: absent, then five dead (¶107–¶108). The "inconnu", "ce gentleman" (¶10–¶11). *(Drafted.)*
- **Places.**
  - The wooded coast from the Reptile-End to the Falls River, three to four miles wide (¶1); the west coast (¶3); the Wreck Point and the Tadorn marsh, named in Cyrus Smith's reading (¶4). *(Drafted.)*
  - The mouth of the Falls River with basalts beyond (¶12–¶13); the river's left bank and valley, six miles from Mount Franklin (¶13, ¶16). *(Drafted.)*
  - The corral: the palisade and the gate, the clearing and its "dangerous zone" of thirty paces, the house (one ground-floor room, the lantern, Ayrton's old bed), the little stream with trees on the right, the ridge of the spur above (¶17, ¶33, ¶78–¶79, ¶99, ¶105). *(Drafted.)*
- **Things.**
  - The cart as store and breastwork (¶55); the onaggas (¶59). *(Drafted.)*
  - The shut gate with its outer bars off, then one leaf open (¶42, ¶60); the lit lantern on the table (¶79). *(Drafted.)*
  - Ayrton's wrist and ankle bruises (¶84). Top's leash (¶58, ¶102). *(Drafted.)*
- **Time.**
  - 18 February 1868: the coast search; evening camp at the Falls River mouth (¶1, ¶12). *(Drafted.)*
  - 19 February: up the valley; the halt about five in the evening (¶13, ¶17); three hours' wait (¶25); eight o'clock, the scouts leave (¶26); five minutes to the clearing (¶32); full night soon after (¶36–¶38); the moon rises in the east (¶100). *(Drafted.)*
  - "Four months before": the convicts' landing (¶108). *(Drafted.)*

- **Clues.**

  - The lit lantern on the table (¶79); Ayrton on his old bed with no one by (¶79–¶80). *(Drafted.)*

## Open and flags
- **The gate.** ¶42 has the gate shut but its outer bars not in place; ¶60 has one leaf open; ¶63 has Pencroff swear it was shut. Both sides:
  - quote: qui, ainsi que le reporter et lui l’avaient supposé, était fermée
  - quote: Un des battants était ouvert
  - quote: cette porte était fermée tout à l’heure
  - The text does not say who opened it. Card neither. *(Drafted.)*
- **The inference at ¶43.** The scouts infer from the shut gate that the convicts hold the corral; Cyrus Smith, at ¶51, infers the opposite. Quotes:
  - quote: les convicts occupaient alors le corral
  - quote: les convicts ne sont pas au corral
  - *(Drafted.)*
- **Cyrus Smith's route for the convicts (¶4)** puts their landing near the Wreck Point and their crossing the Tadorn marsh; I did not have an earlier chapter to check it against. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- **The moon.** ¶100 has the moon rise in the east after eight o'clock on the 19th; I did not check the phase against the calendar. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
