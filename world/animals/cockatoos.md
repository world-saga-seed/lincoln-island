# Cockatoos: the black, white and grey screamers of the creek trees

*Cockatoos black, white and grey that swarm in the eucalyptus and casuarina boughs along Red Creek.*

Keywords: kakatoès, cockatoo, perroquet, oiseaux, Creek-Rouge, eucalyptus, casuarina, caquetage

- **What it is.**
  - Black, white or grey cockatoos swarm among the thin boughs of the eucalyptus and casuarinas, which do not hinder the spread of their wings (I.12 ¶43). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Kakatoès noirs, blancs ou gris
  - They flutter amid a deafening cackle together with parrots, parakeets and the other bright birds (I.12 ¶43). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: voletaient au milieu d’un caquetage assourdissant
- **Appears in:** I.12
