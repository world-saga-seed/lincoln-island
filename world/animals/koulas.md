# Koulas: the sloths of the Jacamar Wood

*A big-dog-sized tree climber with bristly dirty hair and strong claws, that the reporter takes for a bear.*

Keywords: koula, paresseux, sloth, ours, bear, griffes, claws, arbre, tree, feuilles, leaves

- **What it is.**
  - It is a "koula", better known as a "paresseux" (I.15 ¶38). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ce n’était qu’un « koula », plus connu sous le nom de « paresseux »
  - It is the size of a large dog, with bristly hair of a dirty colour and paws armed with strong claws, which let it climb trees and feed on leaves (I.15 ¶38). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: qui avait la taille d’un grand chien, le poil hérissé et de couleur sale
    - quote: les pattes armées de fortes griffes, ce qui lui permettait de grimper aux arbres et de se nourrir de feuilles
  - It does not belong to the family of the plantigrades, the bears (I.15 ¶38). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: n’appartenait point à cette redoutable famille des plantigrades
- **On the page.**
  - The reporter, seeing it between the first branches of a tree in a thick copse, takes it for a bear; once its identity is verified it is left to its business (I.15 ¶38). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un animal qu’il prit pour un ours
    - quote: qu’on ne dérangea point de ses occupations
- **Appears in:** I.15
