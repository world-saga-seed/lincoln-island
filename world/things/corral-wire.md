# The corral wire: a wire between the corral and the Nautilus

*The wire Nemo laid from the corral to the Nautilus, used to call the colonists to him.*

Keywords: cable, line, telegraph, summons, message, alphabetic apparatus

- **What it is.**
  - It links the corral to the Nautilus (III.16 ¶66). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: au moyen d’un fil par lequel il relia le corral au Nautilus
  - The wire stretched by Nemo between the corral and the crypt is cleared by Nab and Harbert on the way back, since it may be used later (III.18 ¶4). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le fil qui avait été tendu par le capitaine entre le corral et la crypte
    - quote: qu’on pourrait utiliser plus tard
- **On the page.**
  - The Nautilus has an alphabetical apparatus, and the wire was used to summon the colonists of Granite-house (III.16 ¶66). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il manda, comme on sait, les colons de Granite-house
- **Appears in:** III.16, III.18
- **Open:**
  - The length and the route of the wire are not stated.
    - quote: il relia le corral au Nautilus
