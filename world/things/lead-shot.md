# The lead shot: the grain of lead found in a young peccary

*A grain of lead that breaks Pencroff’s tooth at dinner on 24 October, found in one of two young peccaries trapped that day; the colonists have no firearm.*

Keywords: grain de plomb, plomb, lead, shot, bullet, firearm, peccary, tooth, pécari, mystery

- **What it is.**
  - It is a grain of lead, not a pebble (I.22 ¶82). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ce n’était point un caillou… C’était un grain de plomb.
  - Pencroff breaks a molar on it at dinner, at about five on 24 October, eating one of the two young peccaries taken that day with their mother (I.22 ¶62, ¶74, ¶75, ¶80, ¶81). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une femelle de pécari et ses deux petits
    - quote: il y a donc des cailloux dans vos pécaris
    - quote: l’objet qui lui coûtait une mâchelière
  - At that time the colonists have no firearm: Smith, lacking a special set of tools, puts off making one (I.22 ¶57). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: sans un outillage spécial, il lui serait presque impossible de fabriquer un fusil
    - quote: remettait l’opération à plus tard
  - The shot is therefore not from the colonists’ own arms (I.22 ¶57, ¶82). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: reculait toujours et remettait l’opération à plus tard
  - It is a grain of metal, found in the body of a creature, and it overthrows all the colonists’ deductions that the island was uninhabited (II.1 ¶1). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un simple grain de métal
    - quote: tout cet échafaudage de déductions tombait
  - It came out of a firearm, and no one but a human being could have used it (II.1 ¶2). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ce plomb était sorti d’une arme à feu
  - The wounded peccary was barely three months old, still suckling when Pencroff found it in the pit; so a gun was fired on the island at most three months ago (II.1 ¶5, ¶6, ¶7). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Il tétait encore sa mère quand je l’ai trouvé dans la fosse
    - quote: depuis trois mois au plus, un coup de fusil a été tiré dans l’île Lincoln
  - The shot reached the young animal, but not mortally (II.1 ¶8). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: a atteint, mais non mortellement, ce petit animal
  - Smith concludes that either the island was inhabited before the colonists’ arrival, or men have landed in the last three months at most; whether they came willingly, are Europeans or Malays, or are still on the island, cannot be known (II.1 ¶9). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ou l’île était habitée avant notre arrivée, ou des hommes y ont débarqué depuis trois mois au plus
    - quote: Européens ou Malais
  - Smith inclines to think those who landed have been there only a very short time or have only passed, since when the colonists explored the island from Mont Franklin they would have seen them or been seen; castaways thrown up by a storm some weeks ago are probable (II.1 ¶15). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: n’y sont que depuis très-peu de temps ou qu’ils n’ont fait qu’y passer
    - quote: depuis quelques semaines seulement, des naufragés ont été jetés par une tempête
  - Smith fears the landing party are Malay pirates (II.1 ¶17). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il est malheureusement à craindre que ce ne soient des pirates malais
  - Smith and Spilett ask whether the incident has some connection with the inexplicable circumstances of the engineer’s rescue and other strange things that had struck them (II.1 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les circonstances inexplicables du sauvetage de l’ingénieur
  - Smith’s opinion is that however closely they explore the island, they will find nothing (II.1 ¶33). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: si minutieusement que nous explorions l’île, nous ne trouverons rien
  - It proves beyond refutation that a gun was fired on the island at most three months before (II.4 ¶41). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: prouvait d’une façon irrécusable que, depuis trois mois au plus, un coup de fusil avait été tiré dans l’île
- **Appears in:** I.22, II.1, II.4

    - quote: Ces cochons de lait étaient vraiment délicieux

    - quote: d’un inoffensif rongeur
