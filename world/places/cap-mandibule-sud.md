# Cap Mandibule-Sud: the southern of the two capes of the Shark-gulf

*The southern jaw of the Shark-gulf, the point that closes the Union Bay on the north; the coast before it is arid.*

Keywords: cap Mandibule-Sud, Mandible-cape, south jaw, north-east, point

- **What it is.**
  - It was named cap Mandibule-Sud when Pencroff gave the two capes of the jaw the names Nord and Sud (I.11 ¶71). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le cap Mandibule-Nord et le cap Mandibule-Sud
  - It is the point that closes the Union Bay (I.14 ¶38). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: cette pointe qui fermait la baie de l’Union, et qui avait reçu le nom de cap Mandibule-Sud
  - All the country to it is very arid, sand and shells mixed with lava debris (I.14 ¶38). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: On n’y voyait que sable et coquilles, mélangés de débris de laves
  - The point of "cap Mandibule" reached on 16 April lies nearly seven miles from the Chimneys; it is read as this cape (I.15 ¶5). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: distante de près de sept milles des Cheminées
  - The two Mandibule capes close the horizon on the north from the windows of Granite-house (I.19 ¶48). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les deux caps Mandibule fermaient au nord
  - The north cape covers it, so that squalls enter the gulf only with difficulty (II.20 ¶9). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le cap du nord couvre-t-il celui du sud
  - From "cap Mandibule" to the mouth of the Mercy it is hardly eight miles (the text does not say which of the two capes is meant) (II.20 ¶29). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: Du cap Mandibule à l’embouchure de la Mercy, on ne comptait guère qu’une huitaine de milles
- **Appears in:** I.11, I.14, I.15, I.19, II.20, III.1, III.3
