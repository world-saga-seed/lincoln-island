# Chile: the coast of South America east of the island's latitude

*The Chilean coast, which Smith places abreast of the island to the east, and the American coast whose distance he reckons.*

Keywords: Chili, Chile, American coast, côte américaine, east, distance

- **What it is.**
  - At the island's latitude it lies to the east, opposite New Zealand to the west, and the distance between the two lands is at least six thousand miles (I.14 ¶54). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: par le travers de la Nouvelle-Zélande à l’ouest, et de la côte du Chili à l’est
  - Smith's bearing puts the island more than forty-five hundred miles from the American coast (I.14 ¶79). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: à plus de quatre mille cinq cents milles de la côte américaine
- **Appears in:** I.14
- **Open:**
  - The text does not say that "the American coast" of I.14 ¶79 is the Chilean coast of ¶54; the two are carded apart.
    - quote: à plus de quatre mille cinq cents milles de la côte américaine
