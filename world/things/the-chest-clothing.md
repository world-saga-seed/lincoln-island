# The chest clothing: the shirts and stockings of vegetable cloth

*Two dozen shirts and three dozen stockings of one cloth like wool but of evidently vegetable origin, the only clothes in the chest.*

Keywords: chemises, bas, shirts, stockings, vêtements, clothes, vegetable cloth, tissu, wool, chest

- **What it is.**
  - There are two dozen shirts of a peculiar cloth that looked like wool but whose origin was evidently vegetable (II.2 ¶53). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: 2 douzaines de chemises d’un tissu particulier qui ressemblait à de la laine, mais dont l’origine était évidemment végétale
  - There are three dozen stockings of the same cloth (II.2 ¶53). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: 3 douzaines de bas de même tissu
