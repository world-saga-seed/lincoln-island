# The beacon fire: the glow on Lincoln that guides the Bonadventure home

*A bright fire seen twenty miles off to the north-east about two in the morning, lit evidently by Smith, which gives Pencroff the bearing of Lincoln.*

Keywords: feu, fire, lueur, glow, signal, phare, beacon, nuit, night, Lincoln, retour, return

- **What it is.**
  - About two in the morning Pencroff, at the helm, sees a bright glow twenty miles to the north-east, and cries "Un feu !" (II.14 ¶105, ¶106, ¶107). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Vers deux heures du matin
    - quote: une vive lueur apparaissait à vingt milles dans le nord-est
  - The narrator says it is evidently lit by Smith, and shows the route to follow (II.14 ¶107). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: cette lueur, évidemment allumée par Cyrus Smith, montrait la route à suivre
  - It shines above the horizon like a star of the first magnitude (II.14 ¶108). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: comme une étoile de première grandeur
- **Appears in:** II.14, II.15
- **Open:**
  - The fire is seen on the night of 18 to 19 October; the next chapter has the boat land at seven in the morning "the next day, 20 October, after four days’ voyage"; card neither (II.14 ¶102; II.15 ¶1). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: La nuit du 18 au 19 fut obscure et froide
    - quote: Le lendemain, — 20 octobre, — à sept heures du matin, après quatre jours de voyage
