# Goats of Tabor: the European goats of Tabor Island

*Goats of European kind that run wild on Tabor Island, left there, it is thought, by some whaler.*

Keywords: chèvre, chèvres, goat, île Tabor, Tabor, baleinier

- **What it is.**
  - They are goats, with the pigs, of European species; the narrator takes it that some whaler had put them ashore, where they multiplied fast (II.13 ¶73). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des chèvres et des porcs, qui, il était facile de le voir, appartenaient aux espèces européennes
    - quote: quelque baleinier les avait débarqués sur l’île, où ils s’étaient rapidement multipliés
  - They run off in hundreds through the woods and thickets near the house (II.14 ¶28). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: chèvres et porcs s’enfuyaient par centaines
  - None is carried off: the party takes pigs only, two pairs, aboard the Bonadventure (II.14 ¶80). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: deux couples de porcs
- **Appears in:** II.13, II.14
