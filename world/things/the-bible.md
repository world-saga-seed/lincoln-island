# The Bible: the English Bible of the chest, with a pencil cross before Matthew VII, 8

*An English quarto Bible, Old and New Testaments, often leafed through, with no publisher or date, from which Smith reads on 29 October at a bookmark and a red pencil cross.*

Keywords: Bible, Évangile, Gospel, Matthew, Mathieu, verset, signet, bookmark, red cross, crayon, quarto, English, chest

- **What it is.**
  - It is a Bible containing the Old and the New Testament (II.2 ¶53). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: 1 Bible contenant l’Ancien et le Nouveau Testament
  - It is printed in English, a quarto remarkable for its typography, and seems to have been often leafed through (II.2 ¶66). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: imprimée en langue anglaise, in-quarto remarquable au point de vue typographique
    - quote: paraissait avoir été souvent feuilleté
  - Like the dictionaries, it bears no publisher’s name and no date (see [the natural sciences dictionary](natural-sciences-dictionary.md)) (II.2 ¶66). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: De même pour la Bible
  - It is read as "l’Évangile": Harbert asks Smith to read a passage of the Gospel on Sunday evening, 29 October (II.2 ¶77). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: quelque passage de l’Évangile
  - It falls open where a bookmark separates the pages (II.2 ¶81). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un signet en séparait les pages
  - A red cross made in pencil stands before verse 8 of chapter VII of the Gospel of Saint Matthew (II.2 ¶82). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une croix rouge, qui, faite au crayon
    - quote: le verset 8 du chapitre VII de l’Évangile de saint Mathieu
