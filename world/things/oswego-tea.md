# The Oswego tea: tea of the monarda from the warren

*A good drink made from the monarda (monarda didyma), Oswego tea, gathered by Harbert at the warren.*

Keywords: thé d’Oswego, monarde, monarda, tea, infusion, herb, boisson, beverage

- **What it is.**
  - The plant is the didymous monarda, known in North America as "Oswego tea", and it produces an excellent drink (I.19 ¶34). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: monardes didymes
    - quote: thé d’Oswego
    - quote: produisent une boisson excellente
  - Harbert gathers a notable quantity of it at the warren with the medicinal herbs (see [the warren](../places/the-warren.md)) (I.19 ¶34). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une notable quantité de monardes didymes
  - The colonists make a very agreeable tea from the monardes brought from the warren (I.20 ¶31). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ils faisaient un thé très-agréable en employant les monardes rapportées de la garenne
  - Nab adds a few cups of it to the cold-meat breakfast on the marsh trip, and it is among the dishes of the 24 October dinner (I.21 ¶20; I.22 ¶74). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: quelques tasses de thé d’Oswego
    - quote: du thé d’Oswego
  - It is among the island's drinks: tea of Oswego, supplied by the didymous monardas (II.8 ¶44). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Au thé d’Oswego fourni par les monardes didymes
- **Appears in:** I.19, I.20, I.21, I.22, II.8
