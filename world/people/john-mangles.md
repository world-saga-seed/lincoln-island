# John Mangles: captain of the Duncan

*The yacht's captain, who marries Robert Grant's sister.*

Keywords: Mangles, capitaine, Duncan, yacht

- **What it is.**
  - He is captain of the Duncan in December 1854, with a crew of fifteen men (II.17 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Le Duncan était commandé par le capitaine John Mangles et monté par un équipage de quinze hommes
  - He is of the party that crosses Australia (II.17 ¶57). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le capitaine Mangles et quelques matelots
  - His wife is the sister of Robert Grant; he visits the colonists in Iowa (III.20 ¶54). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: du capitaine John Mangles et de sa femme, sœur de Robert Grant
- **Appears in:** II.17, III.20
