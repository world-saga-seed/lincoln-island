# Basalt: the volcanic rock strewn over the mountain and the plain at its foot

*The dark volcanic rock of the island: debris on the slopes, great blocks on the plain below, a hollow in which the party camped.*

Keywords: basalte, volcanic rock, blocks, débris, Bischof, plutonic

- **What it is.**
  - Basalt debris is plentiful on the mountain slopes with erratic blocks, pumice and obsidian (I.10 ¶26). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: débris nombreux de basalte
  - Great blocks of basalt litter the plain at the mountain's foot; by Bischof's experiments basalt took three hundred and fifty million years to cool (I.12 ¶18). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: trois cent cinquante millions d’années
- **Appears in:** I.10, I.12
