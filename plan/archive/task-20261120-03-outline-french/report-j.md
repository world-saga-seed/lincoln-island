## The seam

The first beat of 03200 follows from II.9 ¶103: Pencroff's wish to visit Tabor and the need for a boat pick up the "embarcation ... vers le mois d’octobre prochain". II.10 ¶5 gives "six mois" to the fine season, which fits an April start.
