# The sealskins: skins of the islet’s seals, made into shoes

*Seal skins brought back from the 5 June hunt, made in January into wide, long shoes to replace the boots and shoes brought from America.*

Keywords: peaux de phoque, seal skin, hide, shoes, chaussures, leather

- **What it is.**
  - Nab and Pencroff skinned about half a dozen seals killed by the hunters, bringing back to Granite-house only the fat and the skin, the skin to be made into strong shoes (I.20 ¶21). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ne rapportèrent à Granite-house que leur graisse et leur peau, cette peau devant servir à la fabrication de solides chaussures
- **Made from.**
  - By about January the shoes are made of sealskin, to replace the shoes and boots brought from America; they are large and long, never pinching the walkers' feet (II.8 ¶5). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des chaussures en cuir de phoque, qui vinrent remplacer à propos les souliers et les bottes apportés d’Amérique
    - quote: furent larges et longues et ne gênèrent jamais le pied des marcheurs
- **Appears in:** I.20, II.8
