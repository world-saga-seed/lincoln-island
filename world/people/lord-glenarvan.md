# Lord Glenarvan: Scottish laird, owner of the Duncan

*The Scottish laird whose steam yacht the Duncan sought Captain Grant, and who left Ayrton on Tabor.*

Keywords: Glenarvan, Duncan, laird, Écossais, yacht, Grant, Ayrton, Tabor, lord

- **What it is.**
  - He is a Scottish laird and owner of the steam pleasure yacht Duncan (II.17 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un yacht de plaisance à vapeur, le Duncan, appartenant au laird écossais, Lord Glenarvan
  - His wife sails with him on the Duncan, with a major, a French geographer and the two children of Captain Grant; John Mangles commands the yacht (II.17 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Le Duncan était commandé par le capitaine John Mangles
    - quote: À bord de ce yacht étaient Lord Glenarvan, sa femme, un major de l’armée anglaise, un géographe français, une jeune fille et un jeune garçon
  - The English admiralty having hesitated to search for Captain Grant, he resolved to try everything to find him (II.17 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Lord Glenarvan résolut de tout tenter pour retrouver le capitaine
  - The bottle with the Britannia's document was found in the Irish Sea and picked up by the Duncan (II.17 ¶44). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: trouvée dans la mer d’Irlande et ramassée par le Duncan
  - He is "un homme intrépide et généreux" (II.17 ¶72). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: C’était un homme intrépide et généreux.
  - He believed Ayrton's loyalty on his word and the Irishman's warranty, and followed his advice to cross Australia along the thirty-seventh parallel (II.17 ¶57). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Lord Glenarvan crut à la loyauté de cet homme
    - quote: il résolut de traverser l’Australie en suivant le trente-septième parallèle
  - He told Ayrton that at the first port he would be handed to the English authorities; when Ayrton offered, in exchange for what he could say, to be left on a Pacific island, he consented, being resolved to learn all about Captain Grant (II.17 ¶73, ¶74). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: à la première relâche, on le remettrait aux autorités anglaises
    - quote: Lord Glenarvan, décidé à tout pour apprendre ce qui concernait le capitaine Grant, y consentit
  - He kept his word, and the Duncan came to Tabor, where Captain Grant and his two men were found (II.17 ¶76). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Lord Glenarvan tint la parole qu’il avait donnée
    - quote: on retrouva le capitaine Grant et ses deux hommes
  - At the landing he tells Ayrton that he will not be lost or forgotten like Captain Grant: "Je sais où vous êtes, Ayrton, et je sais où vous trouver. Je ne l’oublierai jamais !" (II.17 ¶77). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: vous ne serez ni perdu, ni ignoré comme le fut le capitaine Grant
    - quote: Je sais où vous êtes, Ayrton, et je sais où vous trouver. Je ne l’oublierai jamais !
  - Spilett remembers that the newspapers reported his attempt and its result (II.18 ¶13). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les journaux ont rapporté la tentative faite par Lord Glenarvan
  - Smith says he promised Ayrton to come and take him again from Tabor when he judged his crimes sufficiently expiated, and believes he will return; the colonists reckon it among their chances of salvation (II.19 ¶17, ¶29). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Lord Glenarvan a promis à Ayrton de venir le reprendre à l’île Tabor, quand il jugerait ses crimes suffisamment expiés
    - quote: la meilleure chance qui nous reste
  - Pencroff holds that the lord will return, and soon, but will put in at Tabor and not at Lincoln Island; Harbert adds that Lincoln Island is not even on the chart (II.19 ¶19, ¶20). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Mais où relâchera-t-il ? À l’île Tabor, et non à l’île Lincoln.
    - quote: l’île Lincoln n’est pas même portée sur la carte
  - The colonists mean to leave in the cabin that was Captain Grant's and Ayrton's a notice of the position of their island, for him or his crew to find (II.19 ¶22). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: déposer, dans cette cabane qui fut la demeure du capitaine Grant et d’Ayrton, une notice donnant la situation de notre île
- **Appears in:** II.17, II.18, II.19
- **Open:**
  - What he promised Ayrton: his spoken words at the landing are that men will remember him and that he knows where to find him (II.17 ¶77); Smith's account is that he promised "de venir le reprendre à l’île Tabor, quand il jugerait ses crimes suffisamment expiés" (II.19 ¶17). The tale gives only the first as spoken (II.17 ¶77; II.19 ¶17). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Je sais où vous êtes, Ayrton, et je sais où vous trouver
    - quote: quand il jugerait ses crimes suffisamment expiés
  - His name is spelt "Lord Glenarwan" once, in Smith's speech (II.19 ¶27); everywhere else "Glenarvan" (II.19 ¶27). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Lord Glenarwan ne choisirait pas la saison d’hiver
