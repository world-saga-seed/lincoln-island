# 06 Outline: the arc, then each chapter

*Basic.*

**When:** after setup (and after the checks, if run), inside the horizon. The interview's horizon answer is enough without stage 05.

**Uses:** [scene-outlines](../../plan/workflows/scene-outlines.md) (prompt O) and [reader-tests](../../plan/workflows/reader-tests.md) (**write the spec:** the key) and the [three critics' light pass](../../plan/workflows/actor-log.md#the-light-pass-basic) (logistics and physics, causal chain, character invariant) (Basic); as Refinements, [arc-pitch](../../plan/workflows/arc-pitch.md) (frames, tweens, density, the chapter list), [actor-log](../../plan/workflows/actor-log.md) (full offstage timelines), [aims-sweep](../../plan/workflows/aims-sweep.md), the story questions, the [outline critics](../../plan/workflows/drafting/critics.md#outline-critics), and [ripple](../../plan/workflows/ripple.md) after a settled change.

**What happens:** each chapter gets marked beats, notes for drafting and its reader-test spec, and a key-frame grade:

- **yes:** the writer has settled the chapter's main beat;
- **thin:** a beat is named, but not how it plays;
- **none:** no writer-settled beat.

Prompt O reports the grade per chapter. Once the writer has given a few key frames and the arc is pitched ([arc pitch](../../plan/workflows/arc-pitch.md)), [the interview](../../plan/workflows/arc-pitch.md#the-interview) is one way to fill the rest: Claude scans the proposed outline for missing elements and asks, the writer rules. The gate needs no chapter in the horizon graded none; a thin chapter may proceed, noted.

**For the first chapter,** the arc outline's head is enough: the shape, the ending and the chapter list as far as they are known, filled in by hand from the interview's answers.

**Outputs:** the arc outline, chapter outlines and notes, the critics' `interview.md`, actor logs, the clue register, `plan/reader-tests/`. The kit's example (in the kit's repository, `example/story/outline/` and `example/plan/reader-tests/key.md`) has an arc outline, a chapter outline and a key.

**Done when:** every chapter in the horizon has a marked outline and notes, and a key-frame grade, and the critics' `interview.md` is closed: every finding ruled by the writer.
