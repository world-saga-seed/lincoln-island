# The clearing: the brickworks and pottery works site

*A clearing on the clay ground where the bricks were made and fired and the pottery kiln built; Smith set the workshop at the place of production.*

Keywords: brickworks, briqueterie, clairière, workshop, factory, usine, site

- **What it is.**
  - It is a clearing two hundred paces from Lake Grant, five miles at most from the starting point (I.13 ¶29). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: après une marche de cinq milles au plus, on arrivait à une clairière
  - It stands on the clay ground Smith had noted the day before (I.13 ¶31). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Les colons étaient arrivés sur le terrain reconnu la veille
  - Smith chose to set up the workshop at the place of production, to avoid transport (I.13 ¶16). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: nous établirons notre atelier au lieu même de production
- **On the page.**
  - Branches were cut around the clearing for fuel (I.13 ¶36). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Des branches furent coupées autour de la clairière
  - Hunting was done in a very small radius around the brickworks (I.13 ¶38). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dans un rayon très-restreint autour de la briqueterie
  - Hunters saw recent tracks of large clawed animals near it (I.13 ¶38). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le passage récent d’animaux de grande taille, armés de griffes puissantes
  - The brick firing was done on the clearing on 6 April (I.13 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: réunis sur la clairière, à l’endroit où allait s’opérer la cuisson des briques
  - The clearing was turned into a factory once the pottery kiln was built (I.13 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: La clairière était transformée en usine
  - The kiln went out on 15 April, awaiting a new purpose (I.13 ¶51). *(Drafted.)*
