# 11 Close

*Basic.*

**When:** at the end of every session, task and wave.

**Uses:** [retrospective](../workflows/retrospective.md); the reasons are in [method/session-close.md](../method/session-close.md).

**What happens:** the lists in [retrospective](../workflows/retrospective.md) run in order: the task, the wave, the session.

**Done when:** the roadmap says where the next session starts and nothing finished is still open.
