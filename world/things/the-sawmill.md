# The sawmill: the hydraulic sawmill Smith sets up on the shore fall

*A water-driven sawmill of wheel, two cylinders and pulleys, built by Smith on the fall of the shore, that cuts the tree trunks into planks and beams.*

Keywords: scierie, hydraulique, scie, roue, poulies, planches, madriers, sawmill, water wheel

- **What it is.** A made thing, a machine. Build if needed. *(Drafted.)*
