# The diving suit: Nemo's scaphander, worn to walk under water

*The suit in which Nemo walked beneath the sea off the island and climbed the well of Granite-house.*

Keywords: scaphandre, diver, diving dress, underwater walk

- **What it is.**
  - It is worn by Nemo to walk under the water (III.16 ¶62). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Revêtu de son scaphandre, il se promenait sous les eaux
- **On the page.**
  - With it Nemo could reach the foot of the well of Granite-house (III.16 ¶64). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Au moyen du scaphandre, il lui était facile d’arriver au fond du puits intérieur de Granite-house
- **Appears in:** III.16
