# Harbert wounded and Top sent to Nab (notes)

## Notes for drafting
- **Where it sits.** The French III.7, `texts/fr-1875/49.md`; follows directly from the shot at the corral gate (III.6 ¶92–¶93) and ends on Nab's answer, before III.8. *(Drafted.)*
- **Cast.** Pencroff (¶1), Cyrus Smith (¶3), Gédéon Spilett (¶3), Harbert (¶1), Top (¶70), Nab (not present; his note, ¶96); an unnamed convict killed by Smith (¶8); another convict seen fleeing (¶53); Bob Harvey, named as the band's chief (¶53); Ayrton, absent (¶53). *(Drafted.)*
- **Places.** The corral, its palisade, house and Ayrton's bed (¶8–¶9); the house's fireplace (¶45); the south buttress of Mount Franklin (¶53); the mouth of the Mercy, where a convict boat broke (¶53); Granite-house, spoken of (¶5, ¶59). *(Drafted.)*
- **Things.** Smith's dagger (¶8); Pencroff's shirt turned to lint (¶39); cold-water compresses of linen (¶44); maple sugar and medicinal plants gathered by Harbert on the shores of Lake Grant (¶45); a page of Spilett's notebook, two notes, Top's collar (¶75–¶77, ¶95). The ball passed through Harbert, entering the chest between the third and fourth rib and leaving by the back (¶16–¶18). *(Drafted.)*
- **Time.** 11 November, morning: the shot (continuing III.6 ¶92); the day and night after (¶45). 12 November: Harbert wakes (¶46). "Vingt-quatre heures" at the corral (¶51, ¶67). 12 November, ten o'clock: Top leaves (¶86); a little before eleven the gate watch (¶90); about eleven the return (¶91). The chapter covers about a day and a half. *(Drafted.)*
- **Clues.** The convict's boat "s’était brisé sur les roches, à l’embouchure de la Mercy" (¶53) ties the fleeing man to the shipwreck. Ayrton's ammunition is gone (¶54). No sign of Ayrton (¶53). Nab's note says no pirates near Granite-house (¶96). *(Drafted.)*
- **Length.** 96 paragraphs (¶1–¶96, plus the summary ¶0); 2,742 words in the ¶ lines. *(Drafted.)*

## Open and flags
- Pencroff's "Lui, mon enfant !" and the closing comparison "comme une mère au lit de son enfant" (¶46) and Smith's "votre enfant" (¶24) set Harbert as Pencroff's son in his own eyes; no age is given in this chapter. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: dans l’intérêt même de votre enfant
- The medical account: the ball "entrée par devant" and out at the back (¶26), the lung perhaps crossed (¶49), the heart untouched (¶21). Spilett says first "la balle n’est pas restée dans le corps" and later "peut-être même la balle a-t-elle traversé le poumon". Not a discord, a hedge. *(My reading, to confirm.)*

  - quote: peut-être même la balle a-t-elle traversé le poumon
