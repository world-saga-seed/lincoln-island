# The torpedo: a torpedo placed at the bottom of the channel

*A torpedo laid on the bottom of the channel, which blew up the brig by shock.*

Keywords: mine, explosive, brig, channel, blast

- **What it is.**
  - It lay on the bottom of the channel (III.16 ¶65). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une torpille disposée au fond du canal
  - What is left of it is a piece of a thick iron cylinder, twisted and torn at its edges as if by an explosive, found on 30 November on the beach by Nab (III.4 ¶81). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un morceau d’un épais cylindre de fer, qui portait des traces d’explosion
    - quote: tordu et déchiré sur ses arêtes
  - Smith says it raised the waterspout on which the brig rose before sinking, and that the cylinder is all that remains of a torpedo, of incomparable power (III.4 ¶90, ¶92, ¶95). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: voulez-vous savoir ce qui avait soulevé cette trombe ?
    - quote: ce cylindre est tout ce qui reste d’une torpille
    - quote: son incomparable puissance
  - Asked who set it there, Smith says only that it was not he (III.4 ¶94, ¶95). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Tout ce que je puis vous dire, c’est que ce n’est pas moi
    - quote: mais elle y était
  - Smith, who handled such engines in the war of the Union, cannot be mistaken: a cylinder charged with an explosive, nitro-glycerine, picrate or the like, raised the water of the channel like a waterspout (III.5 ¶1). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: pendant la guerre de l’union avait eu l’occasion d’expérimenter ces terribles engins de destruction
    - quote: chargé d’une substance explosive, nitroglycérine, picrate ou autre matière de même nature
  - It would have destroyed an ironclad frigate as easily as a fishing-boat (III.5 ¶1). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: À une torpille qui eût détruit une frégate cuirassée aussi facilement qu’une simple barque de pêche
  - Its presence in the waters of the channel is the one thing not explained (III.5 ¶2). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: excepté la présence de cette torpille dans les eaux du canal
  - Smith says the unknown sank it in the channel (III.5 ¶3). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: cette torpille qui a détruit le brick, c’est lui qui l’a immergée dans le canal
- **On the page.**
  - The shock of it blew up the brig (III.16 ¶65). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: fit sauter le brick par le choc d’une torpille
- **Appears in:** III.4, III.5, III.16
- **Open:**
  - Nothing is said of how it was made or set off.
    - quote: une torpille disposée au fond du canal
  - The cylinder is found on 30 November (III.4 ¶81), but Smith already explains the explosion by a torpedo in the chapter that dates the cannon trial 8 November (III.5 ¶1, ¶24); card neither date. *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le 30 novembre
    - quote: c’était le 8 novembre
