# The camp at the fall: the night camp of 30 October at the head of the Mercy

*The camp the colonists make where the pirogue can go no farther, under nettle trees by the Mercy fall, about seven miles from Granite-house; the starting point of the westward walk.*

Keywords: campement, camp, night, 30 October, sources of the Mercy, nettle trees, micocouliers, fire, roars

- **What it is.**
  - Smith decides to camp at the very place where, for want of water, navigation will be stopped, since the west coast cannot be reached that day and five or six miles of unknown woods are too much to cross by night (II.3 ¶57). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: résolut de camper à l’endroit même où, faute d’eau, la navigation serait forcément arrêtée
    - quote: cinq ou six milles de la côte
  - The pirogue is moored to a tree trunk near the right bank (II.3 ¶69). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: amarré à un tronc, près de la rive droite
  - It is a charming spot; the fire is lit under a clump of broad nettle trees, in whose branches the colonists could have found refuge for the night (II.3 ¶71). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: On campa en cet endroit même, qui était charmant
    - quote: sous un bouquet de larges micocouliers
  - Smith puts it at about seven miles from Granite-house, allowing for the river’s detours north-west (II.3 ¶64). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: À sept milles à peu près
  - Smith plans to leave the pirogue at daybreak and cross the distance to the coast in two hours (II.3 ¶66). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: nous franchirons en deux heures, j’espère, la distance qui nous sépare de la côte
  - At nightfall a few roars of suspect nature are heard, and the hearth is fed all night to guard the sleepers; Nab and Pencroff watch in turn, and perhaps see shadows of animals prowling round the camp (II.3 ¶72). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: quelques rugissements de nature suspecte s’étant fait entendre avec la tombée du jour
    - quote: quelques ombres d’animaux errer autour du campement
  - Its position is fixed by Mont Franklin, which rises in the north at less than three miles (II.4 ¶2). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le volcan se relevait dans le nord à une distance de moins de trois milles
  - It is called the camp set near the sources of the Mercy (II.4 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: au campement qui avait été établi près des sources de la Mercy
- **Appears in:** II.3, II.4
