# Lady Glenarvan: Lord Glenarvan's wife

*The lord's wife, aboard the Duncan, who overcame Ayrton's silence.*

Keywords: lady Glenarvan, femme, Duncan

- **What it is.**
  - She is the wife of Lord Glenarvan and sails with him on the Duncan (II.17 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Lord Glenarvan, sa femme
  - She is of the party that crosses Australia (II.17 ¶57). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Lord Glenarvan, sa femme, les deux enfants, le major
  - She undertakes to overcome the bandit Ayrton's resistance, and her influence prevails (II.17 ¶74). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: lady Glenarvan entreprit de vaincre la résistance du bandit
    - quote: son influence l’emporta
  - The most beautiful pearl of Nemo's casket is sent to her in the name of the castaways repatriated by the Duncan (III.20 ¶51). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Une seule perle, la plus belle, fut distraite de ce trésor et envoyée à lady Glenarvan
  - She and Lord Glenarvan visit the colonists in Iowa more than once (III.20 ¶54). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la visite de lord et de lady Glenarvan
- **Appears in:** II.17, III.20
