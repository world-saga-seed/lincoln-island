# Gulls and mews: sea birds of the islet and the granite wall

*Noisy sea birds of the coast, nesting in the granite, whose flesh and eggs are poor eating.*

Keywords: goëland, mouette, gull, mew, seabird, oiseaux de mer, palmipède

- **What it is.**
  - In the dark, big heavy-flying birds burst from holes in the ground and others, quicker, rise in flocks; Pencroff believes he recognises gulls and mews, whose shrill whistles struggle with the roar of the sea (I.3 ¶14). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: Le marin croyait reconnaître des goëlands et des mouettes
  - The islet is the dismal refuge of a few sea birds (I.3 ¶26). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: refuge désolé de quelques oiseaux de mer
  - Many web-footed birds (palmipèdes with long, compressed, pointed bills) flutter near the summit of the granite wall, very noisy and little scared by man; small voracious mews nest in the granite hollows (I.4 ¶9). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: de petites mouettes voraces qui nichaient dans les anfractuosités du granit
  - The mews and the skuas are scarcely edible, and their eggs have a detestable taste (I.4 ¶9). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: ces mouettes et ces labbes sont à peine mangeables, et leurs œufs même ont un détestable goût
  - Harbert says the birds on the plateau's southern crest are neither gulls nor mews (I.4 ¶56). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ceux-là ne sont ni des goëlands, ni des mouettes
- **Appears in:** I.3, I.4
