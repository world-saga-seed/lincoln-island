# Report l: II.18 to III.1 (04000 to 04300)

*Returned as the agent's final message and saved here by the main session. One reference, garbled in the returned text as "II.19 ¶92 of III.1", is given here as III.1 ¶92, as its context shows.*

## Beats per chapter

- 04000 (II.18): 10 beats, ¶1 to ¶82, about 3,350 words.
- 04100 (II.19): 11 beats, ¶1 to ¶88, about 3,300 words.
- 04200 (II.20): 10 beats, ¶1 to ¶92, about 3,600 words. Note n1, plate pl1 and ln2 ("fin de la deuxième partie") are carried.

## The seams

II.17 ¶101 ends with Ayrton leaving; II.18 ¶1 opens with Harbert at the door, having seen him slide down the lift rope.

- quote: après avoir vu Ayrton glisser par la corde de l’ascenseur

Within the span: 04000 ends on 26 March, 04100 begins with the two years, and 04200 ends on the ship, which 04300 picks up.

## Discords (neither carded)

1. The hour off the Shark Gulf: Pencroff's forecast (II.19 ¶72) against II.20 ¶1. II.19 ¶77 also has the flood at 7 p.m., where II.20 ¶1 has the ebb running at about 6.
   - quote: nous serons par le travers du cap entre midi et une heure
   - quote: il était environ six heures quand le Bonadventure fut par le travers du golfe
2. Ayrton's move to Granite-house: "vers la mi-avril" (II.20 ¶73), against the bad days of May and his waiting on the beach at the return on 19 April (¶31); II.19 ¶39 had him come while the others were away.
   - quote: Ce qu’il fit vers la mi-avril
   - quote: les mauvais jours étaient venus avec le mois de mai
   - quote: Ayrton les attendait sur la grève
3. The telegraph's receiver: a needle on a dial (II.18 ¶48), a bell in III.1 ¶19.
   - quote: une aiguille disposée sur un cadran
   - quote: le timbre résonnait
4. The document: in company the colonists take it as Ayrton's (II.18 ¶5 to ¶14); Smith says privately that Ayrton did not throw the bottle (¶18); II.20 ¶49 has the document "qu’Ayrton prétend n’avoir jamais écrit".
   - quote: Aussi n’est-ce pas lui qui l’a jetée
   - quote: le document qu’Ayrton prétend n’avoir jamais écrit
5. The fire plan in III.1: a fire at Port Balloon (¶61), then every fire out after the black flag is seen (¶79).
   - quote: Nab et Pencroff se rendraient à Port-Ballon
   - quote: Que tous les feux soient éteints
6. Smaller points, in the notes: "sept ou huit ans" for the bottle (II.18 ¶8) against twelve years since the abandonment (II.19 ¶18); the print's "Glenarwan" (II.19 ¶27), not one of Wikisource's listed corrections; "la côte sud du golfe du Requin" (II.19 ¶64) before they enter the gulf; the brig more than eight miles off (III.1 ¶92), then 1¼ miles after the shot (¶102); a full moon on 18 April (II.19 ¶73) and a new moon on 17 October (III.1 ¶97), noted, not worked out.

## The Wikisource correction

II.20 n1 reads "Environ 83 kilomètres à l’heure"; the print reads 106 (the scan check, page 407). The outline quotes n1, and the notes give both readings (R1120-32).

- quote: Environ 83 kilomètres à l’heure.

## Dates

II.18 ¶82 gives 26 March 1867 as the second anniversary; III.1 ¶1 says "deux ans et demi". The year of the fire is not stated; 1866 is a reading.

## Open questions

1. Ayrton's move: follow II.20 ¶31 and ¶73 together, and let II.19 ¶39 stand as a plan.
2. The hour off the gulf: hold the French times; the 6 p.m. arrival is what puts the boat at sea all night.
3. The year of the fire: 1866, a reading.
4. The telegraph bell: keep it in III.1 as the text has it, noted as an addition to II.18's description.
