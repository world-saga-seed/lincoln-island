*Dropped after the phase 3 check (the critic's judgement; `plan/tasks/task-20261120-04-white-align/report-critic.md`): the labeller's #7 ("Good Luck": White's settled English form of the boat's name; see SUMMARY.md). Later discords renumbered.*

*Not aligned (main session): after "THE END." White prints a glossary of 92 entries, White ¶54 to ¶145 (AGOUTI to TUFA, and a last line "[Illustration: Lincoln Island]"), which the French has nowhere. It is the edition's addition, one for the whole book.*

### 1. changed: Serpentine Peninsula, "so far from the eruptive centre" (III.20 ¶2; White ¶2)
- The French lists the lost places and says of the Serpentine Peninsula that it lay far from the eruptive centre and yet vanished; White drops that remark and says all the lost places were precipitated into the eruptive centre. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: la longue presqu’île Serpentine, si éloignée cependant du centre éruptif !
  - white: Serpentine Peninsula—had been precipitated into the eruptive centre!

### 2. changed: Pencroff's anger becomes a wish to drown himself (III.20 ¶8; White ¶8)
- The French says Pencroff, a prey to a dull anger, paced the rock; White says he was ready to throw himself into the sea. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Pencroff, en proie à une sourde colère, allaient et venaient sur ce roc
  - white: Pencroff, ready to throw himself into the sea

### 3. softened: "Nemo did well to die" (III.20 ¶10; White ¶10)
- Nab says Captain Nemo did well to die; White has him say only "And Captain Nemo is dead!", which drops his bitter judgement. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Le capitaine Nemo a bien fait de mourir !
  - white: “And Captain Nemo is dead!” said Neb.

### 4. changed: what the notice would have said (III.20 ¶12; White ¶12)
- The French says the colonists had been unable to leave a notice telling of the changes in Ayrton's situation; White says the notice would have shown the place where Ayrton was to be found. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: comme les colons n’avaient pu déposer une notice indiquant les changements survenus dans la situation d’Ayrton
  - white: since the colonists had placed no notice there indicating the place where Ayrton was to be found

### 5. changed: "expiation" becomes "expatriation" (III.20 ¶26; White ¶24)
- The French says Ayrton was fetched home after twelve years of expiation; White says twelve years of expatriation, which turns the penance for his crimes into his exile. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: après douze ans d’expiation
  - white: after twelve years of expatriation

### 6. cut: a hundred miles to the north-east (III.20 ¶28; White ¶26)
- Smith asks why the Duncan steered a hundred miles from Tabor to the north-east; White has him say only "in this direction". *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: de faire route à cent milles de là dans le nord-est
  - white: to come in this direction

### 7. softened: "the God of all mercies" becomes "the God of sinners" (III.20 ¶41; White ¶39)
- Smith asks that the God of all mercies receive Nemo's soul; White has "the God of sinners", which gives the prayer a different tone and sets Nemo among sinners. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: que le dieu de toutes les miséricordes reçoive l’âme du capitaine Nemo
  - white: may the God of sinners receive the soul of Captain Nemo

### 8. cut: the reef is to appear on the charts (III.20 ¶49; White ¶47)
- The French says the reef would henceforth appear on the charts of the Pacific, which answers Smith's remark that Lincoln Island was on no map; White drops it. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: cet écueil qui devait désormais figurer sur les cartes du Pacifique
  - white: this remaining reef
