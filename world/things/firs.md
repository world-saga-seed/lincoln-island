# The firs: the tall firs of the forest

*Admirable firs a hundred and fifty feet high among the conifers, from whose young trunks sticks are torn.*

Keywords: sapins, fir, conifer, trees, tall trees, sticks, wood

- **What it is.**
  - There are admirable firs measuring a hundred and fifty feet in height (I.6 ¶27). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des sapins admirables, mesurant cent cinquante pieds de hauteur
  - Three sticks were torn from the trunk of a young fir (see [the clubs](clubs.md)) (I.9 ¶86). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: après avoir arraché trois bâtons au tronc d’un jeune sapin
- **Appears in:** I.6, I.9
