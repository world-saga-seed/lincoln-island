# Snipe: the bécassines of the marsh and lake

*Small marsh game birds found in bands in the south-east marsh and on the frozen lake, shot with arrows.*

Keywords: bécassine, snipe, marais, marsh, gibier, game, oiseau, bird, flèche

- **What it is.**
  - Snipe live in bands over the marsh waters, little afraid of men and easy to approach (I.21 ¶54). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: bécassines y vivaient par bandes
    - quote: ces volatiles peu craintifs se laissaient facilement approcher
  - Snipe flutter by thousands over the frozen lake, among ducks, pintails and guillemots (I.22 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: canards et bécassines, pilets et guillemots
    - quote: Il y en avait des milliers
  - Snipe fall to the arrows of Harbert and Gédéon Spilett (I.22 ¶57). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: bécassines, enfin gibier de poil ou de plume, tombaient sous leurs flèches
  - Snipe are among the water game that crowd the south-east marsh in winter, its winter quarters (II.11 ¶78). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: tout le gibier aquatique, qui établissait là ses quartiers d’hiver
    - quote: bécassines
  - Snipe are shot with ducks on the north coast, near the gulf, in strings (II.19 ¶53, ¶54). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: mouilla dans un léger renfoncement du littoral, au nord de l’île
    - quote: plusieurs chapelets de canards et de bécassines
  - In the great cold, snipe are among the myriads of water-game that Spilett and Harbert shoot in the tadornes' marshes (II.20 ¶77). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: de bécassines
  - Snipe are among the small game that never fail at the house when Harbert and Spilett hunt on the corral road (III.14 ¶20). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: canards, tétras, coqs de bruyère, jacamars, bécassines pour le petit
    - quote: ne manquaient jamais à la maison
- **Appears in:** I.21, I.22, II.11, II.19, II.20, III.14
