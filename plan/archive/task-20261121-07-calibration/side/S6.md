# S6: the French, the render, White

## ¶70

**French:** Il était cinq heures environ. Les derniers rayons du soleil se glissaient sous l’épaisse ramure et frappaient obliquement la petite chute, dont l’humide poussière resplendissait des couleurs du prisme. Au delà, le lit de la Mercy disparaissait sous les taillis, où il s’alimentait à quelque source cachée. Les divers rios qui affluaient sur son parcours en faisaient plus bas une véritable rivière, mais alors ce n’était plus qu’un ruisseau limpide et sans profondeur.

**Render:** It was about five o'clock. The last rays of the sun glided beneath the thick branches and struck obliquely upon the little fall, whose damp spray glittered with the colours of the prism. Beyond, the bed of the Mercy disappeared under the thickets, where it was fed by some hidden spring. The various streams which flowed into it along its course made of it, lower down, a true river, but here it was no more than a limpid brook, without depth.

**White** (¶60): (¶60) It was 5 o’clock, and the level rays of the setting sun illuminated the little fall. Above, the Mercy, supplied from a secret source, was hidden by the bushes. The various streams together had made it a river, but here it was but a shallow, limpid brook.
