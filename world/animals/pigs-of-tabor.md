# Pigs of Tabor: the European pigs of Tabor Island

*Pigs of European kind that run wild on Tabor Island, shy and agile, of which two pairs are carried to Lincoln Island.*

Keywords: porc, porcs, cochon, pig, swine, île Tabor, Tabor, baleinier, étable, race porcine

- **What it is.**
  - They are pigs, with the goats, of European species; the narrator takes it that some whaler had put them ashore, where they multiplied fast (II.13 ¶73). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des chèvres et des porcs, qui, il était facile de le voir, appartenaient aux espèces européennes
    - quote: quelque baleinier les avait débarqués sur l’île, où ils s’étaient rapidement multipliés
  - They run off in hundreds through the woods and thickets, and the individuals are singularly agile and not disposed to let themselves be approached (II.14 ¶28, ¶43). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: chèvres et porcs s’enfuyaient par centaines
    - quote: singulièrement agiles, ne paraissaient pas d’humeur à se laisser approcher
  - Harbert resolves to take one or two pairs alive to Lincoln Island, and Pencroff says they lack such pigs there (II.13 ¶73; II.14 ¶36). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: se promit bien d’en prendre un ou deux couples vivants
    - quote: ces porcs, dont l’île Lincoln est dépourvue
  - A pair that had taken to a thick thicket is seized after half an hour's chase, but escapes when the cries of Harbert are heard; two pairs are in the end put aboard (II.14 ¶43, ¶44, ¶80). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: parvenus à s’emparer d’un couple qui s’était baugé dans un épais taillis
    - quote: deux couples de porcs
  - On Lincoln Island they are led to the stables, where they are expected to acclimatise easily, and the catch is judged very profitable to the island (II.15 ¶45). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ces animaux furent conduits aux étables, où ils devaient s’acclimater facilement
    - quote: devant être très-profitable à l’île Lincoln
  - The pigs of the stables have already produced young, and the care of them takes much of Nab's and Pencroff's time. The text says only "les porcs" (II.18 ¶54). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: Les porcs avaient déjà produit des petits
- **Appears in:** II.13, II.14, II.15, II.18
- **Open:**
  - Whether Lincoln Island has pigs: Pencroff says the island lacks them, while the sangliers taken in the traps looked like American or European pigs (see the wild boars) (II.14 ¶36; I.22 ¶14). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ces porcs, dont l’île Lincoln est dépourvue
    - quote: leur ressemblance avec le cochon d’Amérique ou d’Europe
