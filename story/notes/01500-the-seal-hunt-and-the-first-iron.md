# The Seal Hunt and the First Iron (notes)

## Notes for drafting

- **Where it sits.** The French I.15, from 17 April to 5 May. It records the decision to winter (taken the night of 16 April), the seal hunt on the islet, the sealskin bellows, the move to the ore at the foot of Mount Franklin, and the making of iron, then steel, tools. *(Drafted.)*
- **Cast.** *(Drafted.)* Pencroff (¶1), Gédéon Spilett (¶1), Cyrus Smith (¶4, "the engineer"), Harbert (¶15), Nab (¶15), Top (¶33). Tubal-Cain is named as the first smith (¶42).
- **Places.**
  - The Chimneys (¶5, ¶29). *(Drafted.)*
  - The beach ford and the Salvation Islet, with its north point (¶15–¶17). *(Drafted.)*
  - The tip of Mandibule Cape, "nearly seven miles from the Chimneys", where the dunes end (¶5). *(Drafted.)*
  - The Jacamar Wood, crossed from south-east to north-west (¶33). *(Drafted.)*
  - The hut site, at the east foot of Mount Franklin's buttresses, a few hundred paces from the Red Creek, six miles from the Chimneys (¶31, ¶39–¶40). *(Drafted.)*
  - The iron ore deposit "almost at the very sources of the creek", at the foot of a north-east buttress (¶41); the coal near it (¶43). *(Drafted.)*
  - Far places in speech: the Pomotou archipelago, 1,200 miles (¶6); Corsica (¶41); Sweden and Norway, the United Kingdom (¶9). *(Drafted.)*
- **Things.**
  - Two sealskins (¶28) made into a blowing machine (¶30), with a fireclay pipe made earlier at the pottery kiln (¶44). *(Drafted.)*
  - The two steel blades from Top's collar, the colony's only tools until the iron (¶30). *(Drafted.)*
  - A hurdle of branches for hauling (¶32, ¶44); a hut of branches, lianas and clay (¶40). *(Drafted.)*
  - A heap of ore and coal in layers, worked by the bellows (¶43–¶44); an iron bloom, then a second, forged on a granite anvil with the first as hammer (¶45–¶46). *(Drafted.)*

## Open and flags

- **Where the ore lies: north-west or north-east.** ¶7 says the ore deposits Cyrus observed are "in the north-west part of the island"; ¶31 says the deposits are "at the foot of the north-east spurs of Mount Franklin"; ¶41 "at the foot of the lateral base of one of these north-east spurs". Card neither. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: quelques gisements dans la partie nord-ouest de l’île
  - quote: au bas des contreforts nord-est du mont Franklin
- **The coal, against I.13 ¶46.** I.13 puts an open-cast coal deposit "towards the mouth of Creek-Rouge". Here the ore is "almost at the very sources of the creek" and the coal "non loin". *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Ainsi que le minerai, la houille fut récoltée, sans peine et non loin, à la surface du sol
- **The islet: first or second visit.** ¶16 says Cyrus sets foot on the islet for the first time, his companions for the second, "since it was there that the balloon had first thrown them". I.14 ¶28–¶29 does not mention that. Earlier chapters were not given to me. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: puisque c’était là que le ballon les avait jetés tout d’abord
- **"Cap Mandibule", ¶5.** I.14 ¶27 and ¶38 call it "cap Mandibule-Sud"; ¶5 says "the point of Cape Mandibule" and "the two capes". Probably the same cape. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: jusqu’à la pointe du cap Mandibule
- **The echidna.** The summary says "Capture of an echidna"; ¶34 describes the animal without naming it. *(Drafted.)*
- **Pig iron or iron.** ¶8 offers the catalan method and the blast-furnace method; ¶9 chooses iron, not pig iron. ¶48–¶49 then reach steel by cementation, not from pig iron. No disagreement. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- **Wikisource corrections.** None in this chapter. *(Drafted.)*
- **The seam to I.16 (01600).** This chapter ends on 5 May with the forgers returning to the Chimneys; I did not read 01600. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
