# An Episode of the Civil War (notes)

## Notes for drafting

- **Where it sits.** The back-story of the escape: a long look back from the beach to Richmond, from the capture of Cyrus Smith in February 1865 to the departure on the night of 20 March; it ends by naming the missing man. It follows I.1 directly: I.1 ends on four castaways crying to save the one who is missing; I.2 ¶1 says who they are and ¶58 says who is missing. *(Drafted.)*
- **Cast.** *(Drafted.)* unless marked.
  - Cyrus Smith, engineer, about 45, Massachusetts: ¶3 (named), ¶4 (described). Also "monsieur Smith" ¶25.
  - Gédéon Spilett, reporter of the New York Herald, "forty years old at most": ¶6 (the plate caption after ¶2 names him first).
  - Nab (Nabuchodonosor), thirty, a freed slave, Cyrus's servant: ¶14.
  - Pencroff, sailor, 35–40, a Northern American: ¶24.
  - Harbert Brown, fifteen, of New Jersey, son of Pencroff's captain, an orphan: ¶24; "jeune Harbert Brown" ¶45.
  - Top, Cyrus's dog: ¶53.
  - Jonathan Forster, Southern officer-aeronaut, with five companions: ¶18, ¶19.
  - Named in passing: General Ulysse Grant (¶1), General Lee (¶18), Butler (¶17), Stanley (¶7), the governor of Richmond (¶18), William of Orange (¶4).

## Open and flags

- **Miles.** ¶1: "they were seven thousand miles from that capital". ¶57: "more than six thousand miles from their country". Different distances and different measure (from Richmond; from home). Against I.1 ¶18, "two thousand miles in twenty-four hours". Card neither. *(Drafted.)*
- **Days.** ¶1: "Their aerial voyage had lasted five days." ¶56: "It was only five days later that a break in the clouds showed the immense sea.". ¶57: "left on 20 March ... landed on 24 March" (four days). Against I.1 ¶10 and ¶13, where the sea is already below the basket on 23 March, and I.1 ¶18, "the hurricane had already been going on for five days" on the 23rd. *(Drafted.)*
- **Left in the hurricane.** ¶55 and ¶56 have the balloon go in the storm; against I.1 ¶18, "He obviously could not have left during the hurricane." *(Drafted.)*
- **Wind.** ¶20, "a moderate north-west wind"; I.1 ¶14, "a gale from the north-east". *(Drafted.)*
- **Hour.** ¶44, "Till tonight, at ten o'clock"; ¶49, "At half past nine, Cyrus Smith and his companions slipped ... onto the square". Not a contradiction unless the earlier meeting was meant for ten. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- **Numbers aboard.** ¶19 gives Forster "five of his companions" (six aboard); ¶40 "the balloon was to carry off six". Consistent. Five aboard instead (¶50, ¶57), and I.1 ¶17 "cinq passagers". *(Drafted.)*
- **The plate caption GÉDÉON SPILETT. falls after ¶2**, four paragraphs before he is introduced at ¶6; and the plate "Mr Smith, do you want to escape?" falls after ¶27, though it is Pencroff's question. *(Drafted.)*
- **Diction.** ¶14 calls Nab "un nègre", in the text's period word; ¶0 repeats it. The outline says "a Black man". *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- **Wikisource corrections.** None in I.2 (none listed for it in texts/README.md; no page of I.2 was read in the scan check). The text keeps print's hyphens ("très-instruit", "très-simple"). *(Drafted.)*
