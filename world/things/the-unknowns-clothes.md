# The unknown’s clothes: the scrap of blanket, then the colonists’ clothing

*The castaway of Tabor goes naked but for a scrap of blanket until Smith dresses him at Granite-house.*

Keywords: vêtements, clothes, lambeau, scrap, couverture, blanket, veste, jacket, inconnu, unknown

- **What it is.**
  - He is found almost naked, with only a scrap of blanket about the loins (II.14 ¶56). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: corps à peu près nu, sauf un lambeau de couverture sur les reins
  - At Granite-house Smith, while he sleeps, takes off the scrap of cloth and dresses him more suitably (II.15 ¶64). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Il l’avait aussi vêtu plus convenablement, après l’avoir débarrassé de ce lambeau d’étoffe qui le couvrait
  - On 3 December he wears a jacket, torn at the shoulder over blood (II.16 ¶108). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Le sang coulait à son épaule sous sa veste déchirée
- **Appears in:** II.14, II.15, II.16
