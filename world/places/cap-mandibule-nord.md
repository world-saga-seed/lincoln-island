# Cap Mandibule-Nord: the northern of the two capes of the Shark-gulf

*The northern jaw of the Shark-gulf, one of the two capes that close the Union Bay on the north-east.*

Keywords: cap Mandibule-Nord, Mandible-cape, north, jaw, north-east

- **What it is.**
  - The Union Bay is closed on the north-east by two capes with a narrow gulf between them (I.11 ¶22). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Au nord-est, deux autres caps fermaient la baie
  - It was named cap Mandibule-Nord when Spilett remarked that there are two capes and Pencroff gave them the names Nord and Sud; Spilett wrote them down (I.11 ¶71). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le cap Mandibule-Nord et le cap Mandibule-Sud
  - The two Mandibule capes close the horizon on the north from the windows of Granite-house (I.19 ¶48). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les deux caps Mandibule fermaient au nord
  - The Bonadventure runs up toward it on 18 April with a following wind (II.19 ¶55). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: filait très-rapidement en s’élevant vers le cap Mandibule-nord
  - The cap du nord covers the south one, so as to make it very difficult for squalls to enter the gulf (II.20 ¶9). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le cap du nord couvre-t-il celui du sud, de manière à rendre très-difficile l’entrée des rafales
  - The rocks of "cap Mandibule" lie nearly five miles from Granite-house; the text does not say which of the two capes is meant (III.5 ¶41). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: les roches du cap Mandibule, à près de cinq milles de Granite-house
  - The east coast from the cap de la Griffe to the Mandibule capes is thoroughly known (III.11 ¶24). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: toute la côte orientale depuis le cap Griffe jusqu’aux caps Mandibules
- **Appears in:** I.11, I.19, II.19, II.20, III.1, III.3, III.5, III.11
