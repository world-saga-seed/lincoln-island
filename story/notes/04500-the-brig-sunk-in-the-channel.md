# The brig sunk in the channel (notes)

## Notes for drafting
- **Where it sits.** The French III.3, the morning after Ayrton's swim; the fight with the pirates' boats, the brig entering the canal and sinking. Ends on the sinking, not yet explained (III.4 explains). *(Drafted.)*
- **Cast.**
  - Cyrus Smith (¶3), Nab and Pencroff (¶5), Gédéon Spilett and Ayrton (¶5), Harbert (¶5). *(Drafted.)*
  - Bob Harvey (¶20), the pirates and their boats (¶19, ¶23, ¶43). Top and Jup, shut in Granite-house since the day before (¶92). *(Drafted.)*
- **Places.**
  - The Chimneys (¶7), the mouth of the Mercy, whose bridge and little bridges are raised (¶8), the islet's eastern shore (¶12), Wreck Point (¶32), Cape Claw and the Mandible Capes, Union Bay (¶22), Granite-house, its lift, door, passage and hall (¶92, ¶94). *(Drafted.)*
  - The canal between the islet and the coast, half a mile wide (¶24). *(Drafted.)*
- **Things.**
  - Two precision carbines (range nearly a mile) held by Spilett and Ayrton, and four guns held by Smith, Nab, Pencroff, Harbert (¶5). *(Drafted.)*
  - The pirogue (¶9, ¶45). The Speedy: two anchors (¶16), four guns (¶18), black flag (¶17), windlass (¶70), jib, topsail and spanker (¶70, ¶77). *(Drafted.)*
  - Two ship's boats: the first with seven men then a dozen (¶23, ¶42); the second with eight (¶43), wrecked at the Mercy (¶47). *(Drafted.)*
  - The screen of green branches and creepers masking Granite-house's windows (¶22, ¶94, ¶96). *(Drafted.)*
- **Time.**
  - Dawn after the night of the first day (¶2); 6:30 a.m. when the colonists are in position (¶14); 8 a.m. the first boat is lowered (¶23); "une heure et demie" without sign before that (¶22); twenty minutes after the first shots the boat reaches the Mercy (¶36); half an hour for the boat to regain the brig (¶41). Flood tide begins (¶36); at the council the sea has "devait encore monter pendant une heure et demie" (¶62). *(Drafted.)*
  - The date is not given; by III.4 ¶75 ("19, 20 et 21 octobre" being "Les trois jours qui suivirent") it works out to 18 October. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - The sinking itself has no stated hour. About five hours of morning action. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- **Clues.**
  - The "bruit sourd" and the "trombe liquide" that open the brig in two in under ten seconds (¶97–¶99); the brig had just come "par le travers de la Mercy" (¶77) and was heading for the Chimneys (¶76). *(Drafted.)*
  - Nothing in the chapter says why. *(Drafted.)*
- **Length.** 99 paragraphs (¶1–¶99); 4,151 words in the ¶1–¶99 lines. *(Drafted.)*

## Open and flags
- ¶3 says Smith proposes "trois groupes", Chimneys, Mercy mouth and islet; ¶9 says the islet pair take "deux postes" so that shots come from "quatre points différents". Both quoted; the count of groups and posts differs by design. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- The brig's anchors: ¶16 "mouillé sur deux ancres"; ¶68–¶70 speak of "L’ancre est à pic" and "elle eut été arrachée du fond" in the singular. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- The six survivors' landing: ¶47 says they "parvinrent à prendre pied sur la rive droite de la rivière" and ran "dans la direction de la pointe de l’Épave"; III.4 ¶13 says they "avaient pris pied à la pointe de l’Épave". Card neither. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- ¶20 says Ayrton "avait déchargé son revolver six fois sur eux, qui avait tué un des leurs et blessé deux autres"; III.2 ¶73 has "deux convicts tombèrent". Card neither. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- Counts: ¶39 "quatre hommes blessés grièvement", ¶47 two of eight, ¶64 two on the islet, and Pencroff's "Huit de moins !" (¶66). *(Drafted.)*
