# S3: the French, the render, White

## ¶49

**French:** Ces divers travaux durèrent une huitaine de jours. Ils étaient donc achevés, avant que la transformation du sulfure en sulfate de fer eût été accomplie. Pendant les jours qui suivirent, les colons eurent le temps de fabriquer de la poterie réfractaire en argile plastique et de construire un fourneau de briques d’une disposition particulière qui devait servir à la distillation du sulfate de fer, lorsque celui-ci serait obtenu. Tout cela fut achevé vers le 18 mai, à peu près au moment où la transformation chimique se terminait. Gédéon Spilett, Harbert, Nab et Pencroff, habilement guidés par l’ingénieur, étaient devenus les plus adroits ouvriers du monde. La nécessité est, d’ailleurs, de tous les maîtres, celui qu’on écoute le plus et qui enseigne le mieux.

**Render:** These various labours lasted about eight days. They were therefore finished before the transformation of the sulphide into sulphate of iron had been accomplished. During the days which followed, the colonists had time to make some refractory pottery of plastic clay, and to build a brick furnace of a particular arrangement, which was to serve for the distillation of the sulphate of iron when that should be obtained. All this was finished towards the 18th of May, at about the moment when the chemical transformation was coming to an end. Gédéon Spilett, Harbert, Nab, and Pencroff, skilfully guided by the engineer, had become the most adroit workmen in the world. Necessity, besides, is of all masters the one who is most listened to, and who teaches the best.

**White** (¶44): (¶44) These different undertakings, which occupied eight days, were finished before the sulphate of iron was ready. During the interval the colonists made some refractory pottery in plastic clay, and constructed a brick furnace of a peculiar shape, in which to distil the sulphate of iron. All was finished on the 18th of May, the very day the chemical work was completed.
