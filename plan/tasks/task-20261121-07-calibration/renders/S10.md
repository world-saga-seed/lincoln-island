# S10: III.10 ¶40

¶40

Willow bark, in fact, has been justly considered a substitute for Peruvian bark, as have also the horse-chestnut, the holly leaf, the serpentary, etc. It was evidently necessary to make trial of this substance, although it was not equal to Peruvian bark, and to employ it in its natural state, since the means were wanting to extract its alkaloid, that is to say, the salicine.
