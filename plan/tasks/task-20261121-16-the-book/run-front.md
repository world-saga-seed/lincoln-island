# About This Book

This is the run’s book for *Lincoln Island*: the record of how its missing chapter was made, set beside what it was measured against. The novel itself is the other book, *Lincoln Island*.

Three chapters are given here, each in every version the run made or used:

- **XIX** and **XX**, Verne’s chapters on either side of the insertion;
- **XIX bis**, “The Float in the Bar”, the chapter inserted, which has no French original.

For each chapter, in this order:

- **White’s translation (1876)**, as he printed it, where he has the chapter;
- **the Opus control** and **the Gemini control**: each model given the same short prompt, as someone who knows the book would write it, with no cards, no ledger and no checks;
- **version 1**, the first draft by the World Saga Seed method, from the cards and the ledger;
- for XIX bis only, two more stages: **after the author’s read-aloud**, when the author read version 1 aloud and the chapter was brought to what was read, and **after the character check**, when each beat the read added was tested against what these men would do;
- **the final**, as it stands in the novel.

After the chapters come the three chapters’ notes (the cards the drafters worked from); the ledger, every ruling, one plain sentence each, in the order it was made; the author’s blind scores of the versions; and the author’s read-aloud changes as sent.
