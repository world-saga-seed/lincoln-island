# Jup’s room: the cabin beside the general store

*A room apart for the orang, a cabin with a frame always full of good litter, where his pipe and tobacco hang.*

Keywords: chambre de Jup, Jup, orang, cabine, litière, bed, magasin général, pipe

- **What it is.**
  - Jup has a room apart, near the general store, a kind of cabin with a frame always filled with good litter, which suits him perfectly (II.9 ¶7). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il occupait une chambre à part, près du magasin général, sorte de cabine avec cadre toujours rempli de bonne litière, qui lui convenait parfaitement
  - It is called "the room reserved for Jup", and Pencroff sends him back to it with "à ta chambre" (II.11 ¶71; II.12 ¶66). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: toi, Jup, à ta chambre
    - quote: la chambre réservée à Jup
  - Jup’s pipe, once Pencroff’s, hangs in it beside his supply of tobacco (see [Pencroff’s pipe](../things/pencroff-pipe.md)) (II.12 ¶73). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: qui fut suspendue dans sa chambre, près de sa provision de tabac
  - It lies within Granite-house, the colonists gathering in the great hall at Nab’s call from it (II.12 ¶66). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: Les colons, réunis dans la grande salle, se levèrent à l’appel de Nab, qui était alors dans la chambre réservée à Jup
- **Appears in:** II.9, II.11, II.12
