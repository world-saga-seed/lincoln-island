# The telegraph wire: the iron wire drawn for the line to the corral

*Iron wire drawn by Smith from the island’s iron in lengths of forty to fifty feet, joined and stretched over five miles; broken at pole no. 74 on 11 November.*

Keywords: fil de fer, fil télégraphique, étirage, iron wire, line, filière, rompu

- **What it is.**
  - Smith draws the iron, first prepared in long thin rods, through the draw-plate, and gets wire forty to fifty feet long, easy to join and stretch over the five miles between the corral and Granite-house (II.18 ¶38). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des fils longs de quarante à cinquante pieds
    - quote: cette distance de cinq milles qui séparait le corral de l’enceinte de Granite-house
  - The drawing takes only a few days, his companions turning wire-drawers once the machine is going (II.18 ¶39). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Il ne fallut que quelques jours pour mener à bien cette besogne
    - quote: faire le métier de tréfileurs
  - The wire is stretched a few days after the posts are planted from 6 February (II.18 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Quelques jours après, le fil était tendu
  - On 11 November it hangs evenly along the road for about two miles, then less tight (III.6 ¶75). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le fil régulièrement tendu
    - quote: cette tension paraissait être moins complète
  - At pole no. 74 the iron wire is violently broken and the break is fresh (III.6 ¶81, ¶83). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les deux bouts du fil de fer, qui avait été violemment rompu
    - quote: il y a certainement peu de temps que la rupture a été produite
  - In the part of the corral between the house and the palisaded enclosure there is no telegraph line (III.15 ¶74). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Il n’y avait, dans toute la portion du corral qui séparait la maison de l’enceinte palissadée, aucune communication télégraphique
- **Appears in:** II.18, III.6, III.14, III.15
