# Route du Corral: the road from the Grande-Vue plateau to the corral

*The newly traced road, a five-mile walk under a dome of greenery, that took the name route du Corral; its first stretch was opened earlier between the plateau and the Red Creek's sources.*

Keywords: route du Corral, road, chemin, corral, Red Creek, sources, cart, onaggas

- **What it is.**
  - The road between the Grande-Vue plateau and the Red Creek's sources is already partly cleared on 2 November, and Smith counts on a better cart (II.7 ¶7). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Déjà la route entre le plateau de Grande-Vue et les sources était en partie frayée
  - With the onaggas to help, the way to the creek's sources is only a walk of five miles under a dome of greenery (II.8 ¶31). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une promenade de cinq milles, sous un dôme de verdure
  - The road is newly traced and takes the name "route du Corral" (II.8 ¶31). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: cette route nouvellement tracée, qui prit le nom de « route du Corral »
  - Cutting it had already cost a certain number of trunks (II.8 ¶33). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le percement de la route avait déjà nécessité le sacrifice d’un certain nombre de troncs
  - Each excursion improves the roads, the causeway packing down under the cart's wheels (II.8 ¶14). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Chaque excursion était, en même temps, un moyen d’améliorer les routes, dont la chaussée se tassait peu à peu sous les roues du chariot
  - In February it is improved, and a third road is begun from the corral toward the west coast (see [the road to the west coast](road-to-the-west-coast.md)) (II.8 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: en même temps qu’on améliorait les routes du corral et du port Ballon
  - The telegraph posts, with glass insulators, are planted along it from 6 February, to carry the wire to the corral (II.18 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la plantation des poteaux, munis d’isoloirs en verre
    - quote: la route du corral
- **Appears in:** II.7, II.8, II.18
