# The clubs: stout branches and sticks the hunters use as weapons and tools

*Clubs and sticks cut or broken from trees, with a point worn on a rock, used for fighting through brush, killing birds and beasts.*

Keywords: gourdins, bâtons, sticks, cudgels, branches, weapons, hunting, no knife

- **What it is.**
  - Pencroff broke two stout branches from the first tree at the forest's edge and made them into clubs, and Harbert wore their points on a rock (I.6 ¶18). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Pencroff cassa au premier arbre deux solides branches qu’il transforma en gourdins, et dont Harbert usa la pointe sur une roche
  - They serve to break lianas and thorns on the river bank (I.6 ¶18). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des lianes ou des épines qu’il fallait briser à coups de bâton
  - Harbert, Nab and Pencroff tore three sticks from the trunk of a young fir (I.9 ¶86). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: après avoir arraché trois bâtons au tronc d’un jeune sapin
  - The explorers set out for the mountain armed with sticks (I.10 ¶20). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les explorateurs, armés de bâtons
  - Jup fights the culpeos with a knotty club he brandishes like a mace; it breaks in the fight, and he is found holding a stump of it (II.12 ¶41, ¶51). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Jup, armé d’un gourdin noueux qu’il brandissait comme une massue
    - quote: le tronçon de son bâton rompu
- **Appears in:** I.6, I.9, I.10, II.12
