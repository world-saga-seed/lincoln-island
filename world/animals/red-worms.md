# Red worms: the fat worms used as bait

*Big red worms crawling on the forest floor of the tétras ground; Pencroff's bait for the hooks.*

Keywords: ver, worm, appât, bait, fishing

- **What it is.**
  - They are big red worms that crawl on the ground (I.6 ¶41). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: de gros vers rouges qui rampaient sur le sol
  - Pencroff used them as the bait of his hooks (I.6 ¶41). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Quant à l’appât, de gros vers rouges
  - On the hooks they were worked with small jerks, "as if the worms were still alive", so they were dead or still on the line (I.6 ¶45). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: comme si les vers eussent été encore vivants
  - The tétras swallowed bait and hook together (I.6 ¶47). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: avalèrent à la fois l’appât et l’hameçon
- **On the page.** Seen only in this one passage; the text does not say what kind of worm they are.
- **Appears in:** I.6 ¶41, ¶45, ¶47
