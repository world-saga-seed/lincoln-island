## Open and flags
- Date discord with the next chapter. Here: "on 30 November, Nab, wandering on the shore, had found a piece of a thick iron cylinder" (¶81). In III.5, the torpedo is already Smith's explanation (¶1) and the cannon trial is on "8 November" (¶24), after "quelques jours" of harvest (¶22). Both sides are quoted; card neither. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- Nab brings the cylinder to Smith at "the workshop at the Chimneys" (¶82); III.5 ¶22 puts the harvest and the storing in Granite-house; no conflict stated. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- III.3 ¶62 has the sea still to rise an hour and a half at the council; here ¶17 has the sea "slack then, and very high" soon after the sinking, and ¶44 "the sea was high". The interval between the two is not stated. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- Landing place of the six: ¶13 "set foot at Wreck Point" against III.3 ¶47 "on the right bank of the river". *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- Hull position: ¶8 "lying on its side", ¶47 "almost keel up"; the text sets the second as the later state. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- The text says at ¶75 that Ayrton and Pencroff recovered "the four cannon". III.5 ¶23 has them hoisted to Granite-house. *(Drafted.)*
