# Teal: the sarcelles of the marsh

*A small duck found in bands in the south-east marsh.*

Keywords: sarcelle, teal, canard, duck, marais, marsh

- **What it is.**
  - Teal live in bands over the marsh waters, among wild ducks, pintails and snipe, little afraid of men and easy to approach (I.21 ¶54). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Canards sauvages, pilets, sarcelles, bécassines y vivaient par bandes
    - quote: ces volatiles peu craintifs se laissaient facilement approcher
  - Teal abound at the south-east marsh in winter, where the water game has its winter quarters (II.11 ¶78). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: tout le gibier aquatique, qui établissait là ses quartiers d’hiver
    - quote: sarcelles
- **Appears in:** I.21, II.11
