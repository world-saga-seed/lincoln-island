## a

After: was thoroughly discussed on the 29th of November between Smith, Spilett, and Pencroff,

This talk is told before Spilett’s sorties, the second of which was on the 27th. And the 29th has little room for it: at seven that morning Jup came in with Nab’s note, and by half-past seven the wagon had left the corral. By the order of the account the talk was held before the 27th. I find nothing to give the day.
