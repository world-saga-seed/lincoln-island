# The lint: the shredded linen laid on Harbert’s wounds

*Charpie, made by Pencroff from his own shirt, with which Harbert’s wounds are dressed.*

Keywords: charpie, lint, pansement, chemise, blessure, Pencroff, Harbert

- **What it is.**
  - Pencroff tears his shirt and absently makes lint of it (III.7 ¶39). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Pencroff avait déchiré sa chemise, et, machinalement, il faisait de la charpie
  - The wounds are covered with compresses and lint (III.8 ¶10). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: recouvertes de compresses et de charpie
- **Appears in:** III.7, III.8
