# The revolver from the rack: the pirates’ revolver Ayrton takes aboard the Speedy

*A revolver that Ayrton takes from the rack round the mainmast of the brig and empties in his escape.*

Keywords: revolver, râtelier, rack, armes à feu, Ayrton, Speedy, six coups, pirates

- **What it is.**
  - It is one of the many firearms hung on a rack round the mainmast, by a lantern, between decks (III.2 ¶63). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un râtelier garni d’armes à feu de toutes sortes
  - Ayrton takes it from the rack and sees that it is loaded and primed (III.2 ¶64). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ayrton détacha du râtelier un revolver
    - quote: s’assura qu’il était chargé et amorcé
  - It is fired six times in all, two in the fight, then four more in his flight, the last in the face of a pirate; the pirates reckon he "discharged his revolver six times" at them (III.2 ¶73, ¶76, ¶77; III.3 ¶20). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Quatre coups lui restaient à tirer
    - quote: Un cinquième coup du revolver d’Ayrton
    - quote: qui avait déchargé son revolver six fois sur eux
- **Appears in:** III.2, III.3
