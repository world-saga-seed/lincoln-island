# The descent rope: the rope let down from the door of Granite-house

*A rope that Nab pays out from the door of Granite-house to the ground, by which Jup slides down, and by which Pencroff goes down on 15 February, the last link with the beach.*

Keywords: corde, rope, Granite-house, door, palier, descent, Jup, Pencroff, glisser

- **What it is.**
  - Nab brings Jup to the door of Granite-house and lets a long rope unroll from it to the ground; Jup takes hold of it and slides quickly down to the beach (III.10 ¶13, ¶15). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: de laquelle il laissa dérouler une longue corde jusqu’à terre
    - quote: saisit la corde, se laissa glisser rapidement jusqu’à la grève
  - On 15 February Pencroff, the last to stay in Granite-house, goes down by a rope whose double is held below (III.11 ¶33). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il en redescendit au moyen d’une corde dont le double était maintenu en bas
  - Once drawn to the ground it leaves no communication between the upper landing and the beach (III.11 ¶33). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ne laissa plus subsister aucune communication entre le palier supérieur et la grève
- **Appears in:** III.10, III.11
- **Open:**
  - Whether the rope of III.10 ¶13 is the lift’s rope, by which one slides down (II.18 ¶1), is not said (see [the hydraulic lift](the-hydraulic-lift.md)). *(Drafted.)*
