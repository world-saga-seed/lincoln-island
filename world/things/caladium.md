# The caladium macrorhizum: a herb whose boiled rhizomes feed the colonists

*An arum-family herb whose boiled tubers are nutritious and partly replace bread.*

Keywords: tubers, rhizomes, aracées, sago, starch, vegetable, food plant, bread substitute

- **What it is.**
  - It is a herbaceous plant of the arum family, which in the tropics would take a tree form (I.13 ¶57). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: sorte de plante herbacée de la famille des aracées, et qui, sous la zone tropicale, eût affecté une forme arborescente
  - Its rhizomes have an excellent taste and are very nutritious (I.13 ¶57). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ces rhizomes étaient d’un excellent goût, très-nutritifs
  - They are nearly like what is sold in England as Portland sago (I.13 ¶57). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: sous le nom de « sagou de Portland »
- **On the page.**
  - Boiled tubers were served on 15 April with the agouti pot-au-feu and the cabiai ham (I.13 ¶57). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les tubercules bouillis du « caladium macrorhizum »
  - They could to some extent replace bread, which the colonists still lacked (I.13 ¶57). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ils pouvaient, dans une certaine mesure, remplacer le pain
- **Appears in:** I.13
