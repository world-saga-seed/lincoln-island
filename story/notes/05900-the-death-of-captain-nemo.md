# The death of Captain Nemo (notes)

## Notes for drafting
- **Where it sits.** The French III.17, `texts/fr-1875/59.md`; day in the crypt after the night talk of III.16; Nemo's last wishes, the talk about the Nautilus, his private words to Smith, his death about one in the morning, and the sinking of the Nautilus. It is the last of my chapters; the chapter after it is 06000. *(Drafted.)*
- **Cast.** Captain Nemo, Prince Dakkar (¶1); Cyrus Smith (¶3); Gédéon Spilett (¶3); Pencroff (¶6); Harbert (¶23); Ayrton (¶46); Nab (¶80). *(Drafted.)*
- **Places.** The crypt and the Nautilus (¶1–¶2); the salon with its pictures, bronzes, organ and basin (¶13); the library, dining room and engine room (¶40); the platform and the lens-shaped pane (¶42); the stern, with two taps at the waterline (¶32, ¶89); the island as a naval station for the United States (¶61); the mountains of India (¶63). *(Drafted.)*
- **Things.** The motto, "Mobilis in mobili" (ln1, after ¶13); the casket of diamonds and pearls (¶28); the hatch and its bolts (¶30); the boat (¶32); the two taps and tanks (¶32, ¶89); the lens-shaped pane (¶42); the electric apparatus (¶40). *(Drafted.)*
- **Time.** Day comes (¶1); "demain, je serai mort" said on that day (¶22); the day passes (¶74); night falls (¶74); a little after midnight, the last movement (¶77); about one in the morning, the death (¶78); some hours later the sinking (¶85). The platform rises seven or eight feet above the water (¶42). Nemo says thirty years he has lived on the Nautilus (¶9). The chapter covers about one day and the following early morning; with III.15's arrival that is, by my reading, 16 October into 17 October. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- **Clues.** The private talk between Nemo and Smith, whose content is withheld (¶72); the casket, "a hundred fortunes" (¶86); "And it is here that I shall rest in eternal sleep, if…" and the unfinished sentence (¶68–¶69); the promise to leave Lincoln Island and to return (¶64–¶65); the plan to give the island to the United States as a naval station (¶61); the sinking of the Nautilus (¶90). The "to return there" lines (¶59) tie to the later return. *(Drafted.)*
- **Length.** 91 paragraphs (¶1–¶91, plus the summary ¶0); 2,652 words in the ¶ lines. *(Drafted.)*

## Open and flags
- Height of the platform. ¶42 says it "rose seven or eight feet above the water"; III.15 ¶132 says the apparatus rose "ten to twelve feet above sea level". Both are in the text. Card neither. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: qui s’élevait de sept ou huit pieds au-dessus de l’eau
- Thirty years on the Nautilus. ¶9 says Nemo has lived thirty years on the Nautilus; III.16 ¶19 says thirty years without contact with the inhabited world and ¶45 sets his return to India in 1849 and his revolt in 1857. Both lines are in the text. Card neither. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Il y a trente ans qu’il vit sur le Nautilus
- ¶13 ends on a colon, "the motto of the Nautilus:", and the motto stands as a loose line (ln1). The paragraph's sentence is the text's; the motto is not in a numbered paragraph. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: ses yeux s’arrêtèrent sur cette devise inscrite au fronton de ce musée, la devise du Nautilus
- ¶84 is a row of dots, a mark between the death and the next morning; it is a paragraph of the working text. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- Who knows what. ¶72 says Smith "told them nothing of the secret things". Not to be filled. Gap, asked, not invented. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: il ne leur dit rien des choses secrètes que le mourant avait voulu lui confier
- The boat. ¶32 says "the boat that brought you" and ¶88 says it is moored at the side of the Nautilus; III.15 ¶114 has it at the hatch. *(Drafted.)*

  - quote: Le jour était venu.
- ¶61 has Smith say their plan is to give the island to the United States; III.14 ¶2 speaks of the colony that "would then depend on the mother country". *(Drafted.)*
