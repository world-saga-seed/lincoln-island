# The Pomotou: a Pacific group named as a possible landfall

*A Pacific group of islands named by Smith as a place the wind may have carried them.*

Keywords: Pomotou, Paumotu, Tuamotu, archipel, Pacific, landfall

- **What it is.**
  - Smith named it as a landfall the wind may have carried them to (I.9 ¶70). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: soit sur les Pomotou
  - It is the archipelago nearest the island in latitude (I.14 ¶55). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: l’archipel des Pomotou qui est le plus rapproché de nous en latitude
  - The distance to it from the island is more than twelve hundred miles (I.14 ¶56). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la distance qui nous en sépare est de plus de douze cents milles
  - Tahiti and the Pomotou lie at least twelve hundred miles from the island on Smith's bearing (I.14 ¶79). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: au moins à douze cents milles de Taïti et des îles de l’archipel des Pomotou
- **Appears in:** I.9, I.14
