## Open and flags
- Date discord with the next chapter. Here: "le 30 novembre, Nab, rôdant sur la grève, n’eût trouvé un morceau d’un épais cylindre de fer" (¶81). In III.5, the torpedo is already Smith's explanation (¶1) and the cannon trial is on "le 8 novembre" (¶24), after "quelques jours" of harvest (¶22). Both sides are quoted; card neither. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- Nab brings the cylinder to Smith at "l’atelier des Cheminées" (¶82); III.5 ¶22 puts the harvest and the storing in Granite-house; no conflict stated. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- III.3 ¶62 has the sea still to rise an hour and a half at the council; here ¶17 has the sea "étale alors, et très-haute" soon after the sinking, and ¶44 "la mer était haute". The interval between the two is not stated. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- Landing place of the six: ¶13 "pris pied à la pointe de l’Épave" against III.3 ¶47 "sur la rive droite de la rivière". *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- Hull position: ¶8 "couché sur le côté", ¶47 "presque la quille en l’air"; the text sets the second as the later state. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- The text says at ¶75 that Ayrton and Pencroff recovered "les quatre canons". III.5 ¶23 has them hoisted to Granite-house. *(Drafted.)*
