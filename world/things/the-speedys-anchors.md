# The Speedy’s anchors and chains: the brig’s ground tackle

*Two anchors and their chains, held in the channel while she rides, and found on the bed by Ayrton and Pencroff after the wreck.*

Keywords: ancres, anchors, chaînes, chains, écubiers, guindeau, windlass, mouillage, Speedy

- **What it is.**
  - She anchors with chains running through the hawse-holes, in sight of Granite-house, on the evening of 17 October (III.1 ¶103, ¶104). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un bruit de chaînes qui couraient en grinçant à travers les écubiers
  - She rides at two anchors, head to the north (III.3 ¶16). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: mouillé sur deux ancres, le cap au nord
  - She is weighed with a windlass whose pawl clicks, and one anchor is torn from the bottom (III.3 ¶67, ¶70). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le cliquetis du linguet qui frappait sur le guindeau
    - quote: quand elle eut été arrachée du fond
  - After the sinking Ayrton and Pencroff dive several times to the bed of the channel and find her chains and anchors before the sands swallow them (III.4 ¶75). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: retrouvèrent les chaînes et les ancres du brick
- **Appears in:** III.1, III.3, III.4
