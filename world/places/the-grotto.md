# The grotto: the excavation behind a high dune where Smith lay

*A hollow dug into the back of a high dune among the sand hills, where Smith is found, with a stream a hundred paces off.*

Keywords: grotte, excavation, cave, dune, Smith found, dunes, revers

- **What it is.**
  - It is a sort of excavation hollowed out at the back of a high dune (I.7 ¶85). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une sorte d’excavation creusée au revers d’une haute dune
  - It lies five minutes' walk from the strand (I.7 ¶85). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Cinq minutes après avoir quitté la grève
  - A bed of grass lay inside, on which the body was stretched (I.7 ¶86). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un corps étendu sur un lit d’herbes
  - The narrator calls it a grotto lost in the middle of the dunes (I.8 ¶31). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: cette grotte perdue au milieu des dunes
- **On the page.**
  - A clear stream filtering through the sand lies a hundred paces from it (see [the stream by the grotto](grotto-stream.md)) (I.8 ¶9). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: à cent pas de là un ruisseau limpide
- **Appears in:** I.7, I.8
- **Open:**
  - Distance from the coast: the narrator says more than a mile; Pencroff, asked the distance from the reefs, says about half a mile. The two are measured from different points ("from the coast", "from the reefs"). *(My reading, to confirm.)* Carded neither way.
    - quote: à plus d’un mille de la côte
    - quote: À un demi-mille environ
  - Distance from the Chimneys: at six o'clock on the walk north the party is "about six miles" from the Chimneys and five minutes later reaches the grotto; the chapter's summary says "eight" and the return reckons eight miles. Carded neither way.
    - quote: à six milles environ des Cheminées
    - quote: À huit mille du premier campement
    - quote: C’étaient huit milles à franchir
