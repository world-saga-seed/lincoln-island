# Top: Cyrus Smith's dog

*The engineer's dog, who breaks his chain to follow his master into the balloon and goes after him into the sea.*

Keywords: Top, chien, dog, Cyrus Smith, faithful, balloon, chain

- **What it is.**
  - He is Cyrus Smith's dog; he broke his chain and followed his master, and leapt into the balloon's car as it was about to leave (I.2 ¶53). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: C’était Top, le chien de l’ingénieur, qui, ayant brisé sa chaîne, avait suivi son maître
  - Pencroff accepts him as one more aboard, relieving the car of two bags of sand (I.2 ¶54). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Bah ! un de plus !
  - He is a faithful animal (I.3 ¶1). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Le fidèle animal
  - Nab trusts him to be near his master in the water (I.3 ¶11). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Top est là !
- **On the page.**
  - Cyrus Smith, fearing excess weight, wanted to send the poor animal away (I.2 ¶53). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: voulait renvoyer le pauvre animal
  - He vanishes when the engineer is swept off, having gone of his own will to his master's aid (I.3 ¶1). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: s’était volontairement précipité au secours de son maître
  - The castaways reckon that, if near, Top's barking would reach them should Cyrus be unable to give a sign (I.3 ¶15). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les aboiements du chien Top
  - The reporter reports that no trace was found of the engineer or of the dog Top on the coast (I.5 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: l’ingénieur et du chien Top
- **Appears in:** I.2, I.3, I.5
