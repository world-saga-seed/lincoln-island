# The glycerine: the neutral substance isolated from the dugong’s fat

*Glycerine got by treating the dugong’s fat with soda, with a soluble soap beside it; later concentrated and mixed with nitric acid for the nitro-glycerine.*

Keywords: glycérine, glycerine, fat, graisse, saponification, soap, water bath, sulphuric acid, acides gras

- **What it is.**
  - It is one of the elements of the dugong’s fat, to be isolated by saponifying the fat (I.17 ¶45). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un de ses éléments, la glycérine, en la saponifiant
  - Treated with soda, the fat gave a soluble soap on one side and, on the other, this neutral substance, the glycerine (I.17 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un savon soluble
    - quote: cette substance neutre, la glycérine
  - Smith knew that heating a neutral fat, such as seal fat, with sulphuric acid alone would isolate it, but for candles he chose lime instead (I.20 ¶23). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il pouvait isoler la glycérine
    - quote: il préféra saponifier la graisse au moyen de la chaux
- **Leads to.**
  - It was concentrated beforehand by evaporation on a water bath and put with nitric acid to give the nitro-glycerine (see [the nitro-glycerine](nitro-glycerine.md)) (I.17 ¶58). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: préalablement concentrée par évaporation au bain-marie
- **Appears in:** I.17, I.20
