## Open and flags
- Timing squeeze, not a contradiction. Ayrton leaves at 10:30 (¶33), swims half an hour (¶37), is aboard an hour before exploring (¶57), fights, swims back, and the shots are followed by half an hour's wait (¶82) before the pirogue lands at about 12:30 (¶83). Both sides:
  - "It was half past ten in the evening"
  - "about half past midnight"
  *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- Ayrton's six shots are counted in ¶73 (two), ¶76 (two), ¶77 (one, then a last); III.3 ¶20 says he "had fired his revolver six times", killed one and wounded two. III.2 itself says only that "two convicts fell" (¶73) and that one was thrown down the steps (¶77), and does not say who died. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- ¶44 gives the Speedy's cargo as "bound for one of the Sandwich Islands"; ¶49 says she was at anchor "in sight of Norfolk Island". The two do not disagree; noted for the cards. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
