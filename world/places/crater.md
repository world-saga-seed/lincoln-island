# The crater of Mont Franklin: the extinct crater, with its breach and lava stair on the north

*The crater at the top of Mont Franklin: a vast extinct funnel, entered by a breach on the north whose hardened lava forms a natural stair; its rim is a narrow ridge.*

Keywords: cratère, volcan éteint, cheminée volcanique, égueulement, lava stair, funnel, breach, rim

- **What it is.**
  - Its mouth on the north is a deep breach in the massif, the gullet through which the liquid lavas escaped when the volcano was active (I.10 ¶51). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: C’était l’égueulement du cratère supérieur, le goulot, si l’on veut, par lequel s’échappaient les matières éruptives liquides
  - The hardened lavas and crusted scoriae form a kind of natural stair, with broad steps, that eases the way to the summit (I.10 ¶51). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: formaient une sorte d’escalier naturel, aux marches largement dessinées
  - From there it is a thousand feet more to the top (I.10 ¶53). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: C’était encore une hauteur de mille pieds à franchir
  - Its inner slopes, long and winding, run in a wide screw-thread inside the volcano (I.10 ¶53). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: décrivaient un large pas de vis à l’intérieur du volcan
  - The volcano is completely extinct: no smoke, no flame, no rumble, no sulphurous vapour in the air of the crater (I.10 ¶54). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: C’était plus que le sommeil d’un volcan, c’était sa complète extinction
  - It is a vast funnel that widens to a height of a thousand feet above the plateau, and at the foot of the breach thick lava flows wind down the flanks (I.11 ¶6). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un vaste entonnoir qui allait en s’évasant jusqu’à une hauteur de mille pieds au-dessus du plateau
  - Its inner slope does not pass thirty-five to forty degrees, so the climb meets no obstacle (I.11 ¶7). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dont l’inclinaison ne dépassait pas trente-cinq à quarante degrés
  - It shows traces of very ancient lavas that probably poured over the top of the cone before this lateral breach opened a new way (I.11 ¶7). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les traces de laves très-anciennes
  - The volcanic chimney that joins the underground layers to the crater is lost in darkness, its depth beyond estimating; its complete extinction was not in doubt (I.11 ¶8). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: elle se perdait dans l’obscurité
  - The north rim carries a conical swelling at the summit (I.11 ¶9). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une intumescence conique qui en boursouflait le bord septentrional
  - The rim is a narrow ridge that can be walked all round (I.12 ¶1). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ils firent le tour du cratère par son étroite arête
- **Appears in:** I.10, I.11, I.12
