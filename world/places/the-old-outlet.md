# The old outlet: the passage in the granite by which the lake’s overflow ran to the sea

*The lake’s drain, a hole at its south point that the blast of 21 May left bare: a winding, widening passage of black granite older than the island’s waters, running down to the great cavern and the well.*

Keywords: déversoir, ancien déversoir, outlet, overflow, conduit, boyau, couloir, passage, tunnel, spillway, stair, descent, torches, trop-plein

- **What it is.**
  - The lake’s overflow must leave somewhere, since the Red Creek’s flow is considerable; nature must have given the surplus a spillway (I.16 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il était donc nécessaire qu’en un endroit quelconque, la nature eût offert un déversoir au trop-plein du lac
  - No sign of it is found on the shores, so Smith concludes that it is hollowed inside the granite mass of the coast (I.16 ¶48). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il faut qu’il soit creusé à l’intérieur du massif granitique de la côte
  - Smith could not find by where and how the water was discharged, though he walked the whole shore (I.16 ¶47). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: l’ingénieur n’avait pu découvrir par où et comment s’opérait la décharge des eaux
  - A pronounced current is felt at the small beach; scraps of wood thrown in drift towards the south angle (I.17 ¶10). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un courant assez prononcé
    - quote: ils se dirigeaient vers l’angle sud
  - At the lake’s southern point the water dips, as if it were lost in a fissure, and a subterranean fall can be heard with the ear at the water’s level (I.17 ¶11, ¶12). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une sorte de dépression des eaux

  - The mouth measures about twenty feet wide and only two high, like a sewer mouth at the edge of a pavement (I.18 ¶14). *(Drafted.)*

    - quote: n’accusaient pas une pente de plus de trente à trente-cinq degrés
