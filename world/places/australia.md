# Australia: the continent whose route and whose trees the island is measured against

*The southern continent, named for the route of ships, for its temperate trees found along the Red Creek, and for its hunters' bows.*

Keywords: Australie, Nouvelle-Hollande, Australian, continent, route, trees, hunters

- **What it is.**
  - Smith feared the island lay too far north for the ships that go to Australia by rounding Cape Horn (I.11 ¶43). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: trop au nord pour ceux qui se rendent à l’Australie en doublant le cap Horn
  - The trees on the Red Creek belong to the species that abound in the temperate zone of Australia or Tasmania (I.12 ¶41). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: aux espèces qui abondent dans la zone modérée de l’Australie ou de la Tasmanie
  - The tall grass of the clearings is called "tussac" in New Holland (I.12 ¶41). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ce haut gazon que l’on appelle « tussac » dans la Nouvelle-Hollande
  - Its hunters are skilful with bows and arrows (I.12 ¶52). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: aussi adroits à les manier que des chasseurs australiens
- **Appears in:** I.11, I.12
