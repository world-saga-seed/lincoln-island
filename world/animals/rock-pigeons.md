# Rock pigeons: the cliff birds whose eggs feed the first supper

*Wild pigeons nested by hundreds in the granite crest, whose eggs the castaways gather.*

Keywords: pigeon, pigeon de roche, wild pigeon, eggs, œufs, Harbert

- **What it is.**
  - Some hundreds of birds are nested in the holes of the stone along the southern crest of the granite plateau (I.4 ¶55). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: quelques centaines d’oiseaux nichés dans les trous de la pierre
  - Harbert identifies them as wild pigeons or rock pigeons by the double black band on the wing, the white rump and the ash-blue plumage (I.4 ¶58). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Je les reconnais à la double bande noire de leur aile, à leur croupion blanc, à leur plumage bleu-cendré
  - Harbert reasons that if the rock pigeon is good to eat, its eggs must be excellent (I.4 ¶58). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: si le pigeon de roche est bon à manger, ses œufs doivent être excellents
  - The nests of the rock pigeons that flit about the crest of the granite wall are only holes bored in the crest itself, in the irregularly cut edge of the granite (I.16 ¶21). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Les nids des pigeons de roche qui voletaient à sa cime
    - quote: n’étaient, en réalité, que des trous forés à la crête même et sur la lisière irrégulièrement découpée du granit
- **On the page.**
  - Some dozens of eggs are found in cavities and collected in Pencroff's handkerchief (I.4 ¶62). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: Quelques douzaines furent recueillies, puis placées dans le mouchoir du marin
  - Two dozen eggs are brought by Harbert for the first supper, and are hardened in hot ashes and found excellent (I.5 ¶83). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: Deux douzaines d’œufs furent apportées par Harbert
  - The hardened eggs are excellent (I.5 ¶85). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: Ces œufs durcis étaient excellents
  - Pigeons fly by hundreds at the crest of the plateau; the castaways think of killing some with sticks or stones (I.6 ¶7). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ces pigeons qui volaient par centaines à la crête du plateau
  - Pigeon eggs make up, with lithodomes, the breakfast of 26 March (I.6 ¶10). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: uniquement composé d’œufs de pigeon et de lithodomes
- **Appears in:** I.4, I.5, I.6, I.16
