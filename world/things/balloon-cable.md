# The balloon's cable: the strong cable that moored the basket

*A strong cable run through a ring in the pavement, its double coming back aboard, cast off by Pencroff at departure.*

Keywords: câble, mooring, ring, anneau, pavement, double, cast off

- **What it is.**
  - It is a strong cable passed through a ring sealed in the pavement of the square, its double coming back aboard (I.2 ¶49). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un fort câble passé dans un anneau scellé dans le pavé, et dont le double remontait à bord
- **On the page.**
  - With the ballast released the balloon was held only by the cable's double (I.2 ¶52). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: L’aérostat n’était alors retenu que par le double du câble
  - Pencroff cast off the double and the balloon left on an oblique course (I.2 ¶55). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il largua le double du câble
- **Appears in:** I.2
