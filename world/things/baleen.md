# The baleen: the whale’s horny plates, whalebone

*The eight hundred horny plates of the stranded whale’s upper mouth, six feet long at their teeth, of which Smith keeps a dozen for his traps.*

Keywords: fanons, baleen, whalebone, whale, baleine, lames cornées, peignes, traps

- **What it is.**
  - The upper part of the whale’s mouth carries on both sides eight hundred horny plates, very elastic, fibrous, fringed at their edges like two great combs (II.10 ¶67). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: huit cents lames cornées, très-élastiques, de contexture fibreuse
    - quote: effilées à leurs bords comme deux grands peignes
  - Their teeth are six feet long and hold back the animalcules, small fish and molluscs the whale lives on (II.10 ¶67). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dont les dents, longues de six pieds
    - quote: servent à retenir les milliers d’animalcules, de petits poissons et de mollusques dont se nourrit la baleine
  - The colonists expect to find a use for them, though they carry neither umbrellas nor corsets at Granite-house (II.10 ¶67). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: qui trouveraient, sans doute, leur emploi, bien qu’on ne portât ni parapluies ni corsets à Granite-house
  - Smith takes a dozen, cuts each into six equal parts and sharpens them at the end (see [the baleen traps](baleen-traps.md)) (II.10 ¶69). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Il prit une douzaine de fanons de baleine qu’il coupa en six parties égales et qu’il aiguisa à leur extrémité
- **Appears in:** II.10, II.11
