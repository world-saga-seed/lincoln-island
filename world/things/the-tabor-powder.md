# The Tabor powder: the intact barrel of powder from the Tabor hut

*A barrel of powder found intact on the shelf of the Tabor hut and brought back to Lincoln, where a small magazine is planned for it.*

Keywords: poudre, powder, baril, barrel, tonneau, poudrière, magazine, Tabor, reserve

- **What it is.**
  - A barrel of powder, still intact, stands on the shelf of the Tabor hut (II.14 ¶5). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un baril de poudre encore intact
  - Spilett judges the reserve of powder and lead important (II.14 ¶35). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la réserve de poudre et de plomb est importante
  - At Lincoln the two barrels, of powder and of lead, are very well received (II.15 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Les deux tonneaux contenant de la poudre et du plomb
    - quote: furent très-bien reçus
  - It is agreed to set up a small powder magazine, outside Granite-house or even in the upper cave, where there is no explosion to fear (II.15 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: établir une petite poudrière, soit en dehors de Granite-house, soit même dans la caverne supérieure, où il n’y avait aucune explosion à craindre
  - The pyroxyle is nevertheless to go on being used instead of ordinary powder (see [the pyroxyle](pyroxyle.md); [the powder](the-powder.md)) (II.15 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: l’emploi du pyroxyle dut être continué
- **Appears in:** II.14, II.15
