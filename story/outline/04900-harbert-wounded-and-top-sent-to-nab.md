### 3. Treatment decided: cold water and no closing of the wounds (III.7 ¶30–¶45)

  - quote: Harbert était placé sur le côté gauche

- Spilett sits at the bedside; Smith stands; Pencroff has torn his shirt and mechanically makes lint. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: il faisait de la charpie
