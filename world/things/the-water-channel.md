# The water channel: the lake water led to Granite-house

*A small cut made below the lake’s level that sends twenty-five to thirty gallons a day through the old outlet to a tank in the back store; it did not freeze in winter.*

Keywords: dérivation, rigole, saignée, water supply, eau, lake, conduit, tank, réservoir, trop-plein, gallons

- **What it is.**
  - Smith uses the old outlet to bring to the new dwelling a thread of the lake’s fresh water, by a small cut below its level (I.19 ¶45). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un filet des eaux douces du lac
    - quote: Une petite saignée, faite au-dessous de leur niveau
  - It gives twenty-five to thirty gallons a day, a pure and inexhaustible source, so that water will never fail Granite-house (I.19 ¶45). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: vingt-cinq à trente gallons
    - quote: L’eau ne devait donc jamais manquer à Granite-house
  - The note gives the gallon as about four and a half litres (I.19 n1). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Le gallon vaut environ 4 litres et demi.
  - A plate shows the cut (I.19 pl3). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Une petite saignée…
  - In the cold of winter the water, drawn from below the frozen surface and led by the old outlet, keeps its liquidity (I.21 ¶6). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Prises au-dessous de la surface glacée
    - quote: elles conservaient leur liquidité
  - It reaches a tank dug in the corner of the back store, whose overflow runs off by the well to the sea (see [the well](../places/the-well.md)) (I.21 ¶6). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un réservoir intérieur, qui avait été creusé à l’angle de l’arrière-magasin
    - quote: dont le trop-plein s’enfuyait par le puits jusqu’à la mer
- **Appears in:** I.19, I.21
