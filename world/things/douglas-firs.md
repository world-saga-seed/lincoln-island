# The douglas firs: tall conifers like those of America’s north-west, whose bark covers the pirogue

*Conifers called "douglas" among the trees of the forest, like those of the north-west coast of America; the last hurricane felled some whose bark served for the pirogue.*

Keywords: douglas, sapin, conifer, tree, North-West coast, bark, écorce, pirogue, hurricane

- **What it is.**
  - They are conifers like those that grow on the north-west coast of America, among the trees of the forest with deodars and admirable firs (I.6 ¶27). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des « douglas », semblables à ceux qui poussent sur la côte nord-ouest de l’Amérique
  - The last hurricane had thrown down a quantity of them; their bark is supple and tough and suits the building of a bark boat (see [the pirogue](the-pirogue.md)) (II.1 ¶35). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le dernier ouragan avait abattu une certaine quantité de douglas
    - quote: l’écorce, souple et tenace
  - Some lay on the ground and had only to be stripped, which was the hardest part given the colonists’ imperfect tools (II.1 ¶35). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il n’y avait plus qu’à les écorcer
    - quote: vu l’imperfection des outils que possédaient les colons
  - They are among the trees of the forest cut through on 31 October, most already known round the lake and the Grande-Vue plateau, and generally of medium size (II.4 ¶5). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des déodars, des douglas, des casuarinas
    - quote: la plupart avaient été déjà reconnus aux environs du lac et du plateau de Grande-Vue
  - It is one of the known kinds that follow one another beyond sight in the forest of the Far-West (III.11 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Déodars, douglas, casuarinas, banksias, gommiers, dragonniers et autres essences déjà reconnues, se succédaient au delà des limites du regard
- **Appears in:** I.6, II.1, II.4, III.11
