# The corral house furniture: the bed, table, bench, wardrobe and chest made for Ayrton

*A few pieces of furniture made for the wooden house at the corral.*

Keywords: couchette, lit, table, banc, armoire, coffre, meubles, corral, maison

- **What it is.**
  - A bed, table, bench, wardrobe and chest are made for the wooden house at the corral (II.17 ¶28). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Quelques meubles, couchette, table, banc, armoire, coffre, furent fabriqués
  - The bed is called Ayrton’s bed; the wounded Harbert is laid on it (III.7 ¶9). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: sur le lit d’Ayrton
- **Appears in:** II.17, III.7
