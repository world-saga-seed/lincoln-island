# Albatrosses: the great sea birds of the barren north shore

*Great sea birds seen on the desolate shore toward Cape Mandible-South.*

Keywords: albatros, albatross, oiseau de mer, sea bird, côte, shore, cap Mandibule-Sud

- **What it is.**
  - Great albatrosses frequent the barren shore of sand, shells and lava debris toward the cape that closes Union Bay, with gulls and wild ducks (I.14 ¶38). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des goëlands, de grands albatros
- **Appears in:** I.14
