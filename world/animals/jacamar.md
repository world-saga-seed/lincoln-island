# Jacamar: the metallic marsh bird that ran from Harbert's stone

*A long-billed marsh bird with harsh, metallic-glossed plumage, taken for a kingfisher's cousin.*

Keywords: jacamar, martin-pêcheur, kingfisher, oiseau, bird, marsh

- **What it is.**
  - In a marshy part of the forest Harbert points out a bird with a sharp, elongated bill, anatomically like a kingfisher, but distinguished by its rather harsh plumage with a metallic lustre (I.6 ¶21; II.3 ¶23, ¶28). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un oiseau à bec aigu et allongé, qui ressemblait anatomiquement à un martin-pêcheur
    - quote: son plumage assez rude, revêtu d’un éclat métallique
    - quote: bel oiseau dont le plumage assez rude est revêtu d’un éclat métallique
  - Harbert names it a "jacamar" (I.6 ¶22). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ce doit être un « jacamar »
  - Struck on the wing by Harbert's stone, it runs off at the full speed of its legs and is gone in an instant (I.6 ¶24). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le jacamar s’enfuit de toute la vitesse de ses jambes et disparut en un instant
  - The forest left of the Mercy is named the Jacamar Wood in memory of the bird chased on the first exploration (I.13 ¶36). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: en souvenir du volatile que Pencroff et Harbert avaient poursuivi lors de leur première exploration
  - Pencroff recognises one on the Mercy as the bird that escaped the first excursion and gave its name to that part of the forest; it is shot, and Top brings it back (II.3 ¶26, ¶28). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Le volatile qui nous a échappé à notre première excursion
    - quote: et dont nous avons donné le nom à cette partie de la forêt
    - quote: Top le rapporta au canot
  - Its flesh is somewhat tough, worse eating than the touracos-loris (II.3 ¶28). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le jacamar, dont la chair est un peu coriace
  - The forest is called indifferently the Jacamar Wood or the forests of the Far-West (II.3 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: aussi bien sous le nom de bois du Jacamar que sous celui de forêts du Far-West
  - Jacamars swarm among the birds of the forest strip of the west coast (II.4 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les oiseaux y fourmillaient, jacamars, couroucous
- **Appears in:** I.6, I.13, II.3, II.4
- **Open:**
  - Who killed the jacamar of the Mercy: of Pencroff's shot the text says "son coup partit malgré lui" (II.3 ¶24), and then that the honour of the fine shot went to Harbert (II.3 ¶28). It does not say whether both fired. *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: son coup partit malgré lui
    - quote: Au jeune garçon revint l’honneur de ce beau coup de fusil
