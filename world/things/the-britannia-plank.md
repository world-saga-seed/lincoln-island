# The Britannia plank: the board with half-effaced letters on the Tabor hut

*A plank of the wrecked ship’s bulwarks, built into the hut, on which only "BR..TAN.A" can still be read.*

Keywords: planche, board, lettres, letters, effacées, Britannia, pavois, bulwark, nom, name, Tabor

- **What it is.**
  - Spilett, after going round the Tabor hut, sees on a plank, probably one of those that formed the ship’s bulwarks, letters already half effaced (II.14 ¶23). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: vit sur une planche — probablement une de celles qui formaient les pavois du navire naufragé — ces lettres à demi effacées déjà
  - The letters as printed read "BR..TAN.A" (II.14 ln1). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: BR..TAN.A
  - Pencroff reads the word "Britannia" (see [the Britannia](the-britannia.md)) (II.14 ¶24). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Britannia !
- **Appears in:** II.14
