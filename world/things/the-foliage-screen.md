# The foliage screen: the branches hung over the windows of Granite-house

*A curtain of boughs and lianas hung before the windows of Granite-house on 17 October so that the cliff shows nothing of a dwelling; one ball tears it open the next morning.*

Keywords: feuillage, branchages, lianes, rideau, screen, camouflage, fenêtres, windows, dissimuler, Granite-house, frondaison

- **What it is.**
  - Smith orders the windows of Granite-house hidden under thicker branches, so that nothing betrays the presence of man on the island (III.1 ¶79). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Dissimulons également, sous des branchages plus épais, les fenêtres de Granite-house
    - quote: Que rien enfin ne trahisse la présence de l’homme sur cette île
  - It is made of a great quantity of boughs and lianas brought from the edge of the Jacamar wood by the colonists other than Nab and Ayrton, to look from a distance like natural foliage and veil the openings in the granite wall fairly well (III.1 ¶82). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: allèrent à la lisière du bois de Jacamar
    - quote: une grande quantité de branches et de lianes
    - quote: devaient, à une certaine distance, figurer une frondaison naturelle
  - In the morning it stands out green against the bare wall, but Bob Harvey is not thought to notice it (III.3 ¶22). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ce voile de branches vertes et de lianes qui dissimulait les fenêtres de Granite-house
    - quote: tranchaient sur la muraille nue
  - A ball splits the curtain and shows a gaping opening in the granite, so that the retreat is discovered (III.3 ¶94, ¶96, ¶97). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ayant fendu le rideau de feuillage, laissa voir une ouverture béante dans le granit
    - quote: Leur retraite était découverte
- **Appears in:** III.1, III.3
