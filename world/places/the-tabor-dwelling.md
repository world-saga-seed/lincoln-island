# The Tabor dwelling: the plank hut found empty on Tabor Island

*A one-room hut of ship’s planks under tarred cloth, built on the lee of a small hill among gum trees on Tabor Island; the colonists find it empty and long unused, with a castaway’s tools and arms still in it.*

Keywords: habitation, hut, cabane, maison, Tabor, naufragé, castaway, planches, toile goudronnée, pavois, gommiers, abandoned

- **What it is.**
  - It is built of planks covered with a thick tarred cloth (II.13 ¶92). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: elle avait été construite en planches recouvertes d’une épaisse toile goudronnée
  - It is first seen at dusk, as a confused mass among the trees, and is found empty (II.13 ¶90, ¶92, ¶94). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une masse confuse entre les arbres
    - quote: Aux lueurs du crépuscule
    - quote: L’habitation était vide !
  - It is one small room, with a rough chimney at the back holding cold ashes and an armful of dry wood (II.14 ¶4). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une petite salle
    - quote: une cheminée grossière, avec quelques cendres froides
    - quote: une brassée de bois sec
  - The planks come from the hull or deck of a ship (II.14 ¶22). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ces planches provenaient de la coque ou du pont d’un navire
  - The narrator takes it as probable that a disabled ship was cast on the coast, that at least one of her crew was saved, and that he built the dwelling from the wreck with the tools at his disposal (II.14 ¶22). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un bâtiment désemparé avait été jeté à la côte sur l’île
    - quote: au moyen des débris du navire, cet homme, ayant des outils à sa disposition, avait construit cette demeure
  - Inside stand a bed in disorder (see [the Tabor bed](../things/tabor-bed.md)), two rusted kettles and an overturned pot (see [the Tabor kettles](../things/tabor-kettles.md); [the Tabor pot](../things/tabor-pot.md)), a cupboard with a few half-mouldy sailor’s clothes (see [the Tabor cupboard](../things/tabor-cupboard.md); [the Tabor sailor’s clothes](../things/tabor-sailor-clothes.md)), on the table a tin cutlery and a Bible gnawed by damp (see [the Tabor table](../things/tabor-table.md); [the Tabor tin cutlery](../things/tabor-tin-cutlery.md); [the Tabor Bible](../things/tabor-bible.md)), in a corner a shovel, a pickaxe and a pick (see [the Tabor tools](../things/the-tabor-tools.md)) and two shotguns (see [the Tabor guns](../things/the-tabor-guns.md)), and on a shelf a barrel of powder, a barrel of lead and several boxes of caps (see [the Tabor powder](../things/the-tabor-powder.md); [the Tabor lead](../things/the-tabor-lead.md); [the Tabor primers](../things/the-tabor-primers.md); [the Tabor shelf](../things/tabor-shelf.md)) (II.14 ¶5). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un lit en désordre
    - quote: deux bouilloires couvertes de rouille et une marmite renversée
    - quote: une armoire, avec quelques vêtements de marin à demi moisis
    - quote: un couvert d’étain et une Bible rongée par l’humidité
    - quote: pelle, pioche, pic, deux fusils de chasse, dont l’un était brisé
    - quote: un baril de poudre encore intact, un baril de plomb et plusieurs boîtes d’amorces
  - Everything in it is covered with a thick layer of dust, which long years, perhaps, had gathered (II.14 ¶5). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le tout couvert d’une épaisse couche de poussière, que de longues années, peut-être, avaient accumulée
  - The blankets of the bed are damp and yellowed, which showed that it had not been used for a long time (II.14 ¶5). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dont les couvertures, humides et jaunies, prouvaient qu’il ne servait plus depuis longtemps
  - Harbert says the room has not been lived in for a long while (II.14 ¶8). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Voilà longtemps que cette chambre n’a été habitée
  - It holds a bench (see [the Tabor bench](../things/tabor-bench.md)) and a store of wood in a corner, enough to heat it (see [the Tabor fireplace](../things/tabor-fireplace.md)) (II.14 ¶17). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: assis sur un banc
    - quote: une provision de bois qui se trouvait dans un coin
  - It is built in a happy situation on the reverse of a small hill, which five or six magnificent gum trees shelter (see [the gum trees](../things/gum-trees.md)) (II.14 ¶21). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: au revers d’une petite colline que cinq ou six magnifiques gommiers abritaient
  - In front of its façade the axe has cleared a wide opening through the trees, which lets the eye reach the sea (II.14 ¶21). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la hache avait ménagé une large éclaircie, qui permettait aux regards de s’étendre sur la mer
  - A small lawn, enclosed by a wooden fence falling to ruin (see [the Tabor fence](../things/tabor-fence.md)), leads to the shore, on the left of which opens the mouth of the stream (see [the Tabor stream](tabor-stream.md)) (II.14 ¶21). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Une petite pelouse, entourée d’une barrière de bois qui tombait en ruines
    - quote: sur la gauche duquel s’ouvrait l’embouchure du ruisseau
  - The walk from it to the boat on the east coast is hardly a mile and takes twenty minutes (see [the Tabor landing](tabor-landing.md)) (II.14 ¶28, ¶29). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un mille à peine
    - quote: Vingt minutes après avoir quitté l’habitation
  - It is the only dwelling on the islet, and long abandoned (II.15 ¶20). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la seule habitation de l’îlot était depuis longtemps abandonnée
- **Appears in:** II.13, II.14, II.15
