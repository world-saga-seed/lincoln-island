# The small beach at the lake’s south angle: where the dugong came ashore

*A small beach at the south point of Lake Grant, shallow at its edge, where the dugong’s body stranded on 6 May.*

Keywords: grève, petite grève, beach, shore, south point, pointe sud, angle sud, dugong, shallows, lake

- **What it is.**
  - The dugong’s body, coming out of a sheet of scarlet water, stranded on a small beach at the lake’s south angle (I.16 ¶63). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une petite grève à l’angle sud du lac
  - Where the dugong rose, twenty feet from the bank, the water did not seem deep (I.16 ¶54). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: qui ne paraissaient pas être profondes en cet endroit
  - It lies at the lake’s south point (I.17 ¶2). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: située à la pointe sud du lac
  - On this beach the water is shallow; from there the lake bed sinks little by little (I.17 ¶5). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les eaux étaient peu profondes
    - quote: le fond du lac s’abaissait peu à peu
- **Appears in:** I.16, I.17
- **Open:**
  - The text calls the lake’s lower end "the south angle" (I.16 ¶63), "the south point" (I.17 ¶2) and, where the party arrives, "the south-east angle" (I.16 ¶47); it does not say whether these are one place (I.16 ¶47). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: à l’angle sud-est du lac
    - quote: une petite grève à l’angle sud du lac
    - quote: située à la pointe sud du lac
