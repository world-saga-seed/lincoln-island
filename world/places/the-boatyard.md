# The boatyard: the yard where Pencroff builds the pirogue

*The worksite where the engineer and the sailor work on the pirogue.*

Keywords: chantier, boatyard, shipyard, pirogue, worksite, Pencroff

- **What it is.**
  - The yard where the engineer and the sailor work together is where Harbert tells what has happened (II.1 ¶83). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Arrivé au chantier, où l’ingénieur et le marin travaillaient ensemble
  - A yard for the Bonadventure is prepared eight days after the trees are felled, in the recess between the Chimneys and the wall, and her keel lies along the sand (see [the Bonadventure](../things/bonadventure.md)) (II.10 ¶6). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dans le renfoncement qui existait entre les Cheminées et la muraille, un chantier était préparé
    - quote: une quille, longue de trente-cinq pieds
  - The yard for the new ship is set up at the Chimneys, where the felled trees are hauled (see [the schooner](../things/the-schooner.md)) (III.14 ¶15). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: où fut établi le chantier de construction
  - Smith could not have placed its sheds anywhere else near Granite-house (III.14 ¶27). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les hangars du chantier de construction
    - quote: il n’aurait pu établir en aucun autre endroit, à proximité de Granite-house
  - By the end of September the carcass of the schooner stands on it (III.15 ¶9). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: se dressait sur le chantier de construction
  - The colonists work at it on the strand of Granite-house, from which the volcano cannot be seen (III.18 ¶32). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: qu’on ne pouvait apercevoir, d’ailleurs, de la grève de Granite-house
  - It holds spades, picks and axes, which the colonists fetch to build the dike; it is spared for some days by the lava, and would not have been had the lava reached the beach (III.19 ¶50, ¶56, ¶67). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ils en rapportèrent des pelles, des pioches, des haches
    - quote: le chantier de construction n’eût pas été épargné
- **Appears in:** II.1, II.10, III.14, III.15, III.18, III.19
- **Open:**
  - The text does not say whether the pirogue’s yard of II.1 ¶83 is the Bonadventure’s yard of II.10 ¶6; no place is named for the first. *(Drafted.)*

    - quote: un chantier était préparé
  - The text does not say whether the yard of the schooner (III.14 ¶15) is the Bonadventure’s yard of II.10 ¶6 in the recess between the Chimneys and the wall. (III.14 ¶15). *(Drafted.)*
