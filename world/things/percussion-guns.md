# The percussion guns: the two cap guns of the chest

*Two percussion guns in the chest, left behind on the first exploration because their caps would soon be used up.*

Keywords: fusils à capsule, percussion, cap, amorces, fulminate, fusils à système, arms, chest

- **What it is.**
  - There are two percussion guns in the chest (II.2 ¶53). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: 2 fusils à capsule
  - They are called guns "of the system" and need fulminate primers, which frequent use would quickly exhaust; they are therefore not chosen for the exploration (see [the percussion caps](percussion-caps.md)) (II.3 ¶6). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: fusils à système
    - quote: exigeant des amorces fulminantes, qu’un fréquent usage eût promptement épuisées
- **Appears in:** II.2, II.3
