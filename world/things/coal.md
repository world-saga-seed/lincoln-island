# The coal: houille from an open seam by the Red Creek

*A coal seam open to the sky near the Red Creek mouth, found by Smith, used to charge the pottery kiln.*

Keywords: houille, charcoal, fuel, seam, deposit, mineral

- **What it is.**
  - Smith carried a sample of coal in his pocket with other minerals (see [the mineral samples](mineral-samples.md)) (I.12 ¶77). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ceci du charbon
  - It is a deposit open to the sky near the mouth of the Red Creek (I.13 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un gisement à ciel ouvert vers l’embouchure du Creek-Rouge
  - Fuel, wood or coal, was immediately usable (I.13 ¶10). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le combustible, bois ou charbon de terre, était immédiatement utilisable
  - The coal deposits worked by the colonists lie not far from the iron-ore deposit (I.15 ¶9). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Non loin de ce gisement se trouvaient les gisements de charbon de terre déjà exploités
  - Smith means to work on the very deposit of coal and ore, at the foot of the north-east spurs of Mont Franklin (I.15 ¶31). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: opérer sur le gisement même de houille et de minerai
  - The coal was gathered without trouble, near, at the surface of the ground (I.15 ¶43). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la houille fut récoltée, sans peine et non loin, à la surface du sol
  - The coal deposit abounds in schistose pyrites (I.17 ¶37). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: où abondaient ces pyrites schisteuses
  - In the cold the colonists add several cartloads of coal to the wood fuel, fetched from the foot of the buttresses of Mont Franklin (I.21 ¶5). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: plusieurs charretées de houille
    - quote: au pied des contreforts du mont Franklin
  - The sailor and Nab go again to the coal seam with the cart and bring back some tons of fuel (I.22 ¶37). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: jusqu’au gisement de houille, afin de rapporter quelques tonnes de combustible
  - It is the ground’s fuel for the glass furnace (II.9 ¶34). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le sol fournissait la houille pour chauffer le four à la température voulue
  - Neither wood nor coal is spared in the cold of July, the great hall being warmed with it (II.11 ¶40, ¶41). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: on n’épargna ni le bois, ni le charbon
    - quote: bien chauffée de houille
  - The coal and iron deposits are among the places visited through the winter and spring (II.12 ¶85). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ni aux gisements de houille et de fer
  - The surplus timber of the ship’s building saves the coal, which is harder to carry (III.14 ¶49). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: permit d’économiser la houille, qui exigeait un transport plus pénible
