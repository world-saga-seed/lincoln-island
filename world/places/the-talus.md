# The talus: the long slope west of the granite wall’s north angle

*A long slope of stones, earth and sand, grassed and sprinkled with trees, that runs on west from the granite wall’s north angle.*

Keywords: talus, slope, pente, falaise, plain, sand, north angle, west, wall

- **What it is.** A place, a slope of earth and stones. Build if needed. *(Drafted.)*
