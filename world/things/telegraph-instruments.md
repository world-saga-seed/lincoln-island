# The telegraph instruments: the electromagnet, dial and bell at each end of the line

*The receiver and manipulator at Granite-house and at the corral: an electromagnet of soft iron, a plate of soft iron and a needle on a dial with the letters of the alphabet; an electric bell at Granite-house.*

Keywords: récepteur, manipulateur, électro-aimant, cadran, aiguille, alphabet, fer doux, timbre, instruments

- **What it is.**
  - At both stations the wire winds round an electromagnet of soft iron; a soft-iron plate before it is drawn while the current passes and falls back when it stops, and Smith links to the plate a needle on a dial lettered with the alphabet (II.18 ¶48). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le fil s’enroulait sur un électro-aimant
    - quote: une aiguille disposée sur un cadran, qui portait en exergue les lettres de l’alphabet
  - Granite-house has an electric bell, which announces Ayrton’s replies and falls silent when the line is cut (III.6 ¶7, ¶57). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le timbre électrique annonçait qu’il avait trouvé tout en ordre au corral
    - quote: Le timbre de Granite-house resta muet
- **Appears in:** II.18, III.6
- **Open:**
  - The call is received on a dial needle in II.18 ¶48 and on an electric bell in III.6 ¶7; the text does not say whether the two are one apparatus (II.18 ¶48; III.6 ¶7). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une aiguille disposée sur un cadran
    - quote: le timbre électrique annonçait
