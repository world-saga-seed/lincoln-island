## Discords (both sides kept, neither carded)

1. Port Ballon "la veille" (II.7 ¶14). The day before 3 November is the apes' day, 2 November, spent at Granite-house and the Chimneys. II.5 (02700) dates the balloon's finding to "cette journée du 2 novembre"; to be read at the merge.
   - quote: découvert la veille sur la côte méridionale
2. The bridge: "trois semaines" (II.7 ¶29), against 3 to 20 November, which is 17 days.
   - quote: La construction du pont de la Mercy dura trois semaines
   - quote: Le 20 novembre, le pont fut terminé
3. The corral: begun "vers la fin de janvier" (II.8 ¶30), taking "trois semaines" (¶35), with the battue on 7 February (¶36).
   - quote: Ce fut vers la fin de janvier que les colons entreprirent de grands travaux
   - quote: ne demanda pas moins de trois semaines
   - quote: Cette opération se fit le 7 février
4. Tabor's direction: west and south of Lincoln by the coordinates (II.9 ¶91), north-east in ¶99. (The coordinate gap is about 2° 30′ and 2° 14′, so "deux degrés" is loose too.)
   - quote: deux degrés et demi plus à l’ouest et deux degrés plus au sud que l’île Lincoln
   - quote: À cent cinquante milles environ dans le nord-est
5. The apes and the ladder (II.6): the ladder drawn up (¶24), Pencroff's view that the apes climbed it (¶42), yet the apes do not try to put it back (¶74). The count is never given: three or four (¶43), five or six (¶74), one more in the passage (¶89).
   - quote: Ils ne cherchaient même pas à replacer l’échelle
6. The landing's anniversary is 24 March (II.8 ¶48); I.1 has the balloon over the Pacific on 23 March.
   - quote: le 24 mars, arrivait l’anniversaire de ce jour où le ballon les jeta sur cette côte inconnue
7. The storm ends "vers le 9 mars" (II.9 ¶16); begun on the 2nd and lasting eight days (¶2, ¶5), it ends on the 10th.
8. The elder: its berries as coffee (II.8 ¶9), an infusion of its seeds (II.9 ¶69).
9. Harbert has grown two inches "depuis un an" (II.9 ¶13) in early March, a little short of a year since the landing.

## Dates worked out (marked as readings)

The ladder night is 1 to 2 November 1865; the observation day is 2 April 1866, the day after Easter, 1 April 1866. The year is stated once, II.8 ¶6, "début de l’année 1866".
