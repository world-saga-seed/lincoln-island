# The six convicts: the Speedy's survivors landed on the right bank

*Six men of the second boat, wrecked at the Mercy's mouth, who reach land armed while the brig is sunk with the rest.*

Keywords: six convicts, survivors, Speedy, pointe de l’Épave, Mercy, right bank, rive droite, évadés, armés

- **What it is.**
  - They are six survivors, out of eight men in the Speedy's second boat, which broke on the reefs at the Mercy's mouth after two of its men were mortally struck (III.3 ¶47). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Deux, sur huit, des hommes qui le montaient, furent mortellement frappés
    - quote: Mais les six survivants
  - They hold their arms above their heads to keep them dry and wade to the right bank of the river, then run towards the pointe de l’Épave, out of range (III.3 ¶47). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: élevant leurs armes au-dessus de leur tête pour les préserver du contact de l’eau
    - quote: parvinrent à prendre pied sur la rive droite de la rivière
    - quote: dans la direction de la pointe de l’Épave
  - They cannot reach Granite-house, for they cannot cross the river with its bridges raised (III.3 ¶48). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dans l’impossibilité d’atteindre Granite-house
    - quote: dont les ponts étaient relevés
  - They are armed (III.4 ¶15). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: car ils sont armés
  - After the brig sinks, none is seen, and they are supposed to have fled into the interior (III.4 ¶13, ¶14). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Aucun des fugitifs n’était visible
    - quote: avaient pris la fuite à l’intérieur de l’île
  - The narrator reckons them "chenapans de premier ordre", men for whom a river or a brook is no bar, and dangerous in despair (III.4 ¶72). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des chenapans de premier ordre
    - quote: n’en étaient pas à s’embarrasser d’une rivière ou d’un ruisseau
    - quote: poussés par le désespoir, de tels coquins pouvaient être redoutables

    - quote: il fallait veiller sur les caisses et colis
    - quote: eussent vite fait de les signaler
  - The colonists do not know where they are: Pencroff calls them six scoundrels prowling the island, and the narrator says six convicts, perhaps of the worst sort, prowl on the colonists' island (III.5 ¶44, ¶70). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les six coquins qui rôdent dans l’île
    - quote: six convicts, peut-être de la pire espèce, rôdaient sur leur île
  - They are well armed: Spilett reckons that if each lay in ambush for one of the colonists they would soon be masters of the colony (III.5 ¶62). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ils sont six et bien armés
  - On the road to Port-Ballon no trace of them is found; the narrator thinks they have gone to the least accessible parts of the island, not yet knowing the colonists' number or defences (III.6 ¶17). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ils ne trouvèrent aucune trace des fugitifs
    - quote: avaient dû gagner les portions les moins accessibles de l’île
  - The colonists suppose they cut the wire to the corral: the pole is dug up at its foot and uprooted by a man's hand, the wire freshly broken, and "quels autres que les convicts avaient intérêt à interrompre cette communication ?" (III.6 ¶80, ¶83, ¶86). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il a été déraciné de main d’homme
    - quote: il y a certainement peu de temps que la rupture a été produite
    - quote: quels autres que les convicts avaient intérêt à interrompre cette communication
  - On 11 November one fires from above the corral palisade and strikes Harbert; another, on the left of the enclosure, shoots through Smith's hat and is killed by Smith's dagger (III.6 ¶92, ¶93; III.7 ¶8). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Une détonation éclata au-dessus de la palissade
    - quote: lui traversa le chapeau d’une balle

    - quote: s’enfuyait par le contrefort sud du mont Franklin

  - Spilett reckons that they followed the south shore, went round the Serpentine peninsula's two coasts, did not venture into the Far-West woods, came to the Falls river's mouth and up its right bank to the buttresses of Mount Franklin, and found the empty corral (III.8 ¶2). *(Drafted.)*
