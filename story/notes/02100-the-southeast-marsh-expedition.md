# The Southeast Marsh Expedition (notes)

## Notes for drafting
- **Where it sits.** Follows I.20, which ends with the grain planted on 20 June. It runs from late June to 5 July: the first deep cold, then a day's expedition to the southeast marsh, with a long talk on the history and future of the earth. *(Drafted.)*
- **Cast.** Pencroff (¶1), Nab (¶3), Cyrus Smith (¶6), Gédéon Spilett ("le reporter", ¶10), Harbert (¶14), Top (¶8). All five colonists and the dog go on the expedition (¶8). *(Drafted.)*
- **Places.** Pencroff's "champ de blé" (¶1); the mouth of the Mercy and the lake (¶4); the foot of the Mount Franklin spurs, where coal is dug (¶5); Granite-house, its dining room with a second fireplace, its inner tank in the corner of the back storeroom, and the well (¶5–¶6); Lake Grant (¶6); the southeast marshy part between the Mercy and Claw Cape (¶7); the right bank of the Mercy, with its conifers (¶12); Wreck Point and a long beach on the open sea (¶20); Claw Cape, about four miles southeast (¶20); the Far West forest edge, four miles west (¶20); the marsh, about 20 square miles (¶53); the Tadornes marsh, "Tadorn’s-fens" (¶56). *(Drafted.)*
- **Things.** A Fahrenheit thermometer (conditional, ¶2); drifting timber and coal (¶5); a small stream from Lake Grant, an inner tank, a well overflow (¶6); spears, snares, bows, arrows (¶8); ice bridge over the Mercy (¶9); a camp fire of brushwood and wrack, cold meat, Oswego tea (¶20); a dozen tadorne ducks killed by arrows (¶55). *(Drafted.)*
- **Time.** 29 June, about 20 °F (¶2). 30 June, a Friday, the northern 31 December (¶3). 4 July, 8 °F (¶5). 5 July: leave at 6 a.m. at first light (¶8); crossing the Mercy, a culpeux sighting less than half a mile on (¶13); at the shore at 8 a.m. (¶20); breakfast; the long talk; the marsh; leave at about 5 p.m. (¶56); home at 8 p.m. (¶57). The chapter covers about a week; the expedition is one day. *(Drafted.)* The year is not stated; 30 June as a Friday fits 1865. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- **Clues.** (1) The talk puts the island's variety down to its having been part of a sunken continent (¶24, ¶32), and Cyrus says it is "purement d’origine volcanique" (¶46) and will probably disappear (¶48): a setup for later volcanic matters. *(My reading, to confirm.)* (2) The well in Granite-house carries the inner tank's overflow "jusqu’à la mer" (¶6); it matters for I.22 ¶53–¶54 (Top at the well). *(My reading, to confirm.)* (3) ¶55 speaks of "Un coup de fusil à plomb" as a conditional; the colonists have no firearm, and the lead shot of I.22 ¶82 comes at the end of the next chapter. *(My reading, to confirm.)* (4) Cyrus says "nous finirons peut-être par nous en tirer" (¶50): the colonists' hope of leaving. *(Drafted.)*
- **Length.** 57 paragraphs (¶1–¶57); 3,019 words. One note (n1, to ¶36) and one plate caption (pl1, after ¶9). The talk of ¶21–¶51 takes about a third of the words. *(Drafted.)*

## Open and flags
- ¶3 gives Pencroff's retort that the next year "commençait par un bon" day; 30 June is called a Friday. The weekday agrees with I.20 ¶19 (4 June a Sunday). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- ¶2 and ¶5 give Fahrenheit readings with centigrade equivalents ("vingt degrés seulement au-dessus de zéro (6°,67 centig. au-dessous de glace)"; "huit degrés Fahrenheit (13° centigr. au-dessous de zéro)"). The conversions agree with the Fahrenheit figures. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- ¶8 "de huit à neuf milles à l’aller" for the trip; ¶20 puts Claw Cape "à quatre milles environ" from the halt. Both are given; no clash. *(Drafted.)*
- ¶22 "Devant Granite-house, au moins, il y avait des bancs, un îlot": recalls the landing at the Chimneys. The chapter treats the landing place as the western coast; the I.19 view from Granite-house (the seam) does not conflict. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- ¶16 "Chili, aux Malouines, et sur tous ces parages américains traversés par les trentième et quarantième parallèles": the island is on the thirty-fifth parallel (I.20 ¶6). *(Drafted.)*
- ¶53 "pouvait mesurer vingt milles carrés" is the narrator's estimate. *(Drafted.)*
- No Wikisource correction touches this chapter. *(Drafted.)*
