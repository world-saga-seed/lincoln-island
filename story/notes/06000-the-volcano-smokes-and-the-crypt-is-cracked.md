# The volcano smokes and the crypt is cracked (notes)

## Notes for drafting
- **Where it sits.** The French III.18, chapter 06000, between 05900 (Nemo's death, the Nautilus sunk) and 06100 (the island doomed). It runs from the dawn of 16 October 1868 to 7 January 1869. *(Drafted.)*
- **Cast.** Cyrus Smith (¶1, "the colonists"; named ¶4), Pencroff, Nab, Ayrton (¶2); Harbert (¶4); Gédéon Spilett (¶5); Captain Nemo, dead (¶1, ¶5); Top and Jup do not appear. Onaggas (¶12, ¶61) and the corral's sheep and goats (¶55, ¶70) are present as animals. *(Drafted.)*
- **Places.** The Dakkar crypt, named here (¶1); the corral and the wire to the crypt (¶4); Granite-house (¶6); the shipyard and Port-Ballon (¶30–¶31); the Grande-Vue plateau (¶12, ¶33); Mount Franklin, its north-east breach (¶54); the Red Creek, its sulphurous spring (¶71–¶72); the causeway at the Shark Gulf (¶75); the north-west coast of basalt and porphyry (¶86); the crypt's end wall (¶101). *(Drafted.)*
- **Things.** The sheet-iron boat (¶2, ¶91); the wire (¶4); the new ship's frames and first planks (¶9), the précintes (¶31); the Speedy's rigging, saved whole (¶8); the notice for Tabor (¶7); the lantern and lighter (¶83, ¶93); handkerchiefs against ash (¶85). *(Drafted.)*
- **Time.** Dawn of 16 October 1868 ("night of 15 to 16 October", ¶5; year my reading from ¶9 and ¶11), about nine o'clock arrival at Granite-house (¶6); target of five months, early March (¶8); end of 1868 (¶9); 1 January 1869 storm (¶11); 3 January plume, shipyard work, evening on the plateau (¶12, ¶32–¶33); 4, 5, 6 January (¶52); 7 January dawn trip (¶55, ¶61), at the corral by about 9 (¶76), more than ten o'clock at the coast crest (¶86), twenty-five minutes' row in (¶100), half an hour out (¶106). Chapter covers about twelve weeks, most of it by summary. *(Drafted.)* The 8 January return is stated in III.19 ¶1 and implied by ¶58 ("returned the next day"). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- **Clues.** Smith's "Yes and no" and "I must see… I must visit the mountain" (¶46, ¶50) hold back the danger until III.19. "Captain Nemo had well foreseen it!" (¶74) and "That is what Captain Nemo feared!" (¶98) point to what Nemo told Smith in 05900's private talk. The note for Tabor (¶7) is explained in III.20. Thirteen smoke columns at the spring (¶72). *(Drafted.)*
- **Length.** 106 paragraphs (¶1 to ¶106), 4,459 words; three plate captions. *(Drafted.)*

## Open and flags
- Ten weeks and two and a half months. Spilett says "So the underground fires have smouldered for ten weeks" (¶39) right after Harbert's "that was already two and a half months ago!" (¶38). From about 15 October to 3 January is about eleven and a half weeks. Card neither. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- "After two and a half months, the pairs had been put in place" (¶9) is counted from a start the chapter does not give; with ¶8's "five months" for the whole work and ¶30's "before two months" for the launch, the start of the hull is unfixed here. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- Wikisource correction, ¶62: the print reads "qu’on les as vues"; Wikisource reads "qu’on les a vues". The reading is used; no fact rests on it. *(Drafted.)*
- Seam with 05900: ¶91 of III.17 ends with the Nautilus resting on the sea floor and the crypt dark. III.18 ¶1 begins at "At daybreak" with the colonists back at "the entrance to the cavern"; it follows. ¶94 here recalls the electric light that "did it still spread to the depths of the waters". *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- ¶88 says "no shore was uncovered" outside while ¶2 has "a little beach" inside the crypt; both are about different spots. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
