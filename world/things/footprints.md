# The footprints: the prints of Smith's shoes in the sand

*A line of footprints that Nab found on the northern shore leading to the dunes, later matched to Smith's shoes.*

Keywords: empreintes, traces, prints, tracks, pas, sand, shoes, sleepwalker

- **What it is.**

    - quote: vers cinq heures du soir, je remarquai sur le sable des empreintes de pas

    - quote: au relais de marée, seulement
