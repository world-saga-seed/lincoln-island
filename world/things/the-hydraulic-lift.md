# The hydraulic lift: the lift Smith plans for Granite-house

*A water-driven lift that Smith means to install later to spare the inhabitants of Granite-house all fatigue and loss of time on the ladder.*

Keywords: ascenseur, lift, elevator, hydraulic, water power, ladder, plan

- **What it is.**
  - Smith counts on installing later a hydraulic lift that would spare the inhabitants of Granite-house all fatigue and loss of time (I.19 ¶28). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un ascenseur hydraulique qui éviterait toute fatigue et toute perte de temps aux habitants de Granite-house
- **Appears in:** I.19
- **Open:**
  - Whether it is built is not said in I.19 to I.22; I.19 ¶28 has only the plan. *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un ascenseur hydraulique
