# The Tabor tools: the shovel, pickaxe and pick of the Tabor hut

*A shovel, a pickaxe and a pick from a corner of the Tabor hut, brought back with the castaway’s arms.*

Keywords: outils, tools, pelle, shovel, pioche, pickaxe, pic, Tabor, naufragé

- **What it is.**
  - In a corner of the Tabor hut lie a shovel, a pickaxe and a pick (II.14 ¶5). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: quelques outils, pelle, pioche, pic
  - Pencroff says that castaways set great store by their tools and arms, the last wreckage of the wreck, and would have taken them if they had left the island (II.14 ¶14). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Vous savez le prix que les naufragés attachent à ces objets, qui sont les dernières épaves du naufrage
  - Spilett says these tools and the arms will complete the equipment of Granite-house (II.14 ¶35). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ces armes, ces outils compléteront le matériel de Granite-house
  - Smith, examining the arms and tools, sees nothing that could establish the unknown’s identity (see [the Tabor guns](the-tabor-guns.md)) (II.15 ¶44). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ayant examiné les armes, les outils, ne vit rien qui pût le mettre à même d’établir l’identité de l’inconnu
- **Appears in:** II.14, II.15
