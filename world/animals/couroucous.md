# Couroucous: the small long-tailed birds that let themselves be clubbed

*Small, softly feathered, long-tailed forest birds that wait on low branches for insects, do not fly off, and make delicate eating.*

Keywords: couroucou, trogon, oiseau, bird, game, bâton, club, plumes, mauviette, larks, reserve, larder

- **What it is.**
  - They are small birds of pretty plumage, with long, shimmering tails, in a flock that scatters among the branches (I.6 ¶28). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une volée d’oiseaux de petite taille et d’un joli plumage, à queue longue et chatoyante
  - Their feathers are weakly attached and fall, covering the ground with a fine down (I.6 ¶28). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: semant leurs plumes, faiblement attachées, qui couvrirent le sol d’un fin duvet
  - Harbert names them couroucous from the feathers he picks up (I.6 ¶29). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ce sont des « couroucous »
  - They are good to eat, their flesh very delicate, and easy to approach and kill with sticks (I.6 ¶31). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: leur chair est très délicate
    - quote: il est facile de les approcher et de les tuer à coups de bâton
  - They wait on the low branches for the insects that feed them, gripping the shoots with feathered feet (I.6 ¶32). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: attendaient au passage les insectes qui leur servent de nourriture
    - quote: leurs pattes emplumées
  - They do not think of flying and let themselves be struck down stupidly, in rows, until the rest flee (I.6 ¶33). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ne songeaient point à s’envoler et se laissèrent stupidement abattre
  - Couroucous are among the birds that swarm on the forest strip of the west coast, where there is no tree without a nest (II.4 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les oiseaux y fourmillaient, jacamars, couroucous
    - quote: Pas un arbre qui n’eût un nid
