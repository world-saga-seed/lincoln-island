# The scholar’s notes to chapter XXIX

*Each note is set after the words its “After:” line quotes, exactly as they stand in story/prose/02900-the-bridge-and-the-onaggas.md. Delete this file and the chapter builds without them (R1121-163).*

## a

After: from this point to Balloon Harbor, discovered the day before on the southern coast,

See note (a) to chapter XXVII. The harbor was found on the 1st of November, two days before this; the day before this was spent at the Chimneys and in taking back Granite House.

## b

After: It took three weeks of very steady work to build this bridge.

From the 3rd of November to the 20th, when the bridge was finished, is seventeen days, not three weeks. Both dates are given in this chapter, and the 3rd agrees with the count of days in chapters XXVII and XXVIII (see note (a) to chapter XXVII). I find nothing that moves the 20th, and I take the dates.
