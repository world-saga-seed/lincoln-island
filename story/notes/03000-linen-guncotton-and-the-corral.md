## Open and flags
- Seam with II.7: II.7 ends on the evening of the cart's trip to Port Ballon, end of December 1865; ¶1 here opens `La première semaine de janvier`, a gap of a few days. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- The corral's duration: ¶30 says the works began `vers la fin de janvier` and ¶35 says `trois semaines`, but ¶36 puts the battue at `le 7 février`. End of January plus three weeks falls around 20 February. The two readings are both the text's. Card neither. *(Drafted.)*
- ¶48 gives the anniversary as `le 24 mars` of `ce jour où le ballon les jeta sur cette côte inconnue`; the example fragment for this task gives the balloon over the Pacific on 23 March 1865 (I.1). Whether the landing is 23 or 24 March is for the chapters of Part I, not supplied here. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- ¶48 says `onze mois`; from 24 March 1865 to the evenings of February or early March 1866 is eleven months or less. Consistent with the chapter's own dates. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
