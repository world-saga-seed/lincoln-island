# The seaweed: wrack and algae used as bedding and food

*Wrack, algae and sargasses from the shore, used to make a couch for Smith and eaten dried for their nutritive jelly.*

Keywords: varech, algues, sargasses, wrack, fucaceae, couch, bed, food, shore

- **What it is.**
  - After the storm a thick carpet of marine herbs, wrack and algae covered the whole shore (I.8 ¶90). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un épais tapis d’herbes marines, varechs et algues, couvrait tout le rivage
  - A bed was made for Smith of algae and wrack that had stayed nearly dry (I.9 ¶16). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une couche d’algues et de varechs restés à peu près secs
  - Harbert gathered edible algae from high rocks that the sea wets only at the great tides; they belong to the fucaceae and are sorts of sargassum (I.9 ¶18). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: d’algues comestibles, qu’il ramassa sur de hautes roches dont la mer ne devait mouiller les parois qu’à l’époque des grandes marées
    - quote: des espèces de sargasse
  - Dried, they yield a gelatinous matter rich in nutriment (I.9 ¶18). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: fournissent une matière gélatineuse assez riche en éléments nutritifs
  - On Asian shores they form a notable part of the natives' diet (I.9 ¶18). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: sur les rivages asiatiques, elles entrent pour une notable proportion dans l’alimentation des indigènes
  - The party found them quite bearable in taste (I.9 ¶18). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un goût très-supportable
  - The sargasses, with the pine nuts, completed the supper at the Chimneys (I.10 ¶15). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Les sargasses et les amandes de pin pignon complétèrent ce repas
- **Appears in:** I.8, I.9, I.10
