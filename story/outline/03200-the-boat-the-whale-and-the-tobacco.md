### 9. The baleen traps (II.10 ¶69–¶80)

- Before going back to the yard Smith makes a device that sharpens his companions' curiosity: a dozen whale-bones, each cut in six equal parts and sharpened at one end. *(Drafted.)*

- They are to kill wolves, foxes and even jaguars, not now but this winter, when there is ice. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: À tuer des loups, des renards, et même des jaguars
- Smith explains: the Aleut hunters of Russian America use it.
