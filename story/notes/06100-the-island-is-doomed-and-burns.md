# The island is doomed and burns (notes)

## Notes for drafting
- **Where it sits.** The French III.19, chapter 06100, between 06000 and 06200. It runs from 8 January to the night of 8 to 9 March 1869 (year my reading from III.18 ¶11), ending with the island's destruction. *(Drafted.)*
- **Cast.** Cyrus Smith and Ayrton (¶1), Gédéon Spilett (¶5), Pencroff (¶9), Harbert (¶61), Nab (¶74); Captain Nemo, dead, quoted by Smith (¶6); Top and Jup (¶78). Animals fleeing: jaguars, boars, capybaras, koalas (¶60). *(Drafted.)*
- **Places.** Granite-house (¶1); the Dakkar crypt (¶13); the corral (¶28); the Red Creek valley (¶37) and the Waterfall River valley (¶37, ¶59); the Jacamar woods (¶29); the Far-West forest (¶43, ¶59); Lake Grant (¶51); Grande-Vue plateau (¶34, ¶61); the Mercy mouth, the islet and the granite wall (¶60, ¶68); the Chimneys (¶60); Duck Marsh (¶60); Serpentine Peninsula (¶62); Port-Ballon (¶68); Glycerine Creek (¶78). *(Drafted.)*
- **Things.** The Dakkar crypt's end wall and its fractures (¶13); the upper cone, a thousand feet high (¶27); the onagga cart (¶30); the dike three feet high, built from shovels, picks, axes and felled trees (¶50); the lava jetty in the lake (¶54); the tent (¶60); the ship, half planked about 23 January (¶25), its hull to be launched before its upper seams are caulked (¶79). *(Drafted.)*
- **Time.** 8 January, return to Granite-house (¶1); about 23 January, ship half planked (¶25); night of 23 to 24 January, the cone falls at about 2 a.m. (¶25–¶26); at the corral before 3 a.m. (¶31), burnt an hour later (¶32); day of 24 January (¶34); about 7 a.m. (¶38); the dike "in a few hours" (¶50); the lake three hours after the lava's irruption (¶54); 25 to 30 January (¶57); to 7 February (¶67); 20 February (¶68); 3 March (¶69); "a few days later" (¶74); first week of March (¶78); launch planned for the morning of 9 March (¶80); night of 8 to 9 March, the explosion (¶81). Chapter covers about two months. Season: summer, "chaleur torride" (¶59). *(Drafted.)*
- **Clues.** Smith's own account of what Nemo told him: "a special arrangement that Captain Nemo made known to me" (¶11). The "document found at the corral" is not in this chapter (see III.20 ¶37). Nab's and Pencroff's belief that Nemo could have prevented it (¶74–¶77). No notice has been left on Tabor from the colonists (III.18 ¶7; used in III.20 ¶12). *(Drafted.)*
- **Length.** 81 paragraphs (¶1 to ¶81), 3,966 words; three plate captions. *(Drafted.)*

## Open and flags
- "a special arrangement that Captain Nemo made known to me" (¶11) is never specified; the chapter gives only the wall's cracks (¶13) and the sea's entry (¶15). What the "disposition" is stays open. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- ¶67 says "until 7 February" and then "about that time" for the vibrations; ¶68 jumps to "It was the 20th of February." No events are given from 7 to 20 February. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- "Another month was still needed" on 20 February (¶68) and "ten days or so" on 3 March (¶69) are consistent with a launch about 13 March; the text then launches on 9 March (¶80) with seams uncaulked. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- ¶70 "the fourth year": the arrival date lies in earlier chapters, not given to me. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- ¶74 "a few days later" follows the paragraphs of 3 March (¶69) and precedes "During the first week of March" (¶78). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- Seam with 06000: III.18 ¶106 ends with Smith and Ayrton leaving the crypt; III.19 ¶1 has them back at Granite-house on 8 January "after a day and a night spent at the corral", agreeing with III.18 ¶58. Seam with 06200: ¶81 ends with the sea covering the island; III.20 ¶1 begins on the rock. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
