# The Bonadventure’s sails: sailcloth cut from the balloon’s envelope

*The boat’s sails, cut in the winter from the balloon’s envelope and sewn by Pencroff with Harbert’s help.*

Keywords: voiles, sails, enveloppe, envelope, ballon, voilier, sailmaker, ralingues, Bonadventure

- **What it is.**
  - In winter, work resumed indoors includes the boat’s sails, cut from the balloon’s envelope, called inexhaustible (II.11 ¶39). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des voiles de l’embarcation, qui furent taillées dans l’inépuisable enveloppe de l’aérostat
  - Pencroff, helped by Harbert, who handles the sailmaker’s needle skilfully, finishes them in a week of August (II.12 ¶12). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Pencroff, aidé par Harbert, qui maniait habilement l’aiguille du voilier
    - quote: les voiles de l’embarcation furent terminées
  - They are edged with strong boltropes (II.12 ¶12). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Les voiles furent bordées de fortes ralingues
- **Appears in:** II.11, II.12
