# S11: III.11 ¶30

¶30

The cart was in perfect order. The onaggas, well rested, would be able to go a long stage. Provisions, camp effects, a portable kitchen, and divers utensils were loaded on the cart, as well as the arms and ammunition, chosen with care from the arsenal of Granite-house, now so complete. But it must not be forgotten that the convicts were perhaps roaming the woods, and that, in the midst of those thick forests, a shot was quickly fired and received. Hence the necessity for the little band of colonists to keep close together, and not to separate under any pretext whatever.
