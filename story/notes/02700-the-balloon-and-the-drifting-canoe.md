## Open and flags
- Date discord. II.3 ¶72 ends the night of 30 October: "le lendemain, 31 octobre, à cinq heures du matin, tous étaient sur pied". II.4 is one day from the falls to the promontory (6 a.m. to about 7 p.m.). This chapter then names the next day: "cette journée du 2 novembre" (¶4). A day is missing between 31 October and 2 November, or the 2 is an error for 1 November. Card neither version. *(Drafted.)*
- The date also bears on ¶12: "La pirogue est restée seule pendant un jour aux sources de la Mercy, ... elle peut bien y rester deux jours". If the canoe was left on the morning of 31 October, by the morning of 2 November it has been left two days, not one; the line fits 1 November. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- The moon: II.3 ¶9 on 30 October says the moon will be full "trois jours après", about 2 November. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- Distance from Flotson-point to Granite-house: ¶98 "De la pointe de l’Épave à Granite-house, il restait encore quatre milles"; II.2 ¶13 "environ à trois milles de la Mercy" and II.2 ¶42 "cette distance de trois milles". The shore beyond the Mercy's mouth may account for a mile, but the text does not say. *(Drafted.)*
- Where the party stands at midnight: ¶98 has them at "premier coude formé par la rivière" after following the shore "jusqu’à l’embouchure de la Mercy", then ¶118 reaches "l’embouchure de la Mercy" by canoe. *(Drafted.)*
- The distance to Cape Claw is given as thirty miles from the tip (¶7), then twenty-five from the angle five miles on (¶27), and ten from the harbour (¶44); the walked distances (five, twenty at the bay, then the harbour at "moins de dix milles") are consistent. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- The chest was "trouvée à moins de vingt milles de là" (¶42) from the shore west of the harbour. *(Drafted.)*
- Weapons: ¶25 "les fusils furent chargés à balle", against II.4 ¶61 "du petit plomb". *(Drafted.)*
- Whether the balloon is the envelope that carried them is given in the text as "notre bateau aérien, notre ballon" (¶84). Earlier chapters were not read here. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- No Wikisource correction touches this chapter. *(Drafted.)*
