# 09 Refine

*Basic.*

**When:** for each chapter (or batch) as it lands, while the next one drafts; then once per volume.

**Uses:** **[plan/workflows/drafting/](../../plan/workflows/drafting/README.md)**, steps 2 to 8 of the chain (Basic) and 9 to 13 (Refinements): [mechanical](../../plan/workflows/drafting/mechanical.md), [critics](../../plan/workflows/drafting/critics.md) and [their prompts](../../plan/workflows/drafting/prompts/), [verify](../../plan/workflows/drafting/verify.md), [reviser](../../plan/workflows/drafting/reviser.md), the [invention check](../../plan/workflows/drafting/principles.md#the-invention-check), [draft-cards](../../plan/workflows/draft-cards.md), the [register pass](../../plan/workflows/drafting/register-pass.md) and landing check, the [fold-back](../../plan/workflows/drafting/fold-back.md).

**What happens:** the chain, cheapest checks first ([the chain](../../plan/workflows/drafting/README.md#the-chain-in-order)).

**Outputs:** refined chapters; batch papers; Next pass lists. The kit's example (in the kit's repository, `example/reports/`) has a worked run.

**Done when:** every verified finding has a disposition; every keep line resolves; every settled beat has a landing or is with the writer.
