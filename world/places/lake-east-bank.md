# The east bank of Lake Grant: the granite frame, five hundred paces from the coast

*The east shore of the lake, a bank held back by a granite frame only five hundred paces from the sea; split by the blast of 21 May.*

Keywords: rive est, east shore, frame, cadre de granit, point, pointe, bank, rim, berge, blast, mine, coast

- **What it is.**
