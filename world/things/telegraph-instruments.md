# The telegraph instruments: the electromagnet and dial at each end of the line

*The receiver and manipulator at Granite-house and at the corral: an electromagnet of soft iron, a plate of soft iron and a needle on a dial with the letters of the alphabet.*

Keywords: récepteur, manipulateur, électro-aimant, cadran, aiguille, alphabet, fer doux, instruments

- **What it is.** A made thing, a pair of instruments. Build if needed. *(Drafted.)*
- **Appears in:** II.18 ¶48
