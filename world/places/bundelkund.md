# Bundelkund: the Indian territory of Nemo's birth

*An Indian territory, independent in the prince's youth, with mountains to which he returned before vanishing.*

Keywords: India, territory, rajah, mountains, homeland, Dakkar

- **What it is.**
  - It was an independent territory in the time of the prince's father, a rajah (III.16 ¶38). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: du territoire alors indépendant du Bundelkund
  - The prince returned there in 1849 (III.16 ¶45). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: au Bundelkund dans l’année 1849
  - It has mountains, where the prince returned after the defeat and then disappeared with some twenty companions (III.16 ¶51). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: revint dans les montagnes du Bundelkund
  - Smith speaks of friends Nemo may have left in "those mountains of India" (III.17 ¶63). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: aux amis que vous avez pu laisser dans ces montagnes de l’Inde
- **On the page.**
  - The prince sold what was left of his fortune and gathered some twenty faithful companions (III.16 ¶51). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: réunit une vingtaine de ses plus fidèles compagnons
- **Appears in:** III.16, III.17
- **Open:**
  - These chapters hold only Nemo's own telling of this place. The editor's note warns of a disagreement of dates in his story.
    - quote: sur la discordance de quelques dates
