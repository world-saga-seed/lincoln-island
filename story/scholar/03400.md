## a

After: Ten days after taking to his bed, on the 21st of August, Master Jup got up.

The attack came towards four in the morning, on the night of the 11th, and lasted two hours; if that night is the one that began on the 11th, Jup was hurt on the 12th, and ten days from it is the 22nd. If it is the night that ended on the 11th, the 21st is right. I find nothing to say which night is meant, and leave it open.
