# The telegraph posts: the posts that carry the wire along the corral road

*Posts planted from 6 February along the road to the corral, fitted with glass insulators to carry the wire; numbered, one of them (no. 74) pulled down by hand on 11 November.*

Keywords: poteaux, isoloirs, supports, route du corral, telegraph, posts, numéro 74

- **What it is.**
  - They are planted from 6 February, fitted with glass insulators, to carry the wire along the road to the corral (II.18 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ce fut le 6 février que fut commencée la plantation des poteaux, munis d’isoloirs en verre
    - quote: qui devait suivre la route du corral
  - They are numbered; pole no. 74 stands half-way between Granite-house and the corral (III.6 ¶75, ¶85). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: arrivé au poteau no 74
    - quote: Les colons se trouvaient alors à mi-chemin de Granite-house et du corral
  - On 11 November the posts are in good order and the insulators intact up to pole no. 74 (III.6 ¶75). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Les poteaux étaient en bon état, les isoloirs intacts
  - Pole no. 74 lies across the road; the earth has been dug out at its foot, and it has been uprooted by a man’s hand, not by the wind (III.6 ¶78–¶80). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le poteau renversé se trouvait en travers de la route
    - quote: La terre a été creusée à son pied, et il a été déraciné de main d’homme
- **Appears in:** II.18, III.6
