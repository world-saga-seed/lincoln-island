# Culpeux: the big barking foxes of the right bank

*Large grey-red foxes with black, white-tipped tails that bark, met beyond the Mercy and taken in the traps, good only as bait.*

Keywords: culpeux, renard, fox, Chili, Malouines, aboiement, bark, bait, appât, trappe, pillard

- **What it is.**
  - A whole family of quadrupeds has made its home in a thick copse on the right bank of the Mercy, less than half a mile from the river, and flees at Top's barking (I.21 ¶12, ¶13). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la rive droite de la Mercy
    - quote: d’un épais fourré, s’échappait toute une famille de quadrupèdes, qui y avaient élu domicile
  - They are foxes of very great size that give a kind of bark (I.21 ¶15). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des renards de très-grande taille, qui faisaient entendre une sorte d’aboiement
  - Their coat is reddish grey, with black tails ending in a white tuft (I.21 ¶16). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: gris roussâtres de pelage, à queues noires que terminait une bouffette blanche
  - Their barking gives away their origin: Harbert names them at once "culpeux" (I.21 ¶16). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: par leurs aboiements
    - quote: leur véritable nom de « culpeux »
  - Culpeux are often met in Chile, the Falklands and all the American waters crossed by the thirtieth and fortieth parallels (I.21 ¶16). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ces culpeux se rencontrent fréquemment au Chili, aux Malouines
