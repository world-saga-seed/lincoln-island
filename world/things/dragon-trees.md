# The dragon trees: tall liliaceous trees whose roots can be eaten and fermented

*Dragonniers that Harbert points out in the Jacamar wood and Pencroff calls pretentious leeks; their woody roots, boiled, are excellent food and, fermented, give a sour beer.*

Keywords: dragonniers, dragon trees, dracaena, liliaceae, roots, liqueur, poireaux prétentieux, wood, beer, bière, boisson

- **What it is.**
  - Harbert points out dragon trees among new species in the Jacamar wood on 20 April (I.15 ¶33). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Harbert en signala de nouveaux, entre autres, des dragonniers
  - Pencroff calls them "poireaux prétentieux", for despite their size they belong to the lily family with the onion, chive, shallot and asparagus (I.15 ¶33). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: poireaux prétentieux
    - quote: de cette même famille des liliacées que l’oignon
  - Their woody roots can be had cooked, and are excellent; the colonists lay in a stock (I.15 ¶33). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des racines ligneuses, qui, cuites, sont excellentes
    - quote: On en fit provision
  - The island has them in abundance enough to feed the colonists’ supply of roots through the winter (I.22 ¶28, ¶37). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ses récoltes de rhizomes, d’amandes de pin-pignon et de racines de dragonnier
    - quote: racines de dragonnier
  - It is among the trees of the forest cut through on 31 October, most of which were already known round the lake and the Grande-Vue plateau, generally of medium size because their number has hindered their growth (II.4 ¶5). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des dragonniers
    - quote: généralement de taille médiocre, car leur nombre avait nui à leur développement
  - Their roots give a fermented liquor, one of the island's drinks (II.8 ¶44). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la liqueur fermentée extraite des racines du dragonnier
- **Leads to.**
  - The roots, fermented, give a very agreeable liquor (I.15 ¶33). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: soumises à une certaine fermentation, donnent une très-agréable liqueur
  - The fermented roots give a sour drink, a kind of beer far better than plain water (I.20 ¶31). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une boisson acidulée, sorte de bière bien préférable à l’eau pure
  - Dragon-tree drink is among the dishes of the 24 October dinner (I.22 ¶74). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: de la boisson de dragonnier
- **Appears in:** I.15, I.20, I.22, II.4, II.8, III.6
