# The pine nuts: the edible almonds of a pine found in the forest

*An excellent almond from a pine that Harbert finds in the forest, eaten ripe by Pencroff and Nab as the first vegetable food.*

Keywords: pin pignon, pin pigeon, amandes, almonds, nuts, pine kernels, edible fruit, dessert

- **What it is.**
  - It is the almond of a tree with edible fruit, esteemed in the temperate regions of America and Europe (I.9 ¶97). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: qui produit une amande excellente, très-estimée dans les régions tempérées de l’Amérique et de l’Europe
  - The almonds were in a perfect state of ripeness (I.9 ¶97). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ces amandes étaient dans un parfait état de maturité
  - Pencroff takes them as dessert, with algae for bread and raw mussels for meat (I.9 ¶98). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des amandes pour dessert
- **Appears in:** I.9
- **Open:**
  - The tree's name: I.9 ¶97 reads "pin pigeon"; the chapter's summary (¶0) reads "pin pignon". Carded neither way.
    - quote: C’était le pin pigeon, qui produit une amande excellente
    - quote: Le pin pignon
