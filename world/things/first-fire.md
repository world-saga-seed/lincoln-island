# The first fire: the fire lit with the last match on 25 March

*The first fire of the castaways, lit by Harbert on the hearth from one match, a paper twist and dry moss.*

Keywords: feu, kindling, mousses, paper cornet, 25 March, blaze, first meal

- **What it is.**
  - It was built of a few handfuls of grass, leaves and dry moss under fagots, so that air could circulate (I.5 ¶74). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: quelques poignées d’herbes, de feuilles et de mousses sèches furent placées sous les fagots
  - Pencroff folded the page from Spilett's notebook into a cone, as pipe-smokers do in high winds, and put it among the mosses (I.5 ¶75). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ainsi que font les fumeurs de pipe par les grands vents
  - Harbert lit it with the last match struck on a pebble; the paper caught in a few seconds and the mosses burned at once (I.5 ¶78). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Le papier prit feu en quelques secondes, et les mousses brûlèrent aussitôt
  - The dry wood crackled and a joyful flame, helped by Pencroff's breath, rose in the dark (I.5 ¶79). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: activée par le vigoureux souffle du marin
- **On the page.**
  - The fire had to be kept from going out and some embers always kept under the ash (I.5 ¶82). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il fallait prendre garde de ne plus le laisser éteindre
  - On the evening of 25 March, Pencroff cooked eggs for supper in its hot ashes (I.5 ¶84). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les introduire dans les cendres chaudes
  - On 26 March the fire was alight and it would be easy to keep embers (I.6 ¶7). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Le feu était allumé, et il serait facile de conserver des braises
  - Its smoke twisted at a point of rock outside the Chimneys (I.6 ¶17). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la fumée qui se tordait à une pointe de roc
  - The sea put it out: the fire was dead (I.8 ¶94). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Le feu était éteint.
  - Pencroff tells Spilett they have no fire left (I.9 ¶6). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: nous n’avons plus de feu
  - They have no means of relighting it (I.9 ¶8). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ni aucun moyen de le rallumer.
