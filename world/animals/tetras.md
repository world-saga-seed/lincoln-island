# Tétras: the grouse taken on hook and line

*Hen-sized game birds, called tétras in the United States, that call like trumpets, nest in the marshy forest, and are caught on baited thorns.*

Keywords: tétras, grouse, gallinacé, gélinotte, game, ligne, hameçon, fishing line, nests, jelly, broth, gibier

- **What it is.**
  - They are fowls the Americans call "tétras" (I.6 ¶37). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ces gallinacés que l’on nomme « tétras » aux États-Unis
  - Their call is a true trumpet blast, sounding through the forest (I.6 ¶37). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un véritable appel de trompette résonna dans la forêt
  - Their plumage is varied with fawn and brown, with a brown tail; the males are known by two pointed wing-tufts formed by the raised feathers of the neck (I.6 ¶37). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: au plumage varié de fauve et de brun, et à la queue brune
    - quote: Harbert reconnut les mâles aux deux ailerons pointus, formés par les pennes relevées de leur cou
  - They are as big as a hen, and their flesh is worth that of the hazel grouse (I.6 ¶37). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: gros comme une poule, et dont la chair vaut celle de la gélinotte
  - They peck the aromatic berries of the junipers and will not let anyone approach them (I.6 ¶37). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dont ils becquetaient les baies aromatiques, entre autres des genévriers
    - quote: ils ne se laissaient point approcher
  - They nest in the grass: half a dozen nests, each of two to three eggs, to which the owners are sure to return (I.6 ¶41). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une demi-douzaine de nids de tétras, ayant chacun de deux à trois œufs
    - quote: auxquels leurs propriétaires ne pouvaient manquer de revenir
  - They are voracious: they peck at the baited hooks, and three swallow bait and hook together (I.6 ¶47). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Trois tétras, très-voraces sans doute, avalèrent à la fois l’appât et l’hameçon
  - Tétras are among the birds that swarm on the forest strip of the west coast, where there is no tree without a nest (II.4 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: jacamars, couroucous, tragopans, tétras, loris
    - quote: Pas un arbre qui n’eût un nid
