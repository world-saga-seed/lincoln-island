# About This Book

This is the run’s book for *Lincoln Island*: the record of how its missing chapter was made, set beside what it was measured against. The novel itself is the other book, *Lincoln Island*.

Three chapters are given here, each at every stage the run made or used:

- **XIX** and **XX**, Verne’s chapters on either side of the insertion;
- **XIX bis**, “The Float in the Bar”, the chapter inserted, which has no French original.

For each chapter, in this order:

- **White’s translation (1876)**, as he printed it, where he has the chapter;
- **the Opus control** and **the Gemini control**: each model given the same prompt, a few lines as someone who knows the book would write them, with Verne’s French where there is one and White’s neighbouring chapters pasted in whole, and no cards, no ledger and no checks;
- **version 1**, the first finished version by the World Saga Seed method, drafted from the cards and the ledger and revised after its checks;
- for XIX bis only, two more stages: **after the human’s read-aloud**, when the human read version 1 aloud and the chapter was revised from what was read, and **after the character check**, when each beat the read added was tested against what these men would do;
- **the final**, as it stands in the novel.

After the chapters come the three chapters’ cards, as they now stand; the ledger, every ruling, one row each, in the order it was made; the blind scores the author had taken, Gemini judging each version cold; and the human’s read-aloud changes as sent.
