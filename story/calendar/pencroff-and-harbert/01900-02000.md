## Standing, after 3 June

- They never bring it up: they meant to tell when they had something to show, and nothing ever turns up (R1121-140). It is never spoken of indoors, through III.16, so Nemo never hears it. The other three never learn of it. *(Settled R1121-79.)*
- What is left of the float: shards among the rocks at the Mercy's mouth, below the low-water line, ground by the sea and the river. No one ever finds a piece. *(Settled R1121-66, R1121-67, R1121-68, R1121-136, R1121-140.)*
- The net lies under the bar off the Mercy's mouth, apart from where the Speedy goes down in 1867, and rots to shreds. *(Proposal.)*
