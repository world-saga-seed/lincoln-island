# The maritime pine: a puny pine on the high dune

*A meagre pine, wasted by the wind, whose branches made the litter; a fine clump of them stands at port Ballon.*

Keywords: pin maritime, pine, dune tree, branches

- **What it is.**
  - On the dune a rather puny tree, a sort of maritime pine thinned by the winds, is stripped of its main branches for a litter (I.8 ¶49). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un arbre assez malingre, sorte de pin maritime émacié par les vents
  - A magnificent clump of maritime pines stands at the cove of port Ballon, at whose foot the colonists lunch (see [port Ballon](../places/port-ballon.md)) (II.5 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un magnifique bouquet de pins maritimes
- **Appears in:** I.8, II.5
