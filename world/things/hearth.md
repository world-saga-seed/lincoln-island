# The hearth: flat stones at the foot of the left corridor, with a smoke duct

*Large flat stones laid at the mouth of a narrow smoke duct, the Chimneys' fireplace.*

Keywords: foyer, fireplace, flue, smoke, boyau, flat stones, embers

- **What it is.**
  - It was Harbert who proposed a hearth in the left corridor with an opening kept for the smoke (I.4 ¶26). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Elle le sera si nous pouvons établir un foyer dans le couloir de gauche et y conserver une ouverture pour la fumée
  - It is made of large flat stones laid at the end of the first left corridor, at the mouth of the narrow gut kept for the smoke (I.5 ¶11). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: De larges pierres plates furent disposées au fond du premier couloir de gauche
  - Heat the smoke does not carry outside is enough to keep the inside at a fit temperature (I.5 ¶11). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ce que la fumée n’entraînerait pas de chaleur au dehors suffirait évidemment à maintenir une température convenable au dedans
- **On the page.**
  - The smoke went away easily by the narrow duct, the chimney drew, and a pleasant warmth spread (I.5 ¶81). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: La fumée s’en allait facilement par l’étroit conduit, la cheminée tirait
  - Embers must always be kept under the ash so that the fire is never put out (I.5 ¶82). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: conserver toujours quelque braise sous la cendre
  - A good fire crackled on it, its flame throwing great light into the dark parts of the passage (I.7 ¶20). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Un bon feu pétillait sur le foyer
  - Once the tétras were cooked Pencroff let the fire fall and kept only embers buried under the ashes (I.7 ¶28). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ne conserva plus que des braises enfouies sous les cendres
  - Before leaving he put pieces of wood under the ashes to find fire again on his return (I.7 ¶70). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Il plaça quelques morceaux de bois sous les cendres, de manière à retrouver du feu au retour
  - After the storm the drowned ashes were nothing but mud (I.8 ¶94). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Les cendres noyées n’étaient plus que vase
