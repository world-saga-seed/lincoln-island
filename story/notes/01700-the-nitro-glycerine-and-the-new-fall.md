# The Nitro-Glycerine and the New Fall (notes)

## Notes for drafting

- **Where it sits.** The French I.17, 7 to 21 May. It follows the dugong fight of 6 May. Cyrus finds the lake's outlet, plans to lower the lake, makes sulphuric acid, nitric acid, glycerine and nitro-glycerine, and blasts the lake's east bank. It ends with the new fall. *(Drafted.)*
- **Cast.** *(Drafted.)* Cyrus Smith, Gédéon Spilett (¶1), Nab, Harbert, Pencroff (¶1). Top is not named. The narrator speaks of a "congénère des mastodontes ou autres monstres marins" (¶3), not a cast member.
- **Places.** *(Drafted.)*
  - The small beach at the lake's south point (¶2); the lake's southern point where the water dips (¶10–¶11); the hole one foot below the surface (¶14).
  - The Chimneys (¶27, ¶41, ¶59); the river and coal bed with shaly pyrites (¶37); the saltpetre bed in the north, at the foot of Mount Franklin (¶48).
  - Behind the Chimneys, the level ground for the pyrites heap (¶41); the sea-plant pits on the shore (¶45).
  - The point forming the lake's east bank, five hundred paces from the coast (¶66); the Chimneys more than two miles from the mine (¶75).
- **Things.** *(Drafted.)*
  - Made: the roasted pyrites heap (¶41–¶42); soda from sea plants (¶45); soap and glycerine (¶46); refractory pottery and a special brick furnace (¶49); iron sulphate crystals (¶50); sulphuric acid, 20 May (¶54); nitric acid (¶55); "plusieurs pintes" of nitro-glycerine (¶58); the hammer-and-fibre firing device (¶72).
  - Found: the lake's outlet (¶14); saltpetre (¶48).
  - Quantities: "plusieurs tonnes" of pyrites (¶38); ten litres of nitro-glycerine for the charge (¶68); an iron mass "pesant plusieurs livres" (¶72); a fibre burning twenty-five minutes (¶75).
  - Narrator's aside: dynamite "n’était pas encore connue à l’époque" (¶61).
- **Time.** *(Drafted.)*
  - "Le lendemain, 7 mai" (¶1); the day of hauling pyrites (¶38); "Le lendemain, 8 mai" the heap is started (¶39).
  - The change takes "dix à douze jours" (¶43); other works about eight days (¶49); all done about 18 May (¶49); sulphuric acid on 20 May, "douze jours après avoir commencé" (¶54).
  - Nitro-glycerine made after that, alone (¶58–¶59); "Demain" (¶65), so the blast is on "le lendemain, — 21 mai —", dawn start (¶66); the hole finished about four in the afternoon (¶68); the explosion twenty-five minutes after the fibre is lit (¶75). The chapter covers 7 to 21 May.
  - That the nitro-glycerine was made on 20 May is *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- **Clues.** *(Drafted.)*
  - Cyrus's rescue: how he was "arraché des flots et transporté dans les dunes" (¶9).
    - quote: comment j’ai pu être arraché des flots et transporté dans les dunes
  - The lake fight; the "bras puissant" and "poignard" (¶8).
    - quote: ce même bras, armé d’un poignard, a ensuite donné la mort au dugong
  - Cyrus asks the reporter to keep these things from the others (¶9).
    - quote: n’insistons pas devant nos compagnons sur ces singuliers incidents
  - The cavity Cyrus expects under Grande-Vue (¶34); it is inferred, not seen.
    - quote: une cavité plus ou moins considérable devait exister dans la masse de granit
- **Length.** 77 paragraphs (¶1–¶77), 3,459 words in the ¶ lines. *(Drafted.)*

## Open and flags

- The lake's fall. Cyrus plans to lower it by "trois pieds" (¶17); I.18 ¶3 says "deux pieds, au moins". Card neither version. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: En abaissant de trois pieds le niveau des eaux du lac
- Time of day. The hole is finished "vers quatre heures du soir" on 21 May (¶68), the blast follows twenty-five minutes after the fibre is lit (¶75), yet I.18 ¶83 has the party come out of the outlet "un peu avant quatre heures", the same day (I.19 ¶1 opens "Le lendemain, 22 mai"). Both are quoted in the timeline. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: vers quatre heures du soir, le trou de mine était achevé
- The fall's height is "trois cents pieds" (¶77); the plateau's height elsewhere is not in this chapter. *(Drafted.)*
- "Deux milles" from the mine to the colonists (¶75) against "cinq cents pas seulement de la côte" for the mine (¶66); the text does not say how far the Chimneys lie from the coast. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- Where the east bank meets the lake: "la rive est du lac Grant" (¶66) and "la partie de la rive qui se rapproche le plus près de la côte" (¶21) are not tied together in the text. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- The "claie" (hurdle-sledge, ¶37) is "la claie" with the article, so an earlier chapter may have made it. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- No Wikisource correction touches this chapter. *(Drafted.)*
