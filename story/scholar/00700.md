## a

After: The sailor and his companions were some six miles from the Chimneys,

The heading of this chapter puts the place eight miles from the first encampment, and the text here six. Chapter VIII decides it: when the colonists carry the engineer home from here, they have “eight miles” to go to the Chimneys. The march agrees better with eight as well, for they had made five miles by four o’clock and walked two hours more before this. I take eight.
