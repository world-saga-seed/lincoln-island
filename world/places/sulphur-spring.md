# The sulphur spring: a warm sulphurous spring at the foot of Mont Franklin

*A sulphated, sodic spring running between rocks at the edge of the plain below the mountain, warm as the body; its yellow vapour, first taken for smoke from a camp, turns out to be nature's own.*

Keywords: source sulfureuse, smoke, fumée jaune, hydrogen sulphide, thermal, laryngitis, spring, odour, natives feared

- **What it is.**
  - Its smoke was taken first for the sign of men: Smith feared natives, if there were any on the island, rather than wished for them, and chose not to show themselves before knowing whom they had to deal with (I.12 ¶23). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Évitons de nous montrer avant de savoir à qui nous avons affaire
  - Top, sent ahead, did not bark at the smoke, which Smith found odd (I.12 ¶25). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Et il n’aboie pas ?
  - Its vapour is a yellowish smoke that whirls up among the rocks, with a disagreeable smell that spreads over the air (I.12 ¶29). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: fumée dont la couleur jaunâtre était très-caractérisée
  - Smith knew it for a sulphurous spring, which would let them treat their laryngitis (I.12 ¶33). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Il n’y a là qu’une source sulfureuse, qui nous permettra de traiter efficacement nos laryngites
  - It is a sulphurous sodic spring running fairly abundantly between rocks, its waters giving off a strong smell of hydrosulphuric acid after absorbing the oxygen of the air (I.12 ¶35). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une source sulfurée sodique, qui coulait assez abondamment entre les roches
  - Its water is oily to the touch and a little sweetish to the taste (I.12 ¶36). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: onctueuses au toucher
  - Smith set its temperature at ninety-five degrees Fahrenheit (35 centigrade), because his hand felt neither cold nor heat in it, the temperature of the human body (I.12 ¶36). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il l’estima à quatre-vingt-quinze degrés Fahrenheit
  - It has no present use (I.12 ¶38). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la source sulfurée n’offrant aucune utilisation actuelle
  - It lies a few hundred paces from the thick edge of the forest, where the red-banked creek runs (I.12 ¶38). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: à quelques centaines de pas
