# The potatoes: the tubers of South America, including vitelottes, grown in the garden

*Potatoes, tubers cultivated since long ago in South America, of which over two hundred kinds are counted, brought by Harbert and grown in the garden, with vitelottes.*

Keywords: pommes de terre, potatoes, vitelottes, tubercules, Amérique du Sud, potager

- **What it is.**
  - They are among the precious tubers cultivated since always in South America, of which over two hundred kinds are now counted, and Harbert brings some home from his excursions (II.8 ¶43). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ces précieux tubercules qui ont été cultivés de tout temps dans l’Amérique méridionale, ces pommes de terre, dont on compte aujourd’hui plus de deux cents espèces
    - quote: Harbert ne revenait guère d’une excursion sans rapporter quelques végétaux utiles
  - Vitelottes grow in the small squares of the kitchen garden (see [the kitchen garden](../places/the-kitchen-garden.md)) (II.8 ¶43). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: laitues, vitelottes, oseille, raves, raifort
  - Harbert recognises potatoes in the old Tabor plots, whose seed he means to take to Lincoln (see [the Tabor plots](../places/tabor-vegetable-plots.md); [the Tabor seeds](tabor-seeds.md)) (II.13 ¶80). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des pommes de terre
- **Appears in:** II.8, II.13
