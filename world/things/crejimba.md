# The crejimba: a palm whose branches make bows

*A palm found by Harbert with no edible fruit; South American Indians make bows of its branches.*

Keywords: bow wood, palm, tree, branches, staves

- **What it is.**
  - Harbert found it on the way (I.13 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Harbert avait découvert un arbre
  - South American Indians use its branches to make bows (I.13 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dont les Indiens de l’Amérique méridionale emploient les branches à fabriquer leurs arcs
  - It belongs to the palm family and bears no edible fruit (I.13 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: de la famille des palmiers, qui ne porte pas de fruits comestibles
  - Its flexible rods form the framework of the hull of the pirogue (see [the pirogue](the-pirogue.md)) (II.2 ¶1). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: membrée au moyen de baguettes flexibles de crejimba
- **On the page.**
  - Long straight branches were cut, stripped and trimmed, stronger in the middle and weaker at the ends (I.13 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: plus fortes en leur milieu, plus faibles à leurs extrémités
- **Appears in:** I.13, II.2
- **Open:**
  - The text gives the name in quotation marks and no further description of the tree.
    - quote: C’était le « crejimba »
