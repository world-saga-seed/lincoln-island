# The jaguar’s cave: a cavern in the rocks at the promontoire du Reptile

*A cavern among the shore rocks of the promontoire du Reptile, the jaguar’s den, where the colonists spend the night of 31 October.*

Keywords: grotte, caverne, repaire, jaguar, promontoire du Reptile, bones, night

- **What it is.**
  - It is one of the cavities in the much-divided shore rocks of the promontoire du Reptile, beaten by the sea in south-west winds (II.4 ¶60). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: présentaient des cavités qui devaient leur permettre de dormir à l’abri des intempéries de l’air
  - It is the jaguar's lair: the beast comes out of the mouth of the cavern (II.4 ¶62, ¶77). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un magnifique animal se montrait à l’entrée de la caverne
    - quote: le repaire abandonné
  - Pencroff calls it "la maison des jaguars" (II.4 ¶76). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: À la maison des jaguars, alors !
  - Its sand floor is strewn with bones (II.4 ¶79). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dont le sable était jonché d’ossements
  - The colonists occupy it for the night, and in the morning Smith's party is said to have slept like innocent marmots in the cavern the jaguar had so politely left at their disposal (II.5 ¶1). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la caverne que le jaguar avait si poliment laissée à leur disposition
- **Appears in:** II.4, II.5
