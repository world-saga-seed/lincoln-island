# The fibre ropes: cords of plant fibre

*Ropes of fibre used in the bellows’ mechanism and carried down the old outlet; the plant is not named.*

Keywords: cordes de fibres, rope, cord, fibre, cordage, tie, rappel, mountaineers

- **What it is.**
  - Cords of fibre are part of the seal-skin bellows’ mechanism (I.15 ¶44). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: cordes de fibres
  - Fibre ropes are taken from the Chimneys, with picks and spears, for the descent after the blast (I.18 ¶4). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des cordes de fibres
  - The colonists tie themselves to one another with a rope, as mountaineers do, on the slippery passage (I.18 ¶19). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: liés les uns aux autres au moyen d’une corde
  - Other cords of vegetable fibre are made, and a coarse pulley is fitted at the door (see [the pulley](pulley.md)) (I.19 ¶20). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: D’autres cordes furent également fabriquées avec des fibres végétales
- **Appears in:** I.15, I.18, I.19
- **Open:**
  - The text does not name the plant whose fibre makes these cords (I.15 ¶44). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: cordes de fibres
