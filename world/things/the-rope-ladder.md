# The rope ladder: the hundred-rung ladder that is Granite-house’s only way in

*A rope ladder of eighty feet, uprights of plaited curry-jonc fibre and rungs of a red-cedar kind, made by Pencroff and fixed on 28 May in two lengths with a landing between; drawn up, it makes the dwelling inaccessible.*

Keywords: échelle, échelle de corde, ladder, rungs, échelons, landing, palier, access, climb, door

- **What it is.**
  - Smith intends an outside rope ladder which, once drawn up, will make access to the dwelling impossible, since the old outlet is to be stopped (I.19 ¶5, ¶7). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une échelle de corde, qui, une fois retirée, rendra impossible l’accès de notre demeure
  - It spares the colonists the old road by the plateau and the two-hundred-foot passage, with its loss of time and fatigue (I.19 ¶18). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: perte de temps et fatigues considérables
  - It is fixed on 28 May and has no fewer than a hundred rungs over a perpendicular height of eighty feet (I.19 ¶28). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: L’échelle fut définitivement installée le 28 mai
    - quote: pas moins de cent échelons
    - quote: quatre-vingts pieds
  - It is divided in two by an overhang of the wall about forty feet above the ground, which was levelled with the pick into a landing; the first ladder hangs from the landing and a rope can draw it up to the level of Granite-house, the second is fixed below on the ledge and above at the door itself (I.19 ¶28). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une quarantaine de pieds au-dessus du sol
    - quote: une sorte de palier
    - quote: rattachée à la porte même
  - A plate shows the ladder fixed (I.19 pl1). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: L’échelle fut définitivement installée.
  - Smith counts on a hydraulic lift later (see [the hydraulic lift](the-hydraulic-lift.md)) (I.19 ¶28). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un ascenseur hydraulique
  - In the great cold the rungs burn the fingers, and the colonists’ hands cling to them only with pain (I.22 ¶41). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les échelons leur brûlaient les doigts
  - It is in two lengths: the upper ladder, usually stretched from the landing to the door, and the lower ladder, which can be drawn up to the threshold (II.6 ¶24). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: L’échelle supérieure, ordinairement tendue du palier à la porte
    - quote: l’échelle inférieure avait été retirée et relevée jusqu’au seuil
  - When drawn up from inside it unrolls down to the ground (II.6 ¶81). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: on vit l’échelle glisser sur le seuil de la porte, puis se dérouler et retomber jusqu’au sol
  - Its foot is made fast on 3 November by two stakes driven firmly into the sand (II.7 ¶11, ¶12). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Assujettissons-la par son extrémité inférieure
    - quote: deux pieux, solidement enfoncés dans le sable
  - The lift replaces it as the way up, and no one regrets the first ladder (II.9 ¶32). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: qui remplaça l’échelle primitive, que personne ne songea à regretter
  - The rope ladder, no longer used since the lift was installed and long enough, is the one Smith lets down the well (II.11 ¶83). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: l’échelle de corde qui ne servait plus depuis l’installation de l’ascenseur, et dont la longueur était suffisante
  - The ladders that formerly served for the ascent are carried to the Chimneys and buried deep in the sand, to serve on the return (III.11 ¶33). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: Les échelles qui servaient autrefois à l’ascension furent apportées aux Cheminées et profondément enterrées dans le sable
- **Made from.**
  - The uprights are fibres of the "curry-jonc" plaited with a windlass, as strong as a thick cable (see [the curry-jonc](curry-jonc.md)) (I.19 ¶19). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: formés des fibres du « curry-jonc » tressées au moyen d’un moulinet
    - quote: la solidité d’un gros câble
  - The rungs come from a kind of red cedar with light, resistant branches (I.19 ¶19). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une sorte de cèdre rouge, aux branches légères et résistantes
  - Pencroff worked it with a master’s hand (I.19 ¶19). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: travaillé de main de maître par Pencroff
