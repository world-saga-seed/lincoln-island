# The firing device: an iron mass hung over the mine hole on a fibre, lit by a slow sulphured fibre

*A suspended iron mass that falls on the nitro-glycerine when its fibre burns through; the lighting fibre burns twenty-five minutes.*

Keywords: amorce, device, hammer, masse de fer, fibre soufrée, fuse, mèche, blast

- **What it is.** A made thing, an apparatus. Build if needed. *(Drafted.)*
