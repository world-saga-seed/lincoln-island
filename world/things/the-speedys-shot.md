# The Speedy’s shot: the balls and shells of the pirate brig

*Solid balls and shells fired from the four cannon, and a great quantity of projectiles found beside the powder.*

Keywords: boulets, obus, projectiles, shot, shells, cannon balls, mitraille, Speedy

- **What it is.**
  - The brig fires balls (boulets) at the rocks of the islet, the Mercy post, the Chimneys and Granite-house; Smith fears shells (obus) on the dwelling, and Harvey means to answer with shells and balls (III.3 ¶3, ¶29, ¶37, ¶76, ¶93). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un boulet, frappant le haut des roches
    - quote: répondre par des obus et des boulets
    - quote: d’envoyer là des obus
  - Struck by balls, the granite flies in splinters like grapeshot (III.3 ¶97). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dont les éclats volaient en mitraille autour d’eux
  - Among the powder in the stern a great quantity of projectiles is found (III.4 ¶62). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: au milieu d’une grande quantité de projectiles
  - The size of the balls is eight to ten pounds (see [the cannon](the-speedys-cannon.md)) (III.2 ¶58). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des boulets de huit à dix livres
  - The projectile of the trial at Granite-house is cylindro-conical, and is called a boulet, one of the "jolis boulets" the colonists are supplied with (III.5 ¶26, ¶34). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un de ces jolis boulets dont nous sommes approvisionnés
    - quote: le projectile qu’ils devaient lancer était cylindro-conique
- **Appears in:** III.2, III.3, III.4, III.5
