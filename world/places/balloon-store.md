# The balloon store: the rock cavity at port Ballon where the balloon's cloth and gear are kept

*A fairly vast hollow in the rock on the shore at port Ballon, sheltered by its orientation from wind, rain and sea, with a mouth to be hidden; it holds the balloon's envelope and rigging until the cart fetches them.*

Keywords: cavité, grotte, armoire, magasin, balloon, envelope, cloth, stock de toile, port Ballon, cave

- **What it is.**
  - It is a fairly vast rock cavity, which neither wind, rain nor sea can visit, thanks to its orientation (II.5 ¶94). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une assez vaste cavité rocheuse, que ni le vent, ni la pluie, ni la mer ne pouvaient visiter, grâce à son orientation
  - It is found on the shore, where the colonists dragged the envelope, cordage and anchor (II.5 ¶94). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: traîner le tout jusqu’au rivage, où ils découvrirent
  - Pencroff calls it a wardrobe, and since it cannot be locked he says its opening should be hidden, not for two-legged thieves but for four-legged ones (II.5 ¶95). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Il nous fallait une armoire, nous avons une armoire
    - quote: il sera prudent d’en dissimuler l’ouverture
  - By six in the evening of 2 November everything is stored in it (II.5 ¶96). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: tout était emmagasiné
  - It lies at port Ballon, and is later called a grotto; after 20 November the "stock of cloth" is found to suffer nothing in it (see [port Ballon](port-ballon.md)) (II.7 ¶32). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le « stock de toile » ne souffrait aucunement dans la grotte où il avait été emmagasiné
  - It is emptied at the end of December, when the cart carries away the envelope and the balloon's gear (II.7 ¶63). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: on put y charger l’enveloppe et les divers agrès de l’aérostat
- **Appears in:** II.5, II.7
