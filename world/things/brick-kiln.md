# The brick kiln: the open stack that fired the first bricks

*A cube of dry bricks around wood fascines, with vents, which fired itself in 48 hours.*

Keywords: four, clamp, stack, firing, fascines, fuel, vents, open-air firing

- **What it is.**
  - It is not a built kiln: the mass of bricks is itself an enormous kiln that fires itself (I.13 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: l’agglomération des briques ne serait qu’un énorme four qui se cuirait lui-même
  - The firing is done in the open air and not in kilns (I.13 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: en plein air, et non dans des fours
  - It forms a large cube, with vents at the outside (I.13 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des évents furent ménagés
  - Fascines are placed on the ground and surrounded by several rows of dried bricks (I.13 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: on l’entoura de plusieurs rangs de briques séchées
- **Made from.**
  - The fuel is well prepared fascines (I.13 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Le combustible, fait de fascines bien préparées
  - For two days before the firing the colonists collected fuel: branches cut round the clearing and fallen wood (I.13 ¶36). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: et l’on ramassa tout le bois tombé sous les arbres
  - The bricks are the [dried bricks](bricks.md) (I.13 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: briques séchées
- **Leads to.**
  - The firing lasted forty-eight hours and succeeded perfectly (I.13 ¶44). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: L’opération dura quarante-huit heures et réussit parfaitement
  - The smoking mass then had to cool; meanwhile the lime was gathered (see [the lime](lime.md)) (I.13 ¶44). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Il fallut alors laisser refroidir la masse fumante
- **On the page.**
  - The work of building the stack took the whole day and the fire was lit in the evening (I.13 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le soir seulement, on mit le feu aux fascines
  - Nobody went to bed that night, and the fire was watched (I.13 ¶43). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: personne ne se coucha
- **Appears in:** I.13
- **Open:**
  - Whether the pottery kiln is built of bricks from this stack is not said, only that the kiln is built after the bricks and the lime are ready.
    - quote: On commença donc, sans perdre un instant, la construction d’un four
