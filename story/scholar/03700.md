# The scholar’s notes to chapter XXXVII

*Each note is set after the words its “After:” line quotes, exactly as they stand in story/prose/03700-the-return-and-the-first-tears.md. Delete this file and the chapter builds without them (R1121-163).*

## a

After: The next morning, the 20th of October, at 7 o’clock, after a voyage of four days,

The fire was seen towards two in the morning of the 19th, twenty miles off, and on her way out the boat had made something over three miles an hour (chapter XXXV). The next morning, by that, is the morning of the 19th. The four days’ voyage says the same, for she left Tabor Island at five in the morning of the 15th. Only the date given here says the 20th. I take the 19th.

## b

After: It was the 30th of October, and the Unknown had been a prisoner in Granite House for nine days.

From the 19th (see note (a)) to the 30th is eleven days; from the 20th it is ten. The nine days agree with neither, and I find nothing that decides it. I leave it open.
