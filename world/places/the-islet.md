# The islet: the barren islet first reached by the castaways

*A bare islet of sand and stones, two miles long and under a quarter mile wide, half a mile off the coast, where the four spend the night of 24 March.*

Keywords: îlot, rock, skerry, fondrières, promontory, sandbank, whale carcass, first night

- **What it is.**
  - Its soil is sandy and mixed with stones, very uneven and rough, pitted with small holes (I.3 ¶14). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: criblé de petites fondrières
  - Its northern end is a sharp point on which the sea breaks furiously (I.3 ¶16). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une pointe aiguë, sur laquelle la mer brisait avec fureur
  - Behind that point, on the far side, the shore is steeper, the ground rises, and the sea is calmer, as if a semicircular cove were sheltered by the point (I.3 ¶21). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une anse semi-circulaire
  - Its other end is a fairly high point of slippery rocks (I.3 ¶23). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une pointe assez élevée, faite de roches glissantes
  - It measures no more than two miles in length (I.3 ¶25). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ne mesurait pas plus de deux mille en longueur
  - It is barren, strewn with stones, without vegetation, a desolate refuge of a few sea-birds (I.3 ¶26). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Cet îlot aride, semé de pierres, sans végétation, refuge désolé de quelques oiseaux de mer
  - It has no wood or dry brush for a fire, only sand and stones (I.3 ¶29). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Sable et pierres, il n’y avait pas autre chose
  - Its greatest width does not pass a quarter of a mile (I.3 ¶51). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Son extrême largeur ne dépassait pas un quart de mille
  - It lies half a mile from the shore and looks like an enormous cetacean, of which it is the much enlarged carcass (I.3 ¶51). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ressemblait à un énorme cétacé
- **On the page.**
  - Large heavy-flying birds rose from its holes, and others in flocks; Pencroff took them for gulls and sea-mews (I.3 ¶14). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des goëlands et des mouettes
  - Pencroff took the first point for a promontory and saw on reaching the second that they had walked the whole islet from end to end (I.3 ¶24). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: nous l’avons arpenté d’une extrémité à l’autre
  - The whole islet showed when the fog lifted, as if it had come down from a cloud (I.3 ¶41). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: tout l’îlot apparut, comme s’il fût descendu d’un nuage
  - Shellfish are scattered over its sand and the four ate some there (I.3 ¶50). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: quelques coquillages dont le sable était semé
- **Appears in:** I.3
- **Open:**
  - Whether the islet belongs to a larger archipelago is asked and not answered
    - quote: se rattachait-il à un archipel plus important ?
