# The chicory: the chicoraceous plants whose seed gives an excellent oil

*Specimens of the chicory tribe, brought by Harbert, whose very seed can give an excellent oil by pressing.*

Keywords: chicoracées, chicory, huile, oil, graine, seed

- **What it is.**
  - Harbert brings specimens of the chicory tribe among the wild plants transplanted from the forest to the Grande-Vue plateau (II.8 ¶43). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des échantillons de la tribu des chicoracées
  - Pressing its seed can give an excellent oil (II.8 ¶43). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dont la graine même pouvait fournir par la pression une huile excellente
  - Harbert recognises chicory in the old Tabor plots, whose seed he means to take to Lincoln (see [the Tabor plots](../places/tabor-vegetable-plots.md); [the Tabor seeds](tabor-seeds.md)) (II.13 ¶80). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des chicorées
- **Appears in:** II.8, II.13
