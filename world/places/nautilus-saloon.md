# The saloon: the room where Nemo receives the colonists

*A magnificent saloon whose air is charged with electric effluences, where Nemo lies on a divan.*

Keywords: salon, room, divan, cushions, hall, electric air

- **What it is.**
  - It is a magnificent saloon whose air is saturated with electric effluences (III.16 ¶8). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ce magnifique salon, dont l’air était saturé d’effluences électriques
  - It holds a divan with a back (III.16 ¶2). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le dossier du divan
  - It holds a divan with cushions, on which Nemo falls back (III.16 ¶6). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: retombant sur les oreillers du divan
- **On the page.**
  - The colonists Ayrton, Pencroff, Harbert and Nab stand respectfully aside in a corner of it (III.16 ¶8). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dans un angle de ce magnifique salon
  - Nemo signs to the engineer and the reporter to sit (III.16 ¶9). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: d’un signe il pria l’ingénieur et le reporter de s’asseoir
- **Appears in:** III.16
- **Open:**
  - The text of III.16 does not say that the saloon is aboard the Nautilus, nor where it lies on the island; the chapter opens in the middle of the scene.
    - quote: ce magnifique salon
