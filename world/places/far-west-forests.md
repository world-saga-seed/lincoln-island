# Forêts du Far-West: the mass of impenetrable woods that cover the Serpentine peninsula

*The woods named the forests of the Far-West on 30 March: the whole mass of impenetrable trees covering the Serpentine peninsula, and a way back to the Mercy.*

Keywords: Far-West, forêts, bois, woods, forest, Serpentine, impenetrable, forêt, west

- **What it is.**
  - The name forêts du Far-West was given to all the mass of impenetrable woods that covered the Serpentine peninsula (I.11 ¶81). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: tout ce massif d’impénétrables bois qui couvraient la presqu’île Serpentine, le nom de forêts du Far-West
  - One could reach the Mercy through the woods of the Far-West, as through the lake road, and so come to the Grande-Vue plateau (I.12 ¶15). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: soit que nous rejoignions la Mercy à travers les bois du Far-West
  - The forests of the Far-West are not yet explored; the great excursion is held for the first fine days of spring (I.19 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: n’étaient donc point explorées
    - quote: On réservait cette importante excursion pour les premiers beaux jours du printemps prochain
  - The wooded part is said to stretch from the Mercy to the promontoire du Reptile (I.20 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: toute cette partie boisée qui s’étendait depuis la Mercy jusqu’au promontoire du Reptile
  - The first lines of its trees lie about four miles west of the south-east beach (I.21 ¶20). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les premières lignes d’arbres des forêts du Far-West
  - Felines are supposed to live in its thick forests (see [the claw prints](../things/claw-prints.md)) (I.22 ¶31). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les épaisses forêts du Far-West
  - The unexplored part of the island is the high forests on the right of the Mercy from its mouth to the end of the Serpentine peninsula, and the whole west coast (I.22 ¶60). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: depuis son embouchure jusqu’à l’extrémité de la presqu’île Serpentine
    - quote: sur toute la côte occidentale
- **Appears in:** I.11, I.12, I.19, I.20, I.21, I.22
- **Open:**
  - How much of the great forest the name covers: I.11 ¶32 says the trees make "two thirds" of the island a forest; I.11 ¶27 has the forest cover the whole south from the mountain to the shore; ¶81 gives the name to the woods of the Serpentine peninsula. The three do not say how they overlap, nor whether it takes in [the evergreen forest](the-evergreen-forest.md) or [the wood of the Jacamar](jacamar-wood.md). Card neither.
    - quote: très-boisée dans toute sa portion méridionale depuis la montagne jusqu’au littoral
    - quote: qui faisaient des deux tiers de l’île une forêt immense
