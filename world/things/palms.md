# The palms: the palms Pencroff looked for in vain in the forest

*The palms that serve so many household uses, sought in vain by Pencroff in the forest; their limits give a latitude hint.*

Keywords: palmiers, palm, latitude, parallel, forest, domestic uses, fruit trees

- **What it is.**
  - Pencroff searched in vain for some of these precious palms that serve so many uses of domestic life (I.6 ¶27). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Pencroff cherchait vainement quelques-uns de ces précieux palmiers qui se prêtent à tant d’usages de la vie domestique
  - Their presence has been reported as far as the fortieth parallel in the northern hemisphere and only to the thirty-fifth in the southern (I.6 ¶27). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: jusqu’au quarantième parallèle dans l’hémisphère boréal et jusqu’au trente-cinquième seulement dans l’hémisphère austral
- **Appears in:** I.6
