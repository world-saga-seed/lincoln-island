# The guns: the fowling pieces Pencroff wants and Smith has not yet the means to make

*Hunting guns and the powder for them: wished for by Pencroff, their materials listed by Spilett, put off by Smith for bows and arrows.*

Keywords: fusils, powder, poudre, fulminate, mercury, nitric acid, barrels, shotgun, firearms, plomb
