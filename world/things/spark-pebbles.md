# The spark pebbles: two pebbles struck for sparks

*Two pebbles struck together to get sparks, with dry moss as tinder.*

Keywords: galets, cailloux, flint, silex, sparks, moss

- **What it is.**
  - Nab found some dry mosses and by striking two pebbles got sparks; the moss, not inflammable enough, did not catch (I.9 ¶21). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: en frappant deux galets, il obtint des étincelles
  - The sparks were only incandescent flint, lacking the consistency of those from the steel in the ordinary tinderbox (I.9 ¶21). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: n’avaient pas la consistance de celles qui s’échappent du morceau d’acier dans le briquet usuel
  - Flint was not expected to be lacking in these lands of plutonic origin (I.10 ¶19). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il ne devait pas manquer dans ces terrains d’origine plutonienne
  - At the camp the "briquet" was struck and the burnt linen took the sparks of the flint (I.10 ¶45). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Le briquet fut battu, le linge brûlé recueillit les étincelles du silex
- **Appears in:** I.9, I.10
- **Open:**
  - What served as steel: I.9 ¶21 speaks of "le morceau d’acier dans le briquet usuel" as lacking, and I.10 ¶45 says "Le briquet fut battu". The text does not say what served as the briquet. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
