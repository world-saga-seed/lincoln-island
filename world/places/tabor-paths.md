# The Tabor paths: the overgrown tracks and old felled trees of Tabor’s woods

*Paths and axe-felled trunks in the forest of Tabor Island, rotted and mossed over many years since.*

Keywords: sentiers, paths, troncs, trunks, hache, axe, mousse, moss, forêt, forest, Tabor, human work

- **What it is.**
  - Through the forest are traced paths and trees felled with the axe, everywhere the mark of human work (II.13 ¶74). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des sentiers tracés, des troncs d’arbres abattus à la hache, et partout la marque du travail humain
  - The felled trees, rotting, were overturned many years ago; the axe cuts are velvety with moss (II.13 ¶74). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ces arbres, qui tombaient en pourriture, avaient été renversés depuis bien des années déjà
    - quote: les entailles de hache étaient veloutées de mousse
  - The grass grows long and thick across the paths, which are hard to recognise (II.13 ¶74). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les herbes croissaient, longues et drues, à travers les sentiers, qu’il était malaisé de reconnaître
  - Spilett concludes that men landed and also lived on the islet for some time (II.13 ¶75). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ils l’ont habité pendant un certain temps
  - One of the paths leads back to the cultivated part of the islet (see [the Tabor plots](tabor-vegetable-plots.md)) (II.14 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le sentier qui devait le ramener vers la partie cultivée de l’îlot
- **Appears in:** II.13, II.14
