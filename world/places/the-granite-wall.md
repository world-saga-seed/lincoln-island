# The granite wall: the sheer granite cliff behind the shore

*A cliff of very hard granite, three hundred feet or more high, three miles long, cut by the river mouth.*

Keywords: courtine, muraille, cliff, falaise, pan coupé, scree, prismatic, shore

- **What it is.**
  - It is a sheer granite curtain wall crowned by a ragged ridge at least three hundred feet high (I.3 ¶52). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: à une hauteur de trois cents pieds au moins
  - It runs three miles in length (I.3 ¶52). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: sur une longueur de trois milles
  - Its right end stops short in a cut face that looks as if cut by human hand (I.3 ¶52). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un pan coupé qu’on eût cru taillé de main d’homme
  - Its left end is an irregular cliff of agglomerated rock and scree, breaking into prismatic splinters, falling by a long ramp to the rocks of the southern point (I.3 ¶52). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: s’égrenant en éclats prismatiques
  - The block is solid throughout, with no cleft at its base for a dwelling (I.4 ¶9). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le bloc était plein partout
  - It is a plumb wall of very hard granite that the sea has never gnawed (I.4 ¶9). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un granit très-dur, que le flot n’avait jamais rongé
  - At the river mouth the wall seems split by some violent plutonic effort (I.4 ¶20). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: séparée par quelque violent effort plutonien
  - It is smooth, flat and plumb along the river as well (I.4 ¶22). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: lisse, plane et d’aplomb
- **On the page.**
