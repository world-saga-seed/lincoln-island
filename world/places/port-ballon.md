# Port Ballon: the small hidden natural harbour of the south coast

*A narrow, well-closed cove with a hidden passage between reefs, found on 2 November and named port Ballon that evening; a cut in the rock leads up to the plateau.*

Keywords: port Ballon, Balloon harbour, crique, passe, havre, coupée, natural port, south coast, cove

- **What it is.**
  - It is a narrow, well-closed cove into which no stream runs (II.5 ¶43). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une étroite crique bien fermée, à laquelle n’aboutissait aucun cours d’eau
  - It is a true small natural port, invisible from the open sea, entered by a narrow pass that the reefs leave between them (II.5 ¶43). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un véritable petit port naturel, invisible du large
    - quote: une étroite passe, que les écueils ménageaient entre eux
  - At the back of the cove a violent convulsion has torn the rocky edge, and a cut hollowed in a gentle slope gives access to the upper plateau (II.5 ¶44). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: quelque violente convulsion avait déchiré la lisière rocheuse
    - quote: une coupée, évidée en pente douce, donnait accès au plateau supérieur
  - The upper plateau lies less than ten miles from cap Griffe and so four miles in a straight line from the Grande-Vue plateau (II.5 ¶44). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: à moins de dix milles du cap Griffe
    - quote: à quatre milles en droite ligne du plateau de Grande-Vue
  - The spot where the party halts beside the cove stands fifty to sixty feet above the sea (II.5 ¶47). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: L’endroit était élevé de cinquante à soixante pieds au-dessus du niveau de la mer
  - A fine clump of maritime pines stands there (see [the maritime pine](../things/maritime-pine.md)) (II.5 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un magnifique bouquet de pins maritimes
  - From it the view passes over the last rocks of the cape and reaches the Union Bay, but neither the islet nor the Grande-Vue plateau can be seen, since the lie of the ground and a curtain of tall trees mask the northern horizon (II.5 ¶47). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il allait se perdre jusque dans la baie de l’Union
    - quote: le relief du sol et le rideau des grands arbres masquaient brusquement l’horizon du nord
  - It is named "port Ballon" on 2 November, when the colonists give the little notch that forms the cove that very justified name (II.5 ¶96). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le nom très-justifié de « port Ballon »
  - It lies three miles and a half from the bend of the Mercy, where the bridge is to stand, and was discovered the day before the bridge is begun (II.7 ¶14). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: découvert la veille sur la côte méridionale
    - quote: une distance de trois milles et demi
  - The balloon's envelope and gear are stored in a rock cavity beside it (see [the balloon store](balloon-store.md)) (II.5 ¶94). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une assez vaste cavité rocheuse
  - A road is cut to it through the Far-West forest for the cart (see [the road to port Ballon](port-ballon-road.md)) (II.7 ¶61). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: depuis le coude de la Mercy jusqu’au port Ballon
  - Abreast of it, three or four miles out, the whole island shows with its coast from cap Griffe to the promontoire du Reptile (II.12 ¶98). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: à trois ou quatre milles de la côte, par le travers du port Ballon
  - The passes left between sandbanks and reefs have to be tested and, if need be, buoyed, since the small cove is to be the boat’s home port (see [the Bonadventure](../things/bonadventure.md)) (II.12 ¶122). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les passes ménagées entre les bancs de sable et les récifs

  - Pencroff calls it a little cove covered by rocks, just the port the boat needs (II.15 ¶54). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Cette petite crique, couverte par les roches, me paraît être justement le port qu’il lui faut
  - It is not more than three miles from Granite-house, with a fine straight road to it (II.15 ¶56). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Il ne se trouve pas à plus de trois milles de Granite-house

    - quote: je l’aimerais mieux sous notre surveillance plus immédiate
