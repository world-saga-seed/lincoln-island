## Open and flags
- Date discord. II.3 ¶72 ends the night of 30 October: "the next day, 31 October, at five in the morning, everyone was up". II.4 is one day from the falls to the promontory (6 a.m. to about 7 p.m.). This chapter then names the next day: "that day, 2 November" (¶4). A day is missing between 31 October and 2 November, or the 2 is an error for 1 November. Card neither version. *(Drafted.)*
- The date also bears on ¶12: "The canoe was left alone for a day at the sources of the Mercy, … it may well stay there two days". If the canoe was left on the morning of 31 October, by the morning of 2 November it has been left two days, not one; the line fits 1 November. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- The moon: II.3 ¶9 on 30 October says the moon will be full "three days after", about 2 November. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- Distance from Flotson-point to Granite-house: ¶98 "From Flotson-point to Granite-house there were still four miles."; II.2 ¶13 "about three miles from the Mercy" and II.2 ¶42 "that distance of three miles". The shore beyond the Mercy's mouth may account for a mile, but the text does not say. *(Drafted.)*
- Where the party stands at midnight: ¶98 has them at "the first bend formed by the river" after following the shore "as far as the mouth of the Mercy", then ¶118 reaches "the mouth of the Mercy" by canoe. *(Drafted.)*
- The distance to Cape Claw is given as thirty miles from the tip (¶7), then twenty-five from the angle five miles on (¶27), and ten from the harbour (¶44); the walked distances (five, twenty at the bay, then the harbour at "less than ten miles") are consistent. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- The chest was "found less than twenty miles from there" (¶42) from the shore west of the harbour. *(Drafted.)*
- Weapons: ¶25 "the guns were loaded with ball", against II.4 ¶61 "small shot". *(Drafted.)*
- Whether the balloon is the envelope that carried them is given in the text as "our air-boat, our balloon" (¶84). Earlier chapters were not read here. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- No Wikisource correction touches this chapter. *(Drafted.)*
