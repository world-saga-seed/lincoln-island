# The wheat field: the sheltered plot on the plateau where the one grain was sown

*A cleared, weeded, limed and fenced bed on the plateau above Granite-house, sunk with the single grain on 20 June and called by Pencroff "son champ de blé".*

Keywords: champ de blé, grain, sowing, plot, palisade, garden, seed bed, wheat

- **What it is.**
  - On 20 June the colonists chose a spot on the plateau well sheltered from the wind, to which the midday sun would pour all its heat (I.20 ¶66, ¶67). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un endroit bien abrité du vent
    - quote: le soleil de midi devait verser toute sa chaleur
  - The spot was cleaned, weeded with care and even dug over to drive out insects and worms (I.20 ¶67). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: nettoyé, sarclé avec soin, fouillé même, pour en chasser les insectes ou les vers
  - It is a bed of good earth improved with a little lime, fenced round with a palisade (I.20 ¶67). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une couche de bonne terre amendée d’un peu de chaux
    - quote: on l’entoura d’une palissade
  - The grain was pressed into the damp bed (see [the grain of wheat](../things/grain-of-wheat.md)) (I.20 ¶67). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le grain fut enfoncé dans la couche humide
  - Pencroff calls it, in earnest, "his wheat field", and does not let a day pass without visiting it (I.21 ¶1). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ce qu’il appelait sérieusement son « champ de blé »
  - The plateau climbed to is the one above Granite-house, read as the Grande-Vue plateau (see [the Grande-Vue plateau](grande-vue-plateau.md)) (I.20 ¶67). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: les colons gravirent les hauteurs de Granite-house
- **Appears in:** I.20, I.21
