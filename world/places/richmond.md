# Richmond: the besieged Virginian capital the prisoners flee

*The Confederate capital under Grant's siege, where Smith, Spilett, Nab, Pencroff and Harbert are held and from whose great square the balloon rises.*

Keywords: Virginia, siege, capital, stronghold, great square, prison, escape, Grant, Lee, séparatistes

- **What it is.**
  - It is the capital of Virginia and the separatists' chief stronghold (I.2 ¶1). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la principale place forte des séparatistes
  - It is besieged by General Grant's troops (I.2 ¶1). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: assiégée par les troupes du général Ulysse Grant
  - It fell to Grant on 5 April (I.2 n1). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Le 5 avril, Richmond tombait aux mains de Grant
  - Prisoners like Smith and Spilett were left free in the town, yet it was so strictly guarded that escape was judged impossible (I.2 ¶13). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Richmond était si sévèrement gardée
  - Federal prisoners are watched very closely in it (I.2 ¶16). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: on surveillait de très-près les prisonniers fédéraux
  - The besiegers hold the Confederates in too, so none could leave it (I.2 ¶18). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: l’armée du Nord les investissait
  - Its governor could no longer communicate with General Lee (I.2 ¶18). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ne pouvait plus communiquer avec le général Lee
  - It has a great square where the balloon was inflated (I.2 ¶21). *(Drafted.)*

  - The square is paved, with a ring set in the pavement, and lit by gas lanterns (I.2 ¶49). *(Drafted.)*

  - They left it without the authorities' permission (I.11 ¶4). *(Drafted.)*
