## a

After: “The subterranean fires have been brooding for ten weeks,”

From about the 15th of October to this day, the 3rd of January, is eleven weeks and more. Herbert’s “two months and a half” agrees with the dates; the ten weeks fall short. I take Herbert’s reckoning.
