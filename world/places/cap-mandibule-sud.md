# Cap Mandibule-Sud: the southern of the two capes of the Shark-gulf

*The southern jaw of the Shark-gulf, the point that closes the Union Bay on the north; the coast before it is arid.*

Keywords: cap Mandibule-Sud, Mandible-cape, south jaw, north-east, point

- **What it is.**
  - It was named cap Mandibule-Sud when Pencroff gave the two capes of the jaw the names Nord and Sud (I.11 ¶71). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le cap Mandibule-Nord et le cap Mandibule-Sud
  - It is the point that closes the Union Bay (I.14 ¶38). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: cette pointe qui fermait la baie de l’Union, et qui avait reçu le nom de cap Mandibule-Sud
  - All the country to it is very arid, sand and shells mixed with lava debris (I.14 ¶38). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: On n’y voyait que sable et coquilles, mélangés de débris de laves
- **Appears in:** I.11, I.14
