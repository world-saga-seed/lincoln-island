# Pintails: the pilets of the marsh and lake

*Ducks found in bands in the south-east marsh and on the frozen lake.*

Keywords: pilet, pintail, canard, duck, marais, marsh, lac, lake, gibier, game

- **What it is.**
  - Pintails live in bands over the marsh waters, among wild ducks, teal and snipe, little afraid of men (I.21 ¶54). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Canards sauvages, pilets, sarcelles, bécassines y vivaient par bandes
  - Pintails flutter by thousands on the frozen lake, with ducks, snipe and guillemots (I.22 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: canards et bécassines, pilets et guillemots
    - quote: Il y en avait des milliers
  - Pintails are among the water game that crowd the south-east marsh in winter, its winter quarters (II.11 ¶78). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: tout le gibier aquatique, qui établissait là ses quartiers d’hiver
    - quote: pilets
  - Pintails are among the myriads of water-game shot in the great cold in the tadornes' marshes (II.20 ¶77). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: de pilets
- **Appears in:** I.21, I.22, II.11, II.20
