# The northern anchorage: the slight inlet of the north coast where the Bonadventure spends the night

*A slight hollowing of the north shore, with deep water close in, where the Bonadventure anchors on 17 April and from which the hunters go ashore at dawn.*

Keywords: mouillage, renfoncement, anchorage, inlet, north coast, night, hunting, ducks, snipe

- **What it is.** A cove, an anchorage on the north coast. Build if needed. *(Drafted.)*
- **Appears in:** II.19 ¶53, ¶54
