# Lake Grant: the lake west of the brickworks clearing

*A lake whose west shore has the clay and whose north side has the limestone; seen from Mont Franklin on 30 March, named Lake Grant the same day, a lake of fresh, clear, fish-filled water fed by the Red Creek, its overflow not found.*

Keywords: lac Grant, pond, shore, water, west bank, lac Grant, creek, déversoir, overflow, Ontario, vasque, fed by

- **What it is.**
  - The lake lies between the volcano and the east coast, framed in a border of green trees; its existence had not been suspected (I.11 ¶27). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un lac, encadré dans sa bordure d’arbres verts, dont ils ne soupçonnaient pas l’existence
  - It seemed from the summit to be at sea level, but Smith reasoned that it stands three hundred feet up, the plateau that holds it being the prolongation of the coast plateau (I.11 ¶27). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: l’altitude de cette petite nappe d’eau devait être de trois cents pieds
  - A narrow brook flows into it, its source in the western buttresses (I.11 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un étroit ruisseau, dont la source devait s’épancher dans les contreforts de l’ouest
  - Smith inferred that an outlet to the sea must carry off the overflow, to be looked for on the return (I.11 ¶31). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il est probable que du côté de la mer il existe un déversoir par lequel s’échappe le trop-plein des eaux
  - It was named lake Grant on 30 March, in the proposal Smith put to his friends (I.11 ¶63). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: à ce lac qui s’étend sous nos regards, celui de lac Grant
  - The colonists were already using the new names, the lake's among them, the same day (I.12 ¶14). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ce lac Grant si magnifiquement encadré dans sa bordure d’arbres
  - The Red Creek runs to it over a mile and a half (I.12 ¶40). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: courait ainsi vers le lac sur une longueur d’un mille et demi
  - The colonists reached its west shore where the creek ends (I.12 ¶68). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Les explorateurs étaient arrivés sur la rive occidentale du lac Grant
  - It lies in a border of varied trees, with a glittering horizon of sea seen to the east through a curtain of foliage (I.12 ¶68). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: reposait dans une bordure d’arbres variés
    - quote: un étincelant horizon de mer
  - On the north it is slightly concave, in contrast with the sharp point of its lower end (I.12 ¶68). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Au nord, le lac traçait une courbure légèrement concave
  - A rock rises from its surface a few hundred feet from the south shore, standing for the "thousand islands" of its American namesake, "that little Ontario" (see [the rock in the lake](lake-islet.md)) (I.12 ¶68). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: représentées par un rocher qui émergeait de sa surface
  - Many water birds frequent its banks (I.12 ¶68). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: De nombreux oiseaux aquatiques fréquentaient les rives
  - Its water is fresh, clear and a little dark, and full of fish, to judge by the bubbling and the crossing rings on its surface (I.12 ¶69). *(Drafted.)*

  - Its lower end is an angle at the south where the shores join (I.12 ¶72). *(Drafted.)*

  - It is only an immense basin that filled little by little from the creek, so its overflow must run to the sea by some fall; Smith thought the fall might be used for its power (I.12 ¶73). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il serait peut-être possible d’utiliser cette chute et de lui emprunter sa force
