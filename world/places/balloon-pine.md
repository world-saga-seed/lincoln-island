# The balloon pine: the gigantic pine in whose crown the balloon hung

*A gigantic pine in a clearing a few minutes' walk into the forest from port Ballon, where the balloon's envelope had lodged after its last leap.*

Keywords: pin, pine, clearing, clairière, balloon, aérostat, tree, sommet, cime, port Ballon

- **What it is.**
  - It lies in a sort of clearing bordered by tall trees, seven or eight minutes' walk into the forest from the cove, among the first curtain of great pines (II.5 ¶69, ¶71). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: entre ces grands pins qui formaient le premier rideau de la forêt
    - quote: Après sept à huit minutes de marche
    - quote: une sorte de clairière, bordée de grands arbres
  - It is a gigantic pine (II.5 ¶73). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un gigantesque pin
  - A great whitish rag hangs from its crown: the balloon's envelope, left deflated at the top of the tree (II.5 ¶80, ¶84). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une sorte de grand haillon blanchâtre, accroché à la cime du pin
    - quote: notre ballon qui s’est échoué là-haut, au sommet de cet arbre
  - The way to it shows no footprint, and the brush and lianas are intact and have to be cut with the axe, as in the deepest parts of the forest, so that it is hard to think a human creature had passed there (II.5 ¶70). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ils ne virent encore aucune empreinte de pas
    - quote: Broussailles et lianes étaient intactes
    - quote: il fallut même les couper à la hache
  - The envelope hung there after the balloon's "last leap in the air", and the colonists have the luck to find it fallen on the island (II.5 ¶87). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: après avoir fait son dernier bond dans les airs, fût retombé sur l’île
- **Appears in:** II.5
