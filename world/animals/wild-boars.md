# Wild boars: the sangliers of the island's woods

*Wild boars glimpsed on the march through the Jacamar Wood, that do not try to attack the party.*

Keywords: sanglier, sangliers, wild boar, pig, hog, gibier, game, forêt, Jacamar

- **What it is.**
  - Some wild boars are seen on the march through the wood, and they do not seek to attack the little troop (I.15 ¶38). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: quelques sangliers sauvages, qui ne cherchèrent point à attaquer la petite troupe
  - Wild boars abound in the Jacamar Wood (I.19 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: kangourous et sangliers y abondaient
  - A few of the sangliers reported earlier north of the lake are taken in the traps in the second week of August; they look so like American or European pigs that Pencroff needs no telling they are edible (I.22 ¶14). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: quelques-uns de ces sangliers qui avaient été déjà signalés au nord du lac
    - quote: leur ressemblance avec le cochon d’Amérique ou d’Europe
  - A few wild boars are seen on the march toward the west coast (II.4 ¶8). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: On vit aussi quelques sangliers
  - A few wild boars are caught in the whalebone engines set at the forest edge where the animals pass on their way to the lake, and are found dead with their stomachs pierced (II.11 ¶28, ¶29). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: à l’endroit où passaient communément les animaux pour se rendre au lac
    - quote: quelques sangliers
    - quote: l’estomac perforé par les fanons détendus
  - In the Far-West forests the colonists meet frequently certain sangliers, very wild and very fierce, against which they have to fight seriously (II.18 ¶55, ¶56). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: jusque dans les profondeurs des forêts du Far-West
    - quote: certains sangliers, très-sauvages et très-féroces, contre lesquels il fallait lutter sérieusement
  - Sangliers are among the big game that never fail at the house when Harbert and Spilett hunt on the corral road (III.14 ¶20). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: cabiais, agoutis, kangourous, sangliers, porcs sauvages pour le gros gibier
    - quote: ne manquaient jamais à la maison
  - When the Far-West forest burns, the game, "jaguars, sangliers, cabiais, koulas" among it, flees mad with fear toward the Mercy and the marsh of the tadornes; all the island's animals die in its end (III.19 ¶60; III.20 ¶3). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: jaguars, sangliers, cabiais, koulas, gibier de poil et de plume
    - quote: Les animaux avaient également péri dans la catastrophe
- **Appears in:** I.15, I.19, I.22, II.4, II.11, II.18, III.14, III.19, III.20

  - The beasts of the I.22 traps are first called "sangliers" (I.22 ¶14), then "des pécaris" (I.22 ¶21); the shift and its quotes are on the Open line of [the peccaries](peccaries.md). The two cards stay apart, each with the text's own word (R1121-08). *(Settled R1121-08.)*
