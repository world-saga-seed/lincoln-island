# The Maria-Stella: the Vineyard whaler named on the harpoon

*A fine whaler of the Vineyard, Pencroff’s own country, whose harpoon killed the whale that stranded on 3 May.*

Keywords: Maria-Stella, baleinier, whaler, Vineyard, ship, navire, harpon, Pencroff, pays

- **What it is.**
  - The name on the harpoon is the Maria-Stella, a ship of the Vineyard, which Pencroff calls a fine whaler he knows well (II.10 ¶62, ln3). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Un navire du Vineyard ! Un navire de mon pays !
    - quote: La Maria-Stella ! un beau baleinier, ma foi ! Et que je connais bien !
  - A footnote glosses the Vineyard as a port of the state of New York (see [the Vineyard](../places/vineyard.md)) (II.10 n2). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Port de l’état de New York.
  - The text says the whale was harpooned by her, and that the colonists could not wait for her to come and claim it (II.10 ¶64). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: on ne pouvait attendre que la Maria-Stella vînt réclamer l’animal harponné par elle
- **Appears in:** II.10
