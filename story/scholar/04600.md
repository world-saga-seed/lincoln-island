## a

After: if, on the 30th of November, Neb, rambling along the beach,

The cylinder was found before the trial of the guns, which the next chapter dates the 8th of November, and before Ayrton went to the corral on the 9th (chapter XLVIII), the day before he was taken. The wreck was gone eight days after the 18th of October, which is the 26th. The finding falls between the 26th of October and the 8th of November, and not on the 30th of November. I find nothing to give the true day.
