# The Tabor stream: the brook across Tabor’s meadow to the west coast

*The one watercourse of Tabor Island, crossing a wide meadow to a narrow mouth on the west coast, to the left of the hut’s shore.*

Keywords: ruisseau, brook, creek, prairie, meadow, embouchure, mouth, west coast, Tabor

- **What it is.**
  - A stream flows obliquely to the oval of the islet through a wide meadow and runs into the sea on the west coast by a narrow mouth (II.13 ¶59). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un ruisseau coulait à travers une large prairie et allait se jeter à la mer sur la côte occidentale par une étroite embouchure
  - The route that cuts the islet diagonally brings the explorers alongside it as it runs towards the sea (II.13 ¶78). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la route qui coupait diagonalement l’îlot
    - quote: à côtoyer le ruisseau qui se dirigeait vers la mer
  - The mouth of the stream opens to the left of the shore in front of the Tabor hut (see [the Tabor dwelling](the-tabor-dwelling.md)); the shore there is read as the west coast, where the first paragraph puts the stream’s mouth (II.14 ¶21). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: sur la gauche duquel s’ouvrait l’embouchure du ruisseau
- **Appears in:** II.13, II.14
