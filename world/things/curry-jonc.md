# The curry-jonc: a plant whose fibres make cords

*A plant of the island whose fibres Pencroff plaits into the ladder’s uprights and the snares.*

Keywords: curry-jonc, jonc, fibre, rush, cordage, plaiting, ladder, snares

- **What it is.**
  - Its fibres, plaited with a windlass, have the strength of a thick cable (I.19 ¶19). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des fibres du « curry-jonc » tressées au moyen d’un moulinet
    - quote: la solidité d’un gros câble
  - Pencroff makes snares from its fibres (I.22 ¶13). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les fibres du curry-jonc
- **Leads to.**
  - The ladder’s uprights are made from it (see [the rope ladder](the-rope-ladder.md)) (I.19 ¶19). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ses montants, formés des fibres du « curry-jonc »
  - The snares are made from it (see [the snares](snares.md)) (I.22 ¶13). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: en employant les fibres du curry-jonc
- **Appears in:** I.19, I.22
- **Open:**
  - The plant is not described: what it looks like and where it grows are not said (I.19 ¶19). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: curry-jonc
