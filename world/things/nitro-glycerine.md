# The nitro-glycerine: the explosive Smith makes from the dugong’s fat and the saltpetre

*Several pints of an oily yellowish liquid, made alone by Smith by 20 May from nitric acid and glycerine; used for the lake blast; too shattering for firearms.*

Keywords: nitro-glycérine, nitroglycerine, explosive, explosif, blast, dynamite, brisante, shock, détoner, liquid, charge

- **What it is.**
  - Smith put nitric acid with glycerine concentrated beforehand by evaporation on a water bath, and got, without a freezing mixture, several pints of an oily, yellowish liquid (I.17 ¶58). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: concentrée par évaporation au bain-marie
    - quote: plusieurs pintes d’un liquide huileux et jaunâtre
  - He made this last operation alone, away from the Chimneys, because of the danger of explosion, and brought back one flask of the liquid (I.17 ¶59). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Cette dernière opération, Cyrus Smith l’avait faite seul, à l’écart
    - quote: un flacon de ce liquide
  - It was made on 20 May, the day before the blast, which was "tomorrow" (I.17 ¶65). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: Demain, dès que nous aurons creusé un trou de mine
  - Its explosive power is perhaps ten times that of ordinary powder, and it has caused many accidents (I.17 ¶61). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: peut-être décuple de celle de la poudre ordinaire
  - Dynamite, by which the liquid is made safer, was not yet known at the time (I.17 ¶61). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la dynamite n’était pas encore connue à l’époque
  - It detonates on shock; lit simply, it would burn without bursting (I.17 ¶69, ¶71). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Il faut, en effet, un choc pour provoquer l’explosion
    - quote: la nitro-glycérine a la propriété de détonner au choc
  - Smith meant to use not less than ten litres for the blast (I.17 ¶68). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: moins de dix litres de nitro-glycérine
  - The explosion shook the whole island; a sheaf of stones flew into the air as from a volcano, the rocks of the Chimneys swayed, and the colonists, more than two miles away, were thrown to the ground (I.17 ¶75). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Il sembla que toute l’île tremblait sur sa base
    - quote: furent renversés sur le sol
  - Smith says the liquid could blow up the island, the continents and the earth itself: it is only a question of quantity (I.18 ¶7). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ce n’est qu’une question de quantité
  - It is too shattering to load firearms (I.18 ¶9). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: c’est une substance trop brisante
