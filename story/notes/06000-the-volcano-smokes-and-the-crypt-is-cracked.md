# The volcano smokes and the crypt is cracked (notes)

## Notes for drafting
- **Where it sits.** The French III.18, chapter 06000, between 05900 (Nemo's death, the Nautilus sunk) and 06100 (the island doomed). It runs from the dawn of 16 October 1868 to 7 January 1869. *(Drafted.)*
- **Cast.** Cyrus Smith (¶1, "les colons"; named ¶4), Pencroff, Nab, Ayrton (¶2); Harbert (¶4); Gédéon Spilett (¶5); Captain Nemo, dead (¶1, ¶5); Top and Jup do not appear. Onaggas (¶12, ¶61) and the corral's sheep and goats (¶55, ¶70) are present as animals. *(Drafted.)*
- **Places.** The Dakkar crypt, named here (¶1); the corral and the wire to the crypt (¶4); Granite-house (¶6); the shipyard and Port-Ballon (¶30–¶31); the Grande-Vue plateau (¶12, ¶33); Mount Franklin, its north-east breach (¶54); the Red Creek, its sulphurous spring (¶71–¶72); the causeway at the Shark Gulf (¶75); the north-west coast of basalt and porphyry (¶86); the crypt's end wall (¶101). *(Drafted.)*
- **Things.** The sheet-iron boat (¶2, ¶91); the wire (¶4); the new ship's frames and first planks (¶9), the précintes (¶31); the Speedy's rigging, saved whole (¶8); the notice for Tabor (¶7); the lantern and lighter (¶83, ¶93); handkerchiefs against ash (¶85). *(Drafted.)*
- **Time.** Dawn of 16 October 1868 ("nuit du 15 au 16 octobre", ¶5; year my reading from ¶9 and ¶11), about nine o'clock arrival at Granite-house (¶6); target of five months, early March (¶8); end of 1868 (¶9); 1 January 1869 storm (¶11); 3 January plume, shipyard work, evening on the plateau (¶12, ¶32–¶33); 4, 5, 6 January (¶52); 7 January dawn trip (¶55, ¶61), at the corral by about 9 (¶76), more than ten o'clock at the coast crest (¶86), twenty-five minutes' row in (¶100), half an hour out (¶106). Chapter covers about twelve weeks, most of it by summary. *(Drafted.)* The 8 January return is stated in III.19 ¶1 and implied by ¶58 ("revenus le lendemain"). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- **Clues.** Smith's "Oui et non" and "il faut que je voie… que je visite la montagne" (¶46, ¶50) hold back the danger until III.19. "Le capitaine Nemo l’a bien pressenti !" (¶74) and "Voilà ce que craignait le capitaine Nemo !" (¶98) point to what Nemo told Smith in 05900's private talk. The note for Tabor (¶7) is explained in III.20. Thirteen smoke columns at the spring (¶72). *(Drafted.)*
- **Length.** 106 paragraphs (¶1 to ¶106), 4,459 words; three plate captions. *(Drafted.)*

## Open and flags
- Ten weeks and two and a half months. Spilett says "Les feux souterrains ont donc couvé pendant dix semaines" (¶39) right after Harbert's "voilà déjà deux mois et demi de cela !" (¶38). From about 15 October to 3 January is about eleven and a half weeks. Card neither. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- "Au bout de deux mois et demi, les couples avaient été mis en place" (¶9) is counted from a start the chapter does not give; with ¶8's "cinq mois" for the whole work and ¶30's "Avant deux mois" for the launch, the start of the hull is unfixed here. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- Wikisource correction, ¶62: the print reads "qu’on les as vues"; Wikisource reads "qu’on les a vues". The reading is used; no fact rests on it. *(Drafted.)*
- Seam with 05900: ¶91 of III.17 ends with the Nautilus resting on the sea floor and the crypt dark. III.18 ¶1 begins at "Au point du jour" with the colonists back at "l’entrée de la caverne"; it follows. ¶94 here recalls the electric light that "se propageait-elle encore au fond des eaux". *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- ¶88 says "aucune grève ne découvrait" outside while ¶2 has a "petite grève" inside the crypt; both are about different spots. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
