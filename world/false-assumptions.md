# What is not true here: June 1865

*The defaults a drafter carries in that are false at the date of 01950 and its neighbours (1 to 20 June 1865). Read first, before drafting. Each line cites its source. (Settled R1121-98.) For the whole state of things, see [the tool chain](things/tool-chain.md) and [the world's rules](rules.md), rule 5.*

- **There is no glass anywhere.** The windows have thick shutters "en attendant que l’ingénieur eût eu le temps de fabriquer du verre à vitre" (I.19 ¶46). The first pane comes on 28 March 1866 (II.9). The only glass is Smith's two watch glasses ([the lens](things/lens.md)).
- **No candles before about 5 June.** The light indoors is the fire. The candles are made from the seal fat of the hunt of 5 June ([candles](things/candles.md)).
- **Two knives only,** the halves of Top's collar ([the knife blades](things/knife-blades.md)). The forging made picks, shovels, axes and hammers, but no knives ([iron tools](things/iron-tools.md)).
- **No boat.** The pirogue is begun on 25 October (II.1 ¶34). The channel is crossed on foot at low water (I.20 ¶20).
- **No firearms,** and no instruments or books, until the chest of 29 October (II.2).
- **No Jup.** The orang comes in November 1865 (II.6).
- **Only the clothes they came in:** "Les colons n’avaient d’autres habits que ceux qu’ils portaient" (I.20 ¶3). The sealskin shoes come after 5 June (I.20 ¶21).
- **Soap, since May** ([glycerine and soap](things/glycerine.md), I.17).
- **The way into Granite-house is the rope ladder,** set up on 28 May (I.19 ¶28).
- **The Mercy is unbridged.** The two small bridges come after 5 June (I.20 ¶29), and the bridge over the Mercy in November (II.7).
- **Top stays with Smith** on 1 to 3 June (R1121-75).
