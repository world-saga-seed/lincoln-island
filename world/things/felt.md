# The felt: the fulled wool cloth called "feutre lincolnien"

*A thick felt got by fulling the mouflons’ wool, for clothing and blankets, the new industry of the island.*

Keywords: feutre, felt, foulage, fulling, laine, wool, vêtements, couvertures, blankets, lincolnien

- **What it is.**
  - It is made by fulling and not by spinning and weaving, since Smith has no carding, combing, smoothing, drawing or twisting machines, no "mule-jenny" or "self-acting" for spinning wool, and no loom (II.11 ¶3). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: n’ayant à sa disposition ni cardeuses, ni peigneuses
    - quote: ni métier pour la tisser
  - He uses the property of wool filaments, when pressed in every direction, of tangling and forming by their simple interlacing the cloth called felt (II.11 ¶3). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: s’enchevêtrer et de constituer, par leur simple entrecroisement, cette étoffe qu’on appelle feutre
  - Fulling lessens the suppleness of the cloth but greatly increases its power of keeping heat (II.11 ¶3). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: elle diminue la souplesse de l’étoffe, augmente notamment ses propriétés conservatrices de la chaleur
  - The mouflons’ wool, made of very short strands, is good for felting (II.11 ¶3). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la laine fournie par les mouflons était faite de brins très-courts
  - The wool is scoured of its grease in vats of water at seventy degrees for twenty-four hours, washed thoroughly with soda baths and pressed dry, by Smith with his companions and Pencroff (see [the mouflon wool](mouflon-wool.md)) (II.11 ¶4). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dans des cuves remplies d’eau, qui furent portées à la température de soixante-dix degrés
    - quote: au moyen de bains de soude
  - The cloth is coarse and would have no value in an industrial centre of Europe or America, but is extremely valued in the "marchés de l’île Lincoln" (II.11 ¶4). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: grossière sans doute et qui n’aurait eu aucune valeur dans un centre industriel d’Europe ou d’Amérique
    - quote: on devait faire un extrême cas sur les « marchés de l’île Lincoln »
  - The first woollen fabrics were made by this process, known from the earliest times (II.11 ¶5). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les premières étoffes de laine ont été fabriquées par ce procédé
  - The wool, impregnated beforehand with a soapy solution, comes out of the fulling mill as a thick sheet of felt, fit for clothes or blankets, and is called "feutre lincolnien" (see [the fulling mill](the-fulling-mill.md)) (II.11 ¶8). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: préalablement imprégnée d’une dissolution savonneuse
    - quote: une épaisse nappe de feutre
    - quote: C’était du « feutre lincolnien »
  - With it the colonists have good clothes and thick blankets, and can face the winter of 1866-67 without fear (see [the felt clothes](felt-clothes.md)) (II.11 ¶9). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: de bons vêtements, d’épaisses couvertures
    - quote: l’hiver de 1866-67
- **Appears in:** II.11, II.20
