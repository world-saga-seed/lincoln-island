# S8: the French, the render, White

## ¶29

**French:** Au soir, le croissant de la lune, qui ne devait être dans son premier quartier que le 16, se dessina dans le crépuscule solaire et s’éteignit bientôt. La nuit fut sombre, mais très-étoilée, et une belle journée s’annonçait encore pour le lendemain.

**Render:** In the evening the crescent of the moon, which would not be in her first quarter until the 16th, showed itself in the twilight of the sun, and was soon extinguished. The night was dark, but very starry, and another fine day was promised for the morrow.

**White** (¶23): (¶23) In the evening the slender crescent of the moon glimmered in the twilight. The night came on dark but starlit, with the promise of a fine day on the morrow. Pencroff thought it prudent to take in the topsail, which was perhaps an excess of caution in so still a night, but he was a careful sailor, and was not to be blamed.
