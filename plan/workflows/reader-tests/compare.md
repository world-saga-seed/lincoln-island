# Prompt: compare two drafts

*Run four times: both orders, two model families. Strip the title lines and any "draft N" first. Name the files `draft-1.md` and `draft-2.md` in a folder with a neutral name.*

```
Read only these two files, in full, and nothing else: FOLDER/draft-1.md and FOLDER/draft-2.md. Do not open, list or search any other file or folder.

They are two drafts of the same chapter of a novel, written independently. Neither is a revision of the other. Read them as a demanding fiction editor would.

Answer:
1. Which draft is stronger, and by how much (slightly, clearly, or by a wide margin)?
2. The three to five reasons that decide it, each with a short quote from each draft.
3. What the weaker draft does better: beats or lines worth taking into the stronger one, with quotes.
4. Any continuity slip or confusing beat you notice in either draft.

Write your answer to FOLDER/verdict.md. Reply with only your answer to question 1.
```

Score question 1 across the four reads (see [comparing two drafts](../reader-tests.md#comparing-two-drafts)). From questions 3 and 4, take only what you can check on the page.
