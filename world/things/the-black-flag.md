# The black flag: the pirate ensign of the Speedy

*A black bunting flown at the peak of the brig, which turns "a suspicious ship" into a pirate; flown out of bravado and not as a signal.*

Keywords: pavillon noir, black flag, drapeau, ensign, corne, pirates, forfanterie, navire suspect, Jolly Roger

- **What it is.**
  - It is a flag at the brig’s peak whose colours Pencroff cannot make out at first; when a breeze stretches it Ayrton sees that it is black (III.1 ¶67, ¶73, ¶74). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un pavillon flotte à sa corne
    - quote: Le pavillon noir !
  - It is a sombre piece of bunting at the peak, and from it the brig may fairly be held a suspect ship (III.1 ¶75). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une sombre étamine se développait à la corne du brick
    - quote: c’était à bon droit qu’on pouvait maintenant le tenir pour un navire suspect
  - It is the flag of the sea-robbers; it is also the flag the Duncan would have flown if the convicts had succeeded in their criminal plans (III.1 ¶77). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: C’était bien celui des écumeurs de mer
    - quote: C’était celui que devait porter le Duncan, si les convicts avaient réussi dans leurs criminels projets
  - Smith asks whether the pirates, who hoisted their colours on landing, had visited the island before and left a confederate on it (III.1 ¶94). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: puisque, en y atterrissant, ils avaient hissé leurs couleurs
    - quote: Existait-il dans ses portions non encore explorées quelque complice
  - Ayrton learns that Bob Harvey had never set foot on the island, and that the flag and the shot fired with it were "pure forfanterie", like a warship’s salute at lowering its colours, not a signal; no communication yet existed between the escaped convicts and Lincoln Island (III.2 ¶52, ¶53). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Bob Harvey n’y avait jamais encore mis le pied
    - quote: pure forfanterie de pirates
    - quote: Ce n’était point un signal
    - quote: aucune communication n’existait encore entre les évadés de Norfolk et l’île Lincoln
  - It is still flying at the peak the next morning (III.3 ¶17). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Le sinistre pavillon noir flottait à sa corne
- **Appears in:** III.1, III.2, III.3
