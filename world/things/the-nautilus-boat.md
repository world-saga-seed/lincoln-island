# The Nautilus’s boat: the bolted-iron boat moored in the cave, in which the colonists reach the Nautilus

*A boat of bolted sheet iron, with two oars and a rudder, found floating at the cave’s mouth in the night of 15 October and moored along the Nautilus’s flank until the colonists leave.*

Keywords: canot, boat, tôle boulonnée, sheet iron, aviron, oars, gouvernail, Nautilus, cave, bateau de fer

- **What it is.**
  - Smith sees a black object floating on the sea at the cave’s mouth and draws it to him (III.15 ¶111). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un objet noir qui flottait à la surface de la mer
  - It is a boat, moored by a rope to some projection inside the wall of the cave (III.15 ¶112). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: C’était un canot, amarré par une corde à quelque saillie intérieure de la paroi
  - It is made of bolted sheet iron, and two oars lie in the bottom under the benches (III.15 ¶112). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ce canot était fait en tôle boulonnée
    - quote: Deux avirons étaient au fond, sous les bancs
  - It has a rudder, and carries the six colonists, two at the oars, Pencroff at the helm and Smith in the bow with the lantern on the stem (III.15 ¶114). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Pencroff au gouvernail
    - quote: le fanal posé sur l’étrave
  - It comes alongside the left haunch of the Nautilus (III.15 ¶139). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Le canot accosta la hanche gauche
  - Nemo calls it "the boat that brought you" and bids the colonists leave in it (III.17 ¶32). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le canot qui vous a amenés
  - It lies moored along the flank of the submarine, and is taken astern when the colonists leave (III.17 ¶88, ¶89). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: qui était amarré au flanc du bateau sous-marin
    - quote: Ce canot fut conduit à l’arrière
- **Appears in:** III.15, III.17
- **Open:**
  - The text does not say whose boat it is or how it came to float at the cave’s mouth; Nemo’s words in III.17 ¶32 treat it as the one that brought them. Whether it is the Nautilus’s canot of III.16 ¶58 is not said (see [the Nautilus](nautilus.md)). (III.17 ¶32). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le canot qui vous a amenés
