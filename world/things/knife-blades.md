# The two knife blades: the first tool, made from the dog's collar

*Top's steel collar broken in two, ground on sandstone and fitted to handles: the colony's first tool.*

Keywords: knives, couteaux, cutting edge, blade, tool, steel, hilt, whetstone

- **What it is.**
  - They are two cutting blades with solid handles (I.13 ¶27). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: deux lames tranchantes qu’il avait été facile d’emmancher dans une poignée solide
  - They are the colony's only tools at that point (I.13 ¶27). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: l’outillage de la colonie se composait de deux lames tranchantes
  - They were hailed as the first tool, a precious conquest (I.13 ¶28). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: La conquête de ce premier outil fut saluée comme un triomphe
  - They are the two steel blades from Top’s collar; they are all Smith has to work with when sewing the seal skins into the bellows (I.15 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les deux lames d’acier provenant du collier de Top
- **Made from.**
  - Top's collar was a thin strip of tempered steel (I.13 ¶27). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Le collier de Top était fait d’une mince lame d’acier trempé
  - Smith took it off the dog and broke it in two parts (I.13 ¶25). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il le rompit en deux parties
  - Each part was ground first on [sandstone](sandstone.md) to put the edge angle on the quick (I.13 ¶27). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: l’affûter d’abord sur une pierre de grès, de manière à mettre au vif l’angle du tranchant
  - The burr was then taken off on a finer sandstone (I.13 ¶27). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: enlever le morfil sur un grès plus fin
  - It took about two hours (I.13 ¶27). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: deux heures après
- **Leads to.**
  - Pencroff said that with a knife he would soon make a bow and arrows (see [the bow](bow.md) and [the arrows](arrows.md)) (I.13 ¶20). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: j’aurais vite fait de fabriquer un arc et des flèches
  - Smith had said that the colonists did not even have the tools needed to make tools (I.13 ¶3). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ils ne possédaient même pas les outils nécessaires à faire les outils
- **In XIX bis.** *(The inserted chapter, 01950; R1121-132.)*
  - Pencroff carries one of the two blades on 2 June; he saws blind with it at the cord under the float, and digs Herbert's foot free of the sand with it (XIX bis ¶22, 31, 61). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: the sailor had his knife, one of the two blades made from Top's collar
    - quote: sawed at it blindly a long while
    - quote: Then Pencroff dug with the blade of his knife
- **Appears in:** I.13, I.15, XIX bis
- **Open:**
  - The text does not say that these blades cut the bow branches; I.13 ¶30 says only that the branches were cut, stripped and trimmed.
    - quote: furent coupées, effeuillées, taillées
