# The first plateau: the broad ledge of Mont Franklin's lower cone where the party camped

*The truncated top of the lower cone of Mont Franklin, a circular shelf on a base of rock that carries the upper cone; scant fuel, a cold night; the shelf closes against the upper cone on the north.*

Keywords: plateau, camp, campement, premier plateau, ledge, shelf, basalte, truncation, étage

- **What it is.**
  - It is formed by the truncation of the lower cone (I.10 ¶43). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: du premier plateau, formé par la troncature du cône inférieur
  - It stands on a base of rocks, among which a retreat was easily found (I.10 ¶45). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ce second étage de la montagne s’élevait sur une base de roches, au milieu desquelles on trouva facilement une retraite
  - Fuel is not abundant: mosses and dry brush bristle on parts of it (I.10 ¶45). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Le combustible n’était pas abondant
  - The night there is a little cold (I.10 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la température un peu froide de la nuit
  - It is a wide circular footing that carries the upper cone (I.10 ¶47). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: cette large assise circulaire qui supportait le cône supérieur
  - Going north round its circular edge the path is sometimes wide and sometimes a narrow track, blocked by rubble, where two could not walk abreast (I.10 ¶49). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une étroite sente, sur laquelle deux personnes ne pouvaient marcher de front
  - After twenty minutes' walk north the slopes of the two cones meet flush: there is no shoulder between the two parts of the mountain, and going round on slopes of nearly seventy degrees became impracticable (I.10 ¶49). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le talus des deux cônes affleurait. Plus d’épaulement qui séparât les deux parties de la montagne
  - It is a hole in the basalt, where the night passed quietly (I.11 ¶1). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dans ce trou de basalte
  - The shoulder it forms allows the upper cone to be rounded as far as the mouth of the great breach (I.11 ¶5). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: On contourna le cône par le plateau qui formait épaulement, jusqu’à la gueule de l’énorme crevasse
- **Appears in:** I.10, I.11, I.12
- **Open:**
  - Its altitude is carded neither way: see the Open lines of [Mont Franklin](mont-franklin.md).
