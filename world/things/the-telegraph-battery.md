# The telegraph battery: Smith’s zinc, nitric acid and potash battery

*A battery of glass flasks of nitric acid, each with a clay-plugged tube of potash solution, with two zinc plates, made by Smith after the pattern of Becquerel’s; the zinc is the lining of the chest.*

Keywords: pile, battery, acide azotique, potasse, zinc, flacons, tube, argile, courant constant, Becquerel, pôle

- **What it is.**
  - Smith needs a constant-current battery (II.18 ¶40). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: d’obtenir une pile à courant constant
  - Modern batteries are generally made of retort carbon, zinc and copper; copper is absolutely lacking, Smith having found no trace of it on the island despite his searches (see [the copper](copper.md)) (II.18 ¶40). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: de charbon de cornue, de zinc et de cuivre
    - quote: Le cuivre manquait absolument à l’ingénieur, qui, malgré ses recherches, n’en avait pas trouvé trace dans l’île Lincoln
  - Retort carbon could have been produced, but only with special apparatus, a great labour (II.18 ¶40). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il eût fallu installer des appareils spéciaux, ce qui aurait été une grosse besogne
  - The zinc is the lining of the chest found at the pointe de l’Épave, which could not be better used (see [the zinc](zinc.md); [the chest](the-chest.md)) (II.18 ¶40). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la caisse trouvée à la pointe de l’Épave était doublée d’une enveloppe de ce métal
  - After mature reflection Smith makes a very simple battery, near to the one Becquerel devised in 1820, in which zinc alone is used; the other substances, nitric acid and potash, are at his disposal (II.18 ¶41). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une pile très-simple, se rapprochant de celle que Becquerel imagina en 1820
    - quote: dans laquelle le zinc est uniquement employé
    - quote: acide azotique et potasse, tout cela était à sa disposition
  - Its effects are produced by the reaction of the acid and the potash on each other (see [the nitric acid](nitric-acid.md); [the potash](potash.md)) (II.18 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la réaction de l’acide et de la potasse l’un sur l’autre
  - A number of glass flasks are made and filled with nitric acid, each stoppered with a cork through which passes a glass tube closed at its lower end and meant to dip into the acid by a plug of clay held in a cloth (see [the glassware](glassware.md); [the clay](clay.md)) (II.18 ¶43). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Un certain nombre de flacons de verre furent fabriqués et remplis d’acide azotique
    - quote: un tampon d’argile maintenu par un linge
  - Into each tube is poured, at the top, a solution of potash obtained by burning various plants, so that acid and potash react on each other through the clay (II.18 ¶43). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une dissolution de potasse qu’il avait préalablement obtenue par l’incinération de diverses plantes
    - quote: l’acide et la potasse purent réagir l’un sur l’autre à travers l’argile
  - Two zinc plates are used, one dipped in the nitric acid, the other in the potash solution; a current runs from the plate in the flask to the one in the tube, the tube plate becoming the positive pole and the flask plate the negative (II.18 ¶44). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: deux lames de zinc, dont l’une fut plongée dans l’acide azotique, l’autre dans la dissolution de potasse
    - quote: la lame du tube devint le pôle positif et celle du flacon le pôle négatif
  - Each flask gives its own current, and together they suffice for all the phenomena of electric telegraphy (II.18 ¶44). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Chaque flacon produisit donc autant de courants
  - Two such batteries are made, one for Granite-house and one for the corral (see [the telegraph](the-telegraph.md)) (II.18 ¶47). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Deux piles avaient été fabriquées
- **Appears in:** II.18, III.15
