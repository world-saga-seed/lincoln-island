# The arrows: arrows tipped with porcupine quills and feathered with cockatoo plumes

*Straight knotless branches, quill-tipped and feathered, made by Pencroff; some dozens by early April.*

Keywords: flèches, quiver, missile, quills, feathers, fletching, hunting

- **What it is.**
  - Pencroff had some dozens of them, with very sharp points (I.13 ¶36). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: quelques douzaines de flèches armées de pointes très-acérées
- **Made from.**
  - The shafts are straight, rigid branches without knots (I.13 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des branches droites et rigides, sans nodosités
  - The tips must be some substance to replace iron, which is hard to find (I.13 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une substance propre à remplacer le fer
  - The points are porcupine quills, brought by Top (I.13 ¶36). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: C’était Top qui avait fourni ces pointes, en rapportant un porc-épic
  - The quills are fixed firmly to the end of the shafts (I.13 ¶36). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ces piquants furent ajustés solidement à l’extrémité des flèches
  - The flight is made by a fletching of cockatoo feathers (I.13 ¶36). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un empennage de plumes de kakatoès
- **Leads to.**
  - With the bows they supply game (see [the bows](bow.md)) (I.13 ¶36). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le gibier de poil et de plume abonda-t-il aux Cheminées
- **On the page.**
  - Pencroff trusted that chance would supply the tips (I.13 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le hasard ferait le reste
- **Appears in:** I.13
