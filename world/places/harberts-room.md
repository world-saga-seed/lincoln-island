# Harbert’s room: his room in Granite-house, with a window on the sea

*Harbert’s room in Granite-house, with his couch, a table beside the bed, a window by which his bed stands, and a neighbouring room where the others keep watch.*

Keywords: chambre, room, Harbert, couchette, lit, bed, fenêtre, window, table, Granite-house, mer

- **What it is.**
  - Harbert holds that his strength will return faster in his room, with the air and the view of the sea (III.9 ¶45). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dans sa chambre, avec l’air et la vue de la mer
  - He is laid on his couch in Granite-house on 29 November and smiles to find himself in his room (III.10 ¶3, ¶4). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Harbert était étendu sur sa couchette de Granite-house
    - quote: en se retrouvant dans sa chambre
  - A table stands beside the bed (III.10 ¶65). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la table qui était placée près du lit
  - A neighbouring room is where his companions keep watch (III.10 ¶61). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la chambre voisine, où veillaient ses compagnons
  - In January 1868 his bed is placed near one of the windows of Granite-house, and he breathes the salt air from it (III.11 ¶16). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: de son lit, placé près d’une des fenêtres de Granite-house
    - quote: il humait cet air salubre, chargé d’émanations salines
