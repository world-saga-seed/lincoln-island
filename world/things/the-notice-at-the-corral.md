# The notice at the corral: the paper on the telegraph table, "Follow the new wire"

*A paper left on the table of the corral house, in English, with four words that send the colonists after the new wire.*

Keywords: notice, papier, paper, message, English, Suivez le nouveau fil, table, corral house, Ayrton

- **What it is.**
  - A paper lies on the table, which is set aside for the wire service, and Harbert points it out (III.15 ¶63, ¶68). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la table qui était spécialement affectée au service du fil
    - quote: un papier déposé sur la table
  - It is written in English (III.15 ¶69). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ces mots, en anglais
  - It reads "Suivez le nouveau fil." (Follow the new wire) (III.15 ¶70). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Suivez le nouveau fil.
  - Ayrton, the last colonist to come to the corral before that night, was there four days earlier (III.15 ¶64, ¶65, ¶67). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Qui est venu pour la dernière fois ici
    - quote: Il y a quatre jours
- **Appears in:** III.15
- **Open:**
