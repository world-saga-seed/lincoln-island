# Nab’s warning: the note carried to the corral by Jup

*A note in Nab’s hand, "Vendredi, 6 h. matin. Plateau envahi par les convicts ! Nab.", hung in a small bag at Jup’s neck and brought over the corral palisade on 29 November.*

Keywords: billet, note, message, Nab, Jup, petit sac, bag, convicts, plateau envahi, Vendredi

- **What it is.**
  - Nab, with no way to warn his master, writes a note and ties it to Jup’s neck before day has come (III.10 ¶9, ¶12, ¶13). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Nab eut alors la pensée d’employer Jup et de lui confier un billet
    - quote: Le jour n’avait pas encore paru
    - quote: il l’attacha au cou de Jup
  - Jup brings it in a small bag hung at his neck, a note traced by Nab’s hand (III.9 ¶62). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Au cou de Jup était pendu un petit sac
    - quote: un billet tracé de la main de Nab
  - It reads "Vendredi, 6 h. matin." and "Plateau envahi par les convicts !" (III.9 ¶64, ¶65). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Vendredi, 6 h. matin.
    - quote: Plateau envahi par les convicts !
  - It is signed "Nab." (III.9 ln2). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: « Nab. »
  - It was evidently written and sent as soon as the convicts showed themselves on the plateau (III.9 ¶84). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Le billet de Nab avait évidemment été écrit et envoyé dès que les convicts s’y étaient montrés
- **Appears in:** III.9, III.10
- **Open:**
  - The note is dated six o'clock in the morning (III.9 ¶84), Jup reaches the corral at seven (III.9 ¶48), and he is said to have taken barely three-quarters of an hour for the five miles (III.9 ¶84); the text does not say how the other quarter of an hour passes. *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ce billet était daté de six heures du matin
    - quote: Il était sept heures du matin
    - quote: mis à peine trois quarts d’heure à franchir les cinq milles
