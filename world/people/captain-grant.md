# Captain Grant: master of the Britannia, found on Tabor

*The captain of the Britannia, wrecked in the Pacific, whose document in a bottle sent the Duncan after him and who left Ayrton ashore.*

Keywords: Grant, capitaine, Britannia, naufragé, Tabor, Duncan, document, Mary, Robert

- **What it is.**
  - He is the captain of the three-master Britannia, which was lost with all hands (as it was thought) (II.17 ¶42, ¶50). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dont le navire le Britannia avait péri corps et biens
    - quote: un trois-mâts anglais, le Britannia
  - Mary and Robert Grant, a girl and a boy, are his children (II.17 ¶42, ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ces deux derniers étaient les enfants du capitaine Grant
    - quote: Mary et Robert Grant
  - The document found in the bottle said that three men survived the Britannia's wreck, Captain Grant and two of his men, who had taken refuge on a land whose latitude it gave (37° 11′ south) but whose longitude the sea water had effaced (II.17 ¶44, ¶45). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ces survivants étaient le capitaine Grant et deux de ses hommes
    - quote: dont la longitude, effacée par l’eau de mer, n’était plus lisible
    - quote: Cette latitude était celle de 37° 11′ australe
  - After quarrels with his boatswain Ayrton, who tried to lead the crew into mutiny, he put him ashore on the west coast of Australia on 8 April 1852 and sailed away leaving him (II.17 ¶59). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le capitaine Grant l’avait débarqué, le 8 avril 1852, sur la côte ouest de l’Australie
  - Ayrton believed his captain had perished with all the crew (II.17 ¶55). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il avait cru jusqu’alors que son capitaine avait péri avec tout l’équipage
  - He and his two men are found, "par un vrai miracle", on Tabor, exactly on the thirty-seventh parallel (II.17 ¶76). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: par un vrai miracle, on retrouva le capitaine Grant et ses deux hommes, précisément sur ce trente-septième parallèle
  - Glenarvan says Captain Grant was lost and unknown on Tabor, unlike Ayrton, who will be neither (II.17 ¶77). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ni perdu, ni ignoré comme le fut le capitaine Grant
  - He is called "l’honnête capitaine Grant", and the house on Tabor is said to have been built by him; the colonists later speak of it as the cabin that was his and Ayrton's (II.17 ¶80; II.19 ¶22). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la maison construite par l’honnête capitaine Grant
    - quote: cette cabane qui fut la demeure du capitaine Grant et d’Ayrton
  - His son Robert, by 1869, commands the Duncan (III.20 ¶26). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le fils du capitaine Grant
- **Appears in:** II.17, II.19, III.20
- **Open:**
  - Who built the house on Tabor: Ayrton's tale says "la maison construite par l’honnête capitaine Grant" (II.17 ¶80); the colonists, on first seeing the planks, read the house as the work of one castaway from the wreck (II.14 ¶22) (II.17 ¶80; II.14 ¶22). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la maison construite par l’honnête capitaine Grant
    - quote: au moyen des débris du navire, cet homme, ayant des outils à sa disposition, avait construit cette demeure
