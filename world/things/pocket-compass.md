# The pocket compass: the small compass of the chest

*The chest’s pocket compass, with which Smith steers the whole exploration of 30 and 31 October.*

Keywords: boussole de poche, compass, boussole, direction, instrument, chest, Smith

- **What it is.**
  - There is one pocket compass in the chest (II.2 ¶53). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: 1 boussole de poche
  - Like the other instruments it carries no maker’s mark and seems never to have been used (II.2 ¶64). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ne portaient la marque du fabricant
  - Smith reads the river’s direction by it, finding it south-west to north-east over about three miles from the first bend (II.3 ¶21). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: en consultant sa boussole de poche
    - quote: sensiblement sud-ouest et nord-est
  - Smith steers the cutting of the track with it, compass in hand (II.4 ¶4). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: sa boussole à la main, Cyrus Smith indiqua la route à suivre
  - Spilett never ventures into the dense masses of the Far-West without taking the pocket compass, since the sun hardly pierces the boughs (II.10 ¶19). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: le reporter ne s’y hasardait jamais sans emporter la boussole de poche
    - quote: le soleil perçait à peine les épaisses ramures
- **Appears in:** II.2, II.3, II.4, II.10
