# The Pacific Ocean: the ocean the storm is thought to have carried the balloon across

*The ocean in which Smith presumes the island lies, reached after the balloon crossed Mexico's narrow part.*

Keywords: Pacifique, ocean, sea, océan, Mexico, crossing, presumption

- **What it is.**
  - The narrator names the land the gale carried the prisoners to from Richmond as a land of the Pacific (I.7 ¶32). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: jusqu’à cette terre du Pacifique
  - Smith presumes the hurricane threw them on a land of the Pacific (I.9 ¶70). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les présomptions sont pour une terre du Pacifique
  - On his reckoning the north-east wind carried the balloon across the Carolinas, Georgia, the Gulf of Mexico and Mexico at its narrow part, then a portion of the Pacific Ocean (I.9 ¶70). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: puis une portion de l’océan Pacifique
  - The island seems a monstrous animal asleep on its surface (I.11 ¶20). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: endormi à la surface du Pacifique
  - Smith feared the island lay too far south for the ships that frequent its archipelagos (I.11 ¶43). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: trop au sud pour les navires qui fréquentent les archipels du Pacifique
  - Smith keeps a very clear memory of its southern part but has no chart (I.14 ¶54). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ma mémoire a conservé un souvenir très-net de sa portion méridionale
  - Smith recalls no island at the island's position in that part of it (I.14 ¶80). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dans cette partie du Pacifique
  - The island lies in the open Pacific, exposed to every storm of the sea, where bad weather must be frequent and probably terrible (I.16 ¶2). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les mauvais temps devaient être fréquents, et probablement terribles
  - Smith says that this part of the Pacific is often visited by Malay pirates, bold seamen and redoubtable malefactors (I.16 ¶7, ¶9). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: souvent fréquentée par les pirates malais
    - quote: hardis marins aussi bien que des malfaiteurs redoutables
