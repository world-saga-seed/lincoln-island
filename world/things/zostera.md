# The zostera: the sea grass that stuffs the mattresses and feeds the turtles

*A sea grass (zostère) that fills the colonists’ mattresses and on which the green turtles feed.*

Keywords: zostère, zostera, sea grass, mattress, matelas, turtle, tortue, bedding

- **What it is.**
  - The bed frames of Granite-house have mattresses of zostera (I.20 ¶28). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des cadres de lit, dont toute la literie consista en matelas de zostère
  - The green turtles feed on it, which makes their flesh extremely savoury (II.1 ¶70). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ces tortues, qui se nourrissent de zostères, est extrêmement savoureuse
  - Dry zostera serves as oakum for the Bonadventure: driven with a mallet between the planks of hull, ceiling and deck (see [the Bonadventure](bonadventure.md)) (II.12 ¶82). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: on fit de l’étoupe avec du zostère sec, qui fut introduit à coups de maillet entre les bordages de la coque, du vaigrage et du pont
- **Appears in:** I.20, II.1, II.12
