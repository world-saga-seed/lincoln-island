# The fulling mill: the water-driven stamper for the wool

*A rudimentary mill of cams and vertical stampers worked by the fall on the beach, built by Smith.*

Keywords: moulin à foulon, fulling mill, foulage, pilons, cames, chute d’eau, waterfall, wool

- **What it is.**
  - Smith builds it to full the wool, using the mechanical force that the waterfall of the beach had, unused until then, to drive a fulling mill (II.11 ¶6). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la force mécanique, inutilisée jusqu’alors, que possédait la chute d’eau de la grève, pour mouvoir un moulin à foulon
  - It is quite rudimentary: a shaft fitted with cams that lift and drop vertical stampers in turn, troughs to receive the wool, and a strong timber frame holding and binding the whole (II.11 ¶7). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Un arbre, muni de cames qui soulevaient et laissaient retomber tour à tour des pilons verticaux
    - quote: des auges destinées à recevoir la laine
    - quote: un fort bâtis en charpente contenant et reliant tout le système
  - The wool, first soaked in a soapy solution, comes out of it as a thick sheet of felt, "feutre lincolnien" (see [the felt](felt.md)) (II.11 ¶8). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: sortit du moulin sous forme d’une épaisse nappe de feutre
    - quote: C’était du « feutre lincolnien »
  - Its fall is spoken of as already taken: Smith could have used "la seconde chute qui s’épanchait sur la Mercy" for his new mill, "la première étant déjà occupée à mouvoir les pilons du moulin à foulon"; the first fall is read as the beach fall of II.11 ¶6 (II.16 ¶82). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: la première étant déjà occupée à mouvoir les pilons du moulin à foulon
  - It stands near the great fall on the beach, which Smith uses for its motive force (II.18 ¶36, ¶37). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la grande chute, dont l’ingénieur allait encore utiliser la force motrice
    - quote: là était le moulin à foulon
  - In February 1867 it is not running, but its shaft, driven with extreme power, can serve to draw the wire by winding it round itself (II.18 ¶37). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: qui ne fonctionnait pas alors
    - quote: pouvait servir à étirer le fil, en l’enroulant autour de lui
- **Appears in:** II.11, II.16, II.18
- **Open:**
  - Which falls are "la première" and "la seconde chute qui s’épanchait sur la Mercy" of II.16 ¶82 is not said.
