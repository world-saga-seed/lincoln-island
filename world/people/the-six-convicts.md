# The six convicts: the Speedy's survivors landed on the right bank

*Six men of the second boat, wrecked at the Mercy's mouth, who reach land armed while the brig is sunk with the rest.*

Keywords: six convicts, survivors, Speedy, pointe de l’Épave, Mercy, right bank, rive droite, évadés, armés

- **What it is.**
  - They are six survivors, out of eight men in the Speedy's second boat, which broke on the reefs at the Mercy's mouth after two of its men were mortally struck (III.3 ¶47). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Deux, sur huit, des hommes qui le montaient, furent mortellement frappés
    - quote: Mais les six survivants
  - They hold their arms above their heads to keep them dry and wade to the right bank of the river, then run towards the pointe de l’Épave, out of range (III.3 ¶47). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: élevant leurs armes au-dessus de leur tête pour les préserver du contact de l’eau
    - quote: parvinrent à prendre pied sur la rive droite de la rivière
    - quote: dans la direction de la pointe de l’Épave
  - They cannot reach Granite-house, for they cannot cross the river with its bridges raised (III.3 ¶48). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dans l’impossibilité d’atteindre Granite-house
    - quote: dont les ponts étaient relevés
  - They are armed (III.4 ¶15). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: car ils sont armés
  - After the brig sinks, none is seen, and they are supposed to have fled into the interior (III.4 ¶13, ¶14). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Aucun des fugitifs n’était visible
    - quote: avaient pris la fuite à l’intérieur de l’île
  - The narrator reckons them "chenapans de premier ordre", men for whom a river or a brook is no bar, and dangerous in despair (III.4 ¶72). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des chenapans de premier ordre
    - quote: n’en étaient pas à s’embarrasser d’une rivière ou d’un ruisseau
    - quote: poussés par le désespoir, de tels coquins pouvaient être redoutables
  - The colonists keep watch on the cases heaped by the Chimneys against them, and Jup and Top stand guard (III.4 ¶73, ¶74). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il fallait veiller sur les caisses et colis
    - quote: eussent vite fait de les signaler
- **Appears in:** III.3, III.4
