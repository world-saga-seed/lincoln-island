# Timeline fragment J (03200 to 03500)

The year is not stated in II.10 to II.13 until II.11 ¶9 ("l’hiver de 1866-67"); every 1866 below is my reading from it.

## 03200 (II.10)

- 1866-04, about 8 days after the trees are felled: the keel is laid (II.10 ¶6). *(Drafted.)* The date of the felling is not given.
  - quote: Huit jours après, dans le renfoncement qui existait entre les Cheminées et la muraille, un chantier était préparé
- Six months before the fine season: boat to be built by Smith and Pencroff alone (II.10 ¶5). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: le retour de la belle saison ne s’effectuerait pas avant six mois
- 1866-04-15: the second wheat harvest, five bushels (II.10 ¶9). *(Drafted, year My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: la deuxième récolte de blé, qui se fit le 15 avril
- 1866-04, last fortnight: three koalas killed (II.10 ¶19). *(Drafted, year My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: pendant cette dernière quinzaine d’avril
- 1866-04-30: Spilett finds tobacco in the south-west of the Far-West (II.10 ¶21). *(Drafted, year My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: C’était le 30 avril.
- 1866-05-01: Pencroff's work interrupted by the whale; the whale seen "depuis quelques jours" (II.10 ¶34–¶35). *(Drafted, year My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: interrompue le 1er mai, par une aventure de pêche
- 1866-05-03: the whale is stranded at Wreck Point, dead, with the Maria-Stella harpoon (II.10 ¶47). *(Drafted, year My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: le 3 mai, des cris de Nab
- 1866-05-03 to 1866-05-05, about: the cutting up lasts three days (II.10 ¶66). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: qui dura trois jours
- 1866-05, end: the boat is half planked (II.10 ¶81). *(Drafted, year My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: vers la fin du mois, il était à demi bordé
- 1866-05-31: Pencroff gets his pipe (II.10 ¶82). *(Drafted, year My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: le 31 mai, il devait éprouver une des plus grandes joies de sa vie
- Discord, not reconciled: the tobacco is found on 30 April and given on 31 May, but the preparation is said to take two months (II.10 ¶33).
  - quote: Cela demanda deux mois

## 03300 (II.11)

- 1866-06: winter begins with June (II.11 ¶1). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: l’hiver arrivait avec ce mois de juin
- 1866-06-20, about: great cold; the boat work stops (II.11 ¶10). *(Drafted, year My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: vers le 20 juin
- 1866-06, end: first snow (II.11 ¶27). *(Drafted, year My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: Les premières neiges tombèrent vers la fin du mois de juin.
- 1866-06-30: the albatross is captured and released with the message (II.11 ¶32). *(Drafted, year My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: le 30 juin, capture fut faite
- 1866-07: intense cold; second fireplace in the great hall (II.11 ¶40). *(Drafted, year My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: Pendant le mois de juillet, les froids furent intenses
- 1866-07, rest of the month: rain, cold, storms; the dog and the orang at the well (II.11 ¶66, ¶74). *(Drafted, year My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: Pendant le reste du mois de juillet, il y eut des alternatives de pluie et de froid.
- 1866-08, first week: calm, sharp cold (II.11 ¶77). *(Drafted, year My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: Pendant la première semaine du mois d’août
- 1866-08-03: the Tadorne Marsh hunt and the engineer's exploration of the well (II.11 ¶78–¶90). *(Drafted, year My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: Le 3 août, une excursion, projetée depuis quelques jours
- Discord, not reconciled: Tabor "cent cinquante milles" (II.11 ¶11) against "douze cents milles" to any land (II.11 ¶31); not a date, listed for the clock of the voyage.
  - quote: qui séparait l’île de toute terre et qui était de douze cents milles

## 03400 (II.12)

- 1866-08-03, evening: the hunters return (II.12 ¶1). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: Le soir même, les chasseurs revinrent
- 1866-08-04: Spilett helps Nab; Smith had told him of the well "la veille" (II.12 ¶10). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: l’exploration qu’il avait faite la veille
- 1866-08-04 to about 1866-08-10: a week more of cold; the sails are finished (II.12 ¶11–¶12). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: Les froids persévérèrent pendant une semaine encore
- 1866-08-11, night, about 4 a.m. (so 1866-08-12 by the calendar): the culpeux attack (II.12 ¶15–¶16). *(Drafted, year My reading, to confirm.)*

- 1866-10-10: the launch; Bonadventure named (II.12 ¶87–¶88). *(Drafted, year My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: Le 10 octobre, le bateau fut lancé à la mer.
- 1866-10-10, 10:30 a.m.: the boat sails (II.12 ¶94). *(Drafted, year My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: À dix heures et demie, tout le monde était à bord
- 1866-10-10, afternoon: the bottle is picked up (II.12 ¶122–¶131). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: Une bouteille !
- Discord, not reconciled: Jup took to bed on 11 August by ¶62's count, but the fight ended at dawn after the night of 11 August.
  - quote: dans la nuit du 11 août
