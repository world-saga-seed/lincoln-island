# The new fall: the stream opened from the lake’s east bank, falling three hundred feet to the beach

*The cascade made on 21 May by blasting the granite rim of the lake: a rapid stream across the plateau and over its edge, carrying at least triple the old outlet’s flow.*

Keywords: chute, nouvelle chute, cascade, waterfall, saignée, stream, déversoir, power, mechanical power, plateau, grève
