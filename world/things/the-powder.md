# The powder: two barrels of fifty pounds in all

*Two barrels of gunpowder from the chest, fifty pounds in all, of which a stock is taken on the exploration; Smith counts on making an explosive to spare it.*

Keywords: poudre, powder, barils, barrels, pounds, livres, explosive, gunpowder, chest

- **What it is.**
  - There are two barrels of powder, each able to hold twenty-five pounds (II.2 ¶53). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: 2 barils de poudre pouvant contenir chacun vingt-cinq livres
  - The barrels hold about fifty pounds, and a certain stock of it has to be taken on the exploration (II.3 ¶6). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dont les barils renfermaient environ cinquante livres
    - quote: il fallut bien en emporter une certaine provision
  - Smith counts on making an explosive substance that will allow the powder to be spared (II.3 ¶6). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: l’ingénieur comptait fabriquer une substance explosive qui permettrait de la ménager
- **Appears in:** II.2, II.3
