# 09 Refine

*Basic.*

**When:** for each batch as it lands, while the next batch drafts; then once per volume.

**Uses:** **[workflows/drafting/](../workflows/drafting/README.md)**, steps 2 to 8 of the chain (Basic) and 9 to 13 (Refinements): [mechanical](../workflows/drafting/mechanical.md), [critics](../workflows/drafting/critics.md) and [their prompts](../workflows/drafting/prompts/), [verify](../workflows/drafting/verify.md), [reviser](../workflows/drafting/reviser.md), the [invention check](../workflows/drafting/principles.md#the-invention-check), [draft-cards](../workflows/draft-cards.md), the [register pass](../workflows/drafting/register-pass.md) and landing check, the [fold-back](../workflows/drafting/fold-back.md).

**What happens:** the chain, cheapest checks first ([the chain](../workflows/drafting/README.md#the-chain-in-order)).

**Outputs:** refined chapters; batch papers; Next pass lists. Example: [the worked run](../example/reports/verified-00400.md).

**Done when:** every verified finding has a disposition; every keep line resolves; every settled beat has a landing or is with the writer.
