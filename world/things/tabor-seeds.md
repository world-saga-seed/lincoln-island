# The Tabor seeds: the vegetable seed taken from Tabor to Lincoln

*Seed of the potatoes, chicory, sorrel, carrots, cabbages and turnips of the Tabor plots, gathered by Harbert and sown on the Grande-Vue plateau.*

Keywords: graines, seeds, légumes, vegetables, récolte, Harbert, Tabor, semées, sown

- **What it is.**
  - Harbert, seeing the vegetables of the Tabor plots, notes that it is enough to gather their seed to enrich the soil of Lincoln Island (II.13 ¶80). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dont il suffisait de recueillir la graine pour enrichir le sol de l’île Lincoln
  - He says the seeds will give all the vegetables of the old and the new continent (II.14 ¶37). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: qui nous donneront tous les légumes de l’ancien et du nouveau continent
  - Pencroff leaves the gathering to Harbert, who knows the seeds better than the others (II.14 ¶41). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: que vous connaissez mieux que nous
  - Harbert and Spilett bring back a harvest of vegetable seeds aboard the Bonadventure (II.14 ¶80). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une récolte de graines potagères
  - The seeds are carefully sown on Lincoln, and the Grande-Vue plateau becomes a vast kitchen garden (see [the kitchen garden](../places/the-kitchen-garden.md)) (II.16 ¶79). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les graines récoltées par Harbert à l’île Tabor avaient été soigneusement semées
- **Appears in:** II.13, II.14, II.16
