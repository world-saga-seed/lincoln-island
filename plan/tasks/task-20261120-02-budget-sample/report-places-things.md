# Report: places and things, I.1, I.13, III.16

Source: texts/fr-1875 (01.md, 13.md, 58.md). No git commands run. The READMEs in world/places and world/things were not touched.

## Cards written (49)

- [Granite-house](../../../world/places/granite-house.md): Named only in the last chapter: the colonists' home, with a well inside it that Captain Nemo climbed from below.
- [The Chimneys](../../../world/places/the-chimneys.md): The first lodging of the five colonists: corridors floored with sand, a central chamber, a hearth.
- [The Mercy](../../../world/places/mercy-river.md): A river the colonists follow inland; its left bank bears the Jacamar wood, and it was deep enough to be hard to cross.
- [The Grande-Vue plateau](../../../world/places/grande-vue-plateau.md): A plateau crossed on the way to the clearing; its edge, about fifty feet above the south-side heights, gave Smith a clear sea horizon.
- [Lake Grant](../../../world/places/lake-grant.md): A lake whose west shore has the clay and whose north side has the limestone; the clearing is 200 paces from it.
- [The clearing](../../../world/places/clearing-brickworks.md): A clearing on the clay ground where the bricks were made and fired and the pottery kiln built; Smith set the workshop at the place of production.
- [Lincoln Island](../../../world/places/lincoln-island.md): The island: first seen as a high land thirty miles off to leeward, then a shore of sand; named in I.13 and III.16, not in I.1.
- [Cape Griffe](../../../world/places/cap-griffe.md): A cape near the shore where the colonists stand; it hides the southern horizon, and the heights of the right bank end at it.
- [The wood of the Jacamar](../../../world/places/jacamar-wood.md): The part of the forest on the Mercy's left bank, where most of the game was killed.
- [The Red Creek](../../../world/places/red-creek.md): A stream; a coal seam lies in the open near its mouth.
- [The Nautilus's port](../../../world/places/nautilus-port.md): A sea-port under the island, entered by a crypt; a basalt rise closed it to the Nautilus.
- [Tabor Island](../../../world/places/tabor-island.md): Named once: the island where Ayrton was found through a document in a bottle.
- [Bundelkund](../../../world/places/bundelkund.md): An Indian territory, independent in the prince's youth, with mountains to which he returned before vanishing.
- [The Maelstrom](../../../world/places/maelstrom.md): The whirlpool where the Nautilus was caught and from which the three prisoners fled in its boat.
- [The coral cemetery](../../../world/places/coral-cemetery.md): Where the dead companions of Nemo rest, on the floor of the Pacific.
- [The saloon](../../../world/places/nautilus-saloon.md): A magnificent saloon whose air is charged with electric effluences, where Nemo lies on a divan.
- [The balloon](../../../world/things/balloon.md): A gas balloon of 50,000 cubic feet, torn and losing gas, that falls on the shore and is then blown away.
- [The basket](../../../world/things/nacelle.md): A wicker box that cannot float, cut loose to lighten the balloon.
- [The sack of gold](../../../world/things/gold-sack.md): The last ballast but the basket: a heavy sack with ten thousand francs of gold.
- [The Nautilus](../../../world/things/nautilus.md): An electric submarine built to a prince's plans on a desert Pacific island; its crew is dead and it lies in a port it can no longer leave.
- [The diving suit](../../../world/things/diving-suit.md): The suit in which Nemo walked beneath the sea off the island and climbed the well of Granite-house.
- [The electric bullets](../../../world/things/electric-bullets.md): Electric bullets whose secret Nemo kept, used in his underwater hunts, and which struck the convicts.
- [The torpedo](../../../world/things/torpedo.md): A torpedo laid on the bottom of the channel, which blew up the brig by shock.
- [The corral wire](../../../world/things/corral-wire.md): The wire Nemo laid from the corral to the Nautilus, used to call the colonists to him.
- [The Vigo treasure](../../../world/things/vigo-treasure.md): Millions lost by Spanish galleons in Vigo Bay in 1702, an inexhaustible store from which Nemo gave to peoples fighting for independence.
- [The two knife blades](../../../world/things/knife-blades.md): Top's steel collar broken in two, ground on sandstone and fitted to handles: the colony's first tool.
- [The sandstone](../../../world/things/sandstone.md): Sandstone in two grains, used to sharpen the steel strip.
- [The crejimba](../../../world/things/crejimba.md): A palm found by Harbert with no edible fruit; South American Indians make bows of its branches.
- [The hibiscus heterophyllus](../../../world/things/hibiscus.md): A mallow giving fibres as tough as animal sinews, used for bowstrings.
- [The bows](../../../world/things/bow.md): Bows of fair power made by Pencroff; the reporter and Harbert became good archers.
- [The arrows](../../../world/things/arrows.md): Straight knotless branches, quill-tipped and feathered, made by Pencroff; some dozens by early April.
- [The clay](../../../world/things/clay.md): Potter's clay found on the lake's west shore, from which come the bricks, the kiln, the pottery.
- [The bricks](../../../world/things/bricks.md): Three thousand bricks made in two days by five men, dried, then fired in a stack.
- [The brick kiln](../../../world/things/brick-kiln.md): A cube of dry bricks around wood fascines, with vents, which fired itself in 48 hours.
- [The quicklime](../../../world/things/lime.md): Quicklime from limestone, carried on a hurdle, burnt by heat: a very fat lime as pure as from chalk or marble.
- [The hurdle](../../../world/things/hurdle.md): A hurdle of interlaced branches on which Nab and Pencroff carried limestone.
- [The mortar](../../../world/things/mortar.md): Excellent mortar from lime mixed with sand.
- [The pottery kiln](../../../world/things/pottery-kiln.md): A kiln of bricks begun about 9 April, charged with coal five days later; it fired the pottery and went out on 15 April.
- [The coal](../../../world/things/coal.md): A coal seam open to the sky near the Red Creek mouth, found by Smith, used to charge the pottery kiln.
- [The pottery](../../../world/things/pottery.md): Common but clean pottery made of clay with a little lime and quartz, shaped by hand or on pebbles, fired at a high temperature.
- [The clay pipes](../../../world/things/clay-pipes.md): Some rough pipes Pencroff made to test the clay; he found them charming, but he had no tobacco.
- [The armoise](../../../world/things/artemisia.md): A downy plant of the armoise genus, found on 15 April; dried, it is the colonists' tinder.
- [The tinder](../../../world/things/tinder.md): A very inflammable substance from dried armoise, later soaked in potassium nitrate; the colonists' amadou.
- [The saltpetre](../../../world/things/saltpetre.md): Nitrate of potash, of which the island holds several layers; it makes the tinder more inflammable.
- [The caladium macrorhizum](../../../world/things/caladium.md): An arum-family herb whose boiled tubers are nutritious and partly replace bread.
- [The smoked cabiai ham](../../../world/things/smoked-ham.md): Cabiai hams smoked over a green-wood fire and flavoured with fragrant leaves.
- [The meridian line](../../../world/things/meridian-line.md): A fixed line of two trees lined up with the sun at noon, fixed on 2 April to give the north.
- [The wooden compass](../../../world/things/wooden-compass.md): Two flat rulers hinged by an acacia thorn, fixed with thorns on a third board, to measure the angle of a star above the horizon.
- [The Southern Cross](../../../world/things/southern-cross.md): The Southern Cross, seen inverted from the plateau on 15 April, with alpha 27 degrees from the pole.

## The tool chain, in the order the chapters give it (all I.13)

1. Top's collar, a thin strip of tempered steel, broken in two (¶25, ¶27) and ground on [sandstone](../../../world/things/sandstone.md), then on a finer stone, and handled: [the two knife blades](../../../world/things/knife-blades.md), the first tool, within two hours (¶27).
   - quote: deux heures après
2. [Crejimba](../../../world/things/crejimba.md) branches and [hibiscus](../../../world/things/hibiscus.md) fibre give [the bows](../../../world/things/bow.md) (¶30).
   - quote: Pencroff obtint ainsi des arcs d’une assez grande puissance
3. Straight branches, porcupine quills and cockatoo feathers give [the arrows](../../../world/things/arrows.md) (¶30, ¶36).
   - quote: un empennage de plumes de kakatoès
4. [Clay](../../../world/things/clay.md), degreased with sand, hand-moulded, dried three or four days: [the bricks](../../../world/things/bricks.md), 3,000 in two days (¶31, ¶32).
   - quote: dégraisser cette figuline avec du sable
5. Dried bricks around wood fascines, fired from 6 April: [the brick kiln](../../../world/things/brick-kiln.md), a stack that fires itself (¶42, ¶44).
   - quote: L’opération dura quarante-huit heures
6. Limestone carried on [a hurdle](../../../world/things/hurdle.md), decomposed by heat: [the quicklime](../../../world/things/lime.md) (¶44).
   - quote: donnèrent une chaux vive
7. Lime with sand: [the mortar](../../../world/things/mortar.md) (¶44).
   - quote: cette chaux fournit un mortier excellent
8. Bricks and, by 9 April, lime ready: [the pottery kiln](../../../world/things/pottery-kiln.md), charged with [coal](../../../world/things/coal.md) five days later, chimney about twenty feet (¶45, ¶46). Whether the mortar went into it is not stated.
   - quote: Cinq jours après, le four fut chargé de cette houille
9. Clay with a little lime and quartz, fired hot in the kiln: [the pottery](../../../world/things/pottery.md), then [the clay pipes](../../../world/things/clay-pipes.md) (¶47, ¶48), then the first pot-au-feu (¶57).
   - quote: La matière première était cette argile même du sol
10. Apart from the chain: [the armoise](../../../world/things/artemisia.md), dried, gives [the tinder](../../../world/things/tinder.md), later impregnated with [saltpetre](../../../world/things/saltpetre.md) (¶51 to ¶56); [the meridian line](../../../world/things/meridian-line.md) of two trees (¶35); [the wooden compass](../../../world/things/wooden-compass.md) of two rulers and an acacia thorn, for the latitude from [the Southern Cross](../../../world/things/southern-cross.md) (¶69, ¶76, ¶77).
   - quote: cette armoise, convenablement desséchée
Next step the text names but does not reach: the colonists are to become smiths when Smith chooses; iron and steel are still ore (¶3, ¶50).
   - quote: Quand il conviendrait à Cyrus Smith de les changer en forgerons, ils seraient forgerons

## Disagreements (card neither version)

     - quote: douze heures vingt-quatre minutes s’étaient écoulées
     - quote: le soleil se levait à 5 h. 33 m. du matin, et se couchait à 6 h. 17 m. du soir
- Nemo's numbers: thirty years without contact against sixteen years since the Frenchman; sixty years old when alone against six years in the island port. Held on the Nautilus card Open line, not carded. The editor himself warns.
     - quote: trente années que je n’ai plus aucune communication avec le monde habité
