# The felt clothes: the garments the colonists wear in winter

*Warm garments made of the felt of the island’s wool, which the colonists have in plenty for the third winter and with which Ayrton is also supplied.*

Keywords: vêtements de feutre, habits, hiver, laine, Ayrton, felt clothing, winter

- **What it is.**
  - The colonists have good clothes, with thick blankets, of felt for the winter of 1866-67 (see [the felt](felt.md)) (II.11 ¶9). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: de bons vêtements, d’épaisses couvertures
  - For the third winter the felt clothes do not run short, the mouflons, numerous by then, having abundantly supplied the wool for this warm cloth (II.20 ¶72). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Les vêtements de feutre ne manquaient pas
    - quote: cette chaude étoffe
  - Ayrton is also provided with these comfortable clothes (II.20 ¶73). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ayrton avait été pourvu de ces confortables vêtements
- **Appears in:** II.11, II.20
