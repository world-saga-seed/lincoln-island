# Quinine sulphate: the white alkaloid powder that cures Harbert

*About two hundred grains of bitter white powder, the quinquina alkaloid, found in the small box on the table beside Harbert’s bed on the morning of 9 December.*

Keywords: quinine, sulfate de quinine, quinquina, alcaloïde, fébrifuge, fièvre pernicieuse, powder, grains, medicine

- **What it is.**
  - The colonists have neither quinquina nor quinine sulphate, and the only specific that could conquer the fever is not on the island (III.10 ¶37, ¶58). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Nous n’avons ni quinquina, ni sulfate de quinine !
    - quote: ne se trouvait pas dans l’île Lincoln
  - It is about two hundred grains of a white powder, extremely bitter, the precious alkaloid of quinquina, the anti-periodic above all others (III.11 ¶1). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: environ deux cents grains d’une poudre blanche
    - quote: L’extrême amertume de cette substance
    - quote: le précieux alcaloïde du quinquina, l’anti-périodique par excellence
  - Spilett gives it in a cup of lukewarm coffee, about eighteen grains at a dose (see [the coffee](coffee.md)) (III.11 ¶4). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Gédéon Spilett y jeta environ dix-huit grains
  - On the first day it is given to Harbert about every three hours (III.11 ¶9). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: de trois heures en trois heures environ, le sulfate de quinine fut administré à Harbert
  - The third fit of the pernicious fever does not come, and does not return (III.11 ¶5, ¶6). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le troisième accès de la fièvre pernicieuse ne s’était pas manifesté
    - quote: il ne devait pas revenir
  - It is the specific, and the colonists take it that the man who brought it is not far off (III.11 ¶10). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le spécifique était là, et non loin, sans doute, celui qu’il l’avait apporté
- **Appears in:** III.10, III.11
- **Open:**
  - The dose is given as about eighteen grains (III.11 ¶4), and note n1 on that word gives "10 grammes" (III.11 n1); the two do not agree. Neither is carded. *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: environ dix-huit grains
    - quote: 10 grammes
