# The willow bark: the black willow's bark, powdered to stand for quinine

*Bark cut by Smith from a black willow by the lake, pounded to a powder and given to Harbert as a poor substitute for quinine; it fails.*

Keywords: écorce de saule, willow, saule noir, quinine, salicine, fébrifuge, Harbert

- **What it is.**
  - Willow bark can sometimes replace quinine (III.10 ¶38). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: l’écorce de saule peut quelquefois remplacer la quinine
  - It is held to be a substitute for cinchona, like the horse-chestnut, holly leaf and serpentary (III.10 ¶40). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: a été justement considérée comme un succédané du quinquina
  - It is worth less than cinchona (III.10 ¶40). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: bien qu’elle ne valût pas le quinquina
  - Its alkaloid is salicine (III.10 ¶40). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: c’est-à-dire le salicine
  - The colonists could not extract the alkaloid, so the bark was used in its natural state (III.10 ¶40). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: l’employer à l’état naturel, puisque les moyens manquaient pour en extraire l’alcaloïde
  - Smith and Spilett judge it insufficient against the pernicious fever (III.10 ¶50). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Elle est insuffisante
- **Made from.**
  - The willows grow on the lake's edge (III.10 ¶38). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il y a des saules sur le bord du lac
  - Smith cut pieces of bark himself from the trunk of a kind of black willow (III.10 ¶41). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: couper sur le tronc d’une espèce de saule noir quelques morceaux d’écorce
  - He took them to Granite House and reduced them to powder (III.10 ¶41). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il les réduisit en poudre
- **On the page.**
  - It was given to Harbert the same evening, and again in new doses of pounded bark on 8 December (III.10 ¶41, ¶56). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: On lui avait administré de nouvelles doses d’écorce pilée
  - After the first dose the fever did not return that night or the next day, but the cure did not hold (III.10 ¶42, ¶44). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la fièvre ne reparut pas dans la nuit
  - Spilett expected no more result from it (III.10 ¶56). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le reporter n’en attendait plus aucun résultat
- **Appears in:** III.10 ¶37, ¶38, ¶40, ¶41, ¶42, ¶49, ¶50, ¶56
