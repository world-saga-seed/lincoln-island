# The Mercy fall: the small cascade that ends the navigation of the Mercy

*A small cascade on a natural dam near the head of the Mercy, heard before it is seen, where the pirogue is moored on 30 October; the westward track starts a little above it.*

Keywords: cascade, chute, fall, barrage, dam, head of the Mercy, sources, rios, prism, camp

- **What it is.**
  - It is announced by the sound of a fall a few hundred paces upstream, which shows a natural dam (II.3 ¶68). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le bruit assez accentué d’une chute d’eau
    - quote: la présence d’un barrage naturel
  - At the last bend of the river the cascade appears through the trees (II.3 ¶69). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une cascade apparut à travers les arbres
  - It is a small fall whose damp spray shines with the colours of the prism in the last rays of the sun at about five (II.3 ¶70). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la petite chute, dont l’humide poussière resplendissait des couleurs du prisme
  - Beyond it the bed of the Mercy vanishes under the thickets, fed by some hidden spring (II.3 ¶70). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: disparaissait sous les taillis
    - quote: il s’alimentait à quelque source cachée
  - Below it the various rios that join the river make it a true river, but above it it is only a limpid brook without depth (II.3 ¶70). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Les divers rios qui affluaient sur son parcours en faisaient plus bas une véritable rivière
    - quote: ce n’était plus qu’un ruisseau limpide et sans profondeur
  - The first axe blows of the westward track are struck a little above it, among lentisk bushes (II.4 ¶4). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un peu au-dessus de la cascade
- **Appears in:** II.3, II.4
