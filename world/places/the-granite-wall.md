# The granite wall: the sheer granite cliff behind the shore

*A cliff of very hard granite, three hundred feet or more high, three miles long, cut by the river mouth.*

Keywords: courtine, muraille, cliff, falaise, pan coupé, scree, prismatic, shore

- **What it is.**
  - It is a sheer granite curtain wall crowned by a ragged ridge at least three hundred feet high (I.3 ¶52). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: à une hauteur de trois cents pieds au moins
  - It runs three miles in length (I.3 ¶52). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: sur une longueur de trois milles
  - Its right end stops short in a cut face that looks as if cut by human hand (I.3 ¶52). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un pan coupé qu’on eût cru taillé de main d’homme
  - Its left end is an irregular cliff of agglomerated rock and scree, breaking into prismatic splinters, falling by a long ramp to the rocks of the southern point (I.3 ¶52). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: s’égrenant en éclats prismatiques
  - The block is solid throughout, with no cleft at its base for a dwelling (I.4 ¶9). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le bloc était plein partout
  - It is a plumb wall of very hard granite that the sea has never gnawed (I.4 ¶9). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un granit très-dur, que le flot n’avait jamais rongé
  - At the river mouth the wall seems split by some violent plutonic effort (I.4 ¶20). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: séparée par quelque violent effort plutonien
  - It is smooth, flat and plumb along the river as well (I.4 ¶22). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: lisse, plane et d’aplomb
  - It crowns the plateau of Grande-Vue above the Chimneys (I.11 ¶80). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: qui couronnait la haute muraille de granit, au-dessus des Cheminées
  - It rises plumb, at about five hundred feet from the strand at the point where Smith planted his pole (I.14 ¶8). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: à cinq cents pieds environ de la muraille de granit, qui se dressait perpendiculairement
  - Smith measured it by similar triangles at three hundred and thirty-three feet high (I.14 ¶24). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la muraille de granit mesurait trois cent trente-trois pieds de hauteur
  - The feet are English feet of 30 centimetres, says the note (I.14 n1). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Il s’agit du pied anglais, qui vaut 30 centimètres.
  - Examined from the river’s mouth to its north angle over about two miles, it shows no cavity anywhere, its face being smooth and straight; no hole, not even a crack (I.16 ¶15, ¶21). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: pas un trou, pas même une fente
    - quote: la paroi, unie et droite, ne laissa voir une cavité quelconque
  - The rock-pigeons’ nests are only holes bored in the crest and on its ragged edge (I.16 ¶21). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: n’étaient, en réalité, que des trous forés à la crête même
  - To dig an excavation in the mass with pick or powder was not to be thought of (I.16 ¶22). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il n’y fallait point songer
  - Along this stretch of coast the Chimneys are the only provisionally habitable shelter (I.16 ¶22). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le seul abri provisoirement habitable
  - At its north angle it ends in long slopes that die away on the beach (see [the talus](the-talus.md)) (I.16 ¶23). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ces pentes allongées qui venaient mourir sur la grève
  - The granite mass of the coast, Smith says, is likely to contain a cavity to which the outlet leads (I.17 ¶34). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une cavité plus ou moins considérable devait exister dans la masse de granit
