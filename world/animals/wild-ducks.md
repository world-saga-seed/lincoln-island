# Wild ducks: the game Pencroff cannot reach

*Wild ducks of the lake shore and the barren north coast, which will not settle and must be hit in flight.*

Keywords: canard sauvage, wild duck, gibier, game, vol, flight, flèche, arrow, lac, lake

- **What it is.**
  - Wild ducks strut about the shores and the rock-islet of Lake Grant, among pelicans, moorhens and other water birds (I.12 ¶68). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: se pavanaient des canards sauvages
  - Wild ducks frequent the desolate north coast; they rouse Pencroff's covetousness (I.14 ¶38). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des canards sauvages, qui excitèrent à bon droit la convoitise de Pencroff
  - They hardly ever settle, and must be hit on the wing; Pencroff's arrows fail (I.14 ¶38). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ils ne se posaient guère, et il eût fallu les atteindre au vol
  - Wild ducks, with pintails, teal and snipe, live in bands on the marsh waters and are little afraid, easy to approach (I.21 ¶54). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Canards sauvages, pilets, sarcelles, bécassines y vivaient par bandes
    - quote: ces volatiles peu craintifs se laissaient facilement approcher
  - Wild ducks, snipe, pintails and guillemots flutter in thousands on the frozen lake after the storm (I.22 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: canards et bécassines, pilets et guillemots
    - quote: Il y en avait des milliers
  - Wild ducks fall to the arrows of Harbert and Gédéon Spilett (I.22 ¶57). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: canards sauvages, bécassines, enfin gibier de poil ou de plume, tombaient sous leurs flèches
  - Wild ducks are among the water game that crowd the south-east marsh in winter, its winter quarters (II.11 ¶78). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: tout le gibier aquatique, qui établissait là ses quartiers d’hiver
    - quote: Canards sauvages
  - Game keeps perfectly in the intense cold; wild ducks and the rest are eaten fresh and declared superior to any other water game of the known world (II.12 ¶11). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le gibier, par ce froid intense, se gardait parfaitement
    - quote: canards sauvages et autres furent mangés frais
    - quote: déclarés supérieurs à toutes autres bêtes aquatiques du monde connu
  - Wild ducks are shot in strings, with snipe, on the north coast (II.19 ¶53, ¶54). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: mouilla dans un léger renfoncement du littoral, au nord de l’île
    - quote: plusieurs chapelets de canards et de bécassines
  - In the great cold they swarm by myriads in the tadornes' marshes, with snipe, teal, pintails and lapwings (II.20 ¶77). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: au milieu de ces myriades de canards, de bécassines, de sarcelles, de pilets et de vanneaux
- **Appears in:** I.12, I.14, I.21, I.22, II.11, II.12, II.19, II.20
