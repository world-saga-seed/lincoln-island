# The wild man of Tabor Island (notes)

## Notes for drafting

- **Where it sits.** The French II.14, `texts/fr-1875/36.md`; it follows 03500, whose last paragraph (II.13 ¶94) is "The dwelling was empty!", and it ends with the fire sighted from the boat. Beat 1 opens in the dark of that room (¶1). *(Drafted.)*
- **Cast.** *(Drafted.)*
  - Pencroff (¶1), Harbert (¶1), Gédéon Spilett (¶1).
  - The wild man, called "a gigantic monkey" (¶48), then "a man" (¶55), then "the castaway" (¶59), "the prisoner" (¶63). He has no name here.
  - Cyrus Smith is named only as absent (¶69, ¶107); Nab is not on the page. Top is named only as absent (¶41).
  - The man who built the house is a "castaway" (¶14), unnamed; the ship is the Britannia (¶24).
- **Places.** *(Drafted.)*
  - The house on Tabor, on the back of a small hill with five or six gum trees, a clearing to the sea, a lawn with a ruined fence, the stream's mouth to the left (¶21).
  - The east coast of Tabor and the Bonadventure's anchorage, about a mile, twenty minutes' walk (¶28–¶29).
  - A clump of pines (¶33); a thicket and a clearing north of the islet (¶43, ¶48); the cultivated part (¶42).
  - The forward room or cabin of the Bonadventure (¶81, ¶95).
  - The open sea, then the fire twenty miles north-east (¶107).

- **Time.** *(Drafted unless marked.)*
  - Night of arrival in the house, then daybreak (¶1, ¶20). "la veille" for the first exploration (¶19). *(My reading, to confirm: the first night is 13 to 14 October, the capture day 14 October, because ¶93 puts the departure on "the next day, — 15 October" of the night aboard, ¶92.)*
  - Half the day's search; rest about two in the afternoon (¶32–¶33); half an hour's chase (¶43).
  - 15 October dawn; anchor raised at five (¶93–¶94). 16 October (¶96). Morning of 17th, forty-eight hours out (¶97). "Twenty-four hours later", and "the day of the 18th" (¶98). Night of 18 to 19, wind drops about eleven (¶102). About two in the morning, the fire (¶105).
  - The chapter covers about six days, from the night of arrival to about 2 a.m. on 19 October. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - The year is not given here; II.16 ¶39 gives 1866.
- **Clues.** *(Drafted.)*
  - The name "Britannia" on the plank (¶23–¶24); Pencroff says the ship could be English or American.
  - The house is old: dust, rusted kettles, mouldy clothes, blankets "had not been used for a long time" (¶5); the narrator infers old confinement (¶76).
  - The man's "regard aigu" and the glint of reason that Spilett sees (¶62, ¶73).
  - His sailor's reflex in the storm (¶99); he had "been a sailor" (¶95).
  - He breaks the bulwarks and says nothing (¶99).
  - The wild man's mouth makes only hoarse sounds (¶61).
  - The fire on the horizon "evidently lit by Cyrus Smith" (¶107): the text states it as the narrator's inference.
- **Length.** 108 paragraphs (¶1–¶108); 3,625 words in the ¶ lines (¶0 excluded). 1 loose line (ln1), 2 plate captions (pl2, pl3). *(Drafted.)*

## Open and flags

- **Date count of the voyage back.** Here the fire is seen about two in the morning on the night of 18 to 19 October, twenty miles off (¶102, ¶105, ¶107):
  - quote: La nuit du 18 au 19 fut obscure et froide
  - quote: une vive lueur apparaissait à vingt milles dans le nord-est
  - II.15 ¶1 then lands on "the next day, — 20 October" at seven in the morning "after four days' voyage" (see the notes of 03700). Card neither. *(Drafted.)*
- **Who built the house.** Here the man who built the house is a survivor of the Britannia's crew:
  - quote: au moyen des débris du navire, cet homme, ayant des outils à sa disposition, avait construit cette demeure
  - II.17 ¶80 gives it as built by the captain:
  - quote: la maison construite par l’honnête capitaine Grant
  Card neither. *(Drafted.)*
- **The unknown's age and the house's age.** Here the house shows no use for a long time:
  - quote: prouvaient qu’il ne servait plus depuis longtemps
  II.17 gives twelve years on Tabor (II.16 ¶40, "Twelve years! twelve years!") but he lived in the forests; whether the bed was his is not said. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- **"Quelques mois" and the document.** Not in this chapter; see II.15 ¶24 and II.16 ¶56–¶63.
- **Age guess.** Spilett: "at least fifty years" (¶88). Not checked against later chapters.
- **Ape.** The first sight is of "a gigantic monkey, no doubt" (¶48); Harbert corrects it (¶53). *(Drafted.)*
- **Wikisource corrections.** None touches this chapter (`texts/README.md`: the three fact-changing corrections are in I.12, II.20 and III.10). The spelling "très-" is kept as in the print. *(Drafted.)*
- **Plate and loose line.** ln1 shows "BR..TAN.A" (after ¶23); the text of ¶23 gives only "these letters already half effaced:" and ¶24 gives the word, so the loose line is the only place the half-effaced letters appear. *(Drafted.)*
