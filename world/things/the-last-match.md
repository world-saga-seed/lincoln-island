# The last match: the single match found in Spilett's waistcoat

*One match, found stuck in the lining of Spilett's waistcoat, struck by Harbert to light the first fire.*

Keywords: allumette, fétu, phosphorus, waistcoat lining, fire, only match

- **What it is.**
  - Spilett had said he thought he had thrown out all his matches (I.5 ¶57). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: J’en avais, mais j’ai dû tout jeter
  - It is a small piece of wood with phosphorus, a single match (I.5 ¶66). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Comme ce devait être une allumette, et une seule, il s’agissait de ne point en érailler le phosphore
  - It was stuck in the lining of Spilett's waistcoat, where his fingers could feel it through the cloth but not pull it out (I.5 ¶66). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un petit morceau de bois engagé dans la doublure de son gilet
  - Harbert drew it out without breaking it, and it was intact (I.5 ¶68). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: sans le casser, il parvint à retirer ce petit morceau de bois
- **On the page.**
  - Pencroff made sure it was dry and struck it on a slightly rough pebble wiped clean; the first stroke did nothing for fear of rubbing off the phosphorus (I.5 ¶76). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Pencroff n’avait pas appuyé assez vivement, craignant d’érailler le phosphore
  - At Harbert's second stroke a small crackle sounded and a slight bluish flame leapt up with an acrid smoke (I.5 ¶78). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une légère flamme bleuâtre jaillit en produisant une fumée âcre
  - It was used up in lighting the paper twist that started the first fire (I.5 ¶78). *(Drafted.)*
