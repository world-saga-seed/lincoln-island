# Tobacco: the plant Pencroff wants and the island has not given

*The one thing Pencroff says the island lacks; wished for from the chest and in the forest, and not found on these chapters’ ground.*

Keywords: tabac, tobacco, pipe, Pencroff, demi-livre, plant, mustard, bamboo, smoke, want

- **What it is.**
  - Pencroff had hoped for half a pound of tobacco in the chest, "and nothing would have been wanting to my happiness" (II.2 ¶73). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Une demi-livre de tabac
    - quote: rien n’aurait manqué à mon bonheur
  - He regrets that the chest did not hold, together with his half pound of tobacco, a pair of New Jersey horses (II.3 ¶3). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: sa demi-livre de tabac
  - He has never seen his favourite plant except in the bowl of his pipe; taking a downy plant for tobacco, he is told by Harbert that it is mustard (II.3 ¶15, ¶16). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: n’avait jamais vu sa plante de prédilection que dans le fourneau de sa pipe
    - quote: ce n’est pas du tabac, c’est de la moutarde
  - He asks that if a tobacco plant should turn up, it not be passed over (II.3 ¶17). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: si, par hasard, un plant de tabac se présentait
  - Spilett says it will be found one day, and Pencroff answers that then he would not know what the island lacked (II.3 ¶18, ¶19). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Nous en trouverons un jour
    - quote: je ne sais vraiment plus ce qui manquera à notre île
  - Pencroff sighs that there is everything on the island except tobacco (II.4 ¶26). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: excepté du tabac, il y a de tout dans cette île
  - Bamboo cannot be smoked, Harbert tells him (II.4 ¶58, ¶59). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ça ne se fume pas, mon pauvre Pencroff
- **Appears in:** II.2, II.3, II.4
