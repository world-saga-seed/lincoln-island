## b

After: to come a hundred miles from there to the north-east?”

The direction agrees with the voyage of chapters XXXV and XXXVI, by which the islet lies south-west of Lincoln Island; see note (a) to chapter XXXI. The distance does not agree with the hundred and fifty miles the engineer gave in chapter XXXI, and on which the voyage of chapter XXXV was planned. That voyage bears out the greater figure: the Bonadventure had run a hundred and twenty miles in thirty-six hours and had not yet raised the islet. I take a hundred and fifty, or near it.
