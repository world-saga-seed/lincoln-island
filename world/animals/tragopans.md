# Tragopans: the horned pheasants of the mountain thickets

*Pheasant-family fowl the size of a cock, with a throat wattle and two small horns, one of which the colonists kill and eat on the mountain.*

Keywords: tragopan, faisan, pheasant, gallinacé, gallinacean, gibier, game, wattle, fanon, horns, mountain

- **What it is.**
  - Gallinaceans of the pheasant family, called "tragopans" (I.10 ¶32). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: gallinacés de la famille des faisans. C’étaient des « tragopans »
  - Each has a fleshy wattle hanging at the throat and two thin cylindrical horns set behind the eyes (I.10 ¶32). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ornés d’un fanon charnu qui pendait sur leurs gorges, et de deux minces cornes cylindriques
  - They are the size of a cock; the hen is uniformly brown, the cock resplendent in red plumage sown with small white tears (I.10 ¶32). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: de la taille d’un coq, la femelle était uniformément brune
  - They flutter in pairs under cover in thick copse on the mountain's lower slope (I.10 ¶32). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: voletaient plusieurs couples de gallinacés
  - One killed by the reporter with a thrown stone is called "le faisan" and kept by Nab for the next day (I.10 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: faisan, que Nab réservait pour le lendemain
  - The roast tragopan is the whole of the colonists' breakfast on 30 March (I.11 ¶2). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le tragopan rôti fit tous les frais
- **Appears in:** I.10, I.11
