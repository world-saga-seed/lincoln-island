# The window glass: the panes Smith and Harbert make for Granite-house

*Glass blown from the island's sand, lime and soda in March of the second year; until then thick shutters close the openings of the facade.*

Keywords: verre à vitre, glass, panes, windows, châssis, frames, fenêtres

- **What it is.**
  - Until the window frames are made, Smith means to close the openings with thick shutters (I.19 ¶13). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: en attendant que les châssis des fenêtres fussent faits
  - The shutters serve until the engineer has the time to make window glass (I.19 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: en attendant que l’ingénieur eût eu le temps de fabriquer du verre à vitre
  - The idea comes from the lightning, which fused the sand into fulgurites: Smith sees that thick solid panes could defy wind, rain and hail (see [the fulgurites](fulgurites.md)) (II.9 ¶5). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il serait possible de garnir les fenêtres de vitres épaisses et solides, qui pussent défier le vent, la pluie et la grêle
  - Glass needs only sand, chalk and soda, and the island supplies them: the shore sand, lime for chalk, sea plants for soda, pyrites for sulphuric acid, the ground coal for the furnace (see [the glassworks](the-glassworks.md)) (II.9 ¶34). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le rivage fournissait le sable, la chaux fournissait la craie
    - quote: les pyrites fournissaient l’acide sulfurique, et le sol fournissait la houille
  - The furnace is fired hard on 28 March with a batch of a hundred parts of sand, thirty-five of chalk and forty of sulphate of soda, mixed with two or three parts of powdered charcoal, in crucibles of refractory clay (II.9 ¶36). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Le 28 mars, le four fut chauffé vivement
    - quote: Cent parties de sable, trente-cinq de craie, quarante de sulfate de soude
    - quote: dans les creusets en terre réfractaire
  - Smith gathers the paste on the pipe, turns it on a metal plate and passes the pipe to Harbert, who blows while turning it; more paste is added till the bubble measures a foot across, and Smith swings it into a cylindrical-conical shape (II.9 ¶36, ¶39). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Cyrus Smith « cueillit » avec la canne une certaine quantité de cette pâte
    - quote: une bulle qui mesurait un pied de diamètre
    - quote: une forme cylindro-conique
  - The two hemispherical caps are cut off with an iron wetted with cold water, the cylinder is split lengthwise, softened by a second heating and spread on a plate, and flattened with a wooden roller (II.9 ¶40). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: détachées au moyen d’un fer tranchant mouillé d’eau froide
    - quote: plané au moyen d’un rouleau de bois
  - The first pane made, the operation need only be repeated fifty times; the windows of Granite-house are soon fitted with panes not very white, perhaps, but transparent enough (II.9 ¶41). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il suffisait de recommencer cinquante fois l’opération pour avoir cinquante vitres
    - quote: pas très-blanches peut-être, mais suffisamment transparentes

    - quote: because the engineer had not yet had the time to make glass for its windows
