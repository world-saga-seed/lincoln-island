# The medicinal herbs: basil, rosemary, balm and betony gathered at the warren

*Herbs that Harbert gathers for the sick, though Pencroff holds there is no need while there is no doctor.*

Keywords: herbes médicinales, plantes, basilic, romarin, mélisse, bétoine, infusion, remedies

- **What it is.**
  - Harbert gathers shoots of basil, rosemary, balm and betony in the warren, plants of various healing properties, some pectoral, astringent or febrifuge, others anti-spasmodic or anti-rheumatic (I.19 ¶31). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une certaine quantité de pousses de basilic, de romarin, de mélisse, de bétoine
    - quote: les unes pectorales, astringentes, fébrifuges, les autres anti-spasmodiques ou anti-rhumatismales
  - They are to treat the colonists when sick (I.19 ¶32). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: À nous soigner
  - The harvest is well received at Granite-house (I.19 ¶34). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: sa récolte, qui fut très-bien accueillie à Granite-house
  - Granite-house has a "pharmacie végétale" that supplies the ingredients of a cooling tisane for the wounded Jup (II.12 ¶56). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dont la pharmacie végétale de Granite-house fournit les ingrédients
  - The "pharmacie végétale" is read as these herbs (II.12 ¶56). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: la pharmacie végétale de Granite-house
