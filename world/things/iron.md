# The iron: forged from the island’s ore by the simplified Catalan method

*Iron reduced from the magnetic ore in a heap of ore and coal blown by the seal-skin bellows, forged into bars by 25 April; the first bloom served as hammer.*

Keywords: fer, loupe, bloom, méthode catalane, Catalan, reduction, forge, forging, métallurgique, anvil, enclume, hammer, bars

- **What it is.**
  - Smith wants iron and not cast iron, and must find the quickest method of reduction (I.15 ¶9). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: de fer et non de fonte
  - The oxide must be reduced by charcoal; the Catalan method turns the ore straight into iron in one operation (I.15 ¶8). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: transformer directement le minerai en fer dans une seule opération
  - He chooses the Catalan method simplified, as used in Corsica, without the furnaces and crucibles the full method requires (I.15 ¶41, ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la méthode catalane, mais simplifiée, ainsi qu’on l’emploie en Corse
    - quote: Mais Cyrus Smith prétendait économiser ces constructions
  - The process is a cubic mass of ore and coal with the bellows’ blast directed to its centre, which the narrator calls the process of Tubal-Cain and the first metallurgists (I.15 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une masse cubique au centre de laquelle il dirigerait le vent de son soufflet
    - quote: Tubal-Caïn
  - The ore was broken into small pieces and cleaned by hand; coal and ore were laid in successive layers, as a charcoal-burner lays wood (I.15 ¶43). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: On cassa préalablement le minerai en petits morceaux
    - quote: ainsi que fait le charbonnier du bois qu’il veut carboniser
  - The operation was difficult and called for all the colonists’ patience and ingenuity; the result was a bloom of iron in a spongy state, which had to be hammered and welded to drive out the liquefied gangue (I.15 ¶45). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: L’opération fut difficile
    - quote: une loupe de fer, réduite à l’état d’éponge
  - The first bloom, hafted on a stick, served as the hammer to forge the second on a granite anvil; the metal was coarse but usable (I.15 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: emmanchée d’un bâton, servit de marteau
    - quote: une enclume de granit
    - quote: un métal grossier, mais utilisable
  - By 25 April several bars of iron were forged (I.15 ¶47). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: plusieurs barres de fer étaient forgées
  - The period of the forging, from 20 April, is called "the metallurgical period" by the reporter in his notes (I.15 ¶31). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la période métallurgique
  - An iron mass weighing several pounds is hung from a post over the mine hole as the striker of the firing device (I.17 ¶72). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une masse de fer pesant plusieurs livres
- **Appears in:** I.15, I.17
