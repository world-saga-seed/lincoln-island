# Pencroff's matchbox: the copper box of matches, lost on landing

*A copper box of matches that never left Pencroff and was lost in the fall; its loss leaves the four without fire.*

Keywords: boîte, allumettes, matches, copper, brass, lost, fire, smoker

- **What it is.**
  - Pencroff always kept it on him, being a confirmed smoker (I.5 ¶18). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la boîte qui ne le quittait jamais, car il était un fumeur acharné
  - Being copper it would not have escaped their eyes on sand or rock (I.5 ¶22). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: La boîte était en cuivre et n’eût point échappé à leurs yeux
  - It is a copper box that closes well, holding Pencroff's matches (I.5 ¶29). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Elles étaient dans une boîte en cuivre qui fermait bien
- **On the page.**
  - Pencroff did not throw it out of the basket; he thinks so thin an object may have vanished in the shaking (I.5 ¶24). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un si mince objet peut avoir disparu
  - Its loss is called a grave and, for the moment, irreparable loss (I.5 ¶27). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une perte grave dans la circonstance, et, pour le moment, irréparable
  - He and Harbert searched the sand, the rocks and the river bank, the landing place, the left bank up to the raft's mooring, the plateau and the corridors, and did not find it (I.5 ¶36). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le tout vainement
- **Appears in:** I.5
