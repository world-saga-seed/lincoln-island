# The convicts’ guns: the firearms of the pirates landed on the island

*The firearms the Speedy’s men carry ashore and use at the corral; the five survivors remain well armed.*

Keywords: fusils, armes à feu, convicts, pirates, balle, armed, Speedy

- **What it is.**
  - The men of the Speedy’s first boat carry guns (III.3 ¶23). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ils étaient armés de fusils
  - Spilett counts the six landed men well armed (III.5 ¶62). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ils sont six et bien armés
  - A convict’s gun fires a ball that goes through Smith’s hat; he has no time for a second shot (III.7 ¶8). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: l’ajustant, lui traversa le chapeau d’une balle
    - quote: avant même qu’il eût eu le temps de tirer son second coup
  - The five that survive are well armed, with firearms they use against Harbert (III.8 ¶3, ¶24). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: réduits à cinq, il est vrai, mais bien armés
    - quote: qui possédaient des armes à feu
- **Appears in:** III.3, III.5, III.7, III.8
