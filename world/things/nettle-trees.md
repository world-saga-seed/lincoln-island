# The nettle trees: the micocouliers of the Mercy, whose kernels give oil

*Nettle trees (micocouliers) by the Mercy, whose kernel gives a useful oil and under a clump of which the camp was made.*

Keywords: micocouliers, nettle trees, hackberry, oil, amande, camp, Mercy

- **What it is.**
  - Nettle trees (micocouliers) stand among the Ulmaceae on the right bank of the Mercy, of the same family as the elms, and their kernel produces a very useful oil (II.3 ¶12). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des micocouliers, dont l’amande produit une huile fort utile
    - quote: Sur la rive droite de la rivière s’étageaient de magnifiques échantillons des ulmacées
  - A clump of large nettle trees, among whose branches the colonists could have found a refuge for the night, shelters their camp on the Mercy (II.3 ¶71). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un bouquet de larges micocouliers
  - In October their new foliage appears at the edge of the wood among the persistent foliage of the conifers (II.20 ¶80). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le feuillage nouveau des micocouliers
  - The enormous nettle trees shade the poultry-yard at the south end of the lake, and one is struck by lightning and broken on 1 January 1869 (III.18 ¶11). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un de ces énormes micocouliers qui ombrageaient la basse-cour à l’extrémité sud du lac
- **Appears in:** II.3, II.20, III.18
