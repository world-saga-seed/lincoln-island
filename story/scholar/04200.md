# The scholar’s notes to chapter XLII

*Each note is set after the words its “After:” line quotes, exactly as they stand in story/prose/04200-the-shark-gulf-and-the-sail.md. Delete this file and the chapter builds without them (R1121-163).*

## a

After: Now, as it was about six o’clock when the Bonadventure was abreast of the gulf,

At ten o’clock, fifteen miles from the cape, Pencroff reckoned to be abreast of it between noon and one, when the ebb would begin, and he did not look for the flood until seven in the evening (chapter XLI). This sentence says it turned out as he foresaw, and that the ebb was just then making itself felt. Both agree with an hour soon after noon, and neither with six, an hour before the flood. I take the earlier hour.

## b

After: This he did about the middle of April.

The offer was made when the bad days came with May, and Ayrton was to come to Granite House when the last work at the corral was done. He cannot have come in the middle of April, before the offer was made. I find nothing in the papers that gives the true month, and leave it open.
