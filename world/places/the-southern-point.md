# The southern point: a very sharp point closing the bay to the south

*A barren, wild-looking point buttressed on high granite rocks; beyond it the land cannot be read.*

Keywords: pointe, promontory, southern end, peninsula, horizon

- **What it is.**
  - It closes the bay on the south and is very sharp, without any vegetation, of a very wild look (I.3 ¶51). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dépourvue de toute végétation et d’un aspect très-sauvage
  - It joins the shore by a capricious line and is buttressed on high granite rocks (I.3 ¶51). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: s’arc-boutait à de hautes roches granitiques
  - The left end of the wall slopes down to merge with the rocks of this point (I.3 ¶52). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: se confondait peu à peu avec les roches de la pointe méridionale
  - The right bank of the river falls away to its end (I.4 ¶47). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: jusqu’à l’extrémité de la pointe
- **On the page.**
  - From the plateau a sharp point in the south shuts the horizon, and one could not tell whether the land goes on or turns south-east and south-west, making a very long peninsula (I.4 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une sorte de presqu’île très-allongée
- **Appears in:** I.3, I.4
- **Open:**
  - Whether this point is the same as the cape that hides the south horizon in I.13 is not said; I.13 names that one Cape Griffe, and I.3 and I.4 name none.
    - quote: Dans le sud, une pointe aiguë fermait l’horizon
