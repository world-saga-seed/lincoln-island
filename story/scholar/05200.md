## a

After: the engineer and the reporter passed that day, the 7th of December,

The first fit was on the 6th. The fever did not come back that night, “nor did it return the following day,” and was looked for on the morrow after; by that count the second fit fell on the 8th. The date here gives the 7th, and the next day is the 8th. The third fit is awaited on the 9th, and with the fever coming back every second day, as was feared, that agrees with a second fit on the 7th and not on the 8th. I take the dates.
