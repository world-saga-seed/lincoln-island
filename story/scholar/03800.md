## a

After: since the first grain of wheat had been sown, eighteen months before!

The grain was sown on the 20th of June, 1865 (chapter XX). To the 15th of November, 1866, is a little less than seventeen months. Both days are given by date, and nothing in the papers moves either. I take the dates; the eighteen months is a month too many.
