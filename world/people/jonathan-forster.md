# Jonathan Forster: Southern diehard who built the balloon

*A besieged Confederate who had the balloon made at Richmond to carry word to General Lee and could not launch it in the storm.*

Keywords: Forster, sudiste, Confederate, Richmond, balloon, Lee, aeronaut

- **What it is.**
  - He is a Southerner, a "sudiste enragé", one of the besieged in Richmond (I.2 ¶18). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un certain Jonathan Forster, sudiste enragé
  - He conceived the idea of flying out in a balloon to cross the besieging lines and reach the Confederate camp (I.2 ¶18). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: eut alors l’idée de s’enlever en ballon
  - The governor authorised his attempt; a balloon was built and put at his disposal, with five companions to follow him in the air, armed and provisioned (I.2 ¶19). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Un aérostat fut fabriqué et mis à la disposition de Jonathan Forster
- **On the page.**
  - Pencroff pictures him and his companions finding next morning the place clear and the balloon flown (I.14 ¶47). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Jonathan Forster et de ses compagnons, quand, le lendemain matin, ils auront trouvé la place nette et la machine envolée
- **Appears in:** I.2, I.14
