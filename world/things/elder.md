# The elder: the shrub whose pith gives cellulose and whose berries stand for coffee

*Elder bushes growing near the mouth of the Red Creek, whose pith gives the cellulose for pyroxyle and whose berries the colonists already use as coffee.*

Keywords: sureau, elder, moelle, pith, baies, berries, café, caprifoliacées, cellulose

- **What it is.**
  - Elders abound on the island towards the mouth of the Red Creek, and the colonists already use the berries of this shrub, of the honeysuckle family, in place of coffee (II.8 ¶9). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les sureaux abondaient dans l’île, vers l’embouchure du Creek-Rouge
    - quote: les colons employaient déjà en guise de café les baies de ces arbrisseaux, qui appartiennent à la famille des caprifoliacées
  - The pith of the elder is nearly pure cellulose, which Smith means to harvest for pyroxyle (see [the pyroxyle](pyroxyle.md)) (II.8 ¶9, ¶10). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la moelle de sureau
    - quote: cette mœlle de sureau, c’est-à-dire la cellulose, il suffisait de la récolter
  - An infusion of elder seeds served by Nab takes the place of coffee on the plateau’s veranda (II.9 ¶69). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Quelques tasses de cette infusion de graines de sureau, qui remplaçaient le café
  - In July the elder coffee steams in the cups of the great hall (II.11 ¶41). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le café de sureau fumant dans la tasse
- **Appears in:** II.8, II.9, II.11
