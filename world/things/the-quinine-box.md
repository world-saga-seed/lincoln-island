# The quinine box: the small oblong box that appears on the table by Harbert’s bed

*A small oblong box, its lid marked "Sulfate de quinine", found at dawn on 9 December on the table by Harbert’s bed, with no account of how it came.*

Keywords: boîte, box, quinine, Sulfate de quinine, table, lit, unknown, génie de l’île, inexplicable

- **What it is.**
  - Before it appears the colonists have no remedy, and Top barks in a strange way at about three in the morning (III.10 ¶61, ¶62). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Top, en ce moment, aboya d’une façon étrange
  - At five in the morning of 9 December a sunbeam reaches the table placed beside the bed, and Pencroff points to an object on it (III.10 ¶59, ¶64, ¶65, ¶66). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Un rayon se glissa jusqu’à la table qui était placée près du lit
    - quote: montra un objet placé sur cette table
  - It is a small oblong box whose lid bears the words "Sulfate de quinine" (III.10 ¶67, ln3). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: C’était une petite boîte oblongue, dont le couvercle portait ces mots
    - quote: Sulfate de quinine.
  - It holds about two hundred grains of white powder (see [the quinine sulphate](quinine-sulphate.md)) (III.11 ¶1). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Elle contenait environ deux cents grains d’une poudre blanche
  - The colonists hold that the unknown's intervention is more evident than ever, and that his "influence" has acted again at the supreme moment (III.11 ¶7, ¶8). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: L’influence mystérieuse s’était de nouveau exercée
    - quote: L’intervention de l’inconnu était plus évidente que jamais
  - How it came into Granite-house during the night is "absolutely inexplicable", and the way the "genius of the island" goes about it is no less strange than the genius himself (III.11 ¶8). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Mais comment avait-il pu pénétrer pendant la nuit jusque dans Granite-house ?
    - quote: C’était absolument inexplicable
- **Appears in:** III.10, III.11
