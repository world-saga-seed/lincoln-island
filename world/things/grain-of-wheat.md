# The grain of wheat: the single grain Harbert finds in his jacket

*One grain of wheat, strayed from a holed pocket into the lining of Harbert’s jacket, planted on 20 June in the plateau bed; the hope of bread.*

Keywords: grain de blé, blé, wheat, grain, seed, bread, pain, harvest, moissons, jacket, veste

- **What it is.**
  - Bread is the one lack among the colonists’ foods (I.20 ¶31). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: mais le pain faisait défaut
  - Harbert finds it by the greatest chance in the lining of his jacket, which he is mending (I.20 ¶33). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: par le plus grand hasard, Harbert trouva un jour dans la doublure de sa veste
  - It is a single grain that slipped from his holed pocket into the lining (I.20 ¶36). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un grain, un unique grain qui, de sa poche trouée, s’était introduit dans la doublure de sa veste
  - Its presence is explained by Harbert’s habit, at Richmond, of feeding some wood-pigeons that Pencroff had given him (I.20 ¶37). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: nourrir quelques ramiers dont Pencroff lui avait fait présent
  - Smith examines it and finds it in good condition (I.20 ¶43). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: reconnut qu’il était en bon état
  - Smith reckons that a grain gives ten ears and an ear holds eighty grains on average, so that the fourth harvest would give more than four hundred billion grains, over three million bushels at a hundred and thirty thousand to the bushel (I.20 ¶46, ¶48, ¶54). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Quatre-vingts en moyenne
    - quote: plus de quatre cents milliards de grains
    - quote: cent trente mille par boisseau
  - He counts on three million bushels in four years, or in two if two crops a year can be had in this latitude (I.20 ¶58). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: deux récoltes par année
  - It was sown on 20 June, entrusted to the ground rather than a pot, in a prepared bed (see [the wheat field](../places/the-wheat-field.md)) (I.20 ¶66). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: On était au 20 juin
    - quote: de le confier à la terre
  - It is the only one: no human power could make another if it perished (I.20 ¶68). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: nulle puissance humaine ne leur referait ce grain de blé
- **Appears in:** I.20, I.21
