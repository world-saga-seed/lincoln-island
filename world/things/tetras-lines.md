# The tétras lines: baited lines of liana and thorn set for tétras

*Hook lines of liana, thorn-hooked and baited with worms, that Pencroff set by the tétras nests; they took three birds.*

Keywords: lignes, hameçon, fishing line, liana, thorn, bait, snare, tétras

- **What it is.**
  - They are true hook lines, not snare lines (I.6 ¶41). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: non des lignes à collets, mais de véritables lignes à hameçon
  - Pencroff set them round the tétras nests, about half a dozen, each with two or three eggs (I.6 ¶41). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ce fut autour d’eux qu’il imagina de tendre ses lignes
  - He made them with his own hands as "singular gear" (I.6 ¶41). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il prépara ses engins singuliers
- **Made from.**
  - The lines are thin lianas tied one to another, fifteen to twenty feet long (I.6 ¶41). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Les lignes furent faites de minces lianes, rattachées l’une à l’autre et longues de quinze à vingt pieds
  - The hooks are [dwarf acacia](dwarf-acacia.md) thorns tied to the ends of the lianas (I.6 ¶41). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: De grosses épines très-fortes, à pointes recourbées, que fournit un buisson d’acacias nains
  - The bait is [red worms](../animals/red-worms.md) (I.6 ¶41). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: de gros vers rouges qui rampaient sur le sol en tinrent lieu
- **On the page.**
  - The idea came when the tétras could not be shot on the wing nor approached (I.6 ¶37, ¶38). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: puisqu’on ne peut les tuer au vol, il faut essayer de les prendre à la ligne
  - One end lay by the nests and the other stayed with Pencroff, hidden behind a big tree (I.6 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: alla placer le bout de ses lignes armées d’hameçons près des nids de tétras
  - Small jerks moved the baits, and the birds attacked the hooks (I.6 ¶45, ¶47). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Pencroff alors donna de petites secousses qui agitèrent les appâts
  - They caught three tétras at once; the wing-beats told Pencroff they were taken (I.6 ¶47). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Trois tétras, très-voraces sans doute, avalèrent à la fois l’appât et l’hameçon
  - Pencroff says he did not invent the method (I.6 ¶49). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il n’avait pas le mérite de l’invention
- **Appears in:** I.6 ¶37, ¶38, ¶41, ¶42, ¶45, ¶47, ¶49
