# An Episode of the Civil War (notes)

## Notes for drafting

- **Where it sits.** The back-story of the escape: a long look back from the beach to Richmond, from the capture of Cyrus Smith in February 1865 to the departure on the night of 20 March; it ends by naming the missing man. It follows I.1 directly: I.1 ends on four castaways crying to save the one who is missing; I.2 ¶1 says who they are and ¶58 says who is missing. *(Drafted.)*
- **Cast.** *(Drafted.)* unless marked.
  - Cyrus Smith, engineer, about 45, Massachusetts: ¶3 (named), ¶4 (described). Also "monsieur Smith" ¶25.
  - Gédéon Spilett, reporter of the New York Herald, "quarante ans au plus": ¶6 (the plate caption after ¶2 names him first).
  - Nab (Nabuchodonosor), thirty, a freed slave, Cyrus's servant: ¶14.
  - Pencroff, sailor, 35–40, a Northern American: ¶24.
  - Harbert Brown, fifteen, of New Jersey, son of Pencroff's captain, an orphan: ¶24; "jeune Harbert Brown" ¶45.
  - Top, Cyrus's dog: ¶53.
  - Jonathan Forster, Southern officer-aeronaut, with five companions: ¶18, ¶19.
  - Named in passing: General Ulysse Grant (¶1), General Lee (¶18), Butler (¶17), Stanley (¶7), the governor of Richmond (¶18), William of Orange (¶4).

## Open and flags

- **Miles.** ¶1: "ils se trouvaient à sept mille milles de cette capitale". ¶57: "à plus de six mille milles de leur pays". Different distances and different measure (from Richmond; from home). Against I.1 ¶18, "deux mille milles par vingt-quatre heures". Card neither. *(Drafted.)*
- **Days.** ¶1: "Leur navigation aérienne avait duré cinq jours." ¶56: "Ce fut cinq jours après seulement, qu’une éclaircie laissa voir l’immense mer". ¶57: "partis le 20 mars ... le 24 mars" (four days). Against I.1 ¶10 and ¶13, where the sea is already below the basket on 23 March, and I.1 ¶18, "l’ouragan durait depuis cinq jours déjà" on the 23rd. *(Drafted.)*
- **Left in the hurricane.** ¶55 and ¶56 have the balloon go in the storm; against I.1 ¶18, "Il n’avait évidemment pas pu partir pendant l’ouragan." *(Drafted.)*
- **Wind.** ¶20, "un vent de nord-ouest de moyenne force"; I.1 ¶14, "coup de vent de nord-est". *(Drafted.)*
- **Hour.** ¶44, "À ce soir, dix heures"; ¶49, "À neuf heures et demie, Cyrus Smith et ses compagnons se glissaient ... sur la place". Not a contradiction unless the earlier meeting was meant for ten. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- **Numbers aboard.** ¶19 gives Forster "cinq de ses compagnons" (six aboard); ¶40 "le ballon devait enlever six". Consistent. Five aboard instead (¶50, ¶57), and I.1 ¶17 "cinq passagers". *(Drafted.)*
- **The plate caption GÉDÉON SPILETT. falls after ¶2**, four paragraphs before he is introduced at ¶6; and the plate "Monsieur Smith, voulez-vous fuir ?" falls after ¶27, though it is Pencroff's question. *(Drafted.)*
- **Diction.** ¶14 calls Nab "un nègre", in the text's period word; ¶0 repeats it. The outline says "a Black man". *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- **Wikisource corrections.** None in I.2 (none listed for it in texts/README.md; no page of I.2 was read in the scan check). The text keeps print's hyphens ("très-instruit", "très-simple"). *(Drafted.)*
