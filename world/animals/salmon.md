# Salmon: the shoals that run up the Mercy

*Fish that come in bands up the Mercy to spawn and are caught, salted and stored for winter.*

Keywords: saumon, saumons, Mercy, frai, barrages, salaison, réserve

- **What it is.**
  - Salmon come in bands into the Mercy and go up its course for several miles (II.8 ¶19). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Des saumons vinrent par bandes s’aventurer dans la Mercy et en remontèrent le cours pendant plusieurs milles
  - It is the season when the females, seeking suitable places to spawn, go ahead of the males and make great noise in the fresh water (II.8 ¶19). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les femelles, allant rechercher des endroits convenables pour frayer, précédaient les mâles
  - About a thousand of the fish, measuring up to two and a half feet long, enter the river (II.8 ¶19). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Un millier de ces poissons, qui mesuraient jusqu’à deux pieds et demi de longueur
  - A few weirs are enough to hold back a great number; several hundred are taken, salted and put in reserve for the time when winter ices the streams and makes all fishing impracticable (II.8 ¶19). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il suffit d’établir quelques barrages pour en retenir une grande quantité
    - quote: furent salés et mis en réserve
  - The narrator calls them "ces excellents saumons", which come again into the Mercy's waters; a new fishing is among the riches of the third year (III.14 ¶20). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une nouvelle pêche de ces excellents saumons qui vinrent encore s’engouffrer dans les eaux de la Mercy
- **Appears in:** II.8, III.14
