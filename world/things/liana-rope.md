# The liana rope: dry lianas used as binding and as a plaited rope

*Dry lianas used to bind the raft's logs, and plaited by Pencroff into a rope several fathoms long.*

Keywords: lianes, vine, câble végétal, cord, plait, binding

- **What it is.**
  - Dry lianas bind the logs of the raft together (I.4 ¶39). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: attachés ensemble avec des lianes sèches
  - Pencroff plaited from dry lianas a rope several fathoms long, a vegetable cable (I.4 ¶63). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: tressa rapidement une corde longue de plusieurs brasses au moyen de lianes sèches
  - It was tied to the raft's stern (I.4 ¶63). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: attaché à l’arrière du radeau
- **On the page.**
  - Pencroff held it in his hand while walking the bank (I.4 ¶63). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le marin le tint à la main
- **Appears in:** I.4
- **Open:**
  - The text does not name the liana plant.
    - quote: des lianes sèches
