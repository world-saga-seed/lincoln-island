# The wild spinach: a goosefoot found on the Mercy bank

*A wild spinach of the goosefoot family that Harbert finds on the Mercy bank and carries off, later among the wild vegetables brought back from the forests.*

Keywords: épinards sauvages, chenopodées, spinach, goosefoot, Harbert

- **What it is.**
  - It is a kind of wild spinach of the goosefoot family (II.3 ¶13). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une sorte d’épinards sauvages de la famille des chenopodées
  - It is uprooted with care and carried off in the pirogue (II.3 ¶20). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: avaient été déracinées avec soin
  - It is among the wild plants brought back from the forests in January 1866, to be modified by cultivation (II.8 ¶14). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: épinards, cresson, raifort, raves
    - quote: qu’une culture intelligente devait bientôt modifier
- **Appears in:** II.3, II.8
