# The ship in sight: the vessel that Smith’s long-glass finds on the horizon on 17 October

*A vessel seen from Granite-house at the horizon of the Union Bay on the afternoon of 17 October 1867, first found as a speck on Harbert’s photographic plate.*

Keywords: navire, vaisseau, voile, horizon, 17 octobre, longue-vue, cliché, Harbert, Smith, fin de la deuxième partie

- **What it is.**
  - Smith sweeps the horizon with his long-glass and stops on the suspect point, then lowers it and says only one word, "Navire !" (II.20 ¶91). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: s’arrêta enfin sur le point suspect
    - quote: ne prononça que ce mot : « Navire ! »
  - A ship is in sight of Lincoln Island, with which the second part ends (II.20 ¶92, ln2). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un navire était en vue de l’île Lincoln
  - It was first seen as a speck on Harbert’s plate (see [the photographic plate](the-photographic-plate.md)) (II.20 ¶85, ¶89). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un petit point presque imperceptible qui tachait l’horizon de mer
    - quote: il tendit le cliché et la lentille à l’ingénieur, en lui indiquant la petite tache
  - Only her masts show above the horizon, with no hull to be seen, some twenty miles to the east (III.1 ¶6, ¶11). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: sa mâture seule apparaît au-dessus de l’horizon
    - quote: à une distance de vingt milles dans l’est
  - She proves to be a pirate brig (see [the Speedy](the-speedy.md)) (III.1 ¶56, ¶75). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ce navire était gréé en brick
- **Appears in:** II.20, III.1
