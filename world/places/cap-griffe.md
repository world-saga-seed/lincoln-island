# Cap de la Griffe: the sharp south-east cape of the Union Bay

*The sharp point that closes the Union Bay on the south-east, named by Nab for the claw of the island's fantastic animal; it hides the southern horizon, and the heights of the right bank end at it.*

Keywords: cap Griffe, headland, promontory, point, south shore, cap de la Griffe, Claw-cape, pointe, south-east, griffe, claw

- **What it is.**
  - It closes the bay on the south and is very sharp, without any vegetation, of a very wild look (I.3 ¶51). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dépourvue de toute végétation et d’un aspect très-sauvage
  - It joins the shore by a capricious line and is buttressed on high granite rocks (I.3 ¶51). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: s’arc-boutait à de hautes roches granitiques
  - The left end of the wall slopes down to merge with the rocks of this point (I.3 ¶52). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: se confondait peu à peu avec les roches de la pointe méridionale
  - The right bank of the river falls away to its end (I.4 ¶47). *(Drafted.)*

  - The southern point of I.3 and I.4 is read here as this cape: I.11 gives the same sharp cape to the south-east end of the Union Bay, and I.11 ¶75 names it (I.11 ¶22). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
