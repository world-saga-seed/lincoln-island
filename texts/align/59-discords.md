# III.17: White against the French (discords)

*Labeller r. Beads corrected: none.*

### 1. condensed: the near-unconscious prostration (III.17 ¶3; White ¶3)
- The French says the long prostration kept Nemo almost without consciousness; White says only that they observed his prostration. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: qui le tint presque sans connaissance
  - white: Smith and Spilett observed with great attention his prostration.

### 2. condensed: "I must and will die here" (III.17 ¶11; White ¶11)
- Nemo says he must (je dois) and will die here; White gives only the wish. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Je dois et je veux mourir ici.
  - white: I wish to die here.

### 3. changed: the horizon he had limited to these works (III.17 ¶14; White ¶15)
- The French says Nemo had limited his horizon to these masterpieces during his years in the abyss; White says only that they had been ever visible to him. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: auxquels il avait limité son horizon
  - white: which had been ever visible to him

### 4. changed: "Cap-Nemo" (III.17 ¶16; White ¶17)
- White prints the captain's name as "Cap-Nemo", a form the French does not have here (my reading: a misprint). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: le capitaine Nemo se retourna vers les colons
  - white: Cap-Nemo turned to the colonists

### 5. changed: gratitude becomes "a favor" (III.17 ¶17; White ¶18)
- Nemo asks whether they think they owe him some gratitude; White has him ask whether they wish to do him a favor, which turns his question about their debt into an offer. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Vous croyez, messieurs, me devoir quelque reconnaissance ?…
  - white: “You wish to do me a favor?”

### 6. cut: the entrance has risen (III.17 ¶26; White ¶26)
- Nemo gives the reason the Nautilus cannot leave, that the entrance of the grotto has risen; White drops the clause. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: dont l’entrée s’est exhaussée
  - white: The Nautilus is imprisoned in this grotto.

### 7. cut: "from up there", and "I do not fear them" (III.17 ¶28; White ¶28)
- Nemo says he will be associated with their works "from up there" and that he does not fear them; White gives only that he will be present with them. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Je serai donc, de là-haut, associé à vos œuvres, et je ne les crains pas !
  - white: I shall be ever present with you in your works.

### 8. cut: the water enters the reservoirs (III.17 ¶32; White ¶32)
- The French says the water will fill the reservoirs; White says only that it will penetrate. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: L’eau pénétrera dans les réservoirs
  - white: The water will penetrate

### 9. changed: they lie down by the lens (III.17 ¶42; White ¶42)
- The colonists stretch out beside the lens-shaped pane; White has them "see" it. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Là, ils s’étendirent près d’une épaisse vitre lenticulaire
  - white: Then they saw a thick lenticular glass

### 10. cut: the range of the electric light (III.17 ¶42; White ¶42)
- The French says the rays lit the water over a considerable distance; White ends at "by means of the electric light". *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: que les rayons électriques devaient éclairer sur une distance considérable
  - white: by means of the electric light

### 11. cut: known only a few hours (III.17 ¶43; White ¶43)
- The French says the protector they had known scarcely a few hours was on the eve of death; White drops how short their acquaintance was. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: que ce protecteur qu’ils auraient connu quelques heures à peine
  - white: he, their protector, whose arm had been so often raised to aid them

### 12. changed: "perhaps" removed from Pencroff's remark (III.17 ¶45; White ¶45)
- Pencroff thinks Nemo perhaps found no more peace at the bottom of the sea than elsewhere; White states flatly that he did not find rest even there. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: il n’y a peut-être pas trouvé plus de tranquillité qu’ailleurs
  - white: he has not found rest even there

### 13. cut: the basalt that closed the cavern (III.17 ¶50; White ¶50)
- Smith says the entrance is closed by a rise of the basalt rocks; White says only that the Nautilus cannot get out. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: dont l’entrée est maintenant fermée par un exhaussement des roches basaltiques
  - white: Aside from the fact that it cannot get out of this cavern

### 14. cut: a station "for our navy" (III.17 ¶61; White ¶59)
- Smith says the station would be for the American navy; White says only "a station". *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: d’y fonder pour notre marine une relâche
  - white: to establish a station

### 15. changed: "must return, must die" becomes "wish" (III.17 ¶62; White ¶60)
- Nemo says one must return to the fatherland and must die there; White says "we wish". *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: c’est là qu’il faut retourner ! C’est là que l’on doit mourir !…
  - white: It is there we wish to return! It is there we wish to die!

### 16. changed: "dead to all I knew" becomes "I die long after them" (III.17 ¶64; White ¶62)
- Nemo says he has long been dead to all those he knew; White has him say he dies long after those he knew, which reverses the sense. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: je suis mort depuis longtemps pour tous ceux que j’ai connus
  - white: I die long after those whom I have known

### 17. changed: the charge to leave the island becomes a statement (III.17 ¶64; White ¶62)
- Nemo tells them they must try everything to leave Lincoln Island and see their native soil; White has him say that they wish to leave it and return to their country. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Vous devez donc tout tenter pour quitter l’île Lincoln et pour revoir le sol où vous êtes nés.
  - white: You wish to leave Lincoln Island and to return to your country.

### 18. changed: "a few minutes only" becomes "some time" (III.17 ¶72; White ¶69)
- Smith stays a few minutes only with Nemo; White says "for some time". *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Cyrus Smith resta quelques minutes seulement enfermé avec le capitaine Nemo
  - white: Smith remained for some time alone with Captain Nemo

## Outline slips

- Beat 3 gives Nemo's words "à un jour donné" as "at a fixed day"; the French means "on some day", in the future, and fixes no day. *(Drafted.)*
  - outline: With the treasure they can do good things "at a fixed day"
  - quote: Avec ce trésor, vous pourrez faire, à un jour donné, de bonnes choses.
- Beat 4 says Nemo tells Smith "by a gesture"; the gesture is Smith's, and Nemo answers it. *(Drafted.)*
  - outline: He tells Smith, by a gesture, not to fear
  - quote: Et, sur un geste de Cyrus Smith, le capitaine ajouta :
- Beat 6's heading calls the talk "afternoon"; the French gives no hour for it. *(Drafted.)*
  - outline: ### 6. The afternoon talk; Nemo speaks to Smith alone
  - quote: Là, ils prirent quelque nourriture et rentrèrent dans le salon.
