# The landing place: where the balloon came down and the four came ashore

*The sandy, stony stretch of shore where the balloon last touched; Smith disappeared half a mile to the north.*

Keywords: atterrissage, shore, beach, balloon fall, first land, galets

- **What it is.**
  - Less than two minutes passed between Smith's disappearance and the others' landing (I.3 ¶5). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Il ne s’était pas écoulé deux minutes
  - Smith disappeared in the north of the coast, about half a mile from where they had landed (I.3 ¶13). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: à un demi-mille de l’endroit où les naufragés venaient d’atterrir
  - The ground is sandy mixed with stones, without any vegetation (I.3 ¶14). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ils foulaient du pied un sol sablonneux, mêlé de pierres
  - It lies about two hundred paces from the Chimneys, with pebbles and hollows in the rocks (I.5 ¶26). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: à deux cents pas environ des Cheminées
- **On the page.**
  - Pencroff and Harbert searched it for the lost matchbox among the pebbles and rock hollows and found nothing (I.5 ¶27). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: au milieu des galets, dans le creux des roches, les recherches furent faites minutieusement
- **Appears in:** I.3, I.5
- **Open:**
  - I.3 ¶25 says the castaways were thrown "sur un îlot"; I.5 ¶26 puts the place they landed "à deux cents pas environ des Cheminées", and I.5 ¶42 puts the balloon's next-to-last fall within Nab and Spilett's search of the mainland coast. Carded neither way.
    - quote: non sur un continent, pas même sur une île, mais sur un îlot
