# Tadornes: the shelducks of the south-east marsh

*White-bodied ducks with green heads and a chestnut belt, taken by the dozen in the marsh that now bears their name.*

Keywords: tadorne, shelduck, canard, duck, marais, marsh, Tadorn’s-fens, gibier aquatique, flèche

- **What it is.**
  - Ducks white of body with a cinnamon belt, green head, black, white and rufous wing, and a flattened bill (I.21 ¶55). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: blancs de corps avec ceinture cannelle, tête verte, aile noire, blanche et rousse, bec aplati
  - Harbert names them "tadornes" (I.21 ¶55). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: qu’Harbert reconnut pour des « tadornes »
  - A dozen are taken with arrows, the silent arrow not frightening the birds as a gunshot would (I.21 ¶55). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: d’une douzaine de canards
    - quote: la flèche silencieuse eut l’avantage de ne point effrayer ces volatiles
  - They are a plentiful reserve of water-game; the colonists think several kinds might be acclimatised round the lake, if not domesticated (I.21 ¶55). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une abondante réserve de gibier aquatique
    - quote: sinon domestiqués, du moins acclimatés aux environs du lac
  - Their name is given to the marshy part of the island, the "marais des Tadornes" ("Tadorn’s-fens") (I.21 ¶55, ¶56). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dont le nom fut donné à cette partie marécageuse de l’île
    - quote: le marais des Tadornes (Tadorn’s-fens)
  - The game there is easy to approach (I.22 ¶51). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Le gibier devait être facile à approcher
- **Appears in:** I.21, I.22
