# The dwarf acacia: a bush whose thorns served as hooks

*A bush of dwarf acacias whose strong curved thorns Pencroff used as hooks.*

Keywords: acacias nains, épines, thorns, bush, hooks

- **What it is.**
  - It is a bush of dwarf acacias (I.6 ¶41). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un buisson d’acacias nains
  - It bears big thorns, very strong, with curved points (I.6 ¶41). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: De grosses épines très-fortes, à pointes recourbées
  - Pencroff tied its thorns to the ends of his lianas as hooks (I.6 ¶41). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: furent liées aux extrémités des lianes en guise d’hameçon
- **On the page.** Named once; where the bush stood is not said, only that Pencroff found it on the walk with Harbert.
- **Appears in:** I.6 ¶41
