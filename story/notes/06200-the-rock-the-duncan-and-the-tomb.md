# The rock, the Duncan and the tomb (notes)

## Notes for drafting
- **Where it sits.** The French III.20, chapter 06200, the last of the book, after 06100. It runs from the night of 8 to 9 March 1869 (implied) through the rock, 24 March and the rescue, to the return to America and a closing summary. *(Drafted.)*
- **Cast.** The six colonists and Top (¶2): Cyrus Smith, Gédéon Spilett, Harbert, Pencroff, Nab, Ayrton (¶4); Jup, dead (¶3); Robert Grant, captain of the Duncan (¶26); Lord and Lady Glenarvan, Captain John Mangles and his wife, Major Mac Nabbs (¶51, ¶54); Captain Nemo, dead (¶10, ¶37). *(Drafted.)*
- **Places.** The rock, thirty by fifteen feet, ten feet out of water (¶1); the lost island's parts (¶2); Tabor Island (¶9, ¶12, ¶28); the Duncan's steamer cabin (¶20); a point a hundred miles north-east of Tabor (¶28); America (¶50); the state of Iowa (¶51); the colony with a Mercy, a Franklin, a Lake Grant and Far-West forests (¶52). *(Drafted.)*
- **Things.** The rock (¶1); the food stores (¶6, ¶7); a hollow of rainwater (¶6); the notice left at Tabor (¶34, ¶36); the document found at the corral, same hand (¶37); the Bonadventure (¶38); the casket, saved by Ayrton (¶45); the pearl sent to Lady Glenarvan (¶51); the New Lincoln Herald (¶53). *(Drafted.)*
- **Time.** Nine days on the rock by 18 March (¶6–¶7); five more days (¶11); the morning of 24 March (¶15); fifteen days afterwards, the landing in America (¶50, so about 8 April 1869, my reading); the colony's years (¶53–¶55); "pendant quatre ans" on the island (¶55). *(Drafted.)* Chapter covers about 17 days in scene and the later years in summary. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- **Clues.** Dotted lines in place of ¶18 and ¶19 (the print's gap). The notice's text "résidence actuelle d’Ayrton et de cinq colons américains" (¶36); the same hand as the corral document (¶37); Nemo's trip to Tabor in the Bonadventure (¶38). Nothing is left open about who wrote the notice. *(Drafted.)*
- **Length.** 55 paragraphs (¶1 to ¶55), 1,770 words; one plate caption and one loose line ("fin de la troisième et dernière partie"). *(Drafted.)*

## Open and flags
- Two days of tins, six days of life. ¶7 says on 18 March "il ne leur restait plus de conserves que pour deux jours", and ¶11 says "Pendant les cinq jours qui suivirent" they ate sparingly; the sighting is on 24 March (¶15). The text does not reconcile these. Card neither. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- "après douze ans d’expiation" (¶26): the year Ayrton was left on Tabor is in earlier chapters not given to me; the count should be checked there against 1869. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- "à cent milles de là dans le nord-est" (¶28), asked by Smith of the Duncan's course from Tabor; the distance and bearing to be checked against the island's place in earlier chapters. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- ¶28 has the Duncan "où vous n’aviez plus trouvé Ayrton" at Tabor, while ¶26 says it was sent to fetch him there; the notice found there points to Lincoln (¶34, ¶36). Consistent as read. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- ¶3 lists Jup dead, but ¶53 says "Pas un des anciens colons de l’île Lincoln ne manquait" of the six men only; Top is not mentioned after ¶2. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- ¶44 prints "« Où faut-il déposer ce coffret !" with an exclamation mark at the end of a question, as the text has it. *(Drafted.)*
- No Wikisource correction touches this chapter. *(Drafted.)*
- Seam with 06100: ¶81 ends with the sea covering the island's place; ¶1 begins on the rock; ¶4 puts the colonists in their tent when thrown into the sea, as III.19 ¶60 places the tent by the Mercy mouth. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
