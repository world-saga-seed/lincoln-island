# The firewood: dry dead wood gathered under the conifers

*Dry dead wood lying under the riverside conifers, bundled and floated to the Chimneys.*

Keywords: bois mort, fagots, fuel, kindling, bûches, combustible

- **What it is.**
  - Pencroff also meant it to stop the openings through which the wind blew in the Chimneys (I.4 ¶27). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: pour boucher ces ouvertures
  - Pencroff, not knowing the trees' names, ranks them "bois à brûler" (I.4 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: je sais du moins les ranger dans la catégorie du « bois à brûler »
  - Wood from the forest is dead and very dry, and would burn quickly (I.4 ¶32). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ce bois étant très-sec, devait rapidement brûler
  - It was gathered in bundles tied up, each carrying a load in proportion to his strength (I.4 ¶38). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une charge de bois, liée en fagots
- **On the page.**
  - Its large store was put in one of the Chimneys' chambers (I.5 ¶11). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: La provision de bois fut emmagasinée dans l’une des chambres
  - A few logs mixed with small wood went on the hearth stones (I.5 ¶11). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: quelques bûches, entremêlées de menu bois
  - Pencroff kept the fire up through the first night without sparing it (I.5 ¶89). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: auquel il n’épargna pas le combustible
