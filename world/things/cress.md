# The cress: the wild cress of the Mercy bank

*Wild cress among the crucifers of the cabbage genus, taken up on the Mercy and later grown on the plateau.*

Keywords: cresson, cress, crucifers, cabbage, chou, Harbert

- **What it is.**
  - It is among the crucifers of the cabbage genus that Harbert finds on the Mercy bank and that it will certainly be possible to "civilise" by transplanting (II.3 ¶13). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: appartenant au genre chou, qu’il serait certainement possible de « civiliser » par la transplantation
    - quote: c’étaient du cresson
  - It is uprooted with care and carried off in the pirogue (II.3 ¶20). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: avaient été déracinées avec soin
  - In January 1866 it is among the wild plants brought back from the forests, to be modified by intelligent cultivation and to temper the nitrogenous diet (II.8 ¶14). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: épinards, cresson, raifort, raves
    - quote: qu’une culture intelligente devait bientôt modifier
    - quote: tempérer le régime d’alimentation azotée
- **Appears in:** II.3, II.8
