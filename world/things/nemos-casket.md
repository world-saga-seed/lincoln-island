# Nemo’s casket: the casket of diamonds and pearls Nemo leaves the colonists

*A small casket worth several millions in diamonds, with a collection of pearls, the one memento Nemo allows to survive the Nautilus.*

Keywords: coffret, casket, diamants, perles, treasure, trésor, cent fortunes, souvenir, héritage, Nemo, Dakkar

- **What it is.**
  - It holds diamonds worth several millions, most of them mementos of the time when, as father and husband, Nemo almost believed in happiness (III.17 ¶28). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: renferme pour plusieurs millions de diamants
    - quote: la plupart, souvenirs de l’époque où, père et époux, j’ai presque cru au bonheur
  - It holds also a collection of pearls gathered by Nemo and his friends at the bottom of the sea (III.17 ¶28). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une collection de perles recueillies par mes amis et moi au fond des mers
  - It is the only memento of Prince Dakkar left to the colonists, all else the Nautilus holds being to vanish with him (III.17 ¶28, ¶86). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Un seul souvenir vous restera du prince Dakkar
    - quote: l’unique souvenir que leur eût légué leur bienfaiteur
  - Nemo says that with this treasure the colonists will be able, some day, to do good things, and that in hands like theirs money can be no peril (III.17 ¶28). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Avec ce trésor, vous pourrez faire, à un jour donné, de bonnes choses
    - quote: l’argent ne saurait être un péril
  - It is called a casket that held "a hundred fortunes" (III.17 ¶86). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ce coffret qui renfermait cent fortunes
  - Nemo tells Smith to take it the next day on leaving the saloon, and the colonists carry it out of the Nautilus (III.17 ¶30, ¶86). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Demain, vous prendrez ce coffret
    - quote: après avoir emporté l’unique souvenir
- **Appears in:** III.17
