# Jup’s clothes: the white jacket and breeches and apron, and the padded dressing-gown

*A jacket and short breeches of white cloth and an apron whose pockets Jup will not have searched, given him when he was raised to valet; later a good wadded dressing-gown against the cold.*

Keywords: jaquette, culotte, tablier, apron, Jup, valet de chambre, clothes, poches, robe de chambre, dressing-gown, frileux

- **What it is.**
  - When he is raised to valet’s duties Jup is dressed in a jacket, short breeches of white cloth and an apron (II.8 ¶20). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: fut élevé aux fonctions de valet de chambre
    - quote: Il avait été vêtu d’une jaquette, d’une culotte courte en toile blanche et d’un tablier
  - The pockets of the apron delight him, and he will not have anyone search them (II.8 ¶20). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dont les poches faisaient son bonheur
    - quote: ne souffrait pas qu’on vînt y fouiller
  - Jup is a little chilly, his one fault, and a good wadded dressing-gown has to be made for him (III.14 ¶50). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Maître Jup se montrait un peu frileux
    - quote: il fallut lui faire une bonne robe de chambre, bien ouatée
- **Appears in:** II.8, III.14
