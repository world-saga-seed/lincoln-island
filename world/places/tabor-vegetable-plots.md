# The Tabor plots: the old vegetable beds in Tabor’s clearings

*Abandoned vegetable plantings in the clearings of Tabor Island, going back to a rather remote time, still giving seed.*

Keywords: potager, plots, cultures, plantes potagères, clairières, clearings, abandoned, Tabor, seed, graines

- **What it is.**
  - In places, in the middle of clearings, the ground had been planted with vegetables at a rather remote time, probably (II.13 ¶79). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il était visible que la terre avait été plantée de plantes potagères à une époque assez reculée probablement
  - Harbert knows among them potatoes, chicory, sorrel, carrots, cabbages and turnips (see [the potatoes](../things/potatoes.md); [the chicory](../things/chicory.md); [the sorrel](../things/sorrel.md); [the carrots](../things/carrots.md); [the cabbages](../things/cabbages.md); [the turnips](../things/turnips.md)) (II.13 ¶80). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des pommes de terre, des chicorées, de l’oseille, des carottes, des choux, des navets
  - From their state, Spilett fears the islet has not been inhabited for a long time, and Harbert says that an inhabitant, whoever he was, would not have neglected so important a culture (II.13 ¶82, ¶83). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: on peut craindre que l’îlot ne soit plus habité depuis longtemps
    - quote: un habitant, quel qu’il fût, n’aurait pas négligé une culture si importante
  - Their seed is gathered to sow on Lincoln (see [the Tabor seeds](../things/tabor-seeds.md)) (II.13 ¶80). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il suffisait de recueillir la graine pour enrichir le sol de l’île Lincoln
- **Appears in:** II.13
