# The two bridges: the two small plank bridges over the new outlet

*Two small bridges twenty to twenty-five feet long, of trees merely squared with the axe, one on the plateau and one on the beach, which later become two of the plateau's liftable bridges.*

Keywords: ponceaux, bridges, passerelles, plateau, grève, new outlet, carpentry

- **What it is.**
  - They are two bridges, one on the Grande-Vue plateau and one on the beach itself, needed because the new outlet cut by the mine crosses the plateau and the beach and has to be crossed to go north (I.20 ¶29). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: deux ponceaux, l’un sur le plateau de Grande-Vue, l’autre sur la grève même
  - Each is twenty to twenty-five feet long, framed with a few trees only squared with the axe, and took a few days (I.20 ¶29). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: longs de vingt à vingt-cinq pieds, et dont quelques arbres, seulement équarris à la hache, formèrent toute la charpente
  - In Smith's plan the plateau is guarded on the north by the new stream, and the two small bridges stand upstream and downstream of the fall; two more are to be built, one over the ditch and one on the left bank of the Mercy (II.7 ¶19, ¶23). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les deux ponceaux déjà établis en amont et en aval de la chute
    - quote: l’un sur le fossé que je vous propose de creuser, et l’autre sur la rive gauche de la Mercy
  - Bridges and small bridges are to be raisable at will, so that the plateau is safe from surprise (II.7 ¶23). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: si ces pont et ponceaux peuvent être levés à volonté
  - On 23 December the small bridges are not closed, so two onaggas stray onto the plateau (II.7 ¶53). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dont les ponceaux n’avaient pas été fermés
  - Pencroff tips the small bridge of the Creek-Glycérine and imprisons them (II.7 ¶58). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: jusqu’au ponceau du Creek-Glycérine, le fit basculer
- **Appears in:** I.20, II.7
