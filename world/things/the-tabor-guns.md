# The Tabor guns: the two shotguns of the Tabor hut, one broken

*Two shotguns standing in the Tabor hut, one of them broken, brought back among the castaway’s arms.*

Keywords: fusils, shotguns, armes, arms, brisé, broken, Tabor, naufragé

- **What it is.**
  - The Tabor hut holds two shotguns, one of them broken (II.14 ¶5). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: deux fusils de chasse, dont l’un était brisé
  - Spilett says these arms and tools will complete the equipment of Granite-house (II.14 ¶35). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ces armes, ces outils compléteront le matériel de Granite-house
  - Harbert and Spilett carry the utensils and arms back to the boat (II.14 ¶80). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: rapportant les ustensiles et les armes
  - Smith, examining the arms and tools after the unloading, sees nothing that could establish the unknown’s identity (II.15 ¶44). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ayant examiné les armes, les outils, ne vit rien qui pût le mettre à même d’établir l’identité de l’inconnu
- **Appears in:** II.14, II.15
