# The schooner: the new ship the colonists build to replace the Bonadventure

*A schooner of two hundred and fifty to three hundred tons planned by Smith, its oak keel of a hundred and ten feet laid at the Chimneys about 15 May of the third year, built of Far-West oak and elm with the brig’s ironwork.*

Keywords: goélette, schooner, navire, new ship, vessel, quille, keel, chantier, Pencroff, Ayrton, Tabor document, shipbuilding, brig ironwork

- **What it is.**
  - Harbert says they will make another Bonadventure, a larger one, with the ironwork and rigging of the brig (III.13 ¶59). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: nous ferons un autre Bonadventure, un plus grand
    - quote: toutes les ferrures, tout le gréement du brick
  - The loss is to be repaired as soon as possible (III.13 ¶64). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: cette perte devrait être réparée au plus tôt
  - Building it is urgent, since a document giving Ayrton’s new home must be left on Tabor as soon as possible (III.14 ¶6). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: besogne véritablement urgente
    - quote: déposer le plus tôt possible à l’île Tabor un document qui indiquât la nouvelle résidence d’Ayrton
  - Smith proposes a ship larger than the Bonadventure, able if need be to carry them to the Polynesian archipelagos or to New Zealand (III.14 ¶8). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: construire un navire qui, le cas échéant, pût nous transporter soit aux archipels polynésiens, soit à la Nouvelle-Zélande
  - Its size is put at two hundred and fifty to three hundred tons, and Pencroff reckons seven or eight months at least to build it (III.14 ¶10, ¶11). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un navire de deux cent cinquante à trois cents tonneaux
    - quote: Sept ou huit mois au moins
  - Smith draws the plan and fixes the gauge of the ship (III.14 ¶15). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: faire le plan du navire et d’en déterminer le gabarit
  - Its curves, frame and planking come from oaks and elms of the Far-West forest, felled and hauled to the Chimneys, where the building yard is set up (see [the boatyard](../places/the-boatyard.md)) (III.14 ¶15). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: l’abatage et au charroi des arbres qui devaient fournir les courbes, la membrure et le bordé
    - quote: Ce fut la forêt du Far-West qui donna les meilleures essences en chênes et en ormes
    - quote: transportés aux Cheminées, où fut établi le chantier de construction
  - The timber cannot be used green and must be cut early and left to harden; it is stacked under a plank shed (see [the timber shed](../places/the-timber-shed.md)) (III.14 ¶16, ¶17). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: on ne pouvait les employer verts encore
    - quote: il fallait laisser au temps le soin de les durcir
  - Its keel, laid about 15 May, is of good oak and a hundred and ten feet long, which allows a beam of twenty-five feet (III.14 ¶26). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Cette quille, en bon chêne, mesurait cent dix pieds de longueur
    - quote: une largeur de vingt-cinq pieds
  - The stem and the sternpost, mortised at the two ends of the keel, stand almost upright, and the first frames of the stern are set the week after (III.14 ¶26). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: l’étrave et l’étambot, emmortaisés à chacune de ses extrémités
    - quote: les premiers couples de l’arrière
  - Pencroff and Ayrton are its two most zealous builders; the cold, which hardens the wood to the hardness of iron, makes them give up the building about 10 June (III.14 ¶29). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les deux plus zélés constructeurs du nouveau bâtiment
    - quote: il fallut définitivement abandonner la construction du bateau
  - The brig’s complete rigging is to serve for it (III.14 ¶48). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: du gréement complet, qui servirait au navire en chantier
  - It is to be rigged as a schooner (III.15 ¶9). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: devait être gréé en goëlette
  - Towards the end of September its carcass stands on the yard and its frame is nearly finished, held by a temporary hoop; it is fine forward and very clear aft, fit for a fairly long crossing, but planking, ceiling and deck still need a considerable time (III.15 ¶9). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la carcasse du navire
    - quote: La membrure était presque entièrement terminée
    - quote: fine de l’avant, très-dégagée dans ses façons d’arrière
    - quote: la pose du bordage, du vaigrage intérieur et du pont devait exiger encore un laps considérable de temps
  - Pins and many copper nails drawn from the mutilated planks and curves of the brig save the smiths’ work, though the carpenters have much to do (see [the Speedy’s wreck](the-speedys-wreck.md)) (III.15 ¶9). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les ferrures de l’ancien brick avaient pu être sauvées
    - quote: une grande quantité de clous de cuivre
  - Spilett tells Nemo they are building a ship large enough to carry them to the nearest lands (III.17 ¶65). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Nous construisons un navire, dit Gédéon Spilett, un navire assez grand pour nous transporter aux terres les plus rapprochées
- **Appears in:** III.13, III.14, III.15, III.17
