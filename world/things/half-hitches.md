# The half-hitches: the knot Pencroff always ties

*Two half-hitches, the sailor’s familiar knot that always stays tight, which Pencroff ties instead of the flat knot.*

Keywords: demi-clefs, deux demi-clefs, nœud, noeud, Pencroff, half-hitch

- **What it is.**
  - Pencroff is in the habit of making two half-hitches (III.6 ¶31). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: j’ai l’habitude de faire deux demi-clefs
  - Verne’s note calls it a kind of knot familiar to the sailor, which has the advantage of always staying tight (III.6 n1). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Sorte de nœud familier au marin, et qui a l’avantage de se maintenir toujours serré
- **Appears in:** III.6
