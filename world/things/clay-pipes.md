# The clay pipes: Pencroff's rough pipes, with no tobacco

*Some rough pipes Pencroff made to test the clay; he found them charming, but he had no tobacco.*

Keywords: pipes, smoking, tobacco, bowl, pipe clay

- **What it is.**
  - They are rather rough pipes (I.13 ¶48). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: quelques pipes assez grossières
- **Made from.**
  - Pencroff made them of the prepared clay to see whether it deserved its name of pipe clay (see [the pottery](pottery.md)) (I.13 ¶48). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: désireux de savoir si cette argile, ainsi préparée, justifiait son nom de « terre de pipe »
- **Leads to.**
  - They lack only tobacco, which is a great privation to Pencroff (I.13 ¶48). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: mais auxquelles le tabac manquait, hélas !
  - Pencroff trusts that tobacco will come (I.13 ¶49). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Mais le tabac viendra, comme toutes choses !
- **On the page.**
  - Pencroff took the armoise for tobacco and was told it was not (see [the artemisia](artemisia.md)) (I.13 ¶54). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Est-ce du tabac ?
- **Appears in:** I.13
