# The bows: bows of crejimba wood with hibiscus strings

*Bows of fair power made by Pencroff; the reporter and Harbert became good archers.*

Keywords: arcs, archery, hunting weapon, cord, shooting

- **What it is.**
  - They are bows of fairly great power (I.13 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Pencroff obtint ainsi des arcs d’une assez grande puissance
- **Made from.**
  - The branches came from [the crejimba](crejimba.md) (I.13 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dont les Indiens de l’Amérique méridionale emploient les branches à fabriquer leurs arcs
  - The string came from fibres of [the hibiscus heterophyllus](hibiscus.md) (I.13 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: qui fournit des fibres d’une ténacité remarquable
  - Pencroff made them (I.13 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Pencroff obtint ainsi des arcs
- **Leads to.**
  - Only the arrows were still lacking (see [the arrows](arrows.md)) (I.13 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: auxquels il ne manquait plus que les flèches
  - The reporter and Harbert soon became skilful archers (I.13 ¶36). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: devinrent promptement de très-adroits tireurs d’arc
  - Game was plentiful at the Chimneys (I.13 ¶36). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le gibier de poil et de plume abonda-t-il aux Cheminées
- **On the page.**
  - Pencroff had said that with a knife he would soon make a bow (I.13 ¶20). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: j’aurais vite fait de fabriquer un arc et des flèches
- **Appears in:** I.13
