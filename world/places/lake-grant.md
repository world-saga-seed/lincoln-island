# Lake Grant: the lake west of the brickworks clearing

*A lake whose west shore has the clay and whose north side has the limestone; seen from Mont Franklin on 30 March, named Lake Grant the same day, a lake of fresh, clear, fish-filled water fed by the Red Creek, its overflow not found.*

Keywords: lac Grant, pond, shore, water, west bank, lac Grant, creek, déversoir, overflow, Ontario, vasque, fed by
