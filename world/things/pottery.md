# The pottery: common pipe-clay ware: pots, cups, plates, jars and vats

*Common but clean pottery made of clay with a little lime and quartz, shaped by hand or on pebbles, fired at a high temperature.*

Keywords: poterie, crockery, earthenware, pots, cooking vessels, pipe clay, terre de pipe, kitchenware

- **What it is.**
  - It is plain pottery, very fit for cooking food (I.13 ¶47). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une poterie commune, mais très-propre à la cuisson des aliments
  - Its paste is true pipe clay (I.13 ¶47). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la véritable « terre de pipe »
  - The objects include pots, cups moulded on pebbles of suitable shape, plates, large jars and vats to hold water (I.13 ¶47). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des pots, des tasses qui avaient été moulées sur des galets de formes convenables, des assiettes, de grandes jarres et des cuves pour contenir l’eau
  - Their form was clumsy and defective (I.13 ¶47). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: La forme de ces objets était gauche, défectueuse
  - A plate shows the colonists making it (I.13 pl2). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Les colons fabriquèrent une poterie commune
- **Made from.**
  - The raw material is the [clay](clay.md) of the ground (I.13 ¶47). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: La matière première était cette argile même du sol
  - Smith had a little lime and quartz added (see [the lime](lime.md)) (I.13 ¶47). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un peu de chaux et du quartz
  - The ware is fired at a high temperature in [the pottery kiln](pottery-kiln.md) (I.13 ¶47). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: après qu’ils eurent été cuits à une haute température
- **Leads to.**
  - It supplied the kitchen of the Chimneys with utensils as precious as if the finest kaolin had gone into them (I.13 ¶47). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: aussi précieux que si le plus beau kaolin fût entré dans leur composition
  - Pencroff made some pipes to test the clay (see [the pipes](clay-pipes.md)) (I.13 ¶48). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: se fabriqua quelques pipes assez grossières
  - The first pot-au-feu is named in the chapter summary (I.13 ¶0). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Le premier pot-au-feu.
  - Nab made a pot-au-feu of agouti that evening (I.13 ¶57). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Nab avait préparé un pot-au-feu d’agouti
- **On the page.**
  - The work lasted until 15 April, the colonists doing nothing but pottery (I.13 ¶50). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Les colons, devenus potiers, ne firent pas autre chose que de la poterie
  - The rest of the pottery was carried to the Chimneys on 15 April (I.13 ¶51). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Le reste des poteries fut emporté
- **Appears in:** I.13
