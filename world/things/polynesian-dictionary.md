# The Polynesian dictionary: the English dictionary of the Polynesian languages in the chest

*An English dictionary of the various Polynesian idioms, with no publisher or date.*

Keywords: dictionnaire, dictionary, Polynesian, idiomes, languages, English, chest

- **What it is.**
  - It is a dictionary of the various Polynesian idioms (II.2 ¶53). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: 1 dictionnaire des divers idiomes polynésiens
  - It is in English and bears no publisher’s name and no date of publication (the same is said of the other dictionary) (II.2 ¶65). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: tous deux étaient anglais, mais ils ne portaient aucun nom d’éditeur, ni aucune date de publication
