# The banksias: trees of the Red Creek

*Banksias in the vault of trees over the lower Red Creek.*

Keywords: banksia, Australian tree, Red Creek, gum trees

- **What it is.**
  - The Red Creek runs under the vault of casuarinas, banksias and gigantic gum trees (I.12 ¶66). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: sous la voûte des casuarinas, des banksias et des gommiers gigantesques
  - In October, at the edge of the wood, the new foliage of the nettle trees, banksias and deodars appears among the persistent foliage of the conifers (II.20 ¶80). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: au milieu du feuillage persistant des conifères qui formaient la lisière du bois, apparaissait déjà le feuillage nouveau des micocouliers, des banksias et des deodars
- **Appears in:** I.12, II.20
