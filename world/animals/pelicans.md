# Pelicans: water birds of the lake shore

*Pelicans among the water birds of the banks and rock-islet of Lake Grant.*

Keywords: pélican, pelican, lac Grant, lake, oiseaux aquatiques, water birds, rives, banks

- **What it is.**
  - Pelicans strut on the banks and the rock-islet of the lake, among wild ducks, moorhens, red-bills, honeyeaters and lyrebirds (I.12 ¶68). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: se pavanaient des canards sauvages, des pélicans
