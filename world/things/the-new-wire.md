# The new wire: the insulated wire that runs from the corral line to the cave of the Nautilus

*A second wire joined to the corral telegraph at its first post, wrapped like a submarine cable, laid over the mountain to the west and down into the sea, and on into the cave where Nemo’s boat waits.*

Keywords: fil, wire, câble sous-marin, insulated, fil supplémentaire, nouveau fil, corral, telegraph, isoloir, path

- **What it is.**
  - The colonists understand that the dispatch came not from the corral but from the mysterious retreat, which an additional wire joined to the old one links directly to Granite-house (III.15 ¶71). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la dépêche n’était pas partie du corral
    - quote: un fil supplémentaire, raccordé à l’ancien, réunissait directement à Granite-house
  - It hangs from the insulator of the first post beyond the palisade gate down to the ground (III.15 ¶74). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un nouveau fil retombait de l’isoloir jusqu’à terre
  - It trails on the ground, and along its whole length is wrapped in an insulating substance, as a submarine cable is, so that the currents pass freely (III.15 ¶76). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il était entouré d’une substance isolante, comme l’est un câble sous-marin
  - Its course runs through the woods and the southern buttresses of the mountain, hence towards the west (III.15 ¶76). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il semblait s’engager à travers les bois et les contreforts méridionaux de la montagne
    - quote: il courait vers l’ouest
  - It is stretched now on the low branches of trees, now laid along the ground (III.15 ¶80). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: tantôt tendu sur les basses branches des arbres, tantôt se déroulant à terre
  - It runs on over the south-west buttress and the arid plateau that ends in the wall of basalts, straight to the sea (III.15 ¶82). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Il fallut remonter le contrefort du sud-ouest
    - quote: ce fil courût directement à la mer
  - It enters the rocks by the steep slope of a narrow, capricious ravine (III.15 ¶86). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le fil s’engageait au milieu des roches, en suivant la pente assez raide d’un ravin étroit
  - At the foot of the basalt wall it makes a sharp angle and touches the shore rocks, then follows a narrow shoulder parallel to the sea (III.15 ¶89, ¶90). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le fil, faisant un angle brusque, toucha les roches du littoral
    - quote: Le fil le suivait
  - It plunges into the sea (III.15 ¶91). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il s’enfonçait dans la mer
  - At the cave’s mouth it turns at a right angle and goes in (III.15 ¶103). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le fil, se coudant à angle droit, pénétrait dans cette gueule béante
  - Inside the cave it is hung on projections of the rock along the right-hand wall (III.15 ¶119, ¶120). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: si le fil courait toujours le long de cette paroi
    - quote: Le fil était là, accroché aux saillies du roc
- **Appears in:** III.15
- **Open:**
  - The text does not say when or by whom it was laid, only that it was found hanging from the first post on the night of 15 October (III.15 ¶74). *(Drafted.)*
