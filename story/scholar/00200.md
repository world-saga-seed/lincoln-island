# The scholar’s notes to chapter II

*Each note is set after the words its “After:” line quotes, exactly as they stand in story/prose/00200-an-episode-of-the-civil-war.md. Delete this file and the chapter builds without them (R1121-163).*

## a

After: Their aerial voyage had lasted five days.

The balloon left Richmond on the night of the 20th of March and came down on the 24th, as this chapter says at its close and as chapter I says of the day itself. That is four days, and the close of this chapter gives four. I take the four.

## b

After: It was not until five days later that the breaking of the clouds enabled them to see the vast sea extending below the balloon,

Five days after the night of the 20th is the 25th, the day after the landing. Chapter I has the sea under the basket at four in the afternoon of the 23rd, the third day out, and the coast reached on the 24th. I take chapter I’s dates; the five days agree with nothing else in the papers.
