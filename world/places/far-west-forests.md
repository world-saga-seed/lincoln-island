# Forêts du Far-West: the mass of impenetrable woods that cover the Serpentine peninsula

*The woods named the forests of the Far-West on 30 March: the whole mass of impenetrable trees covering the Serpentine peninsula, and a way back to the Mercy.*

Keywords: Far-West, forêts, bois, woods, forest, Serpentine, impenetrable, forêt, west

- **What it is.**
  - The name forêts du Far-West was given to all the mass of impenetrable woods that covered the Serpentine peninsula (I.11 ¶81). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: tout ce massif d’impénétrables bois qui couvraient la presqu’île Serpentine, le nom de forêts du Far-West
  - One could reach the Mercy through the woods of the Far-West, as through the lake road, and so come to the Grande-Vue plateau (I.12 ¶15). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: soit que nous rejoignions la Mercy à travers les bois du Far-West
  - The forests of the Far-West are not yet explored; the great excursion is held for the first fine days of spring (I.19 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: n’étaient donc point explorées
    - quote: On réservait cette importante excursion pour les premiers beaux jours du printemps prochain
  - The wooded part is said to stretch from the Mercy to the promontoire du Reptile (I.20 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: toute cette partie boisée qui s’étendait depuis la Mercy jusqu’au promontoire du Reptile
  - The first lines of its trees lie about four miles west of the south-east beach (I.21 ¶20). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les premières lignes d’arbres des forêts du Far-West
  - Felines are supposed to live in its thick forests (see [the claw prints](../things/claw-prints.md)) (I.22 ¶31). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les épaisses forêts du Far-West
  - The unexplored part of the island is the high forests on the right of the Mercy from its mouth to the end of the Serpentine peninsula, and the whole west coast (I.22 ¶60). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: depuis son embouchure jusqu’à l’extrémité de la presqu’île Serpentine
    - quote: sur toute la côte occidentale
  - Seen from the top of a kauri, the woods of the Far-West form an impenetrable dome covering several square miles, without a clearing or a gap (II.1 ¶56). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Au milieu des bois du Far-West
    - quote: La forêt formait un impénétrable dôme, mesurant plusieurs milles carrés
  - The wood of the Jacamar and the forests of the Far-West are two names for the forest that the colonists see stretching out of sight on both sides of the upper Mercy; no sign of man is found in it, and no axe has ever notched its trees (II.3 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: aussi bien sous le nom de bois du Jacamar que sous celui de forêts du Far-West
    - quote: jamais la hache du bûcheron n’avait entaillé ces arbres
  - It seems to thin out toward the Far-West, where the trees stand farther apart and are splendid (II.3 ¶33). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la forêt tendait à s’éclaircir du côté du Far-West
  - The part of the Far-West west of the camp seems packed with wood, like an immense thicket of very varied species (II.4 ¶1). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Cette partie du Far-West paraissait serrée de bois
  - Its trees there are mostly of medium size, since their number has hindered their growth (II.4 ¶5). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: généralement de taille médiocre, car leur nombre avait nui à leur développement
  - On the west coast the forest itself forms the shore (see [the west coast](the-west-coast.md)) (II.4 ¶34). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: La forêt formait le littoral
  - The last trees of the forest of the Far-West die at the promontoire du Reptile (II.4 ¶44). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Les derniers arbres de la forêt du Far-West venaient mourir à cette pointe
  - A road has to be broken through its thick masses to bring the cart to port Ballon, and by the end of December a straight cut runs through it from the Mercy bend to the port (see [the road to port Ballon](port-ballon-road.md)) (II.7 ¶32, ¶61). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: nécessité de frayer une route à travers les épais massifs du Far-West
    - quote: une coupée avait été faite à travers la forêt du Far-West
  - Excursions into the forests of the Jacamar and the Far-West bring back a real harvest of wild plants, spinach, cress, horseradish and radishes (II.8 ¶14). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Plusieurs excursions furent faites dans les forêts du Jacamar et du Far-West
  - The great woods that cover the Serpentine peninsula are still the unknown part of the island, where the wild beasts take refuge (II.8 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ces grands bois qui couvraient la presqu’île Serpentine, où se réfugiaient les fauves
  - The cycads, "bread trees", grow in the portion of the Far-West on the left of the Mercy (II.9 ¶44, ¶55). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dans la forêt du Far-West, sur la gauche de la Mercy
    - quote: la portion du Far-West où poussaient ces cycas
  - Its unexplored parts are an inextricable tangle of magnificent trees pressed against one another; the sun hardly pierces the boughs, one needs a compass to find the way, and game is rarer there (II.10 ¶19). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: C’était un inextricable fouillis d’arbres magnifiques et pressés les uns contre les autres
    - quote: le soleil perçait à peine les épaisses ramures
    - quote: le gibier était plus rare en ces endroits
  - In its south-west lies a clearing, where the trees stand wider apart, and where tobacco grows (see [tobacco](../things/tobacco.md)) (II.10 ¶21). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dans le sud-ouest du Far-West
  - Parts of it, still unexplored in the autumn, are rich in game (II.12 ¶85). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: quelques parties jusque-là inexplorées des forêts du Far-West, qui étaient fort giboyeuses
  - In the depths of the forests the explorers need not fear the heat, since the sun’s rays scarcely pierce the thick foliage entangled overhead (II.18 ¶55). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les rayons solaires perçaient à peine l’épaisse ramure

- **Open:**
  - How much of the great forest the name covers: I.11 ¶32 says the trees make "two thirds" of the island a forest; I.11 ¶27 has the forest cover the whole south from the mountain to the shore; ¶81 gives the name to the woods of the Serpentine peninsula. The three do not say how they overlap, nor whether it takes in [the evergreen forest](the-evergreen-forest.md) or [the wood of the Jacamar](jacamar-wood.md). Card neither.
    - quote: très-boisée dans toute sa portion méridionale depuis la montagne jusqu’au littoral
    - quote: qui faisaient des deux tiers de l’île une forêt immense
