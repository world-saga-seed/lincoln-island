# The albatross message: the notice in a gummed sack on the bird’s neck

*Spilett’s notice to the New-York Herald, in a sack of strong gummed canvas tied to a captured albatross and sent off on 30 June.*

Keywords: albatros, message, notice, Herald, New-York Herald, sac, sack, toile gommée, bouteille, pigeons, courrier

- **What it is.**
  - Spilett had thought several times of a notice in a bottle thrown into the sea, or of one entrusted to pigeons (II.11 ¶31). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: soit à jeter à la mer une notice renfermée dans une bouteille
  - This is called the first attempt by the colonists to communicate with their fellow men (II.11 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la première tentative faite par les colons pour communiquer avec leurs semblables
  - A succinct notice written by Spilett is put into a sack of strong gummed canvas, with an urgent request that whoever finds it send it to the offices of the New-York Herald (II.11 ¶35). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une notice succincte qui fut mise dans un sac de forte toile gommée
    - quote: de la faire parvenir aux bureaux du New-York Herald
  - The sack is tied to the albatross’s neck and not its leg, because these birds are used to resting on the sea’s surface (II.11 ¶35). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: attaché au cou de l’albatros, et non à sa patte, car ces oiseaux ont l’habitude de se reposer à la surface de la mer
  - The bird vanishes in the mists of the west; Harbert says it goes towards New Zealand; Pencroff expects little from this means of correspondence (II.11 ¶35, ¶37, ¶38). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: disparaître au loin dans les brumes de l’ouest
    - quote: Vers la Nouvelle-Zélande
    - quote: n’attendait pas grand résultat de ce mode de correspondance
- **Appears in:** II.11
- **Open:**
  - Whether the message reaches anyone is not told in II.11 or II.12. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
