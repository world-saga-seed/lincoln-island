# The burnt linen: a half-burnt rag of Pencroff's handkerchief kept as tinder

*Part of Pencroff's big-checked handkerchief, half burnt, put by in a rock cavity as a stand-in for tinder, and lost to the sea.*

Keywords: linge brûlé, chiffon, mouchoir, handkerchief, rag, amadou, cloth, tinder substitute

- **What it is.**
  - Harbert proposes it as a substitute for tinder (I.6 ¶15). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Le linge brûlé, répondit le jeune garçon. Cela peut, au besoin, servir d’amadou.
  - It is made from part of Pencroff's large-checked handkerchief, soon reduced to a half-burnt rag, at the cost of a piece of the handkerchief (I.6 ¶16). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le mouchoir à grands carreaux de Pencroff fut bientôt réduit, pour une partie, à l’état de chiffon à demi brûlé
  - It was set down in the central chamber of [the Chimneys](../places/the-chimneys.md) as an inflammable material (I.6 ¶16). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Cette matière inflammable fut déposée dans la chambre centrale
- **On the page.**
  - Pencroff regrets having forgotten to bring it on the search, for he could easily have lit it by striking two pebbles (I.8 ¶38). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il avait malheureusement oublié d’emporter le linge brûlé, qu’il eût facilement enflammé au choc de deux cailloux
  - After the storm it was gone, the sea having entered the passages (I.8 ¶94). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Le linge brûlé, qui devait servir d’amadou, avait disparu
- **Appears in:** I.6, I.8
