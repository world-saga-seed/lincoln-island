# The corral stream: the small stream that waters the corral, where five bodies lie

*The little stream that crosses the corral, shaded by big trees on the right of the house at the back, on whose bank the five convicts are found dead on 19 February.*

Keywords: ruisseau, stream, cours d’eau, berge, bank, corral, cinq corps, five bodies, convicts

- **What it is.**
  - A small watercourse waters the corral, which has bouquets of trees and a broad carpet of grass (III.12 ¶100). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le petit cours d’eau qui l’arrosait
  - It runs at the back of the corral, on the right of the house, where Top dashes (III.12 ¶102). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: vers le fond du corral, sur la droite de la maison
  - Its bank is shaded by big trees (III.12 ¶105). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le bord du petit ruisseau, ombragé de grands arbres
  - Five bodies lie stretched on its bank, those of the convicts who had landed on the island four months before (III.12 ¶107, ¶108). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Cinq corps, étendus sur la berge
    - quote: C’étaient ceux des convicts qui, quatre mois auparavant, avaient débarqué sur l’île Lincoln
- **Appears in:** III.12, III.13
- **Open:**
  - Whether this is the same stream as the corral’s "petit rio" that loses itself in the Red Creek (II.8 ¶32) is not said (see [the corral](corral.md) and its Open line). *(Drafted.)* *(Drafted.)*
