# The Micronesian archipelago: the group Smith names as a possible home of the land

*An archipelago of the Pacific that Smith names as the possible home of the coast, if it is a desert island.*

Keywords: Micronésie, Micronesia, archipel, island group, Pacific, settle

- **What it is.**
  - Smith supposes the coast may belong to some desert island of a Micronesian archipelago, which he hopes to recognise from the top of the cone (I.9 ¶70). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: au contraire, cette côte appartient à quelque île déserte d’un archipel micronésien
  - In that case he proposes to settle in as if they were never to leave (I.9 ¶70). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: nous aviserons à nous établir ici, comme si nous ne devions jamais en sortir
- **Appears in:** I.9
