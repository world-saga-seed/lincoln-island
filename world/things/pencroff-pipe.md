# Pencroff's pipe: the sailor's pipe, lost at the landing, and the pipe given him later

*Pencroff's pipe was lost at the landing; on 31 May a filled pipe is given him, which goes to Jup in August. The text does not say whether they are one pipe.*

Keywords: pipe, smoker, tobacco, lost

- **What it is.**
  - Pencroff's pipe was lost along with his matchbox (I.5 ¶24). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ma pipe, elle-même, m’a bien quitté
  - On 31 May Spilett holds out a ready-filled pipe to Pencroff, and Harbert an ember (II.10 ¶89, ¶90). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Une pipe, alors ?
    - quote: une pipe toute bourrée
  - On 25 August Jup is found smoking Pencroff’s pipe, and Pencroff makes him a gift of it (II.12 ¶64, ¶69, ¶70). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Il a pris ma pipe !
    - quote: je t’en fais cadeau
  - From then on it is Jup’s, "the ex-pipe of the sailor", hung in his room near his tobacco; he fills it himself and lights it at a live coal (see [Jup’s room](../places/jups-room.md)) (II.12 ¶73). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: l’ex-pipe du marin
    - quote: Il la bourrait lui-même, il l’allumait à un charbon ardent
- **Appears in:** I.5, II.10, II.12, II.20
- **Open:**
  - The text does not say whether the pipe was found again.
    - quote: Ma pipe, elle-même, m’a bien quitté
  - Whether the pipe given to Pencroff on 31 May, and the one Jup later takes as "ma pipe", is the pipe lost in I.5 is not said (II.10 ¶89; II.12 ¶70). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une pipe toute bourrée
    - quote: Il a pris ma pipe !
