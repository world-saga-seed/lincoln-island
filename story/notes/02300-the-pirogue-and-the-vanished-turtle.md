# The Pirogue and the Vanished Turtle (notes)

## Notes for drafting
- **Where it sits.** The first chapter of the second part, "L'Abandonné" (the heading line is part of this file). It takes up at the dinner table of 24 October, where I.22 ended, and runs to 28 October. *(Drafted.)*
- **Cast.** Pencroff (¶3), Cyrus Smith (¶4), Gédéon Spilett (¶8), Harbert (¶11), Nab (¶13). Top does not appear. *(Drafted.)*
- **Places.** The dining hall of Granite-house (¶3, ¶10); the yard ("chantier") where the pirogue is made (¶83); the Mercy, as the pirogue's water (¶20); the forest near the Mercy, with its kauris (¶48); the kauri's crown, with the view south from Claw Cape to the Reptile Promontory and Mount Franklin to the northwest (¶53); the beach two miles from Granite-house (¶61). *(Drafted.)*
- **Things.** The grain of lead (¶1–¶4); the bark pirogue and its sewn seams (¶34); the Douglas firs felled by the hurricane (¶35); a kauri (¶48); a green turtle, three feet long, about 400 lb (¶68); pebbles wedging it (¶74); the cart (¶74). *(Drafted.)*
- **Time.** "Seven months, to the day" since the landing (¶1) is the evening of 24 October. Pencroff promises the pirogue "in five days at the most" (¶20). The next day work starts, the 25th (¶34). 26 October: Harbert and Spilett hunt and he climbs the kauri (¶37, ¶48). "Two days later, 28 October": the turtle (¶60). The turtle is left at low tide and found gone two hours later (¶74). The chapter covers four days. *(Drafted.)*
- **Clues.** (1) The lead shot: how and by whom (¶1–¶9), Cyrus's "we shall find nothing" (¶33) and the link he and Spilett ask about with "the inexplicable circumstances of the engineer's rescue" (¶30). (2) The smoke Harbert thinks he sees in the west and then doubts (¶58). (3) The turtle that disappears despite the pebbles, and Cyrus's explanation, which the narrator doubts (¶74–¶100). (4) Cyrus's fear of Malay pirates (¶17). *(Drafted.)*
- **Length.** 100 paragraphs (¶1–¶100); 3,048 words. Two plate captions (pl1 after ¶14, pl2 after ¶71); no footnotes; the chapter has two heading lines, "DEUXIÈME PARTIE / L’ABANDONNÉ" and "CHAPITRE I". *(Drafted.)*

## Open and flags
- ¶1 calls the creature in which the shot was found "a harmless rodent"; I.22 ¶62 and ¶74 have a peccary, and II.1 ¶5, ¶12 say "the peccary". A peccary is not a rodent. Both sides quoted; not reconciled. *(Drafted.)*
- II.1 ¶18 has Pencroff propose a canoe as a new idea: "would it not be proper, before going to explore, to build a canoe". I.20 ¶20 already says "it was agreed that a canoe would be built, somehow or other". *(Drafted.)*
- ¶14 Pencroff says "for five or six months now" for a shot that, by ¶7, is under three months old: his joke, not a fact. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- ¶15 "depuis quelques semaines seulement" against ¶7 and ¶9 "for three months at most": Cyrus's range, then his narrower guess. *(Drafted.)*
- ¶37 "it was 26 October" and ¶60 "Two days later, 28 October": agree. ¶34 "From the next day" puts the start on 25 October. Pencroff's five days then run to about 29 or 30 October. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- ¶74 "two hours later" the turtle was left; ¶94 "about fifteen feet at most" from the sea, at low tide. ¶97 Cyrus's tide explanation. ¶100 the narrator doubts it. *(Drafted.)*
- No Wikisource correction touches this chapter. *(Drafted.)*
