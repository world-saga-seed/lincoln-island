# Spilett's watch: the reporter's watch, kept by oversight

*The watch Spilett kept when all else was thrown from the basket.*

Keywords: montre, watch, clock, time, reporter

- **What it is.**
  - Spilett kept it, with a notebook, doubtless by oversight (I.6 ¶2). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un carnet et une montre que Gédéon Spilett avait conservée par mégarde
  - Its glass was taken out to make a lens, and put back (I.10 ¶8). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les deux verres qu’il avait enlevés à la montre du reporter et à la sienne
  - It was spared by the seawater, the reporter having been thrown on the sand beyond the waves (I.12 ¶3). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: celle de Gédéon Spilett avait été respectée par l’eau de mer
  - It is an excellent instrument, a true pocket chronometer, that Spilett never forgot to wind carefully each day (I.12 ¶3). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un véritable chronomètre de poche
  - It kept the time of Richmond, and Smith had him leave the hands alone and only wind it exactly (I.12 ¶11). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ne touchez pas aux aiguilles
  - Smith said the Richmond time "may serve us" (I.12 ¶11). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Cela pourra nous servir.
  - Held in hand at the noon observation on 16 April, it gave the true time at Washington, since true and mean time coincide on that day (I.14 ¶70). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: l’heure donnée par Gédéon Spilett serait l’heure vraie qu’il serait alors à Washington
  - When the shadow began to lengthen it read five hours and one minute (I.14 ¶73). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Cinq heures et une minute
- **Appears in:** I.6, I.10, I.12, I.14
- **Open:**
  - Richmond or Washington: I.12 ¶7 to ¶11 call its meridian "à peu près celui de Washington", and I.14 ¶70 treats the hour as the true hour at Washington. The text calls the two meridians nearly the same, no more.
    - quote: méridien qui est à peu près celui de Washington
    - quote: l’heure vraie qu’il serait alors à Washington
