# The Speedy’s papers: the ship’s papers that are not found

*The brig’s papers, which Smith looks for in the cupboards of the poop and does not find; nothing on her says her owner, her master or her nationality, except that her build seems English.*

Keywords: papiers du bord, papers, armateur, owner, port d’attache, nationality, English build, tableau d’arrière, dunette, Speedy

- **What it is.**
  - Smith searches the cupboards of the poop with care and finds no trace of the ship’s papers (III.4 ¶79). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: bien qu’il eût fouillé minutieusement les armoires de la dunette
    - quote: Cyrus Smith n’en trouva pas trace
  - The pirates had evidently destroyed all that concerned either her captain or her owner (III.4 ¶79). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Les pirates avaient évidemment détruit tout ce qui concernait, soit le capitaine, soit l’armateur du Speedy
  - Her home port is not written on the stern board, so that nothing suggests her nationality (III.4 ¶79). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le nom de son port d’attache n’était pas porté au tableau d’arrière
    - quote: rien ne pouvait faire soupçonner sa nationalité
  - From certain forms of her bow Ayrton and Pencroff seem to believe she is of English build (III.4 ¶79). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: à certaines formes de son avant
    - quote: ce brick devait être de construction anglaise
