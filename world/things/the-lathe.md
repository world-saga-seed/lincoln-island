# The lathe: the turning lathe Smith sets up in Granite-house

*A lathe installed by the engineer in the storm of March, turning small utensils, the buttons the colonists lacked, and later the boat’s pulleys.*

Keywords: tour, lathe, turning, boutons, buttons, ustensiles, utensils, poulies, pulleys, workshop

- **What it is.**
  - Smith installs a lathe in Granite-house during the days of bad weather in March (II.9 ¶5, ¶6). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: L’ingénieur installa un tour
  - It turns a few toilet or kitchen utensils and especially buttons, whose lack was keenly felt (II.9 ¶6). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: tourner quelques ustensiles de toilette ou de cuisine, et particulièrement des boutons, dont le défaut se faisait vivement sentir
  - Smith makes the boat’s pulleys on it, on Pencroff’s advice (II.12 ¶12). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: au moyen du tour qu’il avait installé, Cyrus Smith fabriqua les poulies nécessaires
- **Appears in:** II.9, II.12
- **Open:**
  - II.12 ¶12 says "le tour qu’il avait installé" after naming both Pencroff and Smith, while II.9 ¶6 says the engineer installed the lathe; the card reads "il" as Smith (II.9 ¶6; II.12 ¶12). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: L’ingénieur installa un tour
    - quote: au moyen du tour qu’il avait installé
