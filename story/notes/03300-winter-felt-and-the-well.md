## Open and flags

- **Distance of Tabor and distance of any land.** The Tabor voyage is "cent cinquante milles" (¶11), while the notice's bottle or pigeon would have to cross the distance "qui séparait l’île de toute terre et qui était de douze cents milles" (¶31). Tabor is land at 150 miles; the text does not say why 1,200 is used. Card neither version. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Un voyage de cent cinquante milles
  - quote: qui séparait l’île de toute terre et qui était de douze cents milles
- **The cold of July and of August.** ¶74 gives the maximum cold of July as "huit degrés fahrenheit (13,33° centigrades au-dessous de zéro)", and ¶77 gives the first week of August as "huit degrés Fahrenheit au-dessous de zéro (22° centig. au-dessous de glace)". The printed forms differ ("huit degrés fahrenheit" with the centigrade figure below zero, against "huit degrés Fahrenheit au-dessous de zéro"). Card neither. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: son maximum ne dépassa pas huit degrés fahrenheit (13,33° centigrades au-dessous de zéro)
  - quote: la colonne thermométrique tomba à huit degrés Fahrenheit au-dessous de zéro (22° centig. au-dessous de glace)
- **"Hiver moins froid."** ¶74 says the winter is less cold than the previous one, then ¶77 describes a sharper cold in August. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: La température ne s’abaissa pas autant que pendant le précédent hiver
- **Smith alone.** Smith "prétextant quelque travail" stays behind (¶79); the real aim is told in ¶80–¶82. The reader is told at ¶80. Whether the other colonists were told afterwards is in the next chapter (II.12 ¶10). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: prétextant quelque travail
- **The albatross's course.** The bird vanishes "dans les brumes de l’ouest" (¶35) and Harbert says it goes "Vers la Nouvelle-Zélande" (¶37); New Zealand's bearing from the island is not stated here. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: disparaître au loin dans les brumes de l’ouest
- **The "first attempt to communicate".** ¶30 calls the albatross "la première tentative", yet ¶31 says Spilett had thought "plusieurs fois" of bottle or pigeons. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: la première tentative faite par les colons pour communiquer avec leurs semblables
- **Wikisource corrections.** None of the three that change a fact falls here. I was not given the places of the other five. *(Drafted.)*
