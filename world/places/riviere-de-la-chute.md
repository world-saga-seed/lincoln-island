# La rivière de la Chute: the creek that falls forty feet into the sea on the west coast

*The unknown creek, thirty to forty feet wide, that the colonists meet on 31 October and follow to the west coast, where it drops over the granite; named Falls-river by common accord.*

Keywords: Falls-river, Chute, creek, ruisseau, west coast, côte occidentale, waterfall, tide, marée, cove, crique, Pacific
