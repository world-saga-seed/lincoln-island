# The linen: shirts, socks and sheets sewn from the balloon cloth

*Several dozen white shirts and socks and the sheets of the Granite-house beds, sewn in the first week of January 1866 from the balloon's degreased and bleached cotton.*

Keywords: linge, linen, chemises, shirts, chaussettes, socks, draps, sheets, couture, sewing, aiguilles, needles

- **What it is.**
  - Making linen is one of the two aims of the first projects: the bridge would let the balloon's envelope be carried home and "donnerait le linge" (II.7 ¶6). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le transport de l’enveloppe du ballon, qui donnerait le linge
  - The time has come to use the envelope for it (II.7 ¶47). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le moment était venu d’utiliser, pour la confection du linge, l’enveloppe de l’aérostat
  - It is made in the first week of January, the needles from the chest working between strong if not delicate fingers, and what is sewn is sewn solidly (II.8 ¶1). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: La première semaine de janvier fut consacrée à la confection du linge nécessaire à la colonie
    - quote: Les aiguilles trouvées dans la caisse
  - It is sewn with thread drawn from the seams that had joined the balloon's strips (II.8 ¶2). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: celui qui avait déjà servi à la couture des bandes de l’aérostat
  - Spilett and Harbert unpick the strips, since Pencroff cannot bear that work; once it comes to sewing he has no equal, sailors having a remarkable aptitude for a seamstress's trade (II.8 ¶2). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Gédéon Spilett et Harbert
    - quote: quand il se fut agi de coudre, il n’eut pas son égal
    - quote: les marins ont une aptitude remarquable pour le métier de couturière
  - The cloth is degreased and bleached before it is cut (see [the balloon's envelope](balloon-envelope.md)) (II.8 ¶3). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dégraissées au moyen de soude et de potasse
  - Some dozens of shirts and socks are made, the socks not knitted but made of sewn cloth, and sheets as well (II.8 ¶4). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Quelques douzaines de chemises et de chaussettes
    - quote: non tricotées
    - quote: faites de toiles cousues
    - quote: se coucher entre des draps
  - The linen is white but very rough (II.8 ¶4). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: linge très-rude sans doute
  - The sheets make the Granite-house bunks into quite serious beds (II.8 ¶4). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: firent des couchettes de Granite-house des lits tout à fait sérieux
  - Linen is abundant, and kept with extreme care (III.14 ¶48). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Le linge était abondant aussi, et on l’entretenait, d’ailleurs, avec un soin extrême
  - Only four washings are done a year, as in families of the old days (III.14 ¶48). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: on ne faisait plus que quatre lessives par année
  - It is bleached with the soda and the chlorine that Smith draws from sea salt (see [the soda](soda.md); [the chloride of lime](chloride-of-lime.md)) (III.14 ¶48). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: employés à divers usages domestiques et précisément au blanchiment du linge
- **Appears in:** II.7, II.8, III.14
