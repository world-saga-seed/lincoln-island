# The Sulphur Spring and Lake Grant (notes)

## Notes for drafting

## Open and flags

- **The date, "30 mars" against "the beginning of this April".** I.11 ¶92, given to me, says "Now, this was happening on 30 March 1865". This chapter ¶41 says "at the beginning of this month of April, which in this hemisphere represents October". If this chapter is 30 March, "commencement … d’avril" is a loose phrase, or the day is 31 March or later. Card neither version. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: au commencement de ce mois d’avril, qui représente dans cet hémisphère le mois d’octobre
- **Wikisource correction, ¶41.** Wikisource reads the island's latitude as "sans doute trop haute" for the coconut palm; the print (Gallica p. 108) reads "trop basse" (texts/README.md, scan check). A card resting on this cites both. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: dont la latitude était sans doute trop haute
- **The lake's measures, ¶68.** The text gives "with a circumference of about seven miles and an area of two hundred and fifty acres", and the footnote says "Environs 200 hectares." Two hundred hectares is about 494 acres, not 250; and a seven-mile circuit would hold far more than either. Card none of the figures. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: d’une circonférence de sept milles environ et d’une superficie de deux cent cinquante acres
  - quote: Environs 200 hectares.
  - at: I.12 n2
- **Richmond or Washington, ¶9 and I.14.** Here the reporter's watch is on "Richmond time", "a meridian nearly the same as Washington's". I.14 ¶70 treats it as Washington time. The text calls the two meridians nearly the same; no more. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: méridien qui est à peu près celui de Washington
- **The way home.** Pencroff says that whether they follow the lake road or the Mercy through the Far-West woods they reach the plateau (¶15). The actual way: the lake, then its south angle, then north toward the plateau, then the Mercy's left bank (¶72–¶74). No disagreement, but the "Far-West" route is not taken. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: soit que nous rejoignions la Mercy à travers les bois du Far-West
- **Seam with I.11 ¶92.** The chapter begins on the summit rim on the morning after the plateau night, with the colonists going round the crater "half an hour" before the descent; I.11's last paragraph (the narrator's note on 30 March 1865) does not conflict. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
