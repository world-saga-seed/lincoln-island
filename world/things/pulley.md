# The pulley: a coarse pulley fitted at the door of Granite-house

*A coarse pulley (mouffle) installed at the door to hoist the bricks up to Granite-house.*

Keywords: mouffle, pulley, hoist, block, tackle, bricks, door

- **What it is.** A made thing, a hoist. Build if needed. *(Drafted.)*
