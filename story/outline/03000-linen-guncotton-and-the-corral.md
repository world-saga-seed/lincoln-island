### 3. Saving powder and shot: iron grains and pyroxyle (II.8 ¶7–¶13)

- Cyrus tells the hunters to spare ammunition and plans to replace the powder and lead found in the chest, which he wants to keep for the future, in case he and the others leave their domain. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: dans le cas où ils quitteraient leur domaine
- He found no trace of lead on the island; he makes iron shot, larger and lighter than lead, fewer to a charge; the hunters' skill makes up for it. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: dont Cyrus Smith n’avait rencontré aucune trace dans l’île
- He could make gunpowder (saltpetre, sulphur and charcoal are at hand) but it takes extreme care and special tools. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: du salpêtre, du soufre et du charbon
- He prefers to make pyroxyle (gun-cotton); cellulose is found in cotton, hemp and flax fibres, paper, old linen and elder pith; elders abound near the Red Creek's mouth, and the colonists already use elderberries for coffee. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: les sureaux abondaient dans l’île, vers l’embouchure du Creek-Rouge

  - quote: en attaquant le salpêtre que lui fournissait la nature
- The faults of pyroxyle he accepts: unequal effect, excessive inflammability (it ignites at 170 degrees instead of 240), too instantaneous a burn that can spoil arms; its virtues: no harm from damp, no fouling, four times the force of ordinary powder. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: il s’enflamme à cent soixante-dix degrés au lieu de deux cent quarante
  - quote: sa force propulsive était quadruple de celle de la poudre ordinaire

### 7. The corral built (II.8 ¶30–¶35)

- Toward the end of January the colonists undertake great works in the middle of the island: a corral near the sources of the Red Creek, at the foot of Mount Franklin, for ruminants, especially mouflons, whose wool is for winter clothes. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Ce fut vers la fin de janvier que les colons entreprirent de grands travaux
- Each morning all, or often only Cyrus, Harbert and Pencroff, go to the creek's sources; with the onaggas it is a five-mile walk under greenery along the new road, named the "route du Corral". *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: une promenade de cinq milles
- The site: a meadow dotted with groves on the southern spur of the mountain, closed on one side by a buttress; a small rio crosses it diagonally and runs to the Red Creek; a circular palisade resting on the buttress at both ends will hold about a hundred horned animals and their young. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: au revers même de la croupe méridionale de la montagne

  - quote: une centaine de pieux
- The building takes at least three weeks; Cyrus also raises large plank sheds; the stakes, hardened at the point in fire, are bolted by crosspieces and propped. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: ne demanda pas moins de trois semaines
