# The corral: the palisaded pasture at the Red Creek's sources, for the island's wool-bearing animals

*A meadow at the foot of Mont Franklin's southern croup, enclosed by a round palisade and sheds, built in January and February to hold mouflons and wild goats; the colonists' sheepfold.*

Keywords: corral, enclos, palissade, mouflons, sheepfold, pen, hangars, wool, laine, Red Creek, sources

- **What it is.**
  - It is planned as a pen to lodge the mouflons or other wool-bearing animals that are to be captured, to answer the problem of winter clothes (II.7 ¶5, ¶6). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la fondation d’un corral, destiné au logement des mouflons ou autres animaux à laine qu’il convenait de capturer
    - quote: le corral devait fournir la récolte de laine, qui donnerait les vêtements d’hiver
  - Smith means to put it at the very sources of the Red Creek, where ruminants would find fresh, abundant pasture; it is allowed to be far from Granite-house, unlike the poultry-yard (II.7 ¶7, ¶8). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: aux sources mêmes du Creek-Rouge, là où les ruminants trouveraient des pâturages
    - quote: s’il n’y avait aucun inconvénient à ce que le corral fût éloigné de Granite-house
  - It is founded at the end of January, near the sources of the Red Creek at the foot of Mont Franklin, to hold the ruminants whose presence would be a nuisance at Granite-house, especially the mouflons that are to give wool (II.8 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: vers les sources du Creek-Rouge, au pied du mont Franklin, serait fondé un corral
    - quote: ces mouflons, qui devaient fournir la laine destinée à la confection des vêtements d’hiver
  - Its site is a meadow planted with clumps of trees, on the reverse of the mountain's southern croup, at the foot of a spur that closes it on one side (II.8 ¶32). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: au revers même de la croupe méridionale de la montagne
    - quote: une prairie, plantée de bouquets d’arbres
    - quote: au pied même d’un contrefort qui la fermait sur un côté

    - quote: Un petit rio, né sur ses pentes, après l’avoir arrosée diagonalement, allait se perdre dans le Creek-Rouge
  - The grass is fresh, and the scattered trees let the air move freely over it (II.8 ¶32). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: L’herbe était fraîche
  - It is enclosed by a circular palisade resting at each end on the spur, high enough that even the most agile animals cannot clear it, and can hold about a hundred horned animals with the young born later (II.8 ¶32). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une palissade disposée circulairement, qui viendrait s’appuyer à chaque extrémité sur le contrefort

    - quote: Les pieux, pointus à leur extrémité supérieure, qui fut durcie au feu

  - The entrance in the front of the palisade is wide, and shut by a two-leaf door of strong planks that bars outside strengthen (II.8 ¶34). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une porte à deux battants faits de forts madriers, que devaient consolider des barres extérieures
