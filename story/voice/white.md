# Voice sheet: Verne's narrator, in White's English

*The voice of the page: Stephen W. White's translation (1876), the drafters' style sample [White I.5](samples/white-I.5.md) (R1121-16). Built out in phases 6 and 8; only what is settled stands here.*

## The rule

- **Spelling:** White's American spelling, as he prints it: color, honor, labor, favor, harbor, neighbor (no "-our" form in all his text), "-ize" as he has it (civilized 6 against civilised 2). *(Settled R1121-21.)* The British "-our" forms are on the [banned list](banned.txt). Phase 5 found them in three of twenty renders (`plan/tasks/task-20261121-07-calibration/tells.md`, T1).
- **Names:** the page uses White's forms of the names, of people and places, while the cards keep the French (Herbert for Harbert, Neb for Nab, Gideon Spilett for Gédéon Spilett). *(Settled R1121-20.)* Where a card's French name and White's differ, the page takes White's as he prints it in `texts/white-1876/`.
