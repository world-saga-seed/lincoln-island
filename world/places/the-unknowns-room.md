# The unknown’s room: the room at Granite-house given to the Tabor castaway

*One of the rooms of Granite-house, with a window over the beach, where the castaway of Tabor is lodged and then no longer sleeps.*

Keywords: chambre, room, inconnu, unknown, Granite-house, fenêtre, window, naufragé, Tabor

- **What it is.**
  - It is one of the rooms of Granite-house, from which he cannot escape (II.15 ¶37). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: demeurerait dans une des chambres de Granite-house, d’où il ne pouvait s’échapper, d’ailleurs
  - It has a window, by which the fear is that he will throw himself down onto the beach (II.15 ¶62). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: qu’il ne se précipitât sur la grève par une des fenêtres de Granite-house
  - He is found lying near its window, looking at the sky (II.15 ¶78). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: qu’ils trouvèrent couché près de la fenêtre et regardant le ciel
  - It is "la chambre qui lui avait été assignée", which he leaves each night in November to sleep under clumps of trees or in the rock (II.16 ¶65). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la chambre qui lui avait été assignée
- **Appears in:** II.15, II.16
- **Open:**
  - Which room of Granite-house it is, and how it is closed, the text does not say.
