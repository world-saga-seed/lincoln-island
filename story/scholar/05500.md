# The scholar’s notes to chapter LV

*Each note is set after the words its “After:” line quotes, exactly as they stand in story/prose/05500-ayrtons-tale-and-the-mountain-search.md. Delete this file and the chapter builds without them (R1121-163).*

## a

After: after a hundred and four days of separation.

Ayrton was taken at nightfall on the 10th of November, as he tells a few lines on, and he woke on the morning after the colonists reached the corral, which was the night of the 19th of February (chapter LIV). That is a hundred and two days, not a hundred and four. Both dates are borne out by the days around them, and I take them.

## b

After: This search was begun the same day, the 19th of February,

The colonists reached the corral on the 19th, at night (chapter LIV), and Ayrton told his story the next morning. A search begun after the telling cannot have begun on the 19th; by the count it began on the 20th. The same 19th is carried into the dates of the search a few pages on. I take the 20th.

## c

After: From the 19th to the 25th of February,

See note (b).

## d

After: One month later, on the 25th of March, they celebrated the third anniversary of their arrival on Lincoln Island.

See note (a) to chapter XL. The day was the 24th.
