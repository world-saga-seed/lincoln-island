# S5: the French, the render, White

## ¶14

**French:** Le canot suivit donc le littoral à une distance de deux encâblures au plus, en évitant les écueils dont ces atterrages étaient semés et que la marée montante commençait à couvrir. La muraille allait en s’abaissant depuis l’embouchure de la rivière jusqu’à la pointe. C’était un amoncellement de granits, capricieusement distribués, très-différents de la courtine, qui formaient le plateau de Grande-Vue, et d’un aspect extrêmement sauvage. On eût dit qu’un énorme tombereau de roches avait été vidé là. Point de végétation sur ce saillant très-aigu qui se prolongeait à deux milles en avant de la forêt, et cette pointe figurait assez bien le bras d’un géant qui serait sorti d’une manche de verdure.

**Render:** The boat therefore followed the shore at a distance of two cables' lengths at most, avoiding the reefs with which these approaches were strewn, and which the rising tide was beginning to cover. The wall went on lowering from the mouth of the river to the point. It was a heap of granite rocks, capriciously distributed, very different from the curtain which formed the plateau of Grande-Vue, and of an extremely wild aspect. One would have said that an enormous cart-load of rocks had been emptied out there. There was no vegetation upon this very sharp projection, which ran out two miles in front of the forest, and the point pretty well represented the arm of a giant coming out of a sleeve of verdure.

**White** (¶12): (¶12) The canoe followed along the shore, keeping off some two cables length so as to avoid the line of rocks beginning to be covered by the tide. The cliff, beginning at the mouth of the river, lowered as it approached the promontory. It was a savage-looking, unevenly-arranged heap of granite blocks, very different from the curtain of Prospect Plateau. There was not a trace of vegetation on this sharp point, which projected two miles beyond the forest, like a giant’s arm, thrust out from a green sleeve.
