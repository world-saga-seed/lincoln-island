# The mortar: lime and sand mixed

*Excellent mortar from lime mixed with sand.*

Keywords: mortier, cement, binder, plaster, building

- **What it is.**
  - It is an excellent mortar (I.13 ¶44). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: cette chaux fournit un mortier excellent
- **Made from.**
  - It is [lime](lime.md) mixed with sand (I.13 ¶44). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Mélangée avec du sable
  - The sand lessens the shrinking of the paste when it sets (I.13 ¶44). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dont l’effet est d’atténuer le retrait de la pâte quand elle se solidifie
- **Appears in:** I.13
- **Open:**
  - Whether the mortar was used in the pottery kiln is not stated; ¶46 says only that the kiln was built.
    - quote: On y réussit sans trop de difficulté
