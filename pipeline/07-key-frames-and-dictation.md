# 07 Key frames and dictation

*Refinement. Add this when a chapter is graded thin or none.*

**When:** for every chapter graded thin or none, in story order, with the writer at the keyboard.

**Uses:** [dictation](../workflows/dictation.md), then [ripple](../workflows/ripple.md); the idea is in [method/key-frames.md](../method/key-frames.md).

**What happens:** Claude writes a brief, the writer dictates the scene, and the dictation is carded ([dictation](../workflows/dictation.md)); after a run of them, a ripple scan.

**Outputs:** new key frames, carded. Example: [the brief for 00700](../example/story/notes/1-spring-tides-key-frames.md#brief-00700-caught-on-the-causeway).

**Done when:** no chapter in the horizon is graded none; the ripple scan has run.
