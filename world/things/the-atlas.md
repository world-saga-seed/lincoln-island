# The atlas: the French-lettered atlas of the chest

*A magnificent atlas with maps of the whole world and several Mercator planispheres, lettered in French, with no date or publisher.*

Keywords: atlas, carte, maps, Mercator, planisphère, French, nomenclature, chest

- **What it is.**
  - It is one atlas (II.2 ¶53). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: 1 atlas
  - It is a magnificent work with maps of the whole world and several planispheres drawn on Mercator’s projection (II.2 ¶67). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les cartes du monde entier
    - quote: la projection de Mercator
  - Its nomenclature is in French, unlike the English books, and it bears no date and no publisher’s name (II.2 ¶67). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dont la nomenclature était en français
    - quote: ni date de publication, ni nom d’éditeur
