# Tobacco: the plant Pencroff wants and the island has not given

*The one thing Pencroff says the island lacks; wished for from the chest and in the forest, and not found on these chapters’ ground.*

Keywords: tabac, tobacco, pipe, Pencroff, demi-livre, plant, mustard, bamboo, smoke, want

- **What it is.**
  - Pencroff had hoped for half a pound of tobacco in the chest, "and nothing would have been wanting to my happiness" (II.2 ¶73). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Une demi-livre de tabac
    - quote: rien n’aurait manqué à mon bonheur
  - He regrets that the chest did not hold, together with his half pound of tobacco, a pair of New Jersey horses (II.3 ¶3). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: sa demi-livre de tabac
  - He has never seen his favourite plant except in the bowl of his pipe; taking a downy plant for tobacco, he is told by Harbert that it is mustard (II.3 ¶15, ¶16). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: n’avait jamais vu sa plante de prédilection que dans le fourneau de sa pipe
    - quote: ce n’est pas du tabac, c’est de la moutarde
  - He asks that if a tobacco plant should turn up, it not be passed over (II.3 ¶17). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: si, par hasard, un plant de tabac se présentait
  - Spilett says it will be found one day, and Pencroff answers that then he would not know what the island lacked (II.3 ¶18, ¶19). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Nous en trouverons un jour
    - quote: je ne sais vraiment plus ce qui manquera à notre île
  - Pencroff sighs that there is everything on the island except tobacco (II.4 ¶26). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: excepté du tabac, il y a de tout dans cette île
  - Bamboo cannot be smoked, Harbert tells him (II.4 ¶58, ¶59). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ça ne se fume pas, mon pauvre Pencroff
  - Spilett finds it on 30 April in a clearing in the south-west of the Far-West, where trees stand wider apart (see [the Far-West forests](../places/far-west-forests.md)) (II.10 ¶21). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: C’était le 30 avril
    - quote: dans le sud-ouest du Far-West
  - The plants have straight, cylindrical, branching stems with flowers in clusters and very small seeds, and an odour that struck Spilett first (II.10 ¶22). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: végétaux à tiges droites, cylindriques et rameuses
    - quote: des fleurs disposées en grappes et de très-petites graines
  - It grows very abundantly in the clearing (II.10 ¶25). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: où elle pousse très-abondamment
  - Harbert recognises it: not of first quality, but tobacco all the same (II.10 ¶28). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: s’il n’est pas de première qualité, ce n’en est pas moins du tabac
  - The leaves are dried thin, chopped and roasted on hot stones, out of Pencroff’s knowledge, since he returns to Granite-house only to rest (II.10 ¶33). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: sécher les feuilles minces, les hacher, les soumettre à une certaine torréfaction sur des pierres chaudes
    - quote: toutes ces manipulations purent être faites à l’insu de Pencroff
  - On 31 May Spilett hands Pencroff a filled pipe and Harbert an ember; Smith calls it excellent tobacco (II.10 ¶82, ¶89 to ¶94). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le 31 mai, il devait éprouver une des plus grandes joies de sa vie
    - quote: une pipe toute bourrée
    - quote: et même de l’excellent tabac
  - Pencroff says nothing now lacks the island (II.10 ¶95). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Il ne manque donc plus rien à notre île
  - Credit is shared: Spilett found it, Harbert recognised it, Smith prepared it, Nab kept the secret (II.10 ¶100). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Faites une part dans votre reconnaissance à Harbert qui a reconnu cette plante, à Cyrus qui l’a préparée, et à Nab qui a eu bien de la peine à nous garder le secret
  - In the winter evenings the pipes plume with fragrant smoke in the great hall (II.11 ¶41). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les pipes s’empanachant d’une odorante fumée
  - Jup smokes it too, and keeps a supply of his own tobacco near his pipe (see [Pencroff’s pipe](pencroff-pipe.md)) (II.12 ¶71, ¶73). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Jup lançait gravement d’épaisses bouffées de tabac
    - quote: près de sa provision de tabac
  - Smith cites several examples of tame apes made familiar with tobacco (II.12 ¶72). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il cita plusieurs exemples de singes apprivoisés, auxquels l’usage du tabac était devenu familier
  - The Speedy’s cargo holds hogsheads of tobacco, which Pencroff greets with hurrahs (see [the Speedy’s cargo](the-speedys-cargo.md)) (III.4 ¶71). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des boucauts de tabac
