# The Vineyard: the New York port of the whaler Maria-Stella and of Pencroff

*Pencroff’s native country, glossed in a footnote as a port of the state of New York.*

Keywords: Vineyard, port, New York, Maria-Stella, Pencroff, natal, pays

- **What it is.**
  - Verne’s footnote glosses it as a port of the state of New York (II.10 n2). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Port de l’état de New York
  - The whaler Maria-Stella is of the Vineyard, and Pencroff calls it his country (II.10 ¶62). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un baleinier du Vineyard
    - quote: Un navire de mon pays
  - Pencroff swears by it: "Bonadventure Pencroff, du Vineyard" (III.6 ¶39). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: aussi vrai que je suis Bonadventure Pencroff, du Vineyard
- **Appears in:** II.10, III.6
