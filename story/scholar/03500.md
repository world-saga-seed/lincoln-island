# The scholar’s notes to chapter XXXV

*Each note is set after the words its “After:” line quotes, exactly as they stand in story/prose/03500-the-voyage-to-tabor-island.md. Delete this file and the chapter builds without them (R1121-163).*

## a

After: abandoned some hundreds of miles from us upon Tabor Island!

Pencroff speaks in the first moment. The engineer gave the distance as about a hundred and fifty miles in chapter XXXI, and this chapter gives the same a few lines on. The voyage bears it out: a hundred and twenty miles run in the first thirty-six hours, and the islet in sight the next morning. I take a hundred and fifty.

## b

After: “This is our first separation in fifteen months!”

This is the 11th of October. The colonists were thrown on the island on the 24th of March, 1865 (chapter II), and the year is now 1866, as Herbert says in chapter XXXVIII and as the first anniversary, kept in chapter XXX, requires. That is nineteen months, not fifteen. I take nineteen.
