# The Speedy: the pirates’ brig

*A fast English-built brig of three to four hundred tons, armed with four breech-loading cannon, taken off Norfolk Island by the convict Bob Harvey and turned pirate; she anchors off Granite-house on 17 October and is lost in the channel the next morning.*

Keywords: brick, brig, Speedy, pirates, corsair, Bob Harvey, Norfolk, navire suspect, bâtiment, long-courrier, convicts, vessel

- **What it is.**
  - She is a brig, a long-haul ship and not one of the Malay proas that the pirates of the Pacific usually use (III.1 ¶56). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: c’était un long-courrier, et non un de ces praos malais
    - quote: ce navire était gréé en brick
  - She is of three to four hundred tons, wonderfully fine-lined, boldly masted, admirably cut for speed, a fast runner of the seas; a good goer (III.1 ¶62, ¶66). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: d’une jauge de trois à quatre cents tonneaux
    - quote: merveilleusement effilé, hardiment mâté, admirablement taillé pour la marche
    - quote: C’était un bon marcheur que ce brick
  - Her name is the English word for "active"; her crew say she goes well and earns her name (III.2 ¶40, n1). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Il marche bien, le Speedy
    - quote: Il mérite son nom
    - quote: Mot anglais qui signifie actif.
  - She has two masts, and a poop at the stern where the convicts gather (III.2 ¶50; III.4 ¶10). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les deux mâts, qui avaient été brisés
    - quote: réunis dans la dunette, à l’arrière du navire
  - Her rig is carried by [her sails](the-speedys-sails.md); she rides at [her anchors](the-speedys-anchors.md) and has [two boats](the-speedys-boats.md) (III.3 ¶16, ¶23; III.4 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: mouillé sur deux ancres
    - quote: les deux embarcations du brick
  - She was laden with arms, munitions, utensils and tools of all sorts, meant for one of the Sandwich Islands, when Bob Harvey took her in the waters of Norfolk Island (III.2 ¶44). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: chargé d’armes, de munitions, d’ustensiles et outils de toutes sortes, destinés à l’une des Sandwich
    - quote: s’était emparé, sur les parages de l’île Norfolk, de ce brick
  - Harvey took her while she lay at anchor in sight of Norfolk Island and massacred her crew; for a year she has been a pirate ship under his command (III.2 ¶49). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: mouillé en vue de l’île Norfolk
    - quote: l’équipage avait été massacré
    - quote: depuis un an, ce navire, devenu bâtiment de pirates
  - Her present crew is made up only of English prisoners escaped from Norfolk, about fifty by Ayrton’s hearing (III.2 ¶46, ¶59). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: se composait uniquement de prisonniers anglais, échappés de Norfolk
    - quote: ils étaient une cinquantaine à bord
  - She is "formidablement armé": see [her cannon](the-speedys-cannon.md) and [her black flag](the-black-flag.md) (III.2 ¶3). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le navire était formidablement armé
  - Her end and what was got from her: see [the wreck](the-speedys-wreck.md) and [the cargo](the-speedys-cargo.md) (III.4 ¶75, ¶80). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: on ne voyait plus rien du navire, même à mer basse
  - Smith says the brig could not withstand a torpedo that would have destroyed an ironclad frigate (III.5 ¶1). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le Speedy n’avait pu résister
- **On the page.**
  - Her lights are put out at night, so that she cannot be placed once dark falls (III.1 ¶97). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: tous ses feux étaient condamnés
