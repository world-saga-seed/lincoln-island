# Echidnas: the spiny anteaters of the Jacamar Wood

*A beast that looks much like a hedgehog and an anteater, rolls into a ball and bristles with quills, with a bird's beak and a long sticky tongue.*

Keywords: échidné, echidna, hérisson, hedgehog, fourmilier, anteater, piquants, quills, bec, beak, langue, tongue, insectes

- **What it is.**
  - The chapter's summary calls it an "échidné"; the body of the chapter never gives it a name (I.15 ¶0, ¶34). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Capture d’un échidné.
    - quote: un animal qui ressemblait fort à un hérisson et à un fourmilier
  - It is like a hedgehog because it rolls itself into a ball and bristles with quills (I.15 ¶34). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: au premier, parce qu’il se roulait en boule et se hérissait de piquants
  - It is like an anteater because it has digging nails, a long slender muzzle ending in a bird's beak, and an extensible tongue set with little spines that hold the insects (I.15 ¶34). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des ongles fouisseurs, un museau long et grêle que terminait un bec d’oiseau, et une langue extensible, garnie de petites épines qui lui servaient à retenir les insectes
  - Harbert says that in the pot it will be like an excellent piece of beef (I.15 ¶36). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: À un excellent morceau de bœuf
- **On the page.**
  - Harbert and the reporter kill one with arrows, on the day-long march through the wood, together with two kangaroos (I.15 ¶34). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Harbert et Gédéon Spilett tuèrent deux kangourous à coups de flèche, et de plus un animal
- **Appears in:** I.15
