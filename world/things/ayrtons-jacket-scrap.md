# Ayrton’s jacket scrap: the torn bit of felt Top finds in a bush

*A stained, torn piece of Ayrton’s felt jacket, drawn by Top from a thick bush in the wood south of the mountain on 27 November, the sign that Ayrton resisted.*

Keywords: lambeau, étoffe, veste, jacket, felt, feutre, Ayrton, Top, buisson, bush, resistance

- **What it is.**
  - Spilett, on his second sortie, on 27 November, goes a quarter of a mile under the trees, in the south of the mountain, and finds Top sniffing at something (III.9 ¶31). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: à sa seconde sortie, le 27 novembre
    - quote: s’était aventuré sous bois pendant un quart de mille, dans le sud de la montagne
  - Top rushes at a thick bush and pulls out a scrap of cloth, a piece of clothing, stained and torn (III.9 ¶33, ¶34). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le chien se précipita vers un épais buisson et en tira un lambeau d’étoffe
    - quote: C’était un morceau de vêtement, maculé, lacéré
  - The colonists know it for a piece of Ayrton’s jacket, of the felt made only in the Granite-house workshop (see [the felt](felt.md)) (III.9 ¶35). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un morceau de la veste d’Ayrton, morceau de ce feutre uniquement fabriqué à l’atelier de Granite-house
  - Smith reads in it that Ayrton resisted and was dragged off against his will (III.9 ¶36). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il y a eu résistance de la part du malheureux Ayrton
    - quote: Les convicts l’ont entraîné malgré lui
  - Pencroff draws from it that Ayrton was not killed at the corral but taken away alive, since he resisted, and may live still (III.9 ¶39). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ayrton n’a pas été tué au corral
    - quote: C’est qu’on l’a entraîné vivant, puisqu’il a résisté
- **Appears in:** III.9
