# The forest marsh: the marshy part of the forest where the tétras were taken

*A damp stretch of the forest on the Mercy's side, with a nesting ground of tétras that Pencroff fished with lines.*

Keywords: marais, marécageuse, marsh, bog, tétras ground, nests, swamp

- **What it is.**
  - There is a marshy part of the forest, where Harbert saw a bird like a kingfisher, the jacamar (I.6 ¶21). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dans une partie marécageuse de la forêt
  - Pencroff found in the grass half a dozen tétras nests, each with two or three eggs, around which he set his lines (I.6 ¶41). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une demi-douzaine de nids de tétras, ayant chacun de deux à trois œufs
  - The marshy part is the place where Pencroff had so happily done his tétras fishing, so the nests of I.6 ¶41 lie in it (I.9 ¶88). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: cette partie marécageuse de la forêt, dans laquelle il avait si heureusement opéré sa pêche aux tétras
- **Appears in:** I.6, I.9
