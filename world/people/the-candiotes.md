# The Candiotes: the Cretan insurgents whom Nemo aided from the Vigo treasure

*The people of Crete, in revolt, to whom the Nautilus's captain sent his sunken millions anonymously.*

Keywords: Crète, Crete, Candie, insurrection, insurgés, Cretans, Grecs, Greek, gold, Nicolas, Pesce, independence

- **What it is.**
  - The editor says that the peoples for whom Nemo disposed of the Vigo millions included the Candiotes, whose uprising he aided in these conditions (III.16 n2). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Il s’agit du soulèvement des Candiotes, que le capitaine Nemo aida, en effet, dans ces conditions
    - In the earlier book the professor understands for whom the millions sent by Captain Nemo were meant, when the Nautilus sailed in the waters of insurgent Crete (VML II.8 ¶75). *(Drafted.)*
      - quote: à qui étaient destinés ces millions expédiés par le capitaine Nemo
      - quote: lorsque le Nautilus naviguait dans les eaux de la Crète insurgée
    - Off the Greek archipelago Nemo, who knows Nicolas of Cape Matapan, called the Pesce, a bold diver who goes from island to island and as far as Crete, fills a chest with over a thousand kilograms of gold, nearly five million francs, and writes on its lid an address in what seemed modern Greek (VML II.6 ¶76, ¶82, ¶83). *(Drafted.)*
      - quote: C’est Nicolas, du cap Matapan, surnommé le Pesce
      - quote: plus de mille kilogrammes d’or, c’est-à-dire près de cinq millions de francs
      - quote: une adresse en caractères qui devaient appartenir au grec moderne
    - The professor recognises among the sailors of the Nautilus, of various nations, a Greek or a Candiote (VML I.18 ¶4). *(Drafted.)*
      - quote: un Grec ou un Candiote
- **Appears in:** III.16, VML I.18, VML II.6, VML II.8
- **Open:**
  - Whether the chest of gold of VML II.6 is what VML II.8 ¶75 calls the millions sent is the professor's inference, not Nemo's word. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: Je cherchais une relation entre l’apparition de ce plongeur et ce coffre rempli d’or
