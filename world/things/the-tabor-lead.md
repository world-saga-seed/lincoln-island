# The Tabor lead: the barrel of lead from the Tabor hut

*A barrel of lead found on the shelf of the Tabor hut and brought back to Lincoln with the powder.*

Keywords: plomb, lead, baril, barrel, tonneau, Tabor, shot

- **What it is.**
  - A barrel of lead stands on the shelf of the Tabor hut (II.14 ¶5). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un baril de plomb
  - Spilett judges the reserve of powder and lead important (II.14 ¶35). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la réserve de poudre et de plomb est importante
  - At Lincoln the two barrels, of powder and of lead, are very well received (see [the Tabor powder](the-tabor-powder.md)) (II.15 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Les deux tonneaux contenant de la poudre et du plomb
    - quote: furent très-bien reçus
- **Appears in:** II.14, II.15
