# Vingt mille Lieues sous les mers: the book that tells the professor's months aboard the Nautilus

*The professor's published account of his voyage in the Nautilus, which Smith has read and which Nemo did not know existed.*

Keywords: ouvrage, book, Aronnax, publié, Hetzel, récit, Nautilus, histoire, discordance de dates, Twenty Thousand Leagues

- **What it is.**
  - Smith says that there has appeared, under the title Vingt mille Lieues sous les mers, a work that holds Nemo's history (III.16 ¶24). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il a paru, sous le titre de Vingt mille Lieues sous les mers, un ouvrage qui contient votre histoire
    - In the earlier book the professor says that he revises, among the fishermen who took him in, the narrative of his adventures, and that it is exact: not a fact omitted, not a detail exaggerated (VML II.23 ¶3). *(Drafted.)*
      - quote: que je revois le récit de ces aventures
      - quote: Pas un fait n’a été omis, pas un détail n’a été exagéré
    - He says he crossed twenty thousand leagues in this underwater tour of the world, through the Pacific, the Indian Ocean, the Red Sea, the Mediterranean, the Atlantic and the austral and boreal seas (VML II.23 ¶4). *(Drafted.)*
      - quote: j’ai franchi vingt mille lieues
      - quote: à travers le Pacifique, l’Océan Indien, la mer Rouge, la Méditerranée, l’Atlantique, les mers australes et boréales
  - Nemo calls it the history of only a few months, and Smith agrees that a few months of this strange life were enough to make him known (III.16 ¶25, ¶26). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Mon histoire de quelques mois seulement, monsieur !
    - quote: quelques mois de cette vie étrange ont suffi à vous faire connaître
  - The professor, back in France, published the work in which seven months of the Nautilus's navigation were told and given to public curiosity (III.16 ¶58). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le professeur, à son retour en France, avait publié l’ouvrage dans lequel sept mois de cette étrange et aventureuse navigation du Nautilus étaient racontés
    - In the earlier book the professor asks whether the sea will bring one day the manuscript that holds the whole history of the captain's life (VML II.23 ¶5). *(Drafted.)*
      - quote: Les flots apporteront-ils un jour ce manuscrit qui renferme toute l’histoire de sa vie ?
  - Hetzel's note says that the history of Captain Nemo has been published under the title Vingt mille Lieues sous les mers, and that the discord of some dates calls for the same remark as for Ayrton's adventures (III.16 n1). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: L’histoire du capitaine Nemo a été, en effet, publiée sous le titre de Vingt mille Lieues sous les mers
- **Appears in:** III.16, VML II.23
- **Open:**
  - Months: III.16 ¶58 says "sept mois" of navigation were told; the professor of the earlier book says "en moins de dix mois" and "dix mois de cette existence" (III.16 ¶58; VML II.23 ¶4, ¶6). Carded neither. *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: sept mois de cette étrange et aventureuse navigation
    - quote: en moins de dix mois j’ai franchi vingt mille lieues
    - quote: N’ai-je pas vécu dix mois de cette existence extra-naturelle ?
  - The editor warns of the disagreement of some dates (III.16 n1). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: sur la discordance de quelques dates
