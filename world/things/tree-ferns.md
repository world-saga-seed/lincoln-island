# The tree ferns: new ferns of the Far-West, with drooping fronds

*Tree-ferns found by Harbert on 16 February in the Far-West forest, not seen before on the island, whose fronds pour down like water from a basin.*

Keywords: fougères arborescentes, tree ferns, palmes, fronds, Harbert, essences nouvelles, new species

- **What it is.**
  - Harbert finds on 16 February new kinds of trees, whose presence had not yet been noted on the island, among them tree ferns (III.11 ¶51, ¶53). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Harbert découvrit des essences nouvelles, dont la présence n’avait pas encore été signalée dans l’île
    - quote: des fougères arborescentes
  - Their fronds droop, and seem to spill over like the waters of a basin (III.11 ¶53). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: avec palmes retombantes, qui semblaient s’épancher comme les eaux d’une vasque
- **Appears in:** III.11
