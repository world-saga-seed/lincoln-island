# The household not in the secret (Smith, Spilett, Nab, and Top): 31 May to 5 June 1865 (01900, 01950, 02000)

*The light pass, not a full log (the author, 2026-11-21: "enough to show where they are while the two are out"; [workflow](../../../plan/workflows/actor-log.md#the-light-pass-basic)). In 01950 these three and the dog are off the page while Pencroff and Harbert are out. The log records where they are, so that the two's secrecy holds. A row with no source is a tween, marked (Proposal.).*

Last run: 2026-11-21, against the outlines as of R1121-82 (the commit that follows c51effb).

## The standing state

- Granite-house is finished. Its windows close with thick shutters until there is window glass. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: D’épais volets permettaient de fermer les fenêtres de la façade (I.19 ¶46)
- From the windows the whole of Union Bay is in view, the beach and the islet among it. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Toute la baie de l’Union se développait magnifiquement devant eux (I.19 ¶48)
- June's work: hunting, fishing, the warren, Nab salting and smoking meat. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Nab employait presque tout son temps à saler ou à fumer des viandes (I.20 ¶2)
- Cyrus guards the forge and furnaces at the Chimneys against the wind-driven tides. *(Drafted.)* The text does not date it within June.
  - quote: afin de préserver, autant que possible, la forge et les fourneaux (I.20 ¶1)
- Top stays in Granite-house with Cyrus on 1 to 3 June. *(Settled R1121-75.)*
