# Promontoire du Reptile: the curved tail that ends the Serpentine peninsula

*The recurved end of the south-west tail of the island, named Reptile-end by Spilett on 30 March.*

Keywords: promontoire du Reptile, Reptile-end, queue, reptile, extremity, south-west

- **What it is.**
  - It is the curved tail at the end of the Serpentine peninsula, "truly a reptile's tail", and was named promontoire du Reptile (Reptile-end) by Spilett (I.11 ¶66). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: celui de promontoire du Reptile (Reptile-end) à la queue recourbée qui la termine
  - Smith adopted the name (I.11 ¶67). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Adopté, dit l’ingénieur
  - The wooded part of the island is said to stretch from the Mercy to it (I.20 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: jusqu’au promontoire du Reptile
  - From the top of a kauri the southern part of the island is seen from cap Griffe in the south-east to the promontoire du Reptile in the south-west (II.1 ¶53). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: depuis le cap Griffe, au sud-est, jusqu’au promontoire du Reptile, au sud-ouest
  - It lies about twelve miles from the mouth of the Falls-river (II.4 ¶39). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: douze milles environ
  - It is a sort of strangely cut volute on the sea, where the forest along the peninsula’s shore ends and the south coast takes on its usual look of rocks, reefs and beaches (II.4 ¶43). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: sorte de volute étrangement découpée sur la mer
    - quote: avec ses rochers, ses récifs et ses grèves
  - The last trees of the forest of the Far-West die at the point, among them thick clumps of bamboo (see [the bamboo](../things/bamboo.md)) (II.4 ¶44). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Les derniers arbres de la forêt du Far-West venaient mourir à cette pointe
    - quote: d’épais bouquets de bambous
  - Its shore rocks are much divided, as they must be violently beaten by the sea in south-west winds, and hold cavities that give shelter (see [the jaguar’s cave](the-jaguars-cave.md)) (II.4 ¶60). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: très-divisés, car ils devaient être violemment battus par la mer sous l’influence des vents du sud-ouest
    - quote: présentaient des cavités
  - From the very end of the promontory the horizon is seen over two thirds of its circumference (II.5 ¶2). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: cet horizon, qui était visible sur les deux tiers de sa circonférence
  - Its south coast is straight for three miles; beyond, a notch of the land hides the rest of the coast (II.5 ¶3). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la partie rectiligne qui formait la côte sud du promontoire sur une longueur de trois milles
    - quote: une échancrure des terres dissimulait le reste de la côte
  - From the end of the promontory, which forms the tail of the peninsula, the coast rounds over five miles (II.5 ¶26). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: l’extrémité du promontoire qui formait la queue de la presqu’île
    - quote: la côte s’arrondissait sur une distance de cinq milles
  - Of the ninety miles of the island’s perimeter, the south coast counts about twenty from port Ballon to it (II.19 ¶40). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la côte sud en comptait une vingtaine depuis le port jusqu’au promontoire
- **Appears in:** I.11, I.20, II.1, II.4, II.5, II.19
