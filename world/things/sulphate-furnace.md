# The sulphate furnace: a brick furnace of special design for distilling the iron sulphate

*A brick furnace of special design built by the colonists before 18 May; the sulphuric acid was distilled in it.*

Keywords: fourneau, furnace, brick, distillation, sulphate, special

- **What it is.**
  - It is a brick furnace "of a particular arrangement" (I.17 ¶49). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un fourneau de briques d’une disposition particulière
  - It was to serve for distilling the iron sulphate once that was obtained (I.17 ¶49). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: qui devait servir à la distillation du sulfate de fer
  - It was built in the days after the eight days of the first work, with refractory pottery of plastic clay, and finished about 18 May (I.17 ¶49). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Tout cela fut achevé vers le 18 mai
- **Made from.**
  - Its makers were the colonists under Smith's guidance (I.17 ¶49). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: habilement guidés par l’ingénieur
- **On the page.**
  - The iron sulphate crystals were calcined in closed vessels, the refractory pots, in "the furnace", whose heat was to distil the sulphuric acid (I.17 ¶54). The text says "le four" there; this is the same furnace. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: et le four, dont la chaleur devait distiller l’acide sulfurique
  - The operation was perfectly carried out, and on 20 May, twelve days after it began, Smith had the acid (I.17 ¶54). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: L’opération fut parfaitement conduite
- **Appears in:** I.17 ¶49, ¶54
