# The garniture: the old canvas wrapped round the anchor cable at the hawse

*A piece of old canvas that keeps the anchor cable from wearing where it bears on the hawse; missing from the Bonadventure on 10 November.*

Keywords: garniture, écubier, câble, ancre, toile, hawse, Bonadventure

- **What it is.**
  - It is a piece of old canvas wrapped round the anchor cable so that it does not wear where it bears on the hawse (III.6 n2). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: La garniture est un morceau de vieille toile dont on enveloppe le câble de l’ancre
    - quote: afin qu’il ne s’use pas dans la partie qui porte l’écubier
  - Pencroff finds that cable has been paid out and the Bonadventure’s garniture is no longer at the bearing of the hawse (III.6 ¶35). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: On a filé du câble de l’ancre
    - quote: n’est plus au portage de l’écubier
- **Appears in:** III.6
