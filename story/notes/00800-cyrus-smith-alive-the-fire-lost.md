# Cyrus Smith Alive, the Fire Lost (notes)

## Notes for drafting

- **Where it sits.** The French I.8, the second chapter of the grotto sequence after the body is found (I.7 ¶87); it runs from the revival of Cyrus Smith to the discovery that the sea has put out the fire at the Chimneys. *(Drafted.)*
- **Cast.** *(Drafted.)* Nab (¶1), Pencroff, "le marin" (¶1), Gédéon Spilett, "le reporter" (¶3), Harbert (¶3), Cyrus Smith, "l’ingénieur" (¶4; named at ¶12), Top, the dog (¶15, named by Nab; he appears in the action at ¶27).
- **Places.** *(Drafted.)*
  - The grotto among the dunes where Cyrus lies, "la grotte" (¶9), about half a mile from the reefs by Pencroff (¶64), "à plus d’un mille de la côte" by the narrator (¶31).
  - The clear stream a hundred paces off (¶9); the sheltered slope of the dune where some prints remain (¶72).
  - The coast and line of reefs north of the Chimneys (¶17–¶21); the "haute dune" with stunted trees where the litter's branches are cut (¶47).
  - The Chimneys, eight miles away (¶86); "le pan coupé" before them (¶88); the islet (¶90); the granite wall (¶90).
- **Things.** *(Drafted.)*
  - Handkerchief used to carry water (¶9); the sailor's jacket for rubbing (¶13).
  - The waistcoat pocket with Cyrus's watch, the only thing in his pockets (¶38); the burnt linen left behind at the Chimneys (¶38).
  - Tetras flesh and its jelly (¶39, ¶54); two bivalve shells as cups (¶39).
  - The litter of pine branches, moss and grass (¶49, ¶85).
  - Cyrus's shoes, matched to the prints (¶73, ¶79).
  - Fire and tinder at the Chimneys, destroyed (¶94).
- **Time.** The chapter gives no date of its own. *(Drafted.)*
  - Nab's search and the prints are "la veille" and "hier" (¶17, ¶21), about five in the evening (¶21).
  - The party reaches Cyrus at night's end; it is ten o'clock when the litter is made (¶50), about noon when they leave (¶83), and half past five when they reach the Chimneys (¶88).
  - The storm is "de la veille" (¶90).
  - *(My reading, to confirm.)* The day is 27 March 1865, and "hier" is 26 March, because I.9 ¶30 calls the next day "28 mars". The year rests on I.11 ¶92.
  - The chapter covers about one day, from early morning to 5:30 p.m.
- **Clues.** *(Drafted.)* Things the text leaves unexplained that a later chapter may explain.
  - Cyrus has no wound after crossing the reefs.
    - quote: l’ingénieur ne portait aucune trace des efforts qu’il avait dû faire pour franchir la ligne d’écueils
  - The narrator defers the explanation.
    - quote: l’explication de cette circonstance viendrait plus tard
  - Nothing disturbs the shore: no sign of any landing along the shells.
    - quote: il n’existait pas trace d’un atterrissage, ni ancien, ni récent
  - The grotto is far from the sea: "plus d’un mille" by the narrator and a half mile by Pencroff.
    - quote: cette grotte perdue au milieu des dunes
  - The companions say it is evident that Cyrus came alone but not credible.
    - quote: C’est évident, en effet, fit observer Gédéon Spilett, mais ce n’est pas croyable !
  - Cyrus says unexplained things happened, and defers the talk.
    - quote: Il s’est passé là des choses inexplicables !
  - Cyrus wonders aloud whether Nab had a lapse of memory, and then drops the idea.
    - quote: ce n’est pas toi qui… tu n’aurais pas eu un moment d’absence…
  - The narrator insists that the sleepwalking explanation is the only one.
    - quote: qu’à Top revenait tout l’honneur de l’affaire
  - Top reached the Chimneys by an "instinct", never having been there.
    - quote: guidé par un instinct que l’on peut regarder presque comme surnaturel
- **Length.** *(Drafted.)* 94 paragraphs (¶1–¶94, plus the summary ¶0), 3,112 words in the ¶ lines. One footnote (n1) and two plate captions (pl1 after ¶85, pl2 after ¶94).

## Open and flags

- **The grotto's distance from the coast.** *(Drafted.)* The narrator says more than a mile; Pencroff says half a mile from the reefs. Neither carded.
  - quote: à plus d’un mille de la côte
  - quote: À un demi-mille environ
  - The two are measured from different points ("de la côte" and "des récifs"); this is *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- **The direction of Nab's search.** *(Drafted.)* The text reads "dans la direction du nord-nord"; the print's second compass point appears to be missing or duplicated. The text carries no Wikisource correction here (texts/README.md lists none for I.8).
  - quote: dans la direction du nord-nord
- **Plate captions.** The caption "Il dut s’appuyer sur le marin." is set after ¶94 but repeats words of ¶83. *(My reading, to confirm.)* It is a placement of the print, not a second event.
  - quote: Il dut s’appuyer sur le marin
- **The sea reached the Chimneys.** The narrator says the sea passed over the islet to the foot of the wall. *(Drafted.)* Earlier chapters were not given; the state of the Chimneys before the storm is not checked here.
  - quote: l’effroyable tempête de la veille avait modifié l’aspect des lieux
- **Seam with I.7 ¶87.** I.7 ends: « Ce corps était celui de l’ingénieur Cyrus Smith. » I.8 ¶1 follows: Nab does not move and the sailor says one word, "Vivant !". *(My reading, to confirm.)* The seam holds.
  - quote: Nab ne bougea pas. Le marin ne lui jeta qu’un mot.
- No fact in this chapter rests on a Wikisource correction listed in texts/README.md. *(Drafted.)*
