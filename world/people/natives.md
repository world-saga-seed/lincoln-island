# Natives: the islanders of other islands, feared and never met

*Possible visitors from nearby islands in praos or pirogues, whom Cyrus Smith fears more than he wishes for them, and who are not seen.*

Keywords: indigènes, natives, islanders, praos, pirogues, Polynésiens, Malais, Pacific, précautions, descente, fumée

- **What it is.**
  - Islanders in these small spaces commonly live on the coast; the coast appears wholly deserted (I.11 ¶38). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les insulaires, dans ces étroits espaces émergés des flots du Pacifique, habitent plutôt le littoral
  - The island seems uninhabited, but whether natives of nearby islands visit it, even for a time, is hard to say; fifty miles are easily crossed by Malay praos or great Polynesian pirogues (I.11 ¶40). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: soit par des praos malais, soit par de grandes pirogues polynésiennes
  - Cyrus Smith thinks it fitting to take precautions against a possible descent of neighbouring natives (I.11 ¶40). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il était donc convenable de prendre certaines précautions contre une descente possible des indigènes voisins
  - Cyrus Smith says he fears the natives, if there are any on this island, rather than desires them (I.12 ¶23). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Je redoute plutôt les indigènes, s’il y en a sur cette île, que je ne les désire.
- **On the page.**
  - On seeing smoke, Cyrus Smith will not show himself before knowing whom they have to deal with; it proves to be the sulphur spring (I.12 ¶23). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Évitons de nous montrer avant de savoir à qui nous avons affaire
- **Appears in:** I.11, I.12
