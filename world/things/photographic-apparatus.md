# The photographic apparatus: the boxed camera of the chest

*A box holding a whole photographic apparatus, with lens, plates and chemicals, whose use Smith and Pencroff cannot see.*

Keywords: appareil photographique, camera, photography, objectif, plaques, chemicals, chest

- **What it is.**
  - It is one box holding a whole photographic apparatus, with lens, plates, chemicals and so on (II.2 ¶53). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: 1 boîte renfermant tout un appareil photographique, objectif, plaques, produits chimiques, etc.
  - Like the other instruments it carries no maker’s mark and seems never to have been used (II.2 ¶64). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ne portaient la marque du fabricant
  - Pencroff, rather doubtful, remarks on it among the contents ("Même la boîte à photographie !") (II.2 ¶61). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Même la boîte à photographie
  - Smith does not see its use and says a fuller set of clothes or more munitions would have been better for castaways (II.2 ¶62). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: je n’en comprends pas bien l’utilité
    - quote: un assortiment de vêtements plus complet ou des munitions plus abondantes
- **Appears in:** II.2
