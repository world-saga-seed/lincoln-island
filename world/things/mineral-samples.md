# The mineral samples: the specimens Smith picked up on the walk and showed that night

*The minerals and plants Smith silently pocketed along the way: iron ore, pyrite, clay, lime and coal, "nature's share" of the common work.*

Keywords: échantillons, specimens, minerals, minerai de fer, pyrite, argile, chaux, charbon, pocket

- **What it is.**
  - Smith picked up, now one thing, now another, mineral or vegetable, which he put in his pocket without a word (I.12 ¶16). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ne s’écartant de sa route que pour ramasser, tantôt une chose, tantôt une autre, substance minérale ou végétale, qu’il mettait dans sa poche sans faire aucune réflexion
  - Pencroff, watching, saw nothing worth stooping for (I.12 ¶17). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Que diable ramasse-t-il donc ainsi ?
  - That night he drew small samples of different minerals from his pocket: iron ore, a pyrite (see [iron ore](iron-ore.md), [pyrite](pyrite.md)), clay, lime and coal (I.12 ¶76). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Cyrus Smith tira de sa poche de petits échantillons de minéraux d’espèces différentes
  - He said: "Here is what nature gives us, and here is her share in the common work. Tomorrow ours." (I.12 ¶77). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Voilà ce que nous donne la nature, et voilà sa part dans le travail commun !
- **Appears in:** I.12
- **Open:**
  - Whether the "plants" of ¶16 are among the samples shown is not said: ¶76 shows only minerals.
    - quote: substance minérale ou végétale
