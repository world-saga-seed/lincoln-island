# Timeline fragment K (French II.14 to II.17, chapters 03600 to 03900)

The year of the colonists' dates is 1866, from II.16 ¶39. Dates in 1866 below rest on that paragraph and are marked as readings where the text gives no year.

## 03600 (II.14)

- 1866-10-13, night: the three enter the dark house on Tabor (II.14 ¶1). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: étaient restés silencieux au milieu de l’obscurité
  - quote: reprendre leur exploration de la veille
- 1866-10-14, dawn: day comes; they examine the house (II.14 ¶20). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: Le jour parut
- 1866-10-14, about 2 p.m.: they rest under pines and plan to leave "demain au point du jour" (II.14 ¶33). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: vers deux heures après midi
- 1866-10-14, afternoon: the wild man is captured; the night is passed aboard (II.14 ¶43–¶92). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: La nuit se passa
- 1866-10-15, dawn: the wind has gone north-west (II.14 ¶93). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: le lendemain, — 15 octobre, — le changement de temps prévu par Pencroff s’était produit
- 1866-10-15, 5 a.m.: the Bonadventure weighs anchor (II.14 ¶94). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: À cinq heures du matin, l’ancre fut levée
- 1866-10-16: the wind freshens and veers north (II.14 ¶96). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Le lendemain, — 16 octobre, — le vent fraîchit beaucoup
- 1866-10-17, morning: forty-eight hours out, no island (II.14 ¶97). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: le 17 au matin, il y avait quarante-huit heures que le Bonadventure était parti
- 1866-10-18: the boat is covered by a wave and the prisoner helps (II.14 ¶98–¶99). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: dans la journée du 18, le Bonadventure fut entièrement coiffé par une lame
- 1866-10-18, about 11 p.m.: the wind drops (II.14 ¶102). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: La nuit du 18 au 19 fut obscure et froide. Toutefois, vers onze heures, le vent calmit
- 1866-10-19, about 2 a.m.: the fire is sighted twenty miles north-east (II.14 ¶105–¶107). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Vers deux heures du matin, il se leva tout à coup

## 03700 (II.15)

- 1866-10-20, 7 a.m.: the Bonadventure grounds at the Mercy's mouth. Flagged: the fire was seen at about 2 a.m. on the 19th, twenty miles off; the text says "Le lendemain" (II.15 ¶1). *(Drafted.)*
