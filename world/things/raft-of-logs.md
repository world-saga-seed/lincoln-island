# The raft of logs: a raft of dead wood bound with dry lianas

*A raft built in an hour at an eddy of the river, carrying a load of dead wood for twenty men, floated down on the ebb to the Chimneys.*

Keywords: radeau, train de bois, float, timber, river transport, tide, pole

- **What it is.**
  - The idea was Pencroff's: rafts were not invented for nothing, the river being a road that walks by itself (I.4 ¶35). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les trains de bois n’ont pas été inventés pour rien
  - Pencroff made it of fairly large pieces of wood tied together with dry lianas, in an eddy made by a point of the bank (I.4 ¶39). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des morceaux de bois assez gros qu’ils avaient attachés ensemble avec des lianes sèches
  - It was built in an hour and held the whole harvest of wood, the load of at least twenty men (I.4 ¶39). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: soit la charge de vingt hommes au moins
  - It was moored to the bank to wait for the turn of the tide (I.4 ¶39). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: amarré à la berge, dut attendre le renversement de la marée
- **On the page.**
  - At the turn of the ebb at one o'clock, Pencroff held it by a vine cable from the bank while Harbert kept it in the current with a long pole (I.4 ¶63). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Harbert, repoussant le train avec une longue perche
  - Neither boarded it; it followed the stream and reached the mouth, a few steps from the Chimneys, in under two hours (I.4 ¶64). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: à quelques pas des Cheminées
  - As the first time, Pencroff quickly set up a raft of logs, though for want of fire it seemed to him a useless task (I.9 ¶118). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ainsi qu’il l’avait fait la première fois, Pencroff établit rapidement un train de bois, bien que, faute de feu, cela lui semblât une besogne inutile
  - It followed the current of the river back toward the Chimneys (I.9 ¶118). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le train suivant le fil de l’eau, on revint vers les Cheminées
- **Appears in:** I.4, I.9
