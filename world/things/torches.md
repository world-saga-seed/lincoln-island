# The torches: resinous branches lit for the descent of the old outlet

*Resinous branches cut by the lake’s shore, set up as torches and lit with the briquet, and burning resin carried into the dark tunnels of the mountain.*

Keywords: torches, flambeaux, resinous branches, branches résineuses, pins, light, descent, résine

- **What it is.**
  - Smith asks for light, and Nab and Harbert cut resinous branches from the pines and other evergreens on the lake’s shore and make them into the form of torches (I.18 ¶17, ¶18). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: quelques branches résineuses
    - quote: des branches qu’ils disposèrent en forme de torches
  - They are lit with the briquet (I.18 ¶18). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: allumées au feu du briquet
  - A burning branch thrown down the well lights its inside by the bright resin, its power of light growing with the speed of its fall, and goes out with a slight shudder on meeting the water (I.18 ¶48). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: La résine éclatante, dont le pouvoir éclairant s’accrut encore par la rapidité de sa chute
    - quote: la flamme s’éteignit avec un léger frémissement
  - Burning resins are carried through the dark galleries of Mont Franklin in the search of February (III.13 ¶71). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: on y promena des résines enflammées
- **Appears in:** I.18, III.13
