# The casuarinas: trees of the Red Creek banks

*Casuarinas of the temperate Australian kind that shade the Red Creek.*

Keywords: casuarina, filao, Australian tree, Red Creek, shade

- **What it is.**
  - Casuarinas and eucalyptus line the banks of the Red Creek (I.12 ¶41). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des casuarinas et des eucalyptus
  - Their branches are a little meagre and do not hinder the birds' wings (I.12 ¶43). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ces ramures un peu maigres des eucalyptus et des casuarinas
  - The Red Creek runs under the vault of casuarinas, banksias and gigantic gum trees (I.12 ¶66). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: sous la voûte des casuarinas, des banksias et des gommiers gigantesques
  - It is among the trees of the forest cut through on 31 October, most of which were already known round the lake and the Grande-Vue plateau, generally of medium size because their number has hindered their growth (II.4 ¶5). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des casuarinas
    - quote: généralement de taille médiocre, car leur nombre avait nui à leur développement
  - It is one of the known kinds that follow one another beyond sight in the forest of the Far-West (III.11 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Déodars, douglas, casuarinas, banksias, gommiers, dragonniers et autres essences déjà reconnues, se succédaient au delà des limites du regard
- **Appears in:** I.12, II.4, III.11
