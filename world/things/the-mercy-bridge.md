# The Mercy bridge: the pile bridge with a tipping span, built over the river's bend

*The bridge of piles and planks, fixed on the right bank and tipping on counterweights on the left, built over three weeks at the Mercy's bend and finished on 20 November to connect Granite-house with the south of the island.*

Keywords: pont de la Mercy, bridge, pont-levis, drawbridge, contre-poids, counterweights, piles, sonnette, tablier, bend, coude

- **What it is.**
  - Spilett says a bridge will be useful if the colonists want easy access to the Far-West (II.5 ¶22). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il sera utile de construire un pont, si nous voulons avoir un accès facile dans le Far-West
  - It is one of the two most urgent projects of 2 November: a bridge on the Mercy to put the south of the island in communication with Granite-house, which would also ease the carriage of the balloon's envelope (II.7 ¶5, ¶6). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: l’établissement d’un pont sur la Mercy, afin de mettre la partie sud de l’île en communication avec Granite-house
    - quote: le pont rendrait facile le transport de l’enveloppe du ballon
  - Its site is the bend of the Mercy, reached by going up the left bank, judged suitable on 3 November; from it to port Ballon is a distance of three miles and a half (II.7 ¶12, ¶13, ¶14). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: arrivèrent bientôt au coude formé par la rivière
    - quote: L’endroit parut convenable
  - The river is about eighty feet wide there (II.5 ¶99; II.7 ¶27). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Là, le lit mesurait une largeur de quatre-vingts pieds
    - quote: la Mercy était large de quatre-vingts pieds environ
  - Its southern end is the point to which the closing of the plateau runs along the river from the Mercy's mouth (II.7 ¶21). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: depuis cette embouchure jusqu’au coude de la Mercy où devait être établi le pont
  - Smith means all the bridges and small bridges of the plateau to be raisable at will, so that the plateau is safe from any surprise (II.7 ¶23). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: si ces pont et ponceaux peuvent être levés à volonté
- **Made from.**
  - Trees are chosen, felled, trimmed and sawn into joists, beams and planks (II.7 ¶26). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: débités en poutrelles, en madriers et en planches
  - The part that rests on the right bank is fixed; the part that joins the left bank is mobile and rises on counterweights, like some lock bridges (II.7 ¶26). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: fixe dans la partie qui s’appuyait à la rive droite de la Mercy
    - quote: devait être mobile dans la partie qui se relierait à la rive gauche
    - quote: au moyen de contre-poids, comme certains ponts d’écluse
  - Piles are driven into the riverbed with a pile driver to carry the fixed deck, so that it forms two arches able to bear heavy loads (II.7 ¶27). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: enfoncer des pieux dans le lit de la rivière
    - quote: une sonnette
    - quote: deux arches
    - quote: supporter de lourds fardeaux
  - Saws, axes, chisels and hammers are carried down for the work (II.7 ¶9). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Scies, haches, ciseaux, marteaux
  - The tools for working wood, the ironwork to strengthen it, the engineer's skill and the colonists' seven months' practice are all at hand; Spilett is not the clumsiest (II.7 ¶28). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ni les outils pour travailler le bois, ni les ferrures pour le consolider

  - It takes three weeks, the colonists breakfasting on the spot and returning to Granite-house only for supper (II.7 ¶29). *(Drafted.)*

  - It is finished on 20 November (II.7 ¶31). *(Drafted.)*

  - The loaded cart comes back over it at eight in the evening of the first trip to port Ballon (II.7 ¶64). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: après avoir repassé le pont de la Mercy
