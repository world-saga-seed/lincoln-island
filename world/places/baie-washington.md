# Baie Washington: the large indentation of the south coast

*The wide bay of the island's south, named after Washington on 30 March together with the Union Bay and Mont Franklin.*

Keywords: baie Washington, Washington Bay, south, échancrure, bay, south coast

- **What it is.**
  - Smith proposed the name baie Washington for the large indentation of the south (I.11 ¶63). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: à cette large échancrure du sud, celui de baie Washington
  - It was named at once, with the Union Bay and Mont Franklin (I.11 ¶65). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: on nomma baie de l’Union, baie Washington et mont Franklin
- **Appears in:** I.11
