# The windmill: the wind mill built on the Grande-Vue plateau

*A wind mill built by Smith’s plans on the Grande-Vue plateau, finished on 1 December, a cylindrical pepper-pot cage on a pivot with four sails of balloon cloth.*

Keywords: moulin à vent, windmill, ailes, sails, meules, millstones, poivrière, pivot, trémie, babillard, blutoir, Grande-Vue

- **What it is.**
  - It is built because the grain is there but not the flour; Smith could have used the second fall on the Mercy for his motor, the first being busy driving the stampers of the fulling mill, but after discussion it is decided to put a simple windmill on the heights of Grande-Vue, where the wind is sure to be plentiful (II.16 ¶82). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: on avait le grain, mais non la farine
    - quote: il fut décidé que l’on établirait un simple moulin à vent sur les hauteurs de Grande-Vue
  - Timber is chosen for the cage and the mechanism, large sandstones from the north of the lake serve as millstones, and the balloon’s envelope supplies the cloth for the sails (see [the sandstone](sandstone.md); [the balloon’s envelope](balloon-envelope.md)) (II.16 ¶84). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des bois de charpente pour la cage et le mécanisme du moulin
    - quote: Quelques grands grès qui se trouvaient dans le nord du lac
    - quote: l’inépuisable enveloppe du ballon leur fournirait la toile nécessaire
  - Smith draws the plans; the mill stands a little to the right of the poultry-yard, near the bank of the lake, the whole cage resting on a pivot held in big timbers so as to turn, with its mechanism, according to the wind (II.16 ¶85). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Cyrus Smith fit les plans
    - quote: un peu à droite de la basse-cour, près de la berge du lac
    - quote: Toute la cage devait reposer sur un pivot
  - It is a cylindrical sentry-box, a true pepper-pot, capped with a pointed roof (II.16 ¶86). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une sorte de guérite cylindrique, une vraie poivrière, coiffée d’un toit aigu
  - Its four sail frames are set in the shaft at an angle to it and fixed with iron tenons (II.16 ¶86). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Les quatre châssis qui formaient les ailes
    - quote: au moyen de tenons de fer
  - Inside are a box for the two millstones, the bed stone and the running stone, a hopper, an oscillating trough that regulates the grain’s passage and is called "babillard" for its constant tick-tack, and a bolter that sifts the bran from the flour (II.16 ¶86). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la boîte destinée à contenir les deux meules
    - quote: la trémie
    - quote: « babillard »
    - quote: le blutoir
  - It is built by Nab and Pencroff, now very able carpenters, following the templates given by Smith, with everyone’s help, and is finished on 1 December (II.16 ¶86, ¶87). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Nab et Pencroff étaient devenus de très-habiles charpentiers
    - quote: le 1er décembre il était terminé
  - Smith’s rule: a mill does the most work when the sails’ turns a minute are six times the feet the wind covers in a second; in a medium breeze of twenty-four feet a second it turns the sails sixteen times a minute (II.16 ¶92). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: sextuple du nombre de pieds parcourus par le vent en une seconde
    - quote: il imprimera seize tours aux ailes pendant une minute
  - In the winter gales of 1867 it, with the poultry-yard, suffers particularly, and urgent repairs are made (II.20 ¶75). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Le moulin et la basse-cour eurent particulièrement à souffrir
  - It is too easily recognised from the sea, and its sails can be taken off to hide it (III.1 ¶79). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: est trop facilement reconnaissable
    - quote: Qu’Ayrton et Nab aillent en démonter les ailes
  - Fire destroys it on the night of 28 to 29 November, with the poultry-yard buildings and the onaggas’ stable (III.9 ¶92; III.10 ¶29). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une épaisse fumée qui tourbillonnait au-dessus du moulin
    - quote: Quant au moulin et aux bâtiments de la basse-cour, à l’étable des onaggas, le feu avait tout détruit
  - Smith will not raise it again until the island is cleared of the convicts (III.11 ¶20). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Quant à relever les bâtiments de la basse-cour, le moulin, les écuries, Cyrus Smith préféra attendre
