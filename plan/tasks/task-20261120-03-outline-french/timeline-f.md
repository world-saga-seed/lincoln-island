# Timeline fragment F (I.16 to I.19, chapters 01600 to 01900)

The year 1865 is not stated in these four chapters; it rests on the earlier chapters (I.11 ¶92). Days are given as the date each works out to. Where two lines disagree both are given and flagged.

## I.16 (01600)

- 1865-05-06: the colonists consider winter quarters; the French says it corresponds to 6 November in the northern hemisphere (I.16 ¶1). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: On était au 6 mai, jour qui correspond au 6 novembre
- 1865-05-06, daytime: the wall is searched for two miles and the lake circled (I.16 ¶21, ¶28). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: On sortit donc, et l’exploration fut faite
- 1865-05-06, a half hour before the second plunge: the party reaches the lake's south-east angle (I.16 ¶47). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Une demi-heure après, ils étaient tous arrivés à l’angle sud-est du lac
- 1865-05-06, 5 p.m.: Top plunges in a second time and the dugong fight follows (I.16 ¶53). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: il était cinq heures du soir

## I.17 (01700)

- 1865-05-07, morning: Cyrus and Spilett at the lake; the dugong still on the beach; the outlet found (I.17 ¶1). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Le lendemain, 7 mai
- 1865-05-07, all day: pyrites hauled to the Chimneys (I.17 ¶38). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Toute la journée fut employée à charrier une certaine quantité de ces pyrites
- 1865-05-08: Cyrus begins the pyrites heap (I.17 ¶39). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Le lendemain, 8 mai, l’ingénieur commença ses manipulations.
- 1865-05-18 to 1865-05-20: the heap's change takes ten to twelve days from 8 May (I.17 ¶43). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: dix à douze jours
- 1865-05-08 to about 1865-05-16: the soap, glycerine and saltpetre work lasts about eight days (I.17 ¶49). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: Ces divers travaux durèrent une huitaine de jours.
- 1865-05-18, about: the pottery and furnace are done, as the chemical change ends (I.17 ¶49). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Tout cela fut achevé vers le 18 mai
- 1865-05-20: Cyrus has sulphuric acid, twelve days after starting (I.17 ¶54). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: le 20 mai, douze jours après avoir commencé
- 1865-05-20, after the acid: nitric acid, then nitro-glycerine, made alone and shown to the others (I.17 ¶55–¶60). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: Demain, dès que nous aurons creusé un trou de mine
- 1865-05-21, dawn: the miners go to the lake's east bank (I.17 ¶66). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Le lendemain, — 21 mai, — dès l’aube
- 1865-05-21, about 4 p.m.: the mine hole is finished (I.17 ¶68). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: vers quatre heures du soir, le trou de mine était achevé
- 1865-05-21, after the hole, 25 minutes after the fibre is lit: the explosion (I.17 ¶75). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: vingt-cinq minutes après, une explosion

## I.18 (01800)

- 1865-05-21, after the explosion: the colonists fetch tools and go down the old outlet (I.18 ¶4, ¶18). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: Les colons revinrent aux Cheminées
- 1865-05-21, within an hour of arriving at the lake: the outlet widened by Nab and Pencroff (I.18 ¶14). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: en moins d’une heure
- 1865-05-21, two hours of piercing the wall (I.18 ¶63). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Ce travail durait depuis deux heures déjà
- 1865-05-21, "a little before four o'clock": the party comes out of the outlet (I.18 ¶83). Discord with I.17 ¶68 and ¶75 (hole finished about 4 p.m., explosion after), flagged in the notes. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: un peu avant quatre heures

  - quote: Le lendemain, 22 mai
