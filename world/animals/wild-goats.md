# Wild goats: the chèvres sauvages of the corral beat

*Wild goats driven with the mouflons into the corral, about ten of them.*

Keywords: chèvre, chèvres sauvages, corral, mouflons, battue

- **What it is.**
  - The corral is meant to hold, with about a hundred horned animals, mouflons or wild goats, the young born later (II.8 ¶32). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une centaine d’animaux à cornes, mouflons ou chèvres sauvages
  - They graze with the mouflons and are driven with them in the beat toward the corral (II.8 ¶37). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: rabattre les mouflons et les chèvres
  - About ten wild goats, with some thirty mouflons, are driven back to the corral through its open door and shut in (II.8 ¶39). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une trentaine de ces ruminants et une dizaine de chèvres sauvages
    - quote: purent être emprisonnés
  - The goats of the corral, with the onagga and the ewes, supply the colony's daily milk (II.9 ¶63). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: l’onagga, les chèvres et les brebis du corral fournirent quotidiennement le lait nécessaire à la colonie
  - The corral's flock of mouflons and goats counts a certain number of young that must be housed and fed (II.12 ¶85). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le troupeau de mouflons et de chèvres comptait un certain nombre de petits
  - The corral has goats that Ayrton can bring back: Nab wants a couple of them to acclimatise on the plateau's prairies (III.6 ¶54). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une couple de chèvres que Nab voulait acclimater sur les prairies du plateau
  - Wild goats and mouflons frisk among the rocks in the stony gorge where the island's barren part begins (III.13 ¶68). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Les chèvres sauvages et les mouflons gambadaient entre les roches
  - The corral's flock of mouflons and goats is tended every two days, and its milk supplies Nab's pantry (III.14 ¶20). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: allait soigner le troupeau de mouflons et de chèvres
    - quote: rapportait le lait qui approvisionnait l’office de Nab
- **Appears in:** II.8, II.9, II.12, III.6, III.12, III.13, III.14
