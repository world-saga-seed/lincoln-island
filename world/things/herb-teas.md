# The herb-teas: the cooling drinks made for Harbert

*Cooling tisanes of maple sugar and the medicinal plants Harbert had gathered by Lake Grant, given to him wounded.*

Keywords: tisanes, infusions, sucre d’érable, plantes médicinales, rafraîchissantes, Harbert

- **What it is.**
  - They are made of maple sugar and medicinal plants, those Harbert himself gathered on the banks of Lake Grant; they are cooling (III.7 ¶45). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: permirent de faire quelques rafraîchissantes tisanes
    - quote: celles-là mêmes que le jeune garçon avait cueillies sur les berges du lac Grant
  - He takes them without knowing it, while unconscious (III.7 ¶45). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: on les lui fit prendre sans qu’il s’en rendît compte
  - He does not lack them while on a strict diet (III.8 ¶9). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les tisanes ne lui manquaient pas
