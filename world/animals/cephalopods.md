# Cephalopods: the giant octopus feared in the granite cavities

*A gigantic cephalopod the colonists think of, but do not meet, as they descend the old outlet.*

Keywords: poulpe, octopus, pieuvre, céphalopode, calmar, squid, monstre, monster, prudence, déversoir

- **What it is.**
  - A feared, not a met, animal: as they descend in silence, more than one colonist thinks that some octopus or other gigantic cephalopod may occupy the inner cavities, which communicate with the sea; so they go on only with a certain caution (I.18 ¶20). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: quelque poulpe ou autre gigantesque céphalopode pouvait occuper les cavités intérieures, qui se trouvaient en communication avec la mer
    - quote: ne s’aventurer qu’avec une certaine prudence
- **Appears in:** I.18
