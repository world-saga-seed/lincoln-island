# The dovecote: the pigeon house in a corner of the poultry-yard

*A dovecote built by Smith in a corner of the poultry-yard, lodging a dozen rock pigeons.*

Keywords: pigeonnier, dovecote, pigeons, basse-cour, poultry yard

- **What it is.**
  - Smith sets a dovecote in one corner of the poultry-yard and lodges in it a dozen of the pigeons that haunted the high rocks of the plateau (II.7 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: établit un pigeonnier dans un angle de la basse-cour
    - quote: une douzaine de ces pigeons qui fréquentaient les hauts rocs du plateau
  - The birds soon grow used to returning each evening to their new home, and are more disposed to domestication than the wood-pigeons, which breed only wild (II.7 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: montrèrent plus de propension à se domestiquer que les ramiers leurs congénères
  - In the winter gales the dovecote is unroofed twice (II.11 ¶76). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Le pigeonnier fut décoiffé deux fois
- **Appears in:** II.7, II.11
