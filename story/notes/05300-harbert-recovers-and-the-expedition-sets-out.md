## Open and flags
- **Ten days after 9 December.** ¶11 says "Dix jours après, le 20 décembre". The quinine was given on the morning of 9 December (III.10 ¶59–¶64, III.11 ¶1–¶9), and ten days after that is the 19th. Quote:
  - quote: Dix jours après, le 20 décembre, Harbert entrait en convalescence
  - *(My reading, to confirm.)* Card neither.
- **The weekday.** ¶32 says 14 February was a Sunday. By the Gregorian calendar 14 February 1868 was a Friday. Quote:
  - quote: Le 14 février, veille du départ, était un dimanche
  - *(My reading, to confirm.)* The book may count days on its own reckoning.
- **Distances.** ¶47 puts the first camp at about nine miles and ¶52 gives six miles for the second day, which is fifteen; ¶64 says fourteen. ¶64 then puts the camp five miles from the Reptile-End, 19 in all, while ¶29 gives the whole track as sixteen to seventeen miles. Quotes:
  - quote: neuf milles environ de Granite-house
  - quote: Ce jour-là, on ne put franchir que six milles
  - quote: à quatorze milles de Granite-house
  - quote: sur une longueur de seize à dix-sept milles
  - *(My reading, to confirm.)* Card none.
- **Fire and the first night.** ¶48 says that fires would draw the convicts. ¶45 forbids hunting. No earlier chapter given to me states a rule against fires; none is flagged. *(Drafted.)*
- **Footnote.** n1's "10 grammes" glosses "dix-huit grains" (¶4); the box's "deux cents grains" (¶1) has no gloss. *(Drafted.)*
