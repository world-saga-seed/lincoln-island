# The road to port Ballon: the cut through the Far-West from the Mercy bend to the south coast

*A road planned on 3 November and cut through the Far-West forest by the end of December for the cart, from the Mercy bend to port Ballon; rough at first, improved later.*

Keywords: route, chemin, coupée, port Ballon, cart, road, carrossable, Far-West, bend

- **What it is.**
  - It is planned as a carriage road from the bend of the Mercy to port Ballon, which would make communication easy between Granite-house and the south of the island (II.7 ¶14). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une route carrossable, qui rendrait les communications faciles entre Granite-house et le sud de l’île
  - To carry the cart to port Ballon a road has to be broken through the thick masses of the Far-West, which takes time (II.7 ¶32). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: nécessité de frayer une route à travers les épais massifs du Far-West
  - By the end of December it is a straight road, or rather a cut, through the forest of the Far-West from the bend of the Mercy to port Ballon (II.7 ¶61). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une route droite, ou plutôt une coupée avait été faite à travers la forêt du Far-West, depuis le coude de la Mercy jusqu’au port Ballon
  - It is hardly roughed out when the cart first takes it (II.7 ¶63). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: cette route à peine ébauchée
  - In February it is improved, together with the road to the corral (II.8 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: en même temps qu’on améliorait les routes du corral et du port Ballon
  - The hunters take it to the marsh, setting out from Granite-house over the Mercy bridge (II.11 ¶80). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Les chasseurs prirent donc la route de port Ballon pour se rendre au marais
  - The way to the south coast is three and a half miles long, running between the thick forest on one side and the marsh of the tadornes on the other (III.6 ¶17). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ce n’était qu’une distance de trois milles et demi
    - quote: tant du côté de l’épaisse forêt que du côté du marais des tadornes
- **Appears in:** II.7, II.8, II.11, III.6
