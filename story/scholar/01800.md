## a

After: a little before 4 o’clock, just as the torch of the sailor was burnt out, Smith and his companions emerged from the mouth of the passage.

Chapter XVII has the mine hole finished by four in the afternoon, and the explosion twenty-five minutes after. Since then the colonists have gone back for their tools, widened the outlet, gone down it, and worked at the wall for two hours. They cannot have come out before four. The hours of chapter XVII and the two hours of work in this chapter agree with each other, and I take them against this one. I find nothing that gives the true hour.
