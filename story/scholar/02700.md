## a

After: give up this day, the 2nd of November, to it?

The papers count the days to here one by one. Chapter XXV gives the morning of the 31st of October, at the falls of the Mercy. That day went to the west coast, and its night was passed in the jaguar’s den; this is the next morning, the 1st of November. Two other passages agree. A few lines on, Spilett says the canoe has been left alone “for a day,” which is right on the 1st. And chapter XXIX, after one more day at home, calls the morning that follows the 3rd of November. I take this day for the 1st.
