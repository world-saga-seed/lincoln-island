## a

After: Only a hundred and fifty miles separated them in longitude, and seventy-five miles in latitude.

Chapter XXXI gives the islet’s place from the atlas as two degrees and a half west and two degrees south of Lincoln Island, and chapter XIV reckons sixty miles to the degree. The two degrees and a half make the hundred and fifty miles of longitude here; the two degrees make a hundred and twenty miles of latitude, not seventy-five. By either reckoning the islet lies farther off than the hundred and fifty miles of chapter XXXI. The voyage of chapter XXXV does not settle the latitude, and I leave it open.
