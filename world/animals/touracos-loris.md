# Touracos-loris: the green, crimson-winged climbers of the Far-West

*Pigeon-sized climbing birds, painted green with crimson in the wings and a white-edged crest, better to eat than the jacamar.*

Keywords: touracos-loris, loris, touraco, lori, grimpeurs, huppe, cramoisi, vert, gibier, Far-West, tinamous

- **What it is.**
  - They are "touracos-loris", a sort of climbers the size of a pigeon, painted green all over, with part of the wings crimson and an upright crest festooned with a white edging (II.3 ¶28). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: « touracos-loris »
    - quote: sortes de grimpeurs de la grosseur d’un pigeon
    - quote: tout peinturlurés de vert, avec une partie des ailes de couleur cramoisie et une huppe droite festonnée d’un liseré blanc
  - A dozen are shot at the same time as the jacamar, and Top brings them to the canoe (II.3 ¶28). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Top le rapporta au canot, en même temps qu’une douzaine de « touracos-loris »
  - They make better game than the jacamar, whose flesh is somewhat tough (II.3 ¶28). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Les loris faisaient un gibier meilleur que le jacamar
  - "Loris" are among the birds that swarm in the forest strip of the west coast (II.4 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les oiseaux y fourmillaient, jacamars, couroucous, tragopans, tétras, loris
- **Appears in:** II.3, II.4
