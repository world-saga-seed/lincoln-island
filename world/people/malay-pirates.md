# Malay pirates: the sea-robbers Cyrus Smith fears in this part of the Pacific

*Pirates of the Malay seas, bold seamen and redoubtable malefactors, who often frequent this part of the Pacific, as Cyrus Smith says; none is seen.*

Keywords: pirates malais, pirate, Malais, Malay, Pacifique, Pacific, brigands, malfaiteurs, précautions, danger

- **What it is.**
  - Cyrus Smith says that the colonists are in a part of the Pacific often frequented by Malay pirates (I.16 ¶7). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Nous sommes ici dans une partie du Pacifique souvent fréquentée par les pirates malais
  - They are bold seamen as well as redoubtable malefactors, and the colonists must take measures accordingly, even at this distance from any land (I.16 ¶8, ¶9). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: à une telle distance de toute terre
    - quote: Ces pirates sont de hardis marins aussi bien que des malfaiteurs redoutables
- **Appears in:** I.16
