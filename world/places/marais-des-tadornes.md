# Le marais des Tadornes: the marsh of the south-east, named for its sheldrakes

*The marshy south-east of the island between the Mercy and cape Griffe, about twenty square miles of fed-by-seepage fen with a great stock of water-fowl; named the marsh of the Tadornes (Tadorn’s-fens) on 5 July.*

Keywords: marais, marécage, marsh, fen, tadornes, sheldrake, south-east, wildfowl, game, swamp
