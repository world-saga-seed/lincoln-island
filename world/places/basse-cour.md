# The poultry-yard: the palisaded yard on the lake's south-east shore

*A two-hundred-square-yard enclosure on the south-east bank of Lake Grant, begun in December with huts of branches, a dovecote and a stable beside it, peopled with tinamous, ducks, alectors and other fowl.*

Keywords: basse-cour, poultry yard, volaille, palissade, cahutes, tinamous, canards, alectors, houbara, lake, poulailler
