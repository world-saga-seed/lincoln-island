# The candles: stearic candles made from seal fat

*Candles of seal fat saponified with lime, freed with sulphuric acid and moulded by hand round vegetable-fibre wicks, in a day, on 5 and 6 June; the winter light of Granite-house.*

Keywords: bougies, chandelle, stearic, stéarique, wicks, mèches, light, éclairage, seal fat, graisse de phoque, moulded

- **What it is.**
  - Smith proposes to settle the lighting by a seal hunt: candles, "de la bougie", not tallow (I.20 ¶15, ¶17). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Fi donc ! Pencroff, de la bougie
  - The product is not absolutely perfect but usable (I.20 ¶23). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: du moins étaient-ils utilisables
  - They are true stearic candles, moulded by hand, lacking only bleaching and polishing (I.20 ¶25). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: de véritables bougies stéariques, moulées à la main
    - quote: il ne manqua que le blanchiment et le polissage
  - They lack the advantage of wicks impregnated with boric acid, which vitrify as they burn and are wholly consumed (I.20 ¶25). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: imprégnées d’acide borique
  - They were greatly appreciated on the evenings in Granite-house (I.20 ¶25). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: grandement appréciées pendant les veillées de Granite-house
  - With the shutters stopped tight in the cold of August the consumption of candles was considerable, and the colonists often lit only by the flame of the hearths to save them (I.22 ¶41). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la consommation de bougies fut considérable
    - quote: ne s’éclairèrent souvent qu’avec la flamme des foyers
  - The great hall is lit with candles in the winter evenings (II.11 ¶41). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: bien éclairée de bougies
- **Made from.**
  - Smith had the lime and the sulphuric acid, and the seals of the islet would supply the fat (I.20 ¶18). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il avait de la chaux et de l’acide sulfurique
    - quote: les amphibies de l’îlot lui fourniraient la graisse
  - The hunt gave about three hundred pounds of fat, all to be used for candles (I.20 ¶22). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: environ trois cents livres de graisse
  - Smith chose to saponify the fat with lime, obtaining a lime soap easy to decompose with sulphuric acid, which precipitated the lime as sulphate and freed the fatty acids (see [the lime](lime.md) and [the sulphuric acid](sulphuric-acid.md)) (I.20 ¶23). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il préféra saponifier la graisse au moyen de la chaux
    - quote: qui précipita la chaux à l’état de sulfate et rendit libres les acides gras
  - Of the three acids, oleic, margaric and stearic, the first, being liquid, was driven off by pressure; the other two formed the very substance for moulding the candles (I.20 ¶24). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le premier, étant liquide, fut chassé par une pression suffisante
    - quote: la substance même qui allait servir au moulage des bougies
  - The wicks, after several trials, were made of vegetable fibres dipped in the liquefied substance (I.20 ¶25). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: après plusieurs essais, furent faites de fibres végétales
    - quote: trempées dans la substance liquéfiée
  - The whole operation took no more than twenty-four hours (I.20 ¶25). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ne dura pas plus de vingt-quatre heures
  - Smith also made a fine pair of snuffers (see [the snuffers](snuffers.md)) (I.20 ¶25). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une belle paire de mouchettes
- **Appears in:** I.20, I.22, II.11
