# The telescope: the long glass of the chest

*A long-glass in the chest, taken on the first exploration as "la lunette".*

Keywords: longue-vue, lunette, telescope, glass, spyglass, instrument, chest

- **What it is.**
  - There is one telescope (longue-vue) in the chest, besides a binocular glass (II.2 ¶53). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: 1 longue-vue
  - Like the other instruments it carries no maker’s mark and seems never to have been used (II.2 ¶64). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ne portaient la marque du fabricant
  - Among the instruments the colonists take on the exploration is "la lunette" (the chest has a binocular and a long-glass; the text does not say which) (II.3 ¶5). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: la lunette et la boussole de poche
- **Appears in:** II.2, II.3
