# The refractory pottery: ware of refractory clay for the blast pipe, the crucible and the acid

*Pottery of refractory clay fired in the pottery kiln: the bellows’ pipe, the steel crucible, and the vessels in which the iron sulphate was calcined.*

Keywords: terre réfractaire, refractory clay, crucible, creuset, pipe, tuyau, poterie réfractaire, argile plastique, vessels

- **What it is.**
  - The bellows’ end pipe is of refractory clay, made beforehand in the pottery kiln (I.15 ¶44). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: qui avait été préalablement fabriqué au four à poteries
  - The crucible for the steel is made of refractory clay (I.15 ¶49). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un creuset fait en terre réfractaire
  - In the days after 8 May the colonists make refractory pottery of plastic clay (I.17 ¶49). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: fabriquer de la poterie réfractaire en argile plastique
  - The iron-sulphate crystals are placed in these vessels to be calcined in a closed vessel (I.17 ¶53, ¶54). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: calciner en vase clos les cristaux de sulfate de fer
    - quote: les poteries réfractaires, dans lesquelles furent placés les cristaux
- **Appears in:** I.15, I.17
