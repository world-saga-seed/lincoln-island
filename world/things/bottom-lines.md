# The bottom lines: Pencroff's lines with iron hooks, set in the lake and the Mercy

*Ground lines armed with iron hooks that Pencroff set in the lake and the Mercy, which take trout and other fish.*

Keywords: lignes de fond, hameçons, hooks, fishing, trout, truites, pêche

- **What it is.** A tool, fishing lines. Build if needed. *(Drafted.)*
- **In XIX bis.** *(The inserted chapter, 01950; R1121-132.)*
  - In June 1865 the lines are set at the mouth of the Mercy, armed with iron hooks from the forge; they are Pencroff's own affair, and he visits them every morning (R1121-60) (XIX bis ¶1, 2). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: These lines were his own affair; he had armed them with iron hooks from the forge, and he went every morning to visit them
  - On 2 June the hooks come up with their bait untouched; the lines are the pretext for going out to the bar on 2 and 3 June (XIX bis ¶24, 71). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: the hooks came up with their bait untouched
    - quote: the two went down once more to the lines
