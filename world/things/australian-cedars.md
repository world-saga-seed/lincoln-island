# The Australian cedars: clumps of cedars in the clearings by the Red Creek

*Australian cedars in the clearings of the Red Creek; cedars are met again in the forest of the Far-West.*

Keywords: cèdres australiens, cedar, clearings, tussac, Red Creek, forest

- **What it is.**
  - Clumps of Australian cedars rise in the clearings beside the Red Creek, clothed in the tall grass called tussac in New Holland (I.12 ¶41). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Des bouquets de cèdres australiens s’élevaient aussi dans les clairières
  - "Cèdres" are named among the trees of the forest cut through on 31 October (the text does not say which kind; the card reads them as the same) (II.4 ¶5). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: des hibiscus, des cèdres
- **Appears in:** I.12, II.4
