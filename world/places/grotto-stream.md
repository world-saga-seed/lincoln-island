# The stream by the grotto: a clear stream a hundred paces from the grotto

*A clear stream, swollen by the rains, filtering through the sand a hundred paces from the grotto.*

Keywords: ruisseau, brook, water, sand, rains

- **What it is.**
  - It is a clear stream a hundred paces from the grotto, much swollen by the previous day's rains, filtering through the sand (I.8 ¶9). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un ruisseau limpide, évidemment très-grossi par les pluies de la veille, et qui filtrait à travers le sable
  - The brook near the halt in the dunes, where Harbert fetched fresh water, is read as this stream: the halt lay near where the engineer was found (I.14 ¶65). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: Harbert alla chercher de l’eau douce au ruisseau qui coulait près de là
- **Appears in:** I.8, I.14
