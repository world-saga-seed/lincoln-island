# Errata: Verne's slips in the French print

*Slips in Hetzel's text that the unabridged White meets, and how the render takes each. The text in `fr-1875/` keeps them as printed (world rule 2); this file is where they are named. Paragraphs as in `fr-1875/`.*

| Where | The print | The slip | The render |
|---|---|---|---|
| I.8 ¶17 | "dans la direction du nord-nord" | the compass point is cut short | White's "due north" *(Settled R1121-151.)* |
| I.9 ¶97 | "pin pigeon" | for "pin pignon", as the chapter's own summary prints it | "pignon pine" *(Settled R1121-151.)* |
| I.13 ¶34 and n1 | "Entre ce coucher et ce lever, douze heures vingt-quatre minutes s'étaient écoulées. Donc, six heures douze minutes après son lever…"; n1: "le soleil se levait à 5 h. 33 m. du matin, et se couchait à 6 h. 17 m. du soir" | the note's hours give a night of 11 h. 16 m. and a day of 12 h. 44 m., neither 12 h. 24 m.; and half the night is taken for the morning | as printed, text and note *(Settled R1121-152.)* |
| II.10 n2 | "Port de l'état de New York." | the Vineyard (Martha's Vineyard) is in Massachusetts | as printed *(Resolved by judgment, overturnable.)* |
| II.16 ¶37 | "Décembre, répondit Harbert." | the scene is in November (the 3rd, ¶1; the mill done by 1 December, ¶87) | White's "November" *(Resolved by judgment, overturnable; as R1121-151's "due north".)* |
| II.16 ¶84 | "le nombre de tours … est sextuple du nombre de pieds … vingt-quatre pieds à la seconde, il imprimera seize tours" | six times twenty-four is not sixteen; the rule's ratio is misprinted | as printed *(Resolved by judgment, overturnable.)* |
