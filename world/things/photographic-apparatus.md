# The photographic apparatus: the boxed camera of the chest

*A box holding a whole photographic apparatus, with lens, plates and chemicals, whose use Smith and Pencroff cannot see.*

Keywords: appareil photographique, camera, photography, objectif, plaques, chemicals, chest

- **What it is.**
  - It is one box holding a whole photographic apparatus, with lens, plates, chemicals and so on (II.2 ¶53). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: 1 boîte renfermant tout un appareil photographique, objectif, plaques, produits chimiques, etc.
  - Like the other instruments it carries no maker’s mark and seems never to have been used (II.2 ¶64). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ne portaient la marque du fabricant
  - Pencroff, rather doubtful, remarks on it among the contents ("Même la boîte à photographie !") (II.2 ¶61). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Même la boîte à photographie
  - Smith does not see its use and says a fuller set of clothes or more munitions would have been better for castaways (II.2 ¶62). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: je n’en comprends pas bien l’utilité
    - quote: un assortiment de vêtements plus complet ou des munitions plus abondantes
  - It is very complete, with a powerful lens, and nothing of what is needed for photographic reproduction is lacking: collodion, silver nitrate, hyposulphite of soda, ammonium chloride, acetate of soda and chloride of gold, and the papers already chlorinated (see [the collodion](collodion.md), [the silver nitrate](silver-nitrate.md), [the hyposulphite of soda](hyposulphite-of-soda.md), [the ammonium chloride](ammonium-chloride.md), [the acetate of soda and gold chloride](acetate-of-soda-and-gold-chloride.md), [the chlorinated papers](chlorinated-papers.md)) (II.18 ¶60). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Cet appareil, muni d’un puissant objectif, était très-complet
    - quote: rien ne manquait
    - quote: Les papiers mêmes étaient là, tout chlorurés
  - Spilett and Harbert use it in the spring of 1867, for the first time, to take views of the island (see [the photographic landscapes](the-photographic-landscapes.md); [the portraits](the-portraits.md)) (II.18 ¶59). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: prit plusieurs vues des parties les plus pittoresques de l’île
    - quote: dont on n’avait pas fait usage jusqu’alors
  - The reporter and his assistant soon become skilful operators, and get rather fine prints of landscapes (II.18 ¶61). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: devinrent donc, en peu de temps, d’habiles opérateurs
  - The plates are fixed with the substances kept in a dark closet of Granite-house, and the lens is placed at a window of the great hall (see [the dark closet](../places/the-dark-closet.md)) (II.20 ¶84). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: objectif avait été placé à l’une des fenêtres de la grande salle
    - quote: dans un réduit obscur de Granite-house
- **Appears in:** II.2, II.18, II.20
