# The channel: the half-mile strait between the islet and the coast

*A half-mile channel with a violent ebb current, forded at low tide at about five feet deep.*

Keywords: canal, chenal, strait, current, jusant, ford, swim, tide, low water

- **What it is.**
  - It is half a mile wide and separates the islet from the coast (I.3 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: séparés par un canal large d’un demi-mille
  - A very rapid current runs through it with a loud noise (I.3 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un courant extrêmement rapide se propageait avec bruit
  - Pencroff takes it for an ebb current, since the tide is falling on the sand (I.3 ¶47). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: c’est un courant de jusant
  - Pencroff judges that a swimmer could be carried out to sea by it (I.3 ¶47). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: entraînés au large par le courant
  - At low water most of the sands forming its bed are uncovered and only a narrow channel remains (I.3 ¶59). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Il ne restait entre l’îlot et la côte qu’un chenal étroit
  - The remaining channel is no deeper than five feet (I.3 ¶60). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dont la profondeur ne dépassait pas cinq pieds
  - The canal that the party followed on 16 April is read as this channel: the islet of Safety lies on its other side (I.14 ¶28). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: De l’autre côté, sur l’îlot du Salut
  - At the lowest ebb it leaves a sort of fordable passage, crossed without wetting above the knee (I.15 ¶15). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: sans se mouiller plus haut que le genou
  - It can be crossed back only when the sea has ebbed again (I.15 ¶29). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Les colons durent attendre que la mer eût rebaissé
  - It is waded at low tide on 5 June, and a canoe is planned to ease crossing (I.20 ¶20). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: profiter de la marée basse pour franchir à gué le canal
    - quote: un canot qui rendrait les communications plus faciles
  - The Bonadventure enters it on 19 April, leaving the islet’s point on her left, and anchors at the mouth of the Mercy (II.20 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: entrait dans le canal qui le séparait de la côte
  - At high tide it cannot be crossed (III.2 ¶82). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: La mer, haute en ce moment, rendait le canal infranchissable
  - It is about half a mile wide, as seen from the brig’s masts (III.3 ¶24). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un canal large d’un demi-mille environ
  - The current of the Mercy has hollowed a channel (chenal) in it, which the pirates sound with a lead line (III.3 ¶26). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le chenal creusé par le courant de la Mercy
  - The flood rises in it with its usual violence, caused by the narrowness of the strait, and carries a boat toward the river (III.3 ¶36). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le flot commençait à monter avec sa violence habituelle, que provoquait l’étroitesse du pertuis
    - quote: les convicts se sentirent entraînés vers la rivière
  - At the place where the brig sinks it is not more than twenty feet deep (III.4 ¶8). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le canal en cet endroit ne mesurait pas plus de vingt pieds de profondeur
  - Its bed is of fine sand like the beach itself, which Pencroff has often crossed at low tide, and he knows of no reef in it (III.4 ¶44). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: formé d’un lit de sable fin, comme la grève elle-même
    - quote: il avait souvent traversé à mer basse
  - Its sands are quicksands, in which the brig’s hull will disappear (III.4 ¶67). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les sables mouvants du canal
  - The water of the channel is heaved up like a waterspout by the explosion of the torpedo (III.5 ¶1). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: l’eau du canal s’était soulevée comme une trombe
