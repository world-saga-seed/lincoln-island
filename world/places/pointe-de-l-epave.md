# La pointe de l’Épave: the granite point where the chest ran aground

*A bare, wild heap of granite jutting two miles beyond the forest about three miles from the Mercy, with a small cove where the chest of 29 October lay; named Flotson-point when the pirogue rounds it; a long beach lies beyond.*

Keywords: pointe, Épave, Flotson-point, point, headland, wreck, chest, cove, anse, granite, beach, plage

- **What it is.**
  - It is the farthest point the pirogue aims for, about three miles from the Mercy because of the coast’s curve (II.2 ¶13). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: environ à trois milles de la Mercy
  - From the river’s mouth to the point the wall grows lower, and the point is a heap of granites, capriciously laid and very wild, quite unlike the curtain wall of the Grande-Vue plateau, as if an enormous cartload of rocks had been tipped there (II.2 ¶14). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: La muraille allait en s’abaissant depuis l’embouchure de la rivière jusqu’à la pointe
    - quote: un énorme tombereau de roches avait été vidé là
  - It has no vegetation; a very sharp salient running two miles in front of the forest, like the arm of a giant coming out of a sleeve of greenery (II.2 ¶14). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Point de végétation sur ce saillant très-aigu
    - quote: le bras d’un géant qui serait sorti d’une manche de verdure
  - Its approaches are strewn with reefs, which the rising tide begins to cover (II.2 ¶14). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les écueils dont ces atterrages étaient semés
  - Near its end lies a small cove, where the pirogue lands and where the chest lay (II.2 ¶25). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une petite anse
  - It is named the pointe de l’Épave (Flotson-point) when the pirogue rounds it with the chest in tow (II.2 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: à laquelle fut donné le nom de pointe de l’Épave (Flotson-point)
  - Past it the colonists find a long beach washed by the open sea, with cap Griffe curved like a yataghan about four miles to the south-east (I.21 ¶20). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Après avoir tourné la pointe de l’Épave, les colons trouvèrent une longue plage que baignait la vaste mer
    - quote: à quatre milles environ vers le sud-est
  - It is the place where the colonists found the precious chest; they come back to it at nightfall of 2 November (II.5 ¶97). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la pointe de l’Épave, à l’endroit même où ils avaient découvert la précieuse caisse
  - From it to Granite-house there are still four miles (II.5 ¶98). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: De la pointe de l’Épave à Granite-house, il restait encore quatre milles
  - The chest had been found less than twenty miles from the part of the south shore at the foot of the Washington Bay (II.5 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: comme l’avait été cette caisse, trouvée à moins de vingt milles de là
  - The dead whale is stranded on the beach of the pointe de l’Épave, at high tide, three miles from Granite-house (II.10 ¶49). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: sur la grève de la pointe de l’Épave, à trois milles de Granite-house et à mer haute
  - The Bonadventure, leaving the Union Bay, doubles the pointe de l’Épave and cap Griffe before holding close to the wind along the southern coast (II.12 ¶96). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Après avoir doublé la pointe de l’Épave et le cap Griffe, Pencroff dut tenir le plus près
  - Its rocks can be rounded to reach the marshes of the tadornes, an alternative to the route from port Ballon across the Mercy bridge (II.20 ¶77). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: soit en tournant les roches de la pointe de l’Épave
  - It ends the re-entering part of the shore that the pirates’ boat rounds at five cables’ distance on 18 October (III.3 ¶32). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la partie rentrante du littoral que terminait la pointe de l’Épave
- **Appears in:** I.21, II.2, II.5, II.10, II.12, II.18, II.20, III.3, III.4
- **Open:**
  - I.21 ¶20 already says "la pointe de l’Épave" in July, though II.2 ¶42 gives the point that name only on 29 October. Card neither (I.21 ¶20; II.2 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Après avoir tourné la pointe de l’Épave
    - quote: à laquelle fut donné le nom de pointe de l’Épave (Flotson-point)
  - The distance to Granite-house: II.2 ¶13 puts the point "about three miles from the Mercy"; II.5 ¶98 says "four miles" remain from the point to Granite-house. Card neither (II.2 ¶13; II.5 ¶98). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: environ à trois milles de la Mercy
    - quote: il restait encore quatre milles
  - The distance to Granite-house: II.10 ¶49 says "trois milles", where II.5 ¶98 says "quatre milles"; card neither (II.10 ¶49; II.5 ¶98). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: à trois milles de Granite-house
    - quote: il restait encore quatre milles

  - The six survivors of the pirates’ second boat are said to have landed "sur la rive droite de la rivière" and fled toward the point (III.3 ¶47), and later to have "pris pied à la pointe de l’Épave" (III.4 ¶13). Card neither (III.3 ¶47; III.4 ¶13). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: parvinrent à prendre pied sur la rive droite de la rivière
    - quote: avaient pris pied à la pointe de l’Épave
