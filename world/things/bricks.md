# The bricks: hand-moulded sun-dried bricks

*Three thousand bricks made in two days by five men, dried, then fired in a stack.*

Keywords: briques, brickmaking, moulding, firing, drying, building material

- **What it is.**
  - They were made by hand and not in moulds (I.13 ¶32). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: l’ingénieur se contenta de les fabriquer à la main
  - They were cut in prisms of equal size (I.13 ¶32). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: fut divisée en prismes d’égale grandeur
  - The five colonists made not more than three thousand in two days' work (I.13 ¶32). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: n’en fabriquèrent pas plus de trois mille
  - A skilled worker can make up to ten thousand in twelve hours without a machine (I.13 ¶32). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: jusqu’à dix mille briques par douze heures
  - They were laid side by side until they were dry enough to fire in three or four days (I.13 ¶32). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dans trois ou quatre jours
  - A plate shows three thousand bricks laid out (I.13 pl1). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Trois mille briques furent rangées
  - Some thousands of bricks lie ready when the fitting-out of Granite-house begins; they are carried and laid at its foot, then hoisted to the door by the pulley (see [the pulley](pulley.md)) (I.19 ¶17, ¶20). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les briques furent apportées et déposées au pied de Granite-house
    - quote: quelques milliers de briques étaient là
  - The kitchen flue of Granite-house is made of brick earth (I.19 ¶43). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le fabriquer en terre de brique
  - The kitchen has a brick stove (I.20 ¶28). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: son fourneau de briques
- **Made from.**
  - Clay is degreased with sand (see [the clay](clay.md)) (I.13 ¶31). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dégraisser cette figuline avec du sable
  - The clay, soaked in water, was worked with feet and wrists (I.13 ¶32). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: L’argile, imbibée d’eau, corroyée ensuite avec les pieds et les poignets des manipulateurs
  - They are moulded and fired by the heat of a wood fire (I.13 ¶31). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: de mouler les briques et de les cuire à la chaleur d’un feu de bois
  - They were fired in [the brick kiln](brick-kiln.md), on 6 April (I.13 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: à l’endroit où allait s’opérer la cuisson des briques
- **Leads to.**
  - Smith said the kiln would be made of bricks (see [the pottery kiln](pottery-kiln.md)) (I.13 ¶14). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Avec des briques
  - On 9 April the engineer had some thousands of bricks and some lime (I.13 ¶45). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: quelques milliers de briques
- **On the page.**
  - Smith set the workshop at the place of production, to avoid transport (I.13 ¶16). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Pour éviter les transports
- **Appears in:** I.13, I.19, I.20
- **Open:**
  - Whether all of the three thousand were fired in the stack is not stated; ¶45 says only some thousands.
    - quote: quelques milliers de briques
