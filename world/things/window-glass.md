# The window glass: panes Smith means to make for the facade

*Window glass not yet made: the openings of the facade stand closed by thick shutters until the engineer has time to make it.*

Keywords: verre à vitre, glass, panes, windows, châssis, frames, fenêtres

- **What it is.**
  - Until the window frames are made, Smith means to close the openings with thick shutters (I.19 ¶13). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: en attendant que les châssis des fenêtres fussent faits
  - The shutters serve until the engineer has the time to make window glass (I.19 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: en attendant que l’ingénieur eût eu le temps de fabriquer du verre à vitre
- **Appears in:** I.19
