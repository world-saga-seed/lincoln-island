# The sandstone: a gritty rock found in quantity on the shore

*Sandstone in two grains, used to sharpen the steel strip.*

Keywords: grès, grindstone, whetstone, arenaceous rock, abrasive, rock

- **What it is.**
  - This kind of arenaceous rock is found abundantly on the shore (I.13 ¶27). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ce genre de roche arénacée se rencontrait abondamment sur la grève
  - A coarser stone sets the edge, a finer one removes the burr (I.13 ¶27). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: enlever le morfil sur un grès plus fin
  - Some large sandstones in the north of the lake can easily be made into millstones (II.16 ¶84). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Quelques grands grès qui se trouvaient dans le nord du lac pouvaient facilement se transformer en meules
- **On the page.**
  - It is used to sharpen the broken collar of Top (I.13 ¶27). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: l’affûter d’abord sur une pierre de grès
- **Appears in:** I.13, II.16
