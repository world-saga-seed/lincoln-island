# POV file: Lincoln Island, 01900 to 02000

*How the three chapters are told. The drafter and every critic read it, and where it and the voice sheet differ, this file wins. Every line rests on a ruling.*

- **Person and tense:** Verne's narrator, third person, past tense. *(Settled R1121-90.)*
- **Time stance:** told from after, as Verne tells it. In 01900 and 02000 the narrator looks ahead exactly where the French does and nowhere else. In 01950 he never looks ahead. *(Settled R1121-91.)*
- **Whose head:** in 01900 and 02000, as the French goes, among the five. In 01950 he stays with Pencroff and Herbert. Smith, Spilett and Neb are seen only at home. *(Settled R1121-28, R1121-69, R1121-90.)*
- **Distance:** Verne's: the narrator reports and explains, and gives a thought briefly ("pensa Harbert", II.1 ¶79). He never stays long inside a head. Pencroff's sum in 01950 is counted in his head and never said aloud. *(Settled R1121-82, R1121-90.)*
- **May know:** what the colonists know and suspect in June 1865 is in [what is not true here](../../world/false-assumptions.md). Pencroff and Herbert know nothing of Smith and Spilett's mystery, and no one thinks of a helper. *(Settled R1121-98, R1121-100.)*
- **Never tells, in 01950:** whose the float is; the word or motto the letters belong to, or any N; that it was set close by; any hand behind its loss; that the two keep silent afterward from shame. The reader's questions and their limits are in [the key](../../plan/reader-tests/key.md), K1 to K9 and K15, which drafters do not read. *(Settled R1121-28, R1121-61, R1121-79, R1121-83.)*
- **Witness reach:** in 01950 the float's loss is seen from under the granite wall: a flash on the islet's rocks in the spray. No one sees it break up close, and no one finds a piece. *(Settled R1121-68, R1121-70.)*
- **Register:** [the voice sheet](white.md) and [the drafting rules](../../plan/workflows/drafting/rulings.md). *(Settled R1121-16, R1121-95.)*
- **Samples:** [White I.5](samples/white-I.5.md). *(Settled R1121-16.)*
- **Measures:** White's Part I numbers, in the drafting rules' Texture section. *(Settled R1121-94.)*
