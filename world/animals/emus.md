# Emus: the pair of tall brown Australian runners

*A couple of great brown birds, called casoars and émeus, seen but not approached in the Far-West forest, which outrun Top with ease.*

Keywords: émeu, émeus, casoar, casoars, cassowary, emu, échassier, Australie, Far-West, autruche, runner

- **What it is.**
  - They are "ces grands oiseaux qui sont particuliers à l’Australie", sorts of casoars, called émeus (III.11 ¶54). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ces grands oiseaux qui sont particuliers à l’Australie
    - quote: sortes de casoars, que l’on nomme émeus
  - They stand five feet high, have brown plumage, and are of the order of waders ("échassiers") (III.11 ¶54). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: hauts de cinq pieds et bruns de plumage
    - quote: appartiennent à l’ordre des échassiers
  - The colonists first glimpse a pair of them on 16 February, on the second day of the expedition through the Far-West forest, and cannot get near; they are the only fauna of that day not already known to the hunters (III.11 ¶51, ¶54). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le lendemain, 16 février
    - quote: elle ne présenta pas d’autres échantillons que ceux dont les chasseurs avaient eu connaissance jusqu’alors
    - quote: sans pouvoir l’approcher
  - They are extremely swift: Top dashes after them at full speed and they leave him easily behind (III.11 ¶54). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les casoars le distancèrent aisément
    - quote: tant leur rapidité était prodigieuse
- **Appears in:** III.11
