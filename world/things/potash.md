# The potash: the alkali burnt from plants

*Potash got with soda by burning plants, used to degrease the balloon cloth.*

Keywords: potasse, potash, alkali, soda, incinération, ashes

- **What it is.**
  - Soda and potash got by burning plants are used to degrease the balloon’s canvas, so that the cotton, freed of its varnish, regains its natural suppleness and elasticity (see [the balloon’s envelope](balloon-envelope.md)) (II.8 ¶3). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dégraissées au moyen de soude et de potasse obtenues par incinération de plantes
  - A solution of potash, obtained beforehand by burning various plants, is poured into the tubes of the battery, where it reacts on the nitric acid through the clay (see [the telegraph battery](the-telegraph-battery.md)) (II.18 ¶43). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une dissolution de potasse qu’il avait préalablement obtenue par l’incinération de diverses plantes
- **Appears in:** II.8, II.18
