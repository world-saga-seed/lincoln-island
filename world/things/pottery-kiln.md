# The pottery kiln: a brick kiln with a twenty-foot chimney

*A kiln of bricks begun about 9 April, charged with coal five days later; it fired the pottery and went out on 15 April.*

Keywords: four à poteries, furnace, chimney, stoneware, firing, oven, factory

- **What it is.**
  - It has a chimney about twenty feet high (I.13 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une cheminée haute d’une vingtaine de pieds
  - It was built to fire the various pottery needed for domestic use (I.13 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un four, qui devait servir à la cuisson des diverses poteries indispensables pour les usages domestiques
  - It was built without too much difficulty (I.13 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: On y réussit sans trop de difficulté
  - In the August storm the wind badly damaged its chimney, beheading it by six good feet at least (I.22 ¶37). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la cheminée du four à poteries avait été très-endommagée par le vent et découronnée de six bons pieds au moins
- **Made from.**
  - Smith said the kiln would be of bricks (see [the bricks](bricks.md)) (I.13 ¶14). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Avec des briques
  - The fuel is coal (see [the coal](coal.md)) (I.13 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Cinq jours après, le four fut chargé de cette houille
- **Leads to.**
  - Its purpose is to make the pottery needed (see [the pottery](pottery.md)) (I.13 ¶12). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: À fabriquer la poterie dont nous avons besoin
  - The pot-au-feu had to wait for the pot, and so for the kiln (I.13 ¶37). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il fallait attendre que le pot fût fabriqué, et, par conséquent, que le four fût bâti
  - Smith could later turn the potters into blacksmiths (I.13 ¶50). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Quand il conviendrait à Cyrus Smith de les changer en forgerons, ils seraient forgerons
- **On the page.**
  - The kiln was built after 9 April, when the lime and some thousands of bricks were ready (I.13 ¶45). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le 9 avril, l’ingénieur avait à sa disposition
  - Pencroff almost believed that all the products of modern industry would come out of it (I.13 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: tous les produits de l’industrie moderne
  - The kiln went out on 15 April, awaiting a new purpose (I.13 ¶51). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le four s’éteignit en attendant une destination nouvelle
- **Appears in:** I.13, I.22
- **Open:**
  - The kiln's size apart from its chimney height, and its shape, are not given.
