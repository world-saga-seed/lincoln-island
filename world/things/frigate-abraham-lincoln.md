# The frigate Abraham-Lincoln: the United States frigate that hunted the Nautilus

*The American frigate whose chase of the Nautilus ended in the shock that threw the three men into the sea.*

Keywords: frégate, frigate, Abraham-Lincoln, Farragut, États-Unis, United States Navy, chase, collision, harpoon, narwhal

- **What it is.**
  - It is a frigate of the United States, which gave the Nautilus chase (III.16 ¶55). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la frégate des États-Unis l’Abraham-Lincoln, qui lui donnait la chasse
    - In the earlier book Ned Land is a harpooner aboard the frigate Abraham-Lincoln of the national navy of the United States (VML I.10 ¶5). *(Drafted.)*
      - quote: harponneur à bord de la frégate l’Abraham-Lincoln, de la marine nationale des États-Unis d’Amérique
    - Its commander, Farragut, fits out his ship with every kind of apparatus for catching the gigantic cetacean (VML I.4 ¶5). *(Drafted.)*
      - quote: le commandant Farragut avait soigneusement pourvu son navire d’appareils propres à pêcher le gigantesque cétacé
    - It sails from the pier at Brooklyn, with its coal bunkers full, to hunt the animal (VML I.2 ¶31, ¶32). *(Drafted.)*
      - quote: Ses soutes regorgeaient de charbon
      - quote: Trois heures avant que l’Abraham-Lincoln ne quittât la pier de Brooklyn
  - A shock took place between it and the Nautilus, which threw three men into the sea (III.16 ¶55). See [the three men](../people/the-french-professor-his-servant-and-a-canadian-fisherman.md). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dans un choc qui s’était produit entre le Nautilus et la frégate
    - In the earlier book Ned Land's harpoon strikes the "animal", two great water-spouts fall on the frigate's deck, and a frightful shock throws the professor into the sea (VML I.6 ¶102, ¶103, ¶104). *(Drafted.)*
      - quote: le harpon fut lancé
      - quote: deux énormes trombes d’eau s’abattirent sur le pont de la frégate
      - quote: Un choc effroyable se produisit
    - Its screw and rudder are broken, the only damage it suffers, and it steers no more (VML I.7 ¶20, ¶22). *(Drafted.)*
      - quote: L’hélice et le gouvernail sont brisés
      - quote: Mais, circonstance fâcheuse pour nous, il ne gouverne plus.
    - Nemo says he was attacked and had to defend himself, and merely put the frigate out of condition to harm him, adding that she will not be troubled to repair her damage at the nearest port (VML I.13 ¶37). *(Drafted.)*
      - quote: on m’attaquait et j’ai dû me défendre !
      - quote: mettre la frégate hors d’état de me nuire
- **Appears in:** III.16, VML I.2, VML I.4, VML I.6, VML I.7, VML I.10, VML I.13
