# Capybaras: the big water rodents of the forest pool

*Pig-sized, web-footed rodents of the forest ponds, so little afraid of men that the first one the colonists meet barely flees.*

Keywords: cabiai, capybara, rongeur, rodent, amphibie, pond, mare, ham, game, porc

- **What it is.**
  - Harbert names the animal a cabiai, one of the largest of the rodents (I.9 ¶104). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Harbert crut reconnaître en cet animal un cabiai, c’est-à-dire un des plus grands échantillons de l’ordre des rongeurs
  - It is a kind of pig about two and a half feet long, blackish brown and lighter on the belly, with hard, thin hair, and toes that seem joined by membranes (I.9 ¶103). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une espèce de porc long de deux pieds et demi environ
    - quote: d’un brun noirâtre mais moins foncé au ventre, ayant un poil dur et peu épais
    - quote: semblaient réunis par des membranes
  - Its big eyes are sunk deep in a thick layer of fat (I.9 ¶105). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ses gros yeux profondément engagés dans une épaisse couche de graisse
  - Harbert says it has webbed feet and is almost an amphibian, and that it must come up to breathe (I.9 ¶110, ¶112). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: puisqu’il a les pieds palmés, et c’est presque un amphibie
    - quote: il viendra bientôt respirer à la surface
  - It surfaces after a few minutes (I.9 ¶114). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Après quelques minutes, l’animal remonta au-dessus des eaux
  - Cabiais fall among the game that Harbert and Gédéon Spilett bring down with arrows (I.22 ¶57). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: agoutis, kangourous, cabiais, pigeons, outardes, canards sauvages, bécassines
  - Capybaras ("cabiais") are among the game of the first forest slopes near Granite-house (II.1 ¶36). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: d’agoutis, de cabiais, de kangourous
  - Cabiais swarm in the woods at the start of 1866, with other game of fur and feather (II.8 ¶6). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Agoutis, pécaris, cabiais, kangourous, gibiers de poil et de plume fourmillaient véritablement
  - Capybaras pass within good range of Harbert's gun in the Far-West forest, on the left of the Mercy, and escape it (II.9 ¶44). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: bien des kangourous, des cabiais ou des agoutis passèrent à bonne portée
    - quote: échappèrent pourtant au fusil du jeune garçon
  - Cabiais are among the big game that never fail at the house when Harbert and Spilett hunt on the corral road (III.14 ¶20). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: cabiais, agoutis, kangourous, sangliers, porcs sauvages pour le gros gibier
    - quote: ne manquaient jamais à la maison
  - When the Far-West forest burns, the game, "jaguars, sangliers, cabiais, koulas" among it, flees mad with fear toward the Mercy and the marsh of the tadornes; all the island's animals die in its end (III.19 ¶60; III.20 ¶3). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: jaguars, sangliers, cabiais, koulas, gibier de poil et de plume
    - quote: Les animaux avaient également péri dans la catastrophe
- **Appears in:** I.9, I.22, II.1, II.8, II.9, III.11, III.14, III.19, III.20
