# The convicts’ cave: the dark cave at the foot of Mont Franklin where the pirates lodge and Ayrton is held

*A cave in the valley south of the volcano, at the head of the Falls-river, where the convicts take refuge and keep Ayrton bound and gagged for four months.*

Keywords: caverne, grotte, convicts, pirates, Ayrton, séquestré, vallée du sud, repaire, refuge, hideout, prison

- **What it is.**
  - It is a dark cave at the foot of Mont Franklin, where the convicts took refuge (III.13 ¶6). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une caverne obscure, au pied du mont Franklin, là où les convicts s’étaient réfugiés
  - Ayrton lives in it, tied, gagged and watched, for four months (III.13 ¶10). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: vécut dans cette caverne pendant quatre mois
  - The pirates do not leave it while the colonists are held at the corral by Harbert’s illness (III.13 ¶13, ¶15). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les pirates ne quittèrent pas leur caverne
    - quote: quittèrent rarement leur retraite
  - It lies in the valley that opens to the south of the volcano and gathers the first waters of the Falls-river (see [the Falls-river](riviere-de-la-chute.md)) (III.13 ¶66). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: toute la vallée qui s’ouvrait au sud du volcan et qui recueillait les premières eaux de la rivière de la Chute
  - The valley that leads to the cave is shaded by fine trees, the conifers dominating (III.13 ¶67). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: vallée ombragée de beaux arbres, parmi lesquels dominaient les conifères
  - Found on 19 February or after, the cave is exactly as Ayrton left it, with a quantity of munitions and food that the convicts had carried off to form a reserve (III.13 ¶66). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: absolument dans l’état où Ayrton l’avait laissée
    - quote: une certaine quantité de munitions et de vivres que les convicts avaient enlevés avec l’intention de se créer une réserve
- **Appears in:** III.13
