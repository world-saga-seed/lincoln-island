# The iron tools: the pincers, picks, axes and other tools forged in the metallurgical period

*Tools coarsely shaped from the colonists’ iron and steel between 20 April and 5 May; picks serve to cut granite.*

Keywords: outils, tools, pics, pioches, haches, hachettes, marteaux, clous, tenailles, pinces, rabot, plane, forge, smiths, pick

- **What it is.**
  - To build even a canoe Pencroff says they must first make hammers, axes, adzes, saws, augers and planes, having no tools (I.15 ¶6). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il fallait commencer par fabriquer marteaux, haches, herminettes, scies, tarières, rabots
  - By 25 April several bars were forged and turned into tools: pincers, tongs, picks, pickaxes, which Pencroff and Nab declared real jewels (I.15 ¶47). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: pinces, tenailles, pics, pioches
    - quote: de vrais bijoux
  - Axe heads were made of the steel, and other tools were shaped coarsely: plane blades, axes, hatchets, steel bands to become saws and carpenters’ chisels, then pick heads, shovels, hammers and nails (I.15 ¶51). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: façonnés grossièrement, il va sans dire
    - quote: lames de rabot, haches, hachettes
    - quote: des marteaux, des clous
  - The smiths come back to the Chimneys on 5 May, and new works will soon let them take a new title (I.15 ¶52). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: de nouveaux travaux allaient les autoriser bientôt à prendre une qualification nouvelle
  - By 6 May Pencroff says that neither bricks nor tools are lacking now (I.16 ¶13). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ni les briques, ni les outils ne nous manquent maintenant
  - Pencroff handles a pick adroitly and vigorously, and with Nab’s relief drills the blast hole in granite by four in the afternoon (I.17 ¶67, ¶68). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un pic qu’il maniait adroitement et vigoureusement
    - quote: le trou de mine était achevé
  - With a pick, working in turns, Pencroff, Nab and Spilett pierce a granite wall three feet thick in two hours (I.18 ¶63, ¶65). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ce travail durait depuis deux heures déjà
    - quote: trois pieds d’épaisseur
  - A pick and a mattock finish the pointed frames of the facade openings of Granite-house (I.19 ¶14). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le pic et la pioche achevèrent le dessin ogival
  - In June the tools, which were very rudimentary, are improved and completed (I.20 ¶26). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: On perfectionna les outils, qui étaient fort rudimentaires
- **Appears in:** I.15, I.16, I.17, I.18, I.19, I.20
