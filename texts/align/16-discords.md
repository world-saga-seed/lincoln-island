# I.16: White against the French (discords)

*Labeller f. Beads corrected: none.*

### 1. cut: the island's probable parallels (I.16 ¶1; White ¶1)
- The French gives the island's likely place as between the thirty-fifth and fortieth parallels, from which its climate is argued; White gives "its probable situation in the Southern Hemisphere". *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: située très-vraisemblablement entre le trente-cinquième et le quarantième parallèle
  - white: from its probable situation in the Southern Hemisphere

### 2. changed: falls in temperature become "sudden changes" (I.16 ¶1; White ¶1)
- The French looks for certain lowerings of temperature in the depth of winter; White says "sudden changes in the temperature". *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: certains abaissements de température contre lesquels il convenait de se prémunir
  - white: this island would probably experience sudden changes in the temperature against which it would be well to provide

### 3. changed: enemies within and without (I.16 ¶14; White ¶14)
- Cyrus says a ready-made dwelling would be defended against enemies of the inside as of the outside; White gives "native as well as foreign". *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: aussi bien défendue contre les ennemis du dedans que contre ceux du dehors
  - white: in which we would be safe from enemies, native as well as foreign?

### 4. changed: thick grass becomes thin (I.16 ¶23; White ¶22)
- The French has a fairly thick grass carpeting the slope; White has "a thin carpet of grass". *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: une herbe assez épaisse la tapissait
  - white: there was a thin carpet of grass

### 5. cut: the cascade (I.16 ¶24; White ¶23)
- Cyrus thinks the lake spills as a cascade on that side; White has the overflow "fell in this direction" and loses the cascade. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: que le trop-plein du lac s’épanchait sous forme de cascade
  - white: that the over flow of the lake fell in this direction

### 6. changed: northern and eastern shores become eastern and western (I.16 ¶25; White ¶24)
- The route proposed explores the northern and eastern shores of the lake; White says eastern and western. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: en explorant les rives septentrionales et orientales du lac
  - white: exploring the eastern and western shores of the lake

### 7. condensed: the coot (I.16 ¶33; White ¶31)
- The French describes the bird (short bill, big frontal plate, lobed toes, white-edged wings) and calls the group macrodactyls; White has "a beautiful slate-colored water fowl" and the group as "machio-dactyls". *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: à bec court, à plaque frontale très-développée, aux doigts élargis par une bordure festonnée
  - white: a beautiful slate-colored water fowl

### 8. changed: east bank becomes south bank (I.16 ¶34; White ¶32)
- The colonists follow the eastern bank of the lake; White says the southern bank. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Les colons suivaient alors la rive orientale du lac
  - white: following the southern bank of the lake

### 9. cut: the water is not deep there (I.16 ¶54; White ¶50)
- The French says the water did not seem deep at that spot; White drops it. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: qui ne paraissaient pas être profondes en cet endroit
  - white: when suddenly an enormous head emerged from the water

### 10. cut: the conical head (I.16 ¶55; White ¶51)
- The French describes a conical head with large eyes and silky whiskers; White has "the comical face". *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: cette tête conique à gros yeux
  - white: the comical face, with huge eyes and long silky moustaches

### 11. cut: why it is not a manatee (I.16 ¶57; White ¶53)
- The French gives the reason for the dugong: the nostrils open on the top of the muzzle, and it places the species among the cetaceans; White gives neither. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: car ses narines étaient ouvertes à la partie supérieure de son museau
  - white: Although not a manatee, it was a dugong, which belongs to the same species.

### 12. changed: "the same species" (I.16 ¶57; White ¶53)
- The French names the species as a specimen of the order of cetaceans called "dugong"; White says the dugong belongs to "the same species" as the manatee. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: un spécimen de cette espèce, comprise dans l’ordre des cétacés
  - white: which belongs to the same species

### 13. cut: Cyrus holds Nab back in speech (I.16 ¶60; White ¶55)
- The French gives Cyrus's words ("Non, Nab") as he restrains him; White reports the holding back in a clause. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: « Non, Nab, » dit l’ingénieur, en retenant son courageux serviteur.
  - white: had he not been held back by his master
