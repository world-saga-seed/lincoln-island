# The lens: a lens made of two watch glasses filled with water

*Smith's burning lens: the glasses of two watches filled with water and sealed with clay, which set dry moss alight with the sun.*

Keywords: lentille, burning glass, watch glasses, verres de montre, sun fire, fire without matches

- **What it is.**
  - It is a true lens that concentrates the sun's rays on very dry moss and sets it burning (I.10 ¶8). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une véritable lentille, qui, concentrant les rayons solaires sur une mousse bien sèche, en avait déterminé la combustion
- **Made from.**
  - He made it himself: Harbert asked whether he had a lens, and he answered that he had made one (I.10 ¶7). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: mais j’en ai fait une
  - Smith made it from the two glasses he took off the reporter's watch and his own (I.10 ¶8). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: C’étaient tout simplement les deux verres qu’il avait enlevés à la montre du reporter et à la sienne
  - He filled them with water and made their edges adhere with a little clay (I.10 ¶8). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Après les avoir remplis d’eau et rendu leurs bords adhérents au moyen d’un peu de glaise
- **Leads to.**
  - It lit the fire at the Chimneys while the hunters were away, "the sun" being the answer to Pencroff's "who lit it?" (I.10 ¶5). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: C’était le soleil qui avait fourni cette chaleur dont s’émerveillait Pencroff
  - The glasses were put back in the two watches before the climb (I.10 ¶19). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les verres avaient été remis aux montres de l’ingénieur et du reporter
- **Appears in:** I.10
