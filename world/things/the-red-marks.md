# The red marks: the small red points on the five dead convicts, the only sign of the blow

*A tiny, barely visible red contusion on forehead, chest, back or shoulder of each of the five convicts found dead at the corral, with no wound.*

Keywords: marque rouge, point rouge, contusion, blessure, wound, foudroyé, arme foudroyante, convicts, dead, killed, balles électriques

- **What it is.**
  - The five bodies lie where a death that must have been lightning-swift surprised them, already stiff with cold (III.13 ¶22, ¶24). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une mort qui avait dû être foudroyante
    - quote: raidis par le froid
  - They bear no apparent trace of a wound (III.13 ¶25). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ils ne portaient aucune trace apparente de blessure
  - Pencroff finds on the forehead of one, the chest of another, the back of a third and the shoulder of a fourth a small red point, a sort of barely visible contusion whose origin cannot be recognised (III.13 ¶26). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un petit point rouge, sorte de contusion à peine visible, et dont il était impossible de reconnaître l’origine
  - Smith takes the points for the places where they were struck, by a thunderbolt weapon whose secret the colonists do not hold (III.13 ¶27, ¶29). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: C’est là qu’ils ont été frappés
    - quote: Une arme foudroyante dont nous n’avons pas le secret
  - The weapon is carded as [the electric bullets](electric-bullets.md), which III.16 says Nemo used on the convicts (III.13 ¶29; III.16 ¶65). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: Une arme foudroyante dont nous n’avons pas le secret
    - quote: frappa les convicts de ces balles électriques
- **Appears in:** III.13
