# Ayrton's story of the Duncan (notes)

## Notes for drafting

- **Where it sits.** The French II.17, `texts/fr-1875/39.md`; it follows 03800 (the refused hand, 3 December) and runs from 3 December to 20 December and the night of the confession. The tale (¶42–¶84) is Ayrton's, a recap of *Les Enfants du capitaine Grant* in his voice. *(Drafted.)*
- **Cast.** *(Drafted.)*
  - Cyrus Smith, the unknown (¶7), Pencroff (¶5), Spilett and Harbert (¶3), Nab (¶3).
  - In the tale: Lord Glenarvan, his wife, a major, a French geographer, Mary and Robert Grant (the children of Captain Grant), Captain John Mangles, the second Tom Austin, an Irish farmer (¶42–¶57).
  - Ayrton, also Ben Joyce, boatswain ("contre-maître") of the Britannia (¶55–¶60); Captain Grant and two of his men (¶44, ¶76).
  - The unknown is named Ayrton from ¶59 on, in the tale, and by Cyrus from ¶86.
- **Places.** *(Drafted.)*
  - Granite-house's grand hall (¶31); the corral and its new wooden house, about twenty feet from the stables, with over eighty mouflons (¶28); the plateau (¶29).
  - In the tale: Cape Bernouilli on Australia's west coast at the thirty-seventh parallel (¶42); Patagonia, Cape Corrientes, the Strait of Magellan, Tristan da Cunha, Amsterdam (¶46–¶49); Melbourne (¶63, ¶66); Twofold Bay (¶63); New Zealand's east and west coasts (¶66, ¶72); Tabor island (¶76).
- **Things.** *(Drafted.)*
  - The Duncan, a steam yacht (¶42); the bottle and the document in three languages, with latitude 37° 11′ south (¶44–¶45); the letter for Tom Austin (¶63–¶66); the caronades (¶68).
  - The corral house's furniture (¶28); the Tabor house with arms, munitions, tools, seeds (¶80).
  - Footnote n1 on the dates (¶79).
- **Time.** *(Drafted unless marked.)*
  - 10 December, "une semaine après son retour" (¶10). The corral house is ready within a week (¶28); the installations finished on 20 December, evening at eight (¶30–¶31).
  - The tale: 8 April 1852 (Ayrton landed, ¶59); the Britannia lost "une année auparavant" (¶42); 13 November (¶48); 20 December 1854 (¶42, ¶49); 23 December 1854 (¶58); 3 March (¶68) and 3 March 1855 (¶73); "le 18 courant" (¶70); five weeks' wait (¶72); 18 March 1855 (¶79); "deux ans, trois ans" and "deux ou quatre ans" (¶82–¶83).
  - The chapter covers eighteen days of the colonists' time and three years of tale. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- **Clues.** *(Drafted.)*
  - Plate pl1 names "Ayrton." in the text at ¶1, before the tale.
  - Ayrton says he never threw a document into the sea (¶98). The author of the Tabor bottle is therefore unexplained (the notes of 03800 carry the earlier discussion).
  - Glenarvan's promise: "Je sais où vous êtes, Ayrton, et je sais où vous trouver" (¶77).
  - Hetzel's note on the discord in the dates and on the reason the true dates could not be given at first (n1).
  - The tale says Grant's house was built by Grant (¶80).
- **Length.** 101 paragraphs (¶1–¶101); 3,497 words in the ¶ lines (¶0 excluded). 1 footnote, 3 plate captions (pl1 to pl3). *(Drafted.)*

## Open and flags

- **Years on Tabor.** II.16 ¶40 gives twelve years; ¶79 here gives the landing on 18 March 1855:
  - quote: On était au 18 mars 1855[n1]
  and the colonists' year is 1866 (II.16 ¶39). Card neither. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- **Hetzel's note.** n1 says the dates are discordant:
  - quote: une certaine discordance dans les dates
  - at: II.17 n1
  The text's own dates: the Duncan reached Cape Bernouilli on 20 December 1854 (¶42), the Britannia was lost "une année auparavant" (¶42) and "depuis moins de deux ans" (¶50), Ayrton had served "plus d’un an" (¶57), and Ayrton was landed on 8 April 1852 (¶59). Card neither.
- **Day of the Duncan's departure.** The telegraph says:
  - quote: Duncan parti depuis le 18 courant
  The month is not given (¶70). *(Drafted.)*
- **Ayrton's nationality.** ¶54 "Un Écossais comme vous"; II.16 ¶33 "Anglais". Card neither. *(Drafted.)*
- **Who built the house.** ¶80 "la maison construite par l’honnête capitaine Grant"; II.14 ¶22 has a single survivor building it from wreck planks. Card neither. *(Drafted.)*
- **Dates of the corral house.** ¶28 says the house was ready before the week was out; ¶30 says installations were finished on 20 December. The 10 December request plus a week gives 17 December. Card neither. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- **Three or four years.** ¶82 "Pendant deux ans, trois ans"; ¶83 "après deux ou quatre ans d’abandon". *(Drafted.)*
- **The Tabor document.** Ayrton denies it (¶98); Spilett's and Pencroff's reasoning in II.16 ¶51–¶63 and Cyrus's in II.15 ¶26 rest on one castaway writing it. The Britannia document in ¶44 is another document. Card neither. *(Drafted.)*
- **The French geographer's error.** ¶66: New Zealand's east coast named in the letter by error. Not a flag, a plot fact. *(Drafted.)*
- **Wikisource corrections.** None touches this chapter. *(Drafted.)*
