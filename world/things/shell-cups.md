# The shell cups: bivalve shells used as vessels

*Large bivalve shells fetched from the shore and used to hold and carry liquids, since the castaways have no vessel.*

Keywords: coquilles, bivalves, shell, cup, vessel, bowl, water, mixture

- **What it is.**
  - Harbert came back from the shore with two large bivalve shells, in which Pencroff made up a mixture of water and tétras juice for Smith (I.8 ¶39). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: en revint avec deux grandes coquilles de bivalves
    - quote: Le marin composa une sorte de mixture
  - Smith washed down his meal with a little fresh water drawn from the river in a vast shell (I.9 ¶52). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: puisée à la rivière dans une vaste coquille
- **Appears in:** I.8, I.9
