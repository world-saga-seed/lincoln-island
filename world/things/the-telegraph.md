# The telegraph: the electric line from Granite-house to the corral

*An electric telegraph of iron wire, glass-insulated posts, two zinc batteries and two dial instruments, made by Smith in January and February 1867 to join the corral to Granite-house across five miles.*

Keywords: télégraphe électrique, fil, poteaux, pile, cadran, électro-aimant, corral, Ayrton, communication, dial, wire

- **What it is.**
  - Smith resolves to put the corral into instant communication with Granite-house, so as to be warned of a ship passing offshore, a wreck on the western landfalls, the coming of pirates, or any incident touching the mystery of the island (II.18 ¶26, ¶27, ¶28). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: mettre le corral en communication instantanée avec Granite-house
    - quote: l’arrivée possible de pirates
  - He tells his companions of the plan on 10 January: it is an electric telegraph (II.18 ¶29, ¶31, ¶32, ¶33). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ce fut le 10 janvier qu’il fit part de son projet à ses compagnons
    - quote: Électrique, répondit Cyrus Smith
  - He means to make the battery from what they have, and to draw iron wire through a draw-plate (see [the battery](the-telegraph-battery.md); [the draw-plate](the-draw-plate.md)) (II.18 ¶33). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Nous avons tous les éléments nécessaires pour confectionner une pile
    - quote: au moyen d’une filière
  - The wire is made first, since if it failed the battery and other accessories would be useless (see [the telegraph wire](telegraph-wire.md)) (II.18 ¶35). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: en commençant par le plus difficile, c’est-à-dire par la confection des fils
  - The wire, in lengths of forty to fifty feet easy to join and stretch, has to cover the five miles that separate the corral from the enclosure of Granite-house (II.18 ¶38). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des fils longs de quarante à cinquante pieds
    - quote: cette distance de cinq milles qui séparait le corral de l’enceinte de Granite-house
  - The posts, fitted with glass insulators to carry the wire, begin to be planted on 6 February along the corral road, and a few days later the wire is stretched (see [the telegraph posts](telegraph-posts.md); [the glass insulators](glass-insulators.md)) (II.18 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ce fut le 6 février que fut commencée la plantation des poteaux, munis d’isoloirs en verre
    - quote: le fil était tendu
  - The current, the text says, travels at a hundred thousand kilometres a second, and the earth carries it back to its starting point (II.18 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une vitesse de cent mille kilomètres par seconde
    - quote: que la terre se chargerait de ramener à son point de départ
  - Two batteries are made, one for Granite-house and one for the corral, so that each station can speak to the other (II.18 ¶47). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Deux piles avaient été fabriquées, l’une pour Granite-house, l’autre pour le corral
  - The receiver and the manipulator are very simple: at both stations the wire winds round an electromagnet of soft iron, which magnetises while the current passes and demagnetises when it is broken; a soft-iron plate before it is drawn and released, and to this movement Smith attaches a needle on a dial carrying the letters of the alphabet (see [the telegraph instruments](telegraph-instruments.md)) (II.18 ¶48). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le fil s’enroulait sur un électro-aimant
    - quote: une aiguille disposée sur un cadran, qui portait en exergue les lettres de l’alphabet
  - The whole is completely installed on 12 February, and Smith’s first call to the corral is answered satisfactorily by Ayrton (II.18 ¶49). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Le tout fut complètement installé le 12 février
    - quote: reçut, quelques instants après, une réponse satisfaisante d’Ayrton
  - Its two advantages are that it lets the colonists verify Ayrton’s presence at the corral and that it does not leave him in complete isolation (II.18 ¶51). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: permettait de constater la présence d’Ayrton au corral
    - quote: ne le laissait pas dans un complet isolement
  - It puts the corral and Granite-house in communication, and a call sent from the great hall is answered by Ayrton from the corral (III.1 ¶17, ¶20). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: l’appareil télégraphique qui mettait en communication le corral et Granite-house
    - quote: Je viens
  - If anything happens at the corral, Ayrton is to warn the colonists at once by a telegram addressed to Granite-house (III.6 ¶6). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il en préviendrait immédiatement les colons par un télégramme à l’adresse de Granite-house
  - Ayrton acknowledges every telegram as a rule (III.6 ¶54). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ayrton n’accusa pas réception de la dépêche, ainsi qu’il avait l’habitude de le faire
  - On 11 November the line is found cut at pole no. 74, the pole pulled down by a man’s hand and the wire freshly and violently broken, so that no telegram has passed either way (see [the posts](telegraph-posts.md), [the wire](telegraph-wire.md)) (III.6 ¶78–¶83). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il a été déraciné de main d’homme
    - quote: il était évident que les dépêches de Granite-house n’avaient pu être reçues au corral
  - The wire between the corral and Granite-house is re-established and works when a colonist at the corral finds it necessary to spend the night there (III.14 ¶21). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le fil télégraphique jeté entre le corral et Granite-house avait été rétabli, et qu’il fonctionnait
