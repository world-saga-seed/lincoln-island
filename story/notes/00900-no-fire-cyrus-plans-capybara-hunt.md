# No Fire, Cyrus's Plan, the Capybara Hunt (notes)

## Notes for drafting

- **Where it sits.** The French I.9. It runs from the evening of the day Cyrus is carried back to the Chimneys, through the night and the morning of 28 March, to the afternoon hunt that ends with smoke seen over the rocks. *(Drafted.)*
- **Cast.** *(Drafted.)* Pencroff (¶1), Gédéon Spilett, "le reporter" (¶1), Harbert (¶1), Nab (¶1), Cyrus Smith (¶14, "l’ingénieur" at ¶1 context in ¶15), Top (¶29).
- **Places.** *(Drafted.)*
  - The Chimneys: the central passage where Cyrus sleeps (¶16), the passages with the partitions destroyed (¶17), the sand couch where the others lie (¶29), the granite wall (¶48).
  - The beach and the high rocks where lithodomes and algae are gathered (¶18); the river where Cyrus drinks (¶52).
  - The river bend, "le coude" (¶82, ¶117); the forest, pine-heavy, with clearings of dead wood (¶87); the marshy part of the forest where tetras were caught earlier (¶88); the thicket and the pond shaded by old pines (¶103, ¶108).
  - Cyrus's mountain, "dans l’ouest" (¶60), the "cône" (¶70).
- **Things.** *(Drafted.)*
  - Two pebbles and dry moss (¶21); two sticks for friction (¶22).
  - Chemical matches, planned (¶44).
  - The sticks cut from a young fir (¶86); the pine nuts of the "pin pigeon" (¶97).
  - The capybara, killed by Nab's stick (¶114) and carried by Pencroff (¶116); the wood raft (¶118).
  - Lithodomes, mussels and sargasses as food (¶18, ¶49); a large shell as a cup (¶52).
- **Time.** *(Drafted.)*
  - The first evening and night: night falls, wind to the north-east (¶17); an hour of rubbing sticks (¶23).
  - The morning: "Le lendemain, 28 mars", about eight o'clock (¶30); the hunters leave about ten (¶80); the pine nuts are found before the sun's highest point (¶97); about two o'clock (¶116); half an hour later at the bend (¶117).
  - *(My reading, to confirm.)* The first evening is 27 March 1865, by "le lendemain, 28 mars"; the year rests on I.11 ¶92. The chapter covers about a day and a half, from the evening of 27 March to about 2:30 p.m. on 28 March.
  - Cyrus plans the ascent for "demain", which is 29 March (¶62).
- **Clues.** *(Drafted.)*
  - Smoke over the rocks, with Cyrus and Spilett alone at the Chimneys and Pencroff sure no one else can light a fire; the next chapter explains it, so it is no clue beyond this chapter.
    - quote: Une fumée s’échappait et tourbillonnait au-dessus des roches !
  - The pines are large, which suggests a higher latitude than Cyrus supposed.
    - quote: cette contrée se trouvait plus élevée en latitude que ne le supposait l’ingénieur
  - The capybara does not flee or fight and may be seeing men for the first time.
    - quote: Peut-être voyait-il des hommes pour la première fois.
  - Pencroff recalls the couroucous in reserve as gone; no cause is given beyond the sea having entered.
    - quote: les couroucous qui servaient de réserve avaient disparu
  - Cyrus says the strength of the wind proved its direction held, and gives a distance of six to seven thousand miles.
    - quote: sa violence même prouve que sa direction n’a pas dû varier
  - Cyrus's remark on a Micronesian archipelago, and "comme si nous ne devions jamais en sortir".
    - quote: nous aviserons à nous établir ici, comme si nous ne devions jamais en sortir !
- **Length.** *(Drafted.)* 121 paragraphs (¶1–¶121, plus the summary ¶0), 3,400 words in the ¶ lines. No footnotes; three plate captions (pl1 after ¶22, pl2 after ¶102, pl3 after ¶121).

## Open and flags

- **The pine nut's name.** I.9 ¶97 reads "pin pigeon"; the summary ¶0 of this chapter and I.10 ¶15 read "pin pignon". Card neither.
  - quote: C’était le pin pigeon, qui produit une amande excellente
  - quote: Le pin pignon.
  - quote: les amandes de pin pignon complétèrent ce repas
  - The text carries no Wikisource correction here (texts/README.md lists none for I.9).
- **The direction of the mountain.** Cyrus says the mountain he saw while carried was "dans l’ouest"; I.10 ¶14 places the cone "dans le nord-ouest". Card neither. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: n’ai-je pas aperçu, dans l’ouest, une montagne qui domine cette contrée ?
  - quote: Le mont, situé à six milles environ dans le nord-ouest
- **The wind.** I.9 ¶17 has the wind jump to the north-east on the first night; ¶70 has it blow from the north-east when they left Richmond. These are two different times; no discord. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: modifiée par une saute du vent dans le nord-est
  - quote: le vent soufflait du nord-est
- **Cyrus's lost-fire talk.** The plan to "faire des allumettes" rests on tinder or on chemistry; the text does not say how. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Eh bien, nous ferons des allumettes.
- **Earlier chapter.** ¶41 mentions "leur unique allumette"; the match is an event of an earlier chapter that this agent was not given. Not checked.
  - quote: l’histoire de leur unique allumette
- No fact in this chapter rests on a Wikisource correction listed in texts/README.md. *(Drafted.)*
