# Wild ducks: the game Pencroff cannot reach

*Wild ducks of the lake shore and the barren north coast, which will not settle and must be hit in flight.*

Keywords: canard sauvage, wild duck, gibier, game, vol, flight, flèche, arrow, lac, lake

- **What it is.**
  - Wild ducks strut about the shores and the rock-islet of Lake Grant, among pelicans, moorhens and other water birds (I.12 ¶68). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: se pavanaient des canards sauvages
  - Wild ducks frequent the desolate north coast; they rouse Pencroff's covetousness (I.14 ¶38). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des canards sauvages, qui excitèrent à bon droit la convoitise de Pencroff
  - They hardly ever settle, and must be hit on the wing; Pencroff's arrows fail (I.14 ¶38). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ils ne se posaient guère, et il eût fallu les atteindre au vol
- **Appears in:** I.12, I.14
