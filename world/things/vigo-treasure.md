# The Vigo treasure: the sunken wealth Nemo drew on

*Millions lost by Spanish galleons in Vigo Bay in 1702, an inexhaustible store from which Nemo gave to peoples fighting for independence.*

Keywords: gold, galleons, sunken treasure, riches, funds

- **What it is.**
  - It is the millions lost in Vigo Bay in 1702 by the Spanish galleons (III.16 ¶54). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Les millions perdus dans la baie de Vigo, en 1702, par les galions espagnols
  - It was an inexhaustible mine of wealth for Nemo (III.16 ¶54). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une mine inépuisable de richesses
- **On the page.**
  - He gave from it, always anonymously, to peoples fighting for the independence of their country (III.16 ¶54). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dont il disposa toujours, et anonymement, en faveur des peuples qui se battaient pour l’indépendance de leur pays
  - The editor says this was the rising of the Candiotes, which he aided in these conditions (III.16 n2). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Il s’agit du soulèvement des Candiotes
- **Appears in:** III.16
