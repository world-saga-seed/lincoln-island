# The moon: the thin crescent that set over the western sea on 29 March

*The slender crescent near its setting, whose light and reflection first showed Smith the western sea.*

Keywords: lune, croissant, crescent, moon, reflection, sea, western horizon

- **What it is.**
  - A thin crescent moon, already near setting, showed with a vague gleam low on the horizon as the cloud rose (I.10 ¶59). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le croissant délié de la lune, déjà près de disparaître
  - Its light sufficed to draw the line of the horizon, and the engineer saw its trembling image reflected a moment on a liquid surface (I.10 ¶59). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: son image tremblotante se refléter un instant sur une surface liquide
  - Smith holds the moon to be truly a cooled star, no longer habitable though the sun still pours on it the same heat (I.21 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la lune est bien véritablement un astre refroidi
    - quote: n’est plus habitable
  - On 30 October the moon is to be full three days later (II.3 ¶9). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la lune devait être pleine trois jours après
  - On the evening of 11 October the crescent shows in the sun’s twilight and soon goes out, the first quarter not due before the 16th (II.13 ¶29). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le croissant de la lune, qui ne devait être dans son premier quartier que le 16
  - On the night of 17 October the new moon, lost in the sun’s glare, has disappeared (III.1 ¶97). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: La lune nouvelle, emportée dans l’irradiation solaire, avait disparu
  - On 18 October it has been new for two days (III.4 ¶17). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la lune était nouvelle depuis deux jours
- **Appears in:** I.10, I.21, II.3, II.13, III.1, III.4
- **Open:**
  - The phase: III.1 ¶97 and III.4 ¶17 give a new moon about 16 to 17 October; II.19 ¶73 says "aujourd’hui pleine lune" in April, which six lunations later would put the full moon in mid-October. Card neither (III.1 ¶97; III.4 ¶17; II.19 ¶73). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: c’est aujourd’hui pleine lune
    - quote: la lune était nouvelle depuis deux jours
