# The copper: the metal the island does not give

*Copper, which Smith cannot find on the island, so that his battery must do without it; the chest’s nails and saucepans are copper.*

Keywords: cuivre, copper, métal, absent, pile, clous, casseroles, chest

- **What it is.**
  - The chest is covered with a thick hide held by copper nails (II.2 ¶38). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une peau épaisse que maintenaient des clous de cuivre
  - Copper is absolutely lacking: Smith, despite his searches, has found no trace of it on Lincoln Island, and must do without it in his battery (II.18 ¶40). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Le cuivre manquait absolument à l’ingénieur
    - quote: n’en avait pas trouvé trace dans l’île Lincoln
- **Appears in:** II.2, II.18
