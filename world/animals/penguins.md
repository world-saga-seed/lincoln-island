# Penguins: the braying diving birds of the islet

*Diving birds of the penguin kind, braying like an ass, whose blackish flesh is edible.*

Keywords: manchot, penguin, plongeur, diver, braiment, âne, ass, îlot du Salut, islet, comestible

- **What it is.**
  - Many diving birds of the penguin kind walk gravely on the islet across the channel from the shore (I.14 ¶28). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: C’étaient des plongeurs, de l’espèce des manchots
  - They are recognised by an unpleasant cry like the braying of an ass (I.14 ¶28). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: très-reconnaissables à leur cri désagréable, qui rappelle le braîment de l’âne
  - Their flesh, though blackish, is quite edible (I.14 ¶28). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: leur chair, quoique noirâtre, est fort mangeable
- **Appears in:** I.14
