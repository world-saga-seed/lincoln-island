# The seal-skin bellows: the blowing machine of two seal skins for the iron-making

*Two seals killed on the islet on 17 April were skinned and the untanned skins sewn into a bellows, finished in three days, with a refractory clay pipe at its end.*

Keywords: soufflet, soufflets de forge, machine soufflante, bellows, blast, seal skins, peaux de phoque, phoques, forge, air, hunt

- **What it is.**
  - Smith gives Pencroff no explanation for the seal hunt before it is made (I.15 ¶14). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: sans avoir obtenu d’autre explication
  - He intends forge bellows of the seals: a blowing machine, necessary for working the ore, which he means to make of the amphibians’ skin (I.15 ¶24, ¶26). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Nous en ferons des soufflets de forge
    - quote: une machine soufflante, nécessaire pour le traitement du minerai
  - Two seals, of middle size and under six feet long, with heads like dogs’, were killed on the islet on 17 April (I.15 ¶22, ¶26). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Deux de ces animaux, vigoureusement frappés, restèrent morts sur le sable
    - quote: leur longueur ne dépassait pas six pieds
  - Nab and Pencroff skinned them on the spot in three hours; Smith meant to use the skins as they were, without any tanning (I.15 ¶28). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: sans leur faire subir aucun tannage
  - The skins were stretched on wooden frames to keep them apart and sewn with fibres, so as to hold the air without too many leaks; it had to be redone several times (I.15 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: sur des cadres de bois
    - quote: les coudre au moyen de fibres
    - quote: Il fallut s’y reprendre à plusieurs fois
  - Smith had only the two steel blades from Top’s collar to work with (I.15 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les deux lames d’acier provenant du collier de Top
  - Three days after the hunt the colony’s outfit was increased by a blowing machine, to inject air into the ore when it is heated (I.15 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: trois jours après, l’outillage de la petite colonie s’était augmenté d’une machine soufflante
  - Nab and Pencroff dragged it on a hurdle to the ore on 20 April (I.15 ¶32). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: traînaient sur une claie la machine soufflante
  - At its end it carries a pipe of refractory clay made beforehand in the pottery kiln, and it is driven by a mechanism of frames, fibre cords and counterweights (I.15 ¶44). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: muni à son extrémité d’un tuyau en terre réfractaire
    - quote: châssis, cordes de fibres et contre-poids
  - Its blast throws a supply of air into the mass, raising the temperature and helping the chemical change that gives pure iron (I.15 ¶44). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il lança dans la masse une provision d’air
- **Appears in:** I.15
