## Licence, for the clearance (phase 12b)

- **The text:** White's 1876 translation is in the public domain. Gutenberg's catalogue record says: "Public domain in the USA."
- **Gutenberg's wrapper:** the file carries the old Project Gutenberg "Small Print!" licence. It comes after the line "*** END OF THE PROJECT GUTENBERG EBOOK THE MYSTERIOUS ISLAND ***", with the short header before the start line. Among its terms is a trademark licence fee for distribution under the Gutenberg name. For release, strip the header and footer and the Gutenberg name, or ship the licence whole.
- **The redactor's note:** the "[Redactor's Note: ...]" at the head is Wolcott's 2003 writing. Quote it briefly, with credit; don't ship it.
