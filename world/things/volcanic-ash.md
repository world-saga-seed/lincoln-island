# The volcanic ash: the grey dust and pozzolana that fall from the eruption

*The fine pozzolana, pulverised pumice and grey ash thrown up by Mont Franklin, which falls as a blackish snow on the corral and the north-west coast in January.*

Keywords: cendres, pouzzolane, poussière volcanique, ponce, dust, pumice, pozzolana, éruption

- **What it is.**
  - The clouds over the forest are heavy with scoriae reduced to dust, such as pulverised pozzolana, and grey ashes as fine as the finest starch (III.18 ¶62). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: de la pouzzolane pulvérisée et des cendres grisâtres aussi fines que la plus fine fécule
  - It falls at the corral as a blackish snow like light gunpowder, covering trees and meadows with a layer several inches thick; the north-east wind carries most of the cloud out to sea (III.18 ¶63). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une sorte de neige noirâtre semblable à une légère poudre de chasse
    - quote: une couche mesurant plusieurs pouces d’épaisseur
    - quote: le vent soufflait du nord-est
  - Smith reads in the pozzolana and pulverised pumice, all this mineral dust, how deep the trouble is in the lower layers of the volcano (III.18 ¶65). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Cette pouzzolane, ces pierres ponces pulvérisées
    - quote: démontre combien le trouble est profond dans les couches inférieures du volcan
  - It wads the ground; a breeze raises the layer into an opaque whirl, and the two walkers put handkerchiefs over eyes and mouth against being blinded and choked (III.18 ¶85). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un sol ouaté par les matières pulvérulentes
    - quote: ils couraient le risque d’être aveuglés et étouffés
  - Covering the polished rocks, the layer of ashes gives a firmer foothold on the sloping surfaces; the sea waves on the coast are dirtied with the volcanic dust (III.18 ¶87, ¶88). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la couche de cendres, recouvrant le poli des roches, permettait d’assurer plus solidement le pied
    - quote: salies par la poussière volcanique
  - A cloud of smoke and ashes stands over the new crater when the cone has fallen (III.19 ¶37). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un nuage de fumée et de cendres
- **Appears in:** III.18, III.19
