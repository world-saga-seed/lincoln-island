## Open and flags

- **Distance of the loss from the coast.** ¶42: "a few hundred feet from the coast". I.7 ¶10 works it out as twelve hundred feet. Both quoted; card neither. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: à quelques centaines de pieds de la côte
- **Landing "la veille".** ¶26: "where they had landed the day before, some two hundred paces from the Chimneys". See the note on I.4 ¶8. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: le point où ils avaient atterri la veille, à deux cents pas environ des Cheminées
- **The eight miles.** ¶42 says Nab and Spilett went "more than eight miles", "well beyond the point where the penultimate fall of the balloon had taken place"; the summary of I.7 says Smith is found "Eight miles from the first camp", and I.7 ¶81 puts the party "about six miles from the Chimneys" a few minutes before the cave. The text does not say whether Nab went past the cave. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: sur un espace de plus de huit milles
- **Who threw the matches.** Spilett: "I had to throw everything away" (¶57), and then the match is in his waistcoat. I.6 ¶2 says everything was thrown out, but Spilett kept a notebook and a watch. Not a discord. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: J’en avais, mais j’ai dû tout jeter…
- No Wikisource correction touches this chapter. *(Drafted.)*
