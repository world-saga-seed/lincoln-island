# Abraham Lincoln: the statesman the island is named for

*The leader fighting for the unity of the American republic, who gives the island its name and whose death the narrator foretells.*

Keywords: Lincoln, Abraham, Union, république, Washington, Good Friday, assassin, assassinat, île Lincoln, 1865

- **What it is.**
  - He is a "grand citoyen", who is now fighting to defend the unity of the American republic (I.11 ¶89). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: celui qui lutte maintenant pour défendre l’unité de la république américaine
  - The island is named for him by Cyrus Smith, to three hurrahs (I.11 ¶89). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Appelons-la l’île Lincoln !
  - The colonists look to the North's victory "grâce à Grant, grâce à Lincoln" (I.11 ¶91). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: grâce à Grant, grâce à Lincoln
  - The narrator tells ahead that, sixteen days after 30 March 1865, a fearful crime will be done at Washington, and that on Good Friday Abraham Lincoln will fall under a fanatic's bullet; the colonists do not know it (I.11 ¶92). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ils ne savaient guère que, seize jours après, un crime effroyable serait commis à Washington
    - quote: Abraham Lincoln tomberait sous la balle d’un fanatique
- **Appears in:** I.11
