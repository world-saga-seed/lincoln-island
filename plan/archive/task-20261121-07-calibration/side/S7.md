# S7: the French, the render, White

## ¶90

**French:** Du reste, l’essai en allait être fait, le jour même, dans une excursion au large de la côte. Le temps était beau, la brise fraîche, et la mer facile, surtout sur le littoral du sud, car le vent soufflait du nord-ouest depuis une heure déjà.

**Render:** Moreover, the trial of it was to be made that very day, in an excursion off the coast. The weather was fine, the breeze fresh, and the sea easy, especially along the southern shore, for the wind had already been blowing from the north-west for an hour.

**White** (¶70): (¶70) In order to satisfy Captain Pencroff it was necessary at once to name his ship, and after considerable discussion they decided upon Good Luck—the name chosen by the honest sailor. Moreover, as the weather was fine, the breeze fresh, and the sea calm, the trial must be made at once in an excursion along the coast.
