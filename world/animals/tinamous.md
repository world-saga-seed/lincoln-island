# Tinamous: the gallinaceans of the poultry-yard

*Long-billed, short-winged gallinaceans taken alive on the Mercy, the first guests of the poultry-yard, where they breed.*

Keywords: tinamou, tinamous, gallinacé, basse-cour, poultry-yard, vivant, Mercy, domestication

- **What it is.**
  - They are gallinaceans with long, slender bills and long necks, short wings and no sign of a tail (II.3 ¶22). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des volatiles à becs longs et grêles, à cous allongés, courts d’ailes et sans apparence de queue
  - Harbert names them "tinamous", with reason, and it is resolved that they will be the first guests of the future poultry-yard (II.3 ¶22). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Harbert leur donna, avec raison, le nom de « tinamous »
    - quote: les premiers hôtes de la future basse-cour
  - They are to serve for a first essay in domestication, and Nab wants the fowl within reach of the cook (II.7 ¶8). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: devait servir à un premier essai de domestication
  - The first to be put in the yard, they soon give many young (II.7 ¶45). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Les premiers furent le couple de tinamous, qui ne tardèrent pas à donner de nombreux petits
- **Appears in:** II.3, II.7
- **Open:**
  - Gédéon Spilett takes "deux couples de gallinacés vivants" (II.3 ¶22); the later chapter speaks of "le couple de tinamous" taken on that excursion (II.7 ¶8, ¶45). Card neither number. *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: deux couples de gallinacés vivants
    - quote: le couple de tinamous, pris dans la dernière excursion
