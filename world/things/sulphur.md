# Sulphur: the sulphur of the volcano, wanted for powder

*Sulphur laid down in crystalline concretions on the mountain slopes; one of the substances Smith says they can probably find, for powder.*

Keywords: soufre, solfatare, crystals, powder, poudre, volcanic

- **What it is.**
  - On the slopes of the mountain, in places, sulphur had been deposited in crystalline concretions among the matter that precedes lava flows (I.10 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le soufre avait déposé sous la forme de concrétions cristallines
  - Spilett lists it, with charcoal, among what is needed to make powder (I.14 ¶41). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: du salpêtre, du charbon et du soufre pour la poudre
  - Smith answered that all those substances could probably be found on the island (I.14 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: toutes ces substances, nous pourrons sans doute les trouver dans l’île
- **Appears in:** I.10, I.14
