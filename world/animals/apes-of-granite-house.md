# Apes of Granite-house: the band of quadrumanes that took the dwelling

*A band of apes, at least a handful, that climbed into Granite-house while the colonists were away, were shot at from the beach and fled in sudden terror; their dead were buried in the wood.*

Keywords: singes, quadrumanes, orang, anthropomorphes, bande, farceurs, Granite-house, échelle, terreur, magasin

- **What it is.**
  - Pencroff reads the intruders as monkeys of every kind, which "ont grimpé par l'échelle pendant notre absence" (II.6 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un singe, un macaque, un sapajou, une guenon, un orang, un babouin, un gorille, un sagouin
    - quote: qui ont grimpé par l’échelle pendant notre absence
  - Three or four quadrumanes show themselves at the windows, pushing back the shutters, and greet the owners with a thousand contortions and grimaces (II.6 ¶43). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: trois ou quatre quadrumanes se montraient aux fenêtres
    - quote: mille contorsions et grimaces
  - The one shot, a tall ape, belongs to the first order of quadrumanes, the anthropomorphs; the narrator hesitates among chimpanzee, orang, gorilla and gibbon, but Harbert declares it an orang-outang, "et l'on sait que le jeune garçon se connaissait en zoologie" (II.6 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: appartenait au premier ordre des quadrumanes
    - quote: Que ce fût un chimpanzé, un orang, un gorille ou un gibbon
    - quote: Harbert déclara que c’était un orang-outang
  - Pencroff calls them "malins" and thinks they will not show themselves at the windows again; for two hours they avoid showing themselves, though a muzzle or a paw slips out three or four times (II.6 ¶50, ¶55). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ces singes-là sont malins
    - quote: les singes évitèrent de se montrer
  - The colonists guess that, frightened by the death of one of them and by the detonations, they lie quiet in the rooms or the storeroom (II.6 ¶60). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: effrayés par la mort de l’un d’eux, épouvantés par les détonations des armes
  - Their number is not given: Pencroff asks how many dozens are up there and says that even twenty could be beaten, and "cinq ou six" are in position to be shot when they flee (II.6 ¶52, ¶62, ¶74). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: combien il y en a de douzaines
    - quote: quand même ils seraient une vingtaine
    - quote: cinq ou six furent en position d’être tirés
  - Seized by a sudden fear "provoqué par quelque cause inconnue", they try to flee, two or three running and leaping from window to window with the agility of clowns (II.6 ¶74). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: pris d’un effroi subit, provoqué par quelque cause inconnue
    - quote: avec une agilité de clowns
  - They do not even try to put the ladder back in place, though it would have been easy for them to come down by it (II.6 ¶74). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Ils ne cherchaient même pas à replacer l’échelle, par laquelle il leur eût été facile de descendre
  - After the shots, those wounded or killed fall back inside with shrill cries; the others, thrown out, are broken by the fall; the colonists suppose none is left alive in Granite-house (II.6 ¶74). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: en poussant des cris aigus
    - quote: on pouvait supposer qu’il n’y avait plus un quadrumane vivant dans Granite-house
  - The storeroom was respected by the band, and the chambers were found empty (II.6 ¶87). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le magasin qui avait été respecté par la bande des quadrumanes
  - The band had overturned the furniture of the rooms but broken nothing: "désordre et non dégât" (II.7 ¶2). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: désordre et non dégât
    - quote: n’avaient-ils rien brisé
  - The colonists carry the dead apes into the wood and bury them (II.7 ¶2). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les cadavres des singes furent transportés dans le bois, où on les enterra
  - The narrator offers that the band may have sensed an assault coming by another route, "la seule façon d'interpréter leur mouvement de retraite", their terror being "non moins subite qu'inexplicable" (II.7 ¶1). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une terreur, non moins subite qu’inexplicable
    - quote: C’était à peu près la seule façon d’interpréter leur mouvement de retraite
  - Smith counts the ladder "si opportunément renvoyée des hauteurs de Granite-house" after the apes' invasion among the unexplained facts of the island (II.20 ¶49). *(Drafted.)*

- **Appears in:** II.6, II.7, II.20
- **Open:**
  - The text gives two views of who drew up the lower ladder: that the intruders had done so "pour se mettre à l'abri de toute surprise" (II.6 ¶24), and that the apes, in flight, did not even try to put the ladder back although it would have been easy to come down by it (II.6 ¶74). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Il était plus qu’évident que les intrus avaient voulu se mettre à l’abri de toute surprise
    - quote: Ils ne cherchaient même pas à replacer l’échelle
  - The kind is not fixed beyond "quadrumanes" and, for the one shot, "orang"; the chapter does not say whether all the band are orangs, nor how many there were (II.6 ¶43, ¶46, ¶74). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
