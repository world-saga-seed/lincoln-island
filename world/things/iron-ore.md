# Iron ore: the sample of iron ore Smith shows

*Iron ore from the island, among the samples Smith pockets; the red earth of the Red Creek shows iron oxide.*

Keywords: minerai de fer, iron, ore, oxyde de fer, red earth, fer

- **What it is.**
  - The red earth of the Red Creek's banks shows the presence of iron oxide (I.12 ¶39). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dont la couleur décelait la présence de l’oxyde de fer
  - Smith showed a piece of it among the samples (see [the mineral samples](mineral-samples.md)) (I.12 ¶77). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ceci est du minerai de fer
  - Smith has observed some deposits of iron ore in the north-west of the island (I.15 ¶7). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: quelques gisements dans la partie nord-ouest de l’île
  - The two samples Smith brought were magnetic iron, not carbonated, and a pyrite, iron sulphide (I.15 ¶8). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: l’un du fer magnétique, non carbonaté, l’autre de la pyrite
  - The ore he collected is very pure and rich: the oxidulated ore, in confused dark-grey masses, giving a black powder and crystallising in regular octahedrons, which supplies natural magnets and, in Europe, the first-quality iron of Sweden and Norway (I.15 ¶9). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: très-pur et très-riche
    - quote: fournit les aimants naturels
  - The coal deposits already worked by the colonists lie not far from this deposit, so the elements of the manufacture are close together (I.15 ¶9). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Non loin de ce gisement se trouvaient les gisements de charbon de terre
  - The deposits lie at the foot of the north-east spurs of Mont Franklin, six miles from the Chimneys (I.15 ¶31). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: au bas des contreforts nord-est du mont Franklin
  - Smith found the deposit level with the ground, almost at the creek’s sources, at the foot of the lateral base of one of the north-east spurs; the ore is very rich in iron, in a fusible gangue (I.15 ¶41). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: à fleur de terre, presque aux sources même du creek
    - quote: enfermé dans sa gangue fusible
  - The ore was broken into small pieces and cleaned by hand of the impurities on its surface (I.15 ¶43). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: On cassa préalablement le minerai en petits morceaux
  - The coal and iron deposits are among the places visited through the winter and spring (II.12 ¶85). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ni aux gisements de houille et de fer
