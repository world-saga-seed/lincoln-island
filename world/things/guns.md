# The guns: the fowling pieces Pencroff wants and Smith has not yet the means to make

*Hunting guns and the powder for them: wished for by Pencroff, their materials listed by Spilett, put off by Smith for bows and arrows.*

Keywords: fusils, powder, poudre, fulminate, mercury, nitric acid, barrels, shotgun, firearms, plomb

- **What it is.**
  - Pencroff thought to ask the engineer for powder and one or two fowling pieces, and thought it would present no difficulty (I.12 ¶13). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il pensait que cela ne souffrirait aucune difficulté
  - With stones and sticks the hunters are reduced to primitive weapons; a shot of small lead would have made a slaughter of the birds (I.12 ¶45). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Un coup de fusil à petit plomb eût fait une hécatombe de ces volatiles
  - Smith answered that perhaps they could be made, but they would begin with bows and arrows (I.12 ¶52). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: nous commencerons d’abord par fabriquer des arcs et des flèches
  - Pencroff declared the outfit wanting until they have one or two fowling pieces (I.14 ¶40). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: tant que nous n’aurons pas un ou deux fusils de chasse, notre matériel laissera à désirer
  - Spilett listed what they need: iron for the barrels, steel for the locks, saltpetre, charcoal and sulphur for powder, mercury and nitric acid for the fulminate, and lead for the bullets (I.14 ¶41). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: du fer pour les canons, de l’acier pour les batteries
  - Smith said all those substances could probably be found on the island, but a firearm is a delicate instrument that needs tools of great precision (I.14 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une arme à feu est un instrument délicat et qui nécessite des outils d’une grande précision
  - Smith could make a primer like guncotton for the nitro-glycerine, having nitric acid at his disposal, or fulminate-like substances (I.17 ¶70). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il pouvait facilement obtenir une substance analogue au coton-poudre
  - Pencroff asks whether the nitro-glycerine could load firearms; Smith says no, it is too shattering, but it would be easy to make guncotton or even ordinary powder, since they have nitric acid, saltpetre, sulphur and charcoal; unfortunately it is arms that they lack (I.18 ¶9). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: c’est une substance trop brisante
    - quote: Malheureusement, ce sont les armes que nous n’avons pas
  - A shot of lead from a fowling piece would certainly have brought down some dozens of the marsh birds; the colonists must make do with arrows (I.21 ¶55). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Un coup de fusil à plomb eût certainement atteint quelques douzaines de ces oiseaux
  - Pencroff presses for firearms that he says Smith promised; Smith knows that without a special set of tools he could hardly make a gun of any service, and puts it off again and again (I.22 ¶57). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les armes à feu qu’il affirmait avoir été promises par Cyrus Smith
    - quote: reculait toujours et remettait l’opération à plus tard
  - The chest of 29 October holds two flintlock guns, two percussion guns and two central-fire carbines, with powder and caps (see [the flintlock guns](flintlock-guns.md), [the percussion guns](percussion-guns.md), [the carbines](carbines.md), [the powder](the-powder.md)) (II.2 ¶53). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: 2 fusils à pierre
