# The bottle: the stoppered bottle Harbert takes from the sea off port Ballon

*A closed bottle seized on 10 October a few cable lengths from the coast, holding a wet paper with the position of Tabor Island.*

Keywords: bouteille, bottle, document, message, naufragé, castaway, Tabor, Harbert, papier, latitude, longitude

- **What it is.**
  - Harbert, in the bow of the Bonadventure testing the passes towards port Ballon, plunges his arm into the water and takes up a closed bottle (II.12 ¶122 to ¶130). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Une bouteille !
    - quote: Il tenait à la main une bouteille fermée, qu’il venait de saisir à quelques encâblures de la côte
  - It is found on the day of the launch and trial sail, 10 October (II.12 ¶87, ¶90). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Le 10 octobre, le bateau fut lancé à la mer
    - quote: l’essai en allait être fait, le jour même
  - Smith takes it, pulls the stopper and draws out a wet paper bearing words (II.12 ¶131). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il en fit sauter le bouchon
    - quote: il tira un papier humide, sur lequel se lisaient ces mots
  - The paper reads, as printed: castaway, Tabor Island, 153° W. long., 37° 11′ lat. S. (II.12 ln3). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Naufragé… île Tabor : 153° O. long. — 37° 11′ lat. S.
  - These are the figures Smith read off the atlas for Tabor (II.9 ¶91). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: Cette île est située par 153° de longitude et 37° 11′ de latitude
- **Appears in:** II.9, II.12
- **Open:**
  - Distance from the coast: the Bonadventure is "half a mile from the coast" when she nears port Ballon, and the bottle is taken "a few cable lengths" from it; whether these are the same distance is not said (II.12 ¶123, ¶130). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: On n’était plus qu’à un demi-mille de la côte
    - quote: à quelques encâblures de la côte
  - Language: the paper is printed in French (ln3), while II.13 ¶6 says the document is "écrit en langue anglaise"; card neither (II.12 ln3; II.13 ¶6). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Naufragé… île Tabor : 153° O. long. — 37° 11′ lat. S.
    - quote: puisque le document est écrit en langue anglaise
