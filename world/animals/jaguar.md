# Jaguar: the beast of the cave at the Reptile promontory

*A tawny, black-spotted cat over five feet long, which roars from a cave in the rocks of the west coast and has never smelt man before.*

Keywords: jaguar, fauve, félin, tigre, rival du tigre, couguar, caverne, repaire, rugissements, taches, dépouille, west coast

- **What it is.**
  - An animal resembling a jaguar is seen one day by the reporter and Harbert near the brickworks; fortunately it does not attack them (I.13 ¶39). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un animal qui ressemblait à un jaguar
    - quote: ne les attaqua pas
  - The one met at the Reptile promontory is a jaguar at least the size of its Asiatic kin, measuring over five feet from the end of the head to the root of the tail (II.4 ¶63). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: d’une taille au moins égale à celle de ses congénères d’Asie
    - quote: plus de cinq pieds de l’extrémité de la tête à la naissance de la queue
  - Its coat is tawny, relieved by several rows of regularly ocellated black spots, with a white belly (II.4 ¶63). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Son pelage fauve était relevé par plusieurs rangées de taches noires régulièrement ocellées
    - quote: le poil blanc de son ventre
  - Harbert knows it as the ferocious rival of the tiger, far more redoubtable than the cougar, which is only the wolf's rival (II.4 ¶63). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ce féroce rival du tigre
    - quote: qui n’est que le rival du loup
  - It roars formidably from a cavity in the rocks of the shore, which is its lair; the sand of the cave is strewn with bones (II.4 ¶60, ¶62, ¶73, ¶79). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: de formidables rugissements
    - quote: un magnifique animal se montrait à l’entrée de la caverne
    - quote: ce jaguar a quitté son repaire
    - quote: dont le sable était jonché d’ossements
  - A jaguar is caught in the whalebone traps, among a dozen foxes and some boars, and found dead with its stomach pierced by the unbent whalebone (II.11 ¶29). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Une douzaine de renards, quelques sangliers et même un jaguar s’y laissèrent prendre
    - quote: l’estomac perforé par les fanons détendus
  - A formidable jaguar, like the one killed at the Reptile promontory, is met in the forest across the Creek-Glycérine, on the lake's south side, where dangerous animals had not shown themselves (II.16 ¶98, ¶104). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un formidable jaguar, semblable à celui qui avait été tué au promontoire du Reptile
    - quote: les animaux dangereux ne se montraient pas dans cette partie de l’île
  - It is killed there with a knife by the unknown, who seizes its throat and stabs its heart, as it crouches to spring on Harbert (II.16 ¶104, ¶105, ¶106). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ramassé sur lui-même, allait s’élancer
