# The engine room of the Nautilus: the room at the bow with the electric apparatus

*The room forward in the Nautilus where the electric apparatus gives heat, light and mechanical force.*

Keywords: chambre des machines, engine room, appareils électriques, Nautilus, avant, bow

- **What it is.** A room, aboard the Nautilus. Build if needed. *(Drafted.)*
  - It lies at the bow, where the electric apparatus gives heat and light as well as mechanical force to the Nautilus (III.17 ¶40). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: arrivèrent à l’avant, dans la chambre des machines
    - quote: fournissaient la force mécanique au Nautilus
    - In the earlier book the engine room is twenty metres long and divided in two: the first part holds the cells that produce the electricity, the second the mechanism that carries motion to the propeller (VML I.12 ¶52). *(Drafted.)*
      - quote: ne mesurait pas moins de vingt mètres en longueur
      - quote: la première renfermait les éléments qui produisaient l’électricité, et la seconde, le mécanisme qui transmettait le mouvement à l’hélice
    - Its air carries a smell of its own, from gases released by the use of sodium (VML I.12 ¶53, ¶54). *(Drafted.)*
      - quote: l’odeur sui generis qui emplissait ce compartiment
      - quote: quelques dégagements de gaz, produits par l’emploi du sodium
