# Skuas: gull-like birds of the granite wall

*Gull-like birds, called labbes or stercoraires, that Pencroff names on the great cliff; poor eating.*

Keywords: labbe, stercoraire, skua, gull, palmipède, seabird

- **What it is.**
  - Pencroff recognises several skuas ("labbes"), a kind of gull sometimes called "stercoraires", among the web-footed birds on the granite wall (I.4 ¶9). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: sortes de goëlands auxquels on donne quelquefois le nom de stercoraires
  - They are scarcely edible, and their eggs have a detestable taste (I.4 ¶9). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ces mouettes et ces labbes sont à peine mangeables
- **Appears in:** I.4
