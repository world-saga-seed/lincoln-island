# The claw prints: the clear prints of a feline round the traps

*Claw marks found in the snow round the pit traps, read by Harbert as a feline’s.*

Keywords: empreintes de griffes, griffes, claw marks, prints, feline, féliens, tiger, fauves, wild beast, traces

- **What it is.**
  - Round the traps, in the snow, are many prints, among them some very clearly marked claw marks (I.22 ¶31). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les empreintes étaient nombreuses aux alentours
    - quote: certaines marques de griffes très-nettement accusées
  - Harbert does not hesitate to say that a carnivore of the feline kind passed there, which justifies Smith’s opinion that dangerous wild beasts are on the island (I.22 ¶31). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: quelque carnassier du genre des féliens
    - quote: la présence de fauves dangereux à l’île Lincoln
  - Harbert says the felines called tigers are found in the New World from Mexico to the Pampas of Buenos Aires, and the island lies at about the latitude of the provinces of La Plata (I.22 ¶33, ¶35). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: depuis le Mexique jusqu’aux Pampas de Buenos-Ayres
    - quote: sous la même latitude que les provinces de la Plata
