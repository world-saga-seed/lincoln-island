# The harpoon: the Maria-Stella’s harpoon found in the dead whale

*A harpoon that stands in the left flank of the stranded whale, bearing the name of the Vineyard whaler Maria-Stella.*

Keywords: harpon, harpoon, Maria-Stella, Vineyard, whale, baleine, inscription, flank, whaler

- **What it is.**
  - A harpoon stands out of the dead whale’s left flank (II.10 ¶54). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un harpon sortait de son flanc gauche
  - Smith suggests that whalers often engrave the name of their ship on it (II.10 ¶60). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: suivant un usage assez répandu, les baleiniers ont-ils gravé sur celui-ci le nom de leur navire
  - Pencroff pulls it out and reads an inscription: Maria-Stella, Vineyard (see [the Maria-Stella](maria-stella.md)) (II.10 ¶61, ln3). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ayant arraché le harpon que l’animal avait au flanc, y lut cette inscription
    - quote: Maria-Stella / Vineyard.
  - Pencroff says it proves nothing: whales have been seen to go thousands of miles with a harpoon in the flank, and this one might have been struck in the north of the Atlantic and died in the south of the Pacific (II.10 ¶58). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: On a vu des baleines faire des milliers de milles avec un harpon au flanc
    - quote: frappée au nord de l’Atlantique et serait venue mourir au sud du Pacifique
  - Spilett, whom Pencroff’s assertion did not satisfy, objects "Cependant…"; Smith answers that this is perfectly possible (II.10 ¶59, ¶60). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: que l’affirmation de Pencroff ne satisfaisait pas
    - quote: Cela est parfaitement possible
- **Appears in:** II.10
