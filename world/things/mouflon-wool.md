# The mouflon wool: the fleeces shorn at the corral

*The short-fibred wool of the corral’s mouflons, scoured and washed before fulling.*

Keywords: laine, wool, mouflons, suint, soude, dégraissage, bain, fibres courtes, short fibres

- **What it is.**
  - The mouflons of the corral have been shorn of their wool, a precious textile matter still to be turned into cloth (II.11 ¶2). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Les mouflons du corral avaient été dépouillés de leur laine
  - It is made of very short strands, a good condition for felting (see [the felt](felt.md)) (II.11 ¶3). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la laine fournie par les mouflons était faite de brins très-courts
  - It is first cleared of the oily, greasy substance called suint, in vats of water at seventy degrees for twenty-four hours, then washed thoroughly with soda baths and pressed dry (II.11 ¶4). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: cette substance huileuse et grasse dont elle est imprégnée et qu’on nomme le suint
    - quote: au moyen de bains de soude
  - The mouflons, numerous by the winter of 1867, have supplied abundantly the wool needed for felt clothes (II.20 ¶72). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les mouflons, nombreux alors, avaient abondamment fourni la laine nécessaire
- **Appears in:** II.11, II.20
