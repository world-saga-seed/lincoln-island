# Traps, Snowstorm and the Lead Shot (notes)

## Notes for drafting
- **Where it sits.** The last chapter of the first part. It follows I.21 (5 July) and covers the rest of the winter, from July to 24 October, ending on the evening of 24 October with the lead shot in Pencroff's tooth. The second part begins in II.1 with the same dinner. *(Drafted.)*
- **Cast.** Pencroff (¶1), Nab (¶3), Harbert (¶6), Gédéon Spilett (¶6), Cyrus Smith (¶23), Top (¶52). The five colonists and the dog. *(Drafted.)*
- **Places.** The plateau of Grande-Vue and the edge of the forest, where the traps stand (¶6); the right bank of the Mercy (¶7); the warren (¶13); north of the lake, where "sangliers" had been noted (¶14); Granite-house (¶23); the Jacamar woods (¶23); the osier bed near the point the lake projects to the north (¶28); the beach and plateau under snow, the Mercy under ice, the lake, the cascade (¶29–¶30); the coal bed, the pottery kiln (¶37); the Chimneys (¶39); the dunes, the Tadornes marsh (¶51); the well in the back storeroom (¶53); the Serpentine peninsula and the west coast (¶60); the hall and the kitchen of Granite-house (¶73–¶75). *(Drafted.)*
- **Things.** Pit traps, baited with culpeux carcasses (¶7, ¶12); snares of curry-rush fibre (¶13); baskets and hampers of purple osier (¶28); furniture of stored planks (¶27); maple-sugar loaves and tablets in clay moulds (¶49); a wooden panel over the well (¶53); Top's growling (¶53); the bows and arrows of Harbert and Spilett (¶57); the wished-for firearms (¶57); the female peccary and her two young (¶62); the dinner (¶74); the lead shot (¶82). *(Drafted.)*
- **Time.** Cold until 15 August (¶1); second week of August, peccaries trapped (¶14); about 15 August, wind to the northwest, snow (¶22); shut in 20 to 25 August (¶23); last week of August, the storm calms (¶29); "Vers le 25", wind to the southeast, 8 °F below zero (¶41); cold until mid-September (¶50); the cold ends, spring (¶56); second half of September, much hunting (¶57); "un mois devait s’écouler encore" before the exploration (¶60); 24 October, the trapped peccaries and the dinner at five (¶62, ¶75). The chapter covers about three and a half months. *(Drafted.)*
- **Clues.** (1) Top's behaviour at the well, which Cyrus tells only to Spilett (¶53–¶55). (2) Clear claw prints round the traps, read by Harbert as a feline; Cyrus's view that "fauves dangereux" are on the island (¶31). (3) Pencroff's remark on bears that "ils ne s’étaient pas montrés jusqu’alors" (¶5). (4) The lead shot in a young peccary (¶82), the chapter's last line. *(Drafted.)*
- **Length.** 82 paragraphs (¶1–¶82); 4,086 words. Two plate captions (pl1 after ¶20, pl2 after ¶22) and a loose line "fin de la première partie" (ln3, after ¶82). No footnotes. *(Drafted.)*

## Open and flags
- ¶23 "ils restèrent renfermés pendant cinq jours, du 20 au 25 août" and ¶29 "Pendant la dernière semaine de ce mois d’août, le temps se modifia encore une fois" and ¶41 "Vers le 25, après une nouvelle alternative de neige et de pluie, le vent sauta au sud-est": the storm ends on the 25th in ¶23, and ¶41 puts a new cold, after another turn of snow and rain, at about the same day. Both quoted; not reconciled. *(Drafted.)*
- ¶21 calls the animals "des pécaris" of the "tajassous" kind; ¶14 first calls them "sangliers", and ¶14 says "sangliers qui avaient été déjà signalés au nord du lac". Both are given. *(Drafted.)*
- ¶62 "une femelle de pécari et ses deux petits" and ¶72 "Ces deux petits pécaris n’ont pas seulement trois mois": the shot in II.1 is said to have wounded "le pécari" without saying which of the two. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- ¶70 "il y a sept mois" and II.1 ¶1 "sept mois, jour pour jour": agree if the landing was 24 March (earlier chapters; not in this span). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- ¶57 "les armes à feu qu’il affirmait avoir été promises par Cyrus Smith": Pencroff's claim; no earlier promise is in this span. *(Drafted.)*
- ¶41 gives "huit degrés au-dessous de zéro (22°, 22 centigr. au-dessous de glace)"; the conversion agrees with the Fahrenheit figure. I.21 ¶5 had 8 °F above zero (4 July). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- No Wikisource correction touches this chapter. The print's "complèta" (¶27) is as the text reads. *(Drafted.)*
