### 1. cut: the overflow runs out by the well (I.21 ¶6; White ¶3)
- The French says the reservoir's overflow ran off by the well to the sea; White says it "emptied itself into the sea". *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: dont le trop-plein s’enfuyait par le puits jusqu’à la mer
  - white: which, when too full, emptied itself into the sea

### 2. changed: the Falklands become the "Saint Malo group" (I.21 ¶16; White ¶8)
- The French places the culpeux in Chile and the Malouines (the Falkland Islands); White has "the Saint Malo group". *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Ces culpeux se rencontrent fréquemment au Chili, aux Malouines
  - white: These culpeux are often met with in Chili, in the Saint Malo group

### 3. changed: the poultry yard still to come becomes the kitchen already built (I.21 ¶19; White ¶12)
- The French has Pencroff remark that when a poultry yard is set up at Granite-house it will be well to guard against these raiders; White has him saying so ever since the kitchen was established. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: lorsqu’une basse-cour serait établie à Granite-house, il fit observer qu’il serait bon de prendre quelques précautions
  - white: Ever since the kitchen had been established at Granite House he had been saying that precautions ought to be taken

### 4. changed: the south-east shore becomes the south (I.21 ¶23; White ¶18)
- Spilett says the island's northern and south-eastern shores seem to lie on an Arctic sea; White has "northern and southern coasts". *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: ses rivages du nord et du sud-est s’étendent sur une sorte de mer Arctique
  - white: the northern and southern coasts extended into a sort of Arctic Sea

### 5. condensed: the drowned continent stood above the waters in antehistoric ages (I.21 ¶26; White ¶21)

  - quote: mais qui dominait les eaux aux époques antéhistoriques
  - white: but which formerly rose majestically from these waters

### 6. changed: forty-seven million to weigh a grain (I.21 ¶36; White ¶23)

### 8. softened: "may change" becomes "will change" (I.21 ¶36; White ¶24)
- Cyrus says that, as centuries follow centuries, the Pacific "will be able to" change into a continent; White makes it a flat future. *(My reading, to confirm.)*

  - quote: puisque nous n’avons aucune envie d’y mourir et que nous finirons peut-être par nous en tirer
  - white: we will get away
