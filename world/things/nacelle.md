# The basket: the wicker car of the balloon

*A wicker box that cannot float, cut loose to lighten the balloon.*

Keywords: nacelle, car, gondola, wicker, cords

- **What it is.**
  - It is a sort of wicker box, unable to float (I.1 ¶34). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une sorte de caisse d’osier, impropre à flotter
  - It hung under the lower appendix and held five passengers (I.1 ¶17). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une nacelle, qui contenait cinq passagers
  - It had held five passengers and a dog (I.1 ¶63). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: La nacelle avait contenu cinq passagers, plus un chien
  - The narrator calls it a frail basket (I.2 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dans cette frêle nacelle
  - The basket was held by a strong cable through a ring in the pavement, the cable's double returning aboard (I.2 ¶49). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la nacelle était retenue par un fort câble
  - A dog leapt into it, climbing the side in a bound (I.2 ¶53). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un chien escalada d’un bond la nacelle
- **On the page.**
