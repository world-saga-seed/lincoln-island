# The lantern: the lantern the colonists carry into dark places

*A lantern lit by Smith to go down the well and carried by Smith and Ayrton into the crypt Dakkar, struck alight with the briquet and set on the boat’s stem.*

Keywords: lanterne, lantern, lamp, puits, well, light, fanal, lanterne, briquet

- **What it is.**
  - Smith lights a lantern and takes it down the well, carrying also a revolver and a cutlass at his belt (II.11 ¶83). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ayant allumé une lanterne, pris un revolver et passé un coutelas à sa ceinture
  - He passes the lantern carefully over the well’s projections to look for marks (II.11 ¶85). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: en promenant avec soin sa lanterne sur ces saillies
  - Smith tells Ayrton to take a "fanal" and a briquet for the crypt; Ayrton strikes the briquet and lights the lantern, which is set on the boat’s stem to throw its rays ahead, and the light, though insufficient, lets Smith follow the right wall (III.18 ¶83, ¶93, ¶95). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Prenez un fanal et un briquet, Ayrton
    - quote: après avoir battu le briquet, alluma le fanal
    - quote: de manière à projeter ses rayons en avant
  - Smith fixes it to the end of an oar to light the end wall at a greater height (III.18 ¶102). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: fixa le fanal à l’extrémité d’un aviron
- **Appears in:** II.11, III.18
