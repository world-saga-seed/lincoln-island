# The furniture: the tables, chairs, wardrobes and beds of Granite-house

*Heavy, solid furniture of sawn planks: tables, seats, wardrobes and bed frames with zostera mattresses, and the kitchen’s shelves, brick stove and sink.*

Keywords: meubles, mobilier, tables, chaises, armoires, lits, matelas, zostère, planks, carpentry, kitchen

- **What it is.**
  - The carpenters made tables, seats and wardrobes for the main rooms, and bed frames whose whole bedding consisted of zostera mattresses (I.20 ¶28). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des tables, des sièges, des armoires
    - quote: des cadres de lit, dont toute la literie consista en matelas de zostère
  - The kitchen has its shelves, on which stand earthenware utensils, a brick stove and a washing-up stone, and Nab works in it as gravely as in a chemist’s laboratory (I.20 ¶28). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ses planches, sur lesquelles reposaient les ustensiles en terre cuite
    - quote: son fourneau de briques, sa pierre à relaver
  - In the storm confinement of August the colonists finished the furniture in tables and chairs, solid for certain since the material was not spared, and a little heavy (I.22 ¶27). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: on complèta le mobilier, en tables et en chaises, solides à coup sûr
    - quote: Ces meubles, un peu lourds
  - They were the pride of Nab and Pencroff, who would not have changed them for Boule furniture (I.22 ¶27). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ils firent l’orgueil de Nab et de Pencroff
- **Made from.**
  - The work became possible with the hand saw, an égohine, made at great cost of pains, which divided the fibres of the wood (see [the hand saw](hand-saw.md)) (I.20 ¶28). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: put diviser les fibres ligneuses du bois
  - The planks lay in the store, wood sawn into boards (I.22 ¶27). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Le bois, débité en planches, ne manquait pas dans le magasin
- **Appears in:** I.20, I.22
