## a

After: At 4 o’clock it was plain that the balloon could no longer sustain itself.

This is the second four o’clock in a few lines. At the first, land was cried thirty miles to leeward, a full hour’s run off; half an hour after this second one, the land was a mile off. The two hours cannot both stand with that hour’s run between them. The heading of chapter III gives five o’clock in the afternoon for the landing and the search that began within two minutes of it, which agrees with land sighted at four and reached an hour later. I take the first four o’clock, and read this one as some time after it.
