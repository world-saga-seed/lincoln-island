# 04 Card the existing work

*Basic.*

**When:** after setup; before any new outlining.

**Uses:** [scene-outlines](../workflows/scene-outlines.md) (prompt D: existing prose taken apart into numbered beats) and [character-sheet](../workflows/character-sheet.md) (Basic); as Refinements, [habits](../workflows/habits.md), [capture-notes](../workflows/capture-notes.md) (outside notes), [proposals](../workflows/proposals.md) and [daily-life-study](../workflows/daily-life-study.md) (where the setting is thin), [distill](../workflows/distill.md), [card-audit](../workflows/card-audit.md).

**What happens:** facts go onto marked cards and sheets, and each existing scene becomes a numbered outline; the questions go to the writer in batches ([method/cards.md](../method/cards.md)).

**Outputs:** `world/`, `story/notes/characters/`, chapter outlines and notes, the voice sheet. Example: [the cards](../example/world/README.md), [Wren's sheet](../example/story/notes/characters/wren.md).

**Done when:** every named thing in the manuscripts has a card or is a crowd figure; every fact is marked; every existing scene has an outline; links and cards at 0.
