# The steel: cementation steel made from the colonists’ own iron

*Steel by carburation of the iron, heated with powdered charcoal in a refractory clay crucible; used for axe heads, plane blades and bands for saws.*

Keywords: acier, steel, cémentation, cementation, crucible, creuset, temper, trempe, charcoal, carburation, tools

- **What it is.**
  - Spilett lists steel for the locks among what guns would need (I.14 ¶41). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: de l’acier pour les batteries
  - Steel is a combination of iron and carbon; natural or puddled steel comes from cast iron by decarburation, cementation steel from iron by adding carbon (I.15 ¶48). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: l’acier est une combinaison de fer et de charbon
    - quote: l’acier de cémentation
  - Smith chooses cementation steel, since he has pure iron, and makes it by heating the metal with powdered charcoal in a crucible of refractory clay (I.15 ¶49). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Il y réussit en chauffant le métal avec du charbon en poudre dans un creuset fait en terre réfractaire
  - The steel is malleable hot and cold, and Smith works it with the hammer (I.15 ¶50). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: malléable à chaud et à froid
  - Axe heads of it, heated red and plunged into cold water, take an excellent temper (I.15 ¶50). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: acquirent une trempe excellente
  - Bands of it are meant to become saws (I.15 ¶51). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: bandes d’acier qui devaient être transformées en scies
- **Appears in:** I.14, I.15
