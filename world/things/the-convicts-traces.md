# The convicts’ traces: the signs they leave in the Far-West forest

*Broken branches, the ashes of a dead fire and footprints in the clay in the forest of the Far-West, among them the prints of five men and not six; none on the west coast.*

Keywords: traces, empreintes, pas, footprints, brisées, cendres, foyer, five convicts, six, Ayrton, campement

- **What it is.**
  - The animals of the Far-West forest, quadrupeds and birds, are more timid than before, which Smith takes to mean that the convicts have lately been through (III.11 ¶43). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ces animaux, quadrupèdes et volatiles, sont plus craintifs qu’autrefois
    - quote: Ces bois ont donc été récemment parcourus par les convicts
  - In many places the colonists see the more or less recent passing of a band of men: branches broken from trees, perhaps to mark the way, the ashes of a dead fire, and footprints kept by patches of clay (III.11 ¶44). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des brisées faites aux arbres, peut-être dans le but de jalonner le chemin
    - quote: des cendres d’un foyer éteint, et des empreintes de pas que certaines portions glaiseuses du sol avaient conservées
  - They show nothing that looks like a permanent camp (III.11 ¶44). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: rien qui parût appartenir à un campement définitif
  - Near a fire that seemed lately put out, measured one by one by length and width, the footprints are those of five men (III.11 ¶55). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: on retrouva aisément la trace des pieds de cinq hommes
  - No sixth print is found, which would have been Ayrton’s (III.11 ¶55). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: on ne put découvrir une sixième empreinte
    - quote: qui, dans ce cas, eût été celle du pied d’Ayrton
  - On the west coast no more traces are found, however carefully sought: no footprints, no broken branches, no cold ashes, no abandoned camps (III.12 ¶3). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Plus d’empreintes de pas, plus de brisées aux arbres, plus de cendres refroidies, plus de campements abandonnés
  - Smith reads this to mean that the convicts landed near the pointe de l’Épave, crossed the Tadornes marsh into the forests of the Far-West, and so followed about the road the colonists took from Granite-house (III.12 ¶4). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Les convicts ont abordé l’île aux environs de la pointe de l’Épave
    - quote: après avoir traversé le marais des Tadornes
    - quote: Ils ont donc suivi à peu près la route que nous avons prise en quittant Granite-house
- **Appears in:** III.11, III.12
