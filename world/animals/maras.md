# Maras: the large hare-sized rodents Top ran down

*Yellow, green-spotted, almost tailless rodents, a sort of agouti, that the colonists grill for supper.*

Keywords: mara, agouti, rongeur, rodent, lièvre, hare, grillades, nichée, litter, lapin d’Amérique, Top

- **What it is.**
  - They live in a litter; Top fell on one and killed three (I.12 ¶62). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le chien était tombé sur une nichée
  - Rodents larger than a hare, with a yellow coat mixed with greenish spots and only a rudimentary tail (I.12 ¶63). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dont la taille dépassait celle d’un lièvre
  - The colonists name them "maras", a sort of agouti, a little bigger than their tropical kin, true "lapins d’Amérique" with long ears and five molars on each side of the jaw (I.12 ¶64). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: C’étaient des « maras », sorte d’agoutis, un peu plus grands que leurs congénères des contrées tropicales
  - Nab and Pencroff grill them for the evening meal, and the party do them honour (I.12 ¶75). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: préparèrent lestement des grillades d’agoutis
- **Appears in:** I.12
- **Open:**
  - The text calls the animals "maras, sorte d’agoutis", yet says the five molars "les distingue précisément des agoutis"; the meal made of them is called "des grillades d’agoutis" (I.12 ¶75). Card neither as one name. (I.12 ¶64). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: sorte d’agoutis
    - quote: les distingue précisément des agoutis
