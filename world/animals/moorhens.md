# Moorhens: the poules d'eau of the lake shore

*Water birds of the banks and rock-islet of Lake Grant that come of themselves to the shore of the poultry-yard.*

Keywords: poule d’eau, moorhen, oiseau aquatique, lac Grant, basse-cour

- **What it is.**
  - Moorhens strut on the banks and the rock-islet of the lake, among wild ducks, pelicans, red-bills, honeyeaters and lyrebirds (I.12 ¶68). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des poules d’eau
  - Like the pelicans and kingfishers, they come of their own accord to the shore of the poultry-yard and settle in with the rest (II.7 ¶45). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: aux poules d’eau, ils vinrent d’eux-mêmes au rivage de la basse-cour
- **Appears in:** I.12, II.7
