# The hurdle: a woven-branch carrier for the limestone

*A hurdle of interlaced branches on which Nab and Pencroff carried limestone.*

Keywords: claie, wattle, sledge, carrier, basket, woven branches, barrow

- **What it is.**
  - It is a hurdle made of interlaced branches (I.13 ¶44). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: sur une claie faite de branchages entrelacés
  - Nab and Pencroff drag the seal-skin bellows and provisions on a hurdle to the ore on 20 April (I.15 ¶32). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: traînaient sur une claie la machine soufflante
  - Smith, Harbert and Spilett drag it up the river to the coal deposit to fetch pyrites (I.17 ¶37). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: traînant la claie et remontant la rivière
  - The hurdle was really too inconvenient, and a rough cart replaced it (see [the cart](the-cart.md)) (I.20 ¶29). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: l’ancienne claie vraiment trop incommode
- **Made from.**
  - The material is interlaced branches (I.13 ¶44). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: branchages entrelacés
- **Leads to.**
  - It carried several loads of limestone (see [the lime](lime.md)) (I.13 ¶44). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: plusieurs charges de carbonate de chaux
- **Appears in:** I.13, I.15, I.17, I.20
- **Open:**
  - The size of the hurdle is not given.
    - quote: sur une claie
