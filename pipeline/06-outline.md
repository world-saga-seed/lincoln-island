# 06 Outline: the arc, then each chapter

*Basic.*

**When:** after the checks, inside the horizon.

**Uses:** [scene-outlines](../workflows/scene-outlines.md) (prompt O) and [reader-tests](../workflows/reader-tests.md) (**write the spec:** the key) (Basic); as Refinements, [arc-pitch](../workflows/arc-pitch.md) (frames, tweens, density, the chapter list), [actor-log](../workflows/actor-log.md) (offstage timelines), [aims-sweep](../workflows/aims-sweep.md), the story questions, the [outline critics](../workflows/drafting/critics.md#outline-critics), and [ripple](../workflows/ripple.md) after a settled change.

**What happens:** each chapter gets marked beats, notes for drafting and its reader-test spec, and is graded **yes**, **thin** or **none** for key frames.

**Outputs:** the arc outline, chapter outlines and notes, actor logs, the clue register, `plan/reader-tests/`. Example: [the arc](../example/story/outline/1-spring-tides.md), [a chapter](../example/story/outline/00400-the-forty-minutes.md), [the key](../example/plan/reader-tests/key.md).

**Done when:** every chapter in the horizon has a marked outline and notes, and a key-frame grade.
