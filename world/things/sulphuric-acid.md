# The sulphuric acid: acid distilled from the iron sulphate on 20 May

*Sulphuric acid got twelve days after beginning, by calcining the iron-sulphate crystals in closed vessels, for the nitric acid; later used with lime to free the fatty acids for candles.*

Keywords: acide sulfurique, sulphuric acid, vitriol, Nordhausen, Bohemia, distillation, bougies, candles

- **What it is.**
  - Smith means to extract it from the iron sulphate obtained from the pyrites (I.17 ¶39). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: on en extrairait l’acide sulfurique
  - It is one of the most used agents, and a nation’s industrial importance can be measured by the consumption of it; Smith will later find it of extreme use for candles and for tanning skins, but at this moment reserves it for another use (I.17 ¶40). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: L’acide sulfurique est un des agents les plus employés
    - quote: l’ingénieur le réservait à un autre emploi
  - Industrially it needs costly plant, but in Bohemia it is made by simpler means that also give a more concentrated acid, the acid called Nordhausen (I.17 ¶52). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: en Bohême particulièrement, on fabrique l’acide sulfurique par des moyens plus simples
    - quote: l’acide connu sous le nom d’acide de Nordhausen
  - Smith had the lime and the sulphuric acid he needed for the candles (I.20 ¶18). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il avait de la chaux et de l’acide sulfurique
  - Concentrated sulphuric acid, mixed with ordinary nitric acid, does the work of fuming nitric acid in making pyroxyle (II.8 ¶13). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: d’acide sulfurique concentré
- **Made from.**
  - He had only to calcine the crystals of iron sulphate in a closed vessel, so that the acid distilled in vapours and condensed (see [the iron sulphate](iron-sulphate.md)) (I.17 ¶53). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: calciner en vase clos les cristaux de sulfate de fer
  - The refractory pottery held the crystals and the furnace distilled the acid (see [the refractory pottery](refractory-pottery.md)) (I.17 ¶54). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les poteries réfractaires
  - The operation succeeded and on 20 May, twelve days after beginning, Smith had the acid (I.17 ¶54). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le 20 mai, douze jours après avoir commencé
- **Leads to.**
  - It attacks the saltpetre to give the nitric acid (see [the nitric acid](nitric-acid.md)) (I.17 ¶55). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le salpêtre, attaqué par l’acide sulfurique, lui donna précisément cet acide par distillation
  - In the candle-making it decomposes the lime soap, precipitating the lime as sulphate and freeing the fatty acids (see [the candles](candles.md)) (I.20 ¶23). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: facile à décomposer par l’acide sulfurique
  - With sulphuric acid alone Smith could have heated the neutral fats and isolated the glycerine (see [the glycerine](glycerine.md)) (I.20 ¶23). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: qu’en chauffant cet acide avec les corps gras neutres
- **Appears in:** I.17, I.20, II.8
