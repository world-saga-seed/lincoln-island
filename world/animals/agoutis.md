# Agoutis: the rodents of the stew-pot and the grill

*Rodents counted among the island's small game, grilled or stewed, and shot with arrows.*

Keywords: agouti, rongeur, rodent, mara, grillade, pot-au-feu, gibier, game, flèche

- **What it is.**
  - The text calls the maras "sorte d’agoutis"; their five molars on each side "les distingue précisément des agoutis" (I.12 ¶64). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: sorte d’agoutis
    - quote: ce qui les distingue précisément des agoutis
  - Nab and Pencroff make "grillades d’agoutis", to which the party do honour (I.12 ¶75). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: grillades d’agoutis
  - Agoutis are among the hair-and-feather game that abounds at the Chimneys, taken with arrows in the Jacamar Wood on the left bank of the Mercy (I.13 ¶36). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: cabiais, pigeons, agoutis, coqs de bruyère
  - Nab prepares an agouti stew ("pot-au-feu d’agouti") for the first supper in the central room (I.13 ¶57). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Nab avait préparé un pot-au-feu d’agouti
  - Agoutis are among the animal stores renewed after the storm (I.22 ¶37), and fall to the arrows of Harbert and Gédéon Spilett (I.22 ¶57). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: lapins de garenne, agoutis et kangourous
    - quote: agoutis, kangourous, cabiais, pigeons
  - Agoutis are among the game of the first forest slopes near Granite-house (II.1 ¶36), and some are seen on the march to the west coast (II.4 ¶8). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: d’agoutis, de cabiais, de kangourous
    - quote: des agoutis, des kangourous et autres rongeurs
- **Appears in:** I.12, I.13, I.22, II.1, II.4
- **Open:**
  - The text calls the maras "sorte d’agoutis" yet says the five molars "distingue précisément" them from agoutis, and calls the meal of maras "grillades d’agoutis" (I.12 ¶64, ¶75). Card neither as one name; see maras.md. *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: sorte d’agoutis
    - quote: ce qui les distingue précisément des agoutis
    - quote: grillades d’agoutis
