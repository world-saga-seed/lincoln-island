## a

After: “This was the 18th of March, 1855(1).

By this date Ayrton had been on the islet eleven years and seven months when Herbert found him in October of 1866. The colonists speak of twelve years then and in the year after (chapters XXXVIII, XLI and XLIII), which is near enough. But in March of 1869 the yacht comes for him “after twelve years of expiation” (chapter LXII), and by this date that would be fourteen. The date and the three earlier passages agree; the one in chapter LXII stands alone, and I take the date against it. The colonists’ own note (1), below, remarks on a discordance in the dates of this story, and says the true dates could not at first be given. It gives no other date, and I have none to offer.
