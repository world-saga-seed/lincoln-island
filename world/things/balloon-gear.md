# The balloon's gear: the net, the retaining ring and the anchor saved with the envelope

*The net with its ropes and cords, the retaining ring and the anchor, lowered from the pine on 2 November with the envelope, stored at port Ballon and brought to Granite-house by cart.*

Keywords: filet, net, cercle de retenue, hoop, ancre, anchor, cordages, ropes, agrès, rigging, balloon, aérostat

- **What it is.**
  - It comprises the net, that is a considerable lot of ropes and cords, the retaining circle and the balloon's anchor, all brought to the ground with the envelope (II.5 ¶89). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le filet, c’est-à-dire un lot considérable de cordages et de cordes, le cercle de retenue et l’ancre du ballon
  - It is heavy, a load of cloth, ropes and cordage whose weight is considerable (II.5 ¶94). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: cette charge de toile, de cordes, de cordages, dont le poids était considérable
  - It is stored with the envelope in a rock cavity at port Ballon (see [the balloon store](../places/balloon-store.md)) (II.5 ¶94, ¶96). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: tout était emmagasiné
  - The canoe could not carry it, so the cart is to come back for it (II.5 ¶96). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le canot n’eût pu suffire à le transporter
  - Carried with the envelope to Granite-house by cart at the end of December, as the "divers agrès de l’aérostat" (II.7 ¶63). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: l’enveloppe et les divers agrès de l’aérostat
- **Appears in:** II.5, II.7
