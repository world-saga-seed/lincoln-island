# S6: II.3 ¶70

¶70

It was about five o'clock. The last rays of the sun glided beneath the thick branches and struck obliquely upon the little fall, whose damp spray glittered with the colours of the prism. Beyond, the bed of the Mercy disappeared under the thickets, where it was fed by some hidden spring. The various streams which flowed into it along its course made of it, lower down, a true river, but here it was no more than a limpid brook, without depth.
