# 02 Interview

*Basic.*

**When:** after the intake; one or two sessions.

**Uses:** [interview/README.md](../interview/README.md) (the rules) and [interview/questions.md](../interview/questions.md) (the script).

**What happens:** questions by section, each answer a ruling on the ledger, each section read back ([the rules](../interview/README.md#the-rules-of-the-interview)).

**Outputs:** `plan/interview.md`; ledger rows; the first lines of `CLAUDE.md`'s "About the writer", the voice sheet and the arc outline. Example: [the interview record](../example/interview-record.md).

**Done when:** every section was read back and agreed; open questions are held in `plan/state.md`.
