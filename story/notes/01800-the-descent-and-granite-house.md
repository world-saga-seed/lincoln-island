# The Descent into the Old Outlet and Granite-House (notes)

## Notes for drafting

- **Where it sits.** The French I.18, 21 May after the blast. The colonists find the old outlet bare, descend by torchlight to a great cavern, learn the well's depth, pierce the front wall and name the cavern Granite-house. The party climbs back before nightfall. *(Drafted.)*
- **Cast.** *(Drafted.)* Cyrus Smith, Harbert, Nab, Pencroff (¶1), Gédéon Spilett (¶23), Top (¶4). Nothing else living is found: "pas un animal, pas un être vivant" (¶42).
- **Places.** *(Drafted.)*
  - The new outflow on the plateau (¶3); the Chimneys (¶4); the lake's point and the old outlet's mouth, now bare (¶12–¶14).
  - The outlet passage: about a hundred feet to the small upper cave (¶22), about fifty feet further (¶35), then sixty feet lower to the great cavern (¶40). The well in the cavern's end (¶47). The front wall, three feet thick, eighty feet above the ground (¶65–¶66).
  - The cavern: left part thirty feet high and wide by a hundred long; right part over eighty feet high (¶67).
- **Things.** *(Drafted.)* Picks, iron-shod spears, fibre ropes, a lighter and tinder (¶4); resinous torches (¶18); a rope tying the party (¶19); the well, about ninety feet to the water (¶49); the pick that fell through the wall (¶63). Footnote n1 glosses "house" (¶72).
- **Time.** *(Drafted.)* The same day as the blast, 21 May *(My reading, to confirm)*: the party goes at once (¶4); the opening is widened "en moins d’une heure" (¶14); piercing takes two hours (¶63); they come out "un peu avant quatre heures" (¶83); the work is put off "au lendemain" (¶73). Top's barking a quarter of an hour before (¶54).
- **Clues.** *(Drafted.)*
  - Top's rage and what he found; Cyrus murmurs that Top knows more (¶56).
    - quote: Top en sait plus long que nous sur bien des choses
  - The cavern is empty; something may have lived there and fled by the well (¶52–¶53).
    - quote: l’être quelconque, amphibie ou autre, s’est enfui par cette issue
  - The well to the sea and the underground conduit under the beach (¶74), where no sound comes up.
    - quote: le conduit souterrain qui se prolongeait sous la grève
  - The passage "datait de l’origine même de l’île" and bears traces of an eruptive work (¶19).
    - quote: les traces d’un travail éruptif
  - Cyrus stays silent and absorbed at the well (¶75).
    - quote: l’ingénieur, immobile, l’oreille attentive, le regard plongé dans le gouffre
  - Cephalopod fears (¶20).
- **Length.** 83 paragraphs (¶1–¶83), 2,909 words in the ¶ lines. *(Drafted.)*

## Open and flags

- The lake's fall: I.17 ¶17 "trois pieds"; here "deux pieds, au moins". Both quoted, neither carded. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: le niveau du lac aurait baissé de deux pieds, au moins
- The hour of coming out: "un peu avant quatre heures" (¶83) against I.17 ¶68, the hole "vers quatre heures du soir" and the explosion twenty-five minutes after. Both quoted, neither carded. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: un peu avant quatre heures
- The depths. The cavern floor is "quatre-vingt-dix pieds au-dessus du niveau de la mer" (¶50); the opening in the wall is "quatre-vingts pieds" above the ground (¶66); I.19 ¶18 says "deux cents pieds par le couloir". The passage measures 100 + 50 + 60 feet (¶22, ¶35, ¶40) and Harbert says they are at "un tiers de la hauteur" (¶28); the text does not total them. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: quatre-vingts pieds
- The narrator's "Granite-house" is introduced with a footnote and the French form is kept (¶72, n1). *(Drafted.)*
- "Celui de ses compagnons qui se fût approché de lui l’eût entendu" (¶55): Cyrus's words are not meant for the others. *(Drafted.)*
- Pencroff's "Monsieur Smith" (¶77) is the only time in the chapter he does not say "monsieur Cyrus". *(My reading, to confirm.)*
