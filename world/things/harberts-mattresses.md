# Harbert’s mattresses: the mattresses he lies on at the corral and on the way back

*The mattresses on which Harbert rests at the corral, laid in the cart for the return and then on a litter of branches.*

Keywords: matelas, mattresses, Harbert, chariot, civière, transport

- **What it is.**
  - They are the mattresses on which Harbert lies at the corral; Smith and Spilett lift them and lay them on the floor of the cart between the two side-rails (see [the cart](the-cart.md)) (III.9 ¶72, ¶73). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les matelas sur lesquels reposait Harbert
    - quote: entre les deux ridelles
  - At the river’s bend, branches arranged as a stretcher receive the mattresses, Harbert lying fainted on them (see [the litter](litter.md)) (III.10 ¶2). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: quelques branches, disposées en forme de civière, reçurent les matelas sur lesquels reposait Harbert évanoui
- **Appears in:** III.9, III.10
