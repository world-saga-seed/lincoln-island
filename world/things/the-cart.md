# The cart: the crude cart that replaced the hurdle

*A rough cart that Nab and Pencroff trundle to the oyster bed and, later, to the coal seam.*

Keywords: chariot, cart, wagon, hauling, hurdle, claie, transport

- **What it is.**
  - It is a sort of crude cart that replaced the old hurdle, which was really too awkward (see [the hurdle](hurdle.md)) (I.20 ¶29). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une sorte de grossier chariot, qui remplaçait l’ancienne claie vraiment trop incommode
  - It is kept for fetching what is too heavy to carry back from the shore (II.1 ¶72). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: nous reviendrons le reprendre avec le chariot
  - It is heavy and coarse and has to be dragged by hand, which makes it less handy than the pirogue for a long trip (II.3 ¶3). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ce chariot, lourd et grossier, il fallait le traîner
  - Smith counts on a cart in better condition than the first, to carry loads to the Red Creek's sources, especially if a draught animal can be captured (II.7 ¶7). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: avec un chariot mieux conditionné que le premier, les charrois seraient plus faciles
  - The colonists work to make the heavy cart handier and lighter (II.7 ¶48). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: rendre leur lourd chariot plus maniable et plus léger
  - It is ready to be harnessed at the end of December, with harness and traces of plant fibre (see [the harness](harness.md)) (II.7 ¶61). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le chariot était prêt à être attelé
  - Aided by the onaggas, it makes the road to the corral a walk of five miles, and brings wood and coal (II.8 ¶14, ¶31). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: On véhicula également de notables quantités de bois et de charbon
    - quote: les onaggas aidant, ce n’était plus qu’une promenade de cinq milles
  - A kind of light carriole has replaced the cart, and makes frequent trips to the corral (II.9 ¶63). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le chariot, ou plutôt une sorte de carriole légère qui l’avait remplacé
    - quote: faisait-elle de fréquents voyages au corral
  - Jup, taken along by Pencroff, drives it, cracking his whip, with his usual intelligence (II.9 ¶63). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: il emmenait Jup et le faisait conduire, ce dont Jup, faisant claquer son fouet, s’acquittait avec son intelligence habituelle
  - The onaggas are harnessed to it to carry wood and coal to Granite-house and the mineral products that Smith uses (II.18 ¶54). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: on les attelait aussi au chariot, soit pour transporter à Granite-house le bois et la houille
  - The expedition’s cart is to be loaded with camp effects and utensils to ease the halts, but one of the onaggas is lame from a leg wound and cannot be harnessed (III.6 ¶2). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: charger le chariot de divers effets de campement et d’ustensiles
    - quote: un des onaggas, blessé à la jambe, ne pouvait être attelé
  - It can be drawn by a single onagga (III.6 ¶7). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: emmenant le chariot, attelé d’un seul onagga
- **Appears in:** I.20, I.22, II.1, II.3, II.5, II.7, II.8, II.9, II.18, III.6
- **Open:**
  - Who made the cart, of what, and when is not said (I.20 ¶29). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une sorte de grossier chariot
