# Phase 5: the candidate tells

*A candidate tell is a way the renders depart from the French, or from White's 1876 voice, that White did not: a habit a modern model brings that an 1876 translator would not. For the kit's tell lists. Every quote resolves in the render or in `texts/fr-1875/` (checked by the main session).*

## What the renders did not do

The facts critic found no addition, loss, change or softening in any of the 20 renders, and a calibration run (five errors planted, `calibration-plants.md`) found all five and nothing else, so the clean result holds for errors of that size. The period critic found no word, sense or science later than 1876.
- **A1, the winter digression** (III.14 ¶30 to ¶41): no Coriolis, no conveyor belt, no circumpolar current, no later science; Verne's own errors (the earth at a focus of the ellipse; "apogee" and "perigee" for the earth's orbit) kept, not corrected.
- **A6, from the French alone** (I.15 ¶26): "a blowing-machine", no "huge pair of bellows". The render did not add what White added.
- **The politics** (A4, P1, P2): kept at the French's force where White softens: "put perhaps under the ban of humanity" (White: "a scourge to humanity"); "the great revolt of the Sepoys" (White: "mutiny"); "Captain Nemo did well to die!" (White: "And Captain Nemo is dead!").
- **The figures** (A2, A3, A5): the barometer, the longitude sum with the Greenwich note, "more than eighty head" and the dates 1854, 1852, 1855, as the French gives them; White has 24 animals, 1861 and 1832.

## Not tells

- **The names.** The renders use the French forms (Harbert, Gédéon, Nab) where White has Herbert, Gideon and Neb, because the renderer's prompt said to. A question for the author (the insert's names), not a habit of the model.
- **Learned words** the script flagged ("hyperborean", "ineluctable", "stercoraries", "cosmographical", "serpentary"): all period, by Webster or older use; the script missed them through Webster's OCR.
