# The soap: the soluble soap that came with the glycerine

*A soluble soap from treating dugong fat with soda, fit for household cleaning.*

Keywords: savon, soap, soluble, cleaning, soda, fat

- **What it is.**
  - Smith treats the dugong fat with soda, which gives on one side a soluble soap and on the other the neutral substance glycerine (I.17 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un savon soluble, et, de l’autre, cette substance neutre, la glycérine
  - Soda gives a soluble soap that finds its use in household cleaning, where lime would give only insoluble, useless lime soaps (I.17 ¶45). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un savon soluble, qui trouverait son emploi dans les nettoyages domestiques
    - quote: ne devait donner que des savons calcaires, insolubles et par conséquent inutiles
  - The stock of soap is exhausted by 3 December, so that Smith and the reporter are making soda at the Chimneys (see [the soda](soda.md)) (II.16 ¶99). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la provision de savon étant épuisée
- **Appears in:** I.17, II.16
