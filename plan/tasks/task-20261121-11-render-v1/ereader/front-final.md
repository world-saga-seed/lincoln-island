# About this book

This is a short stretch of Jules Verne's *The Mysterious Island* (*L'Île mystérieuse*, Hetzel, 1875), chapters XIX and XX of the first part, with one chapter inserted between them.

It reads in this order:

1. **XIX, in White's translation** (Stephen W. White, Philadelphia, 1876), as he printed it.
2. **XIX, rendered anew** from Verne's French, in White's voice, with nothing cut.
3. **XIX bis, the missing chapter.** It has no French original and no translation by White.
4. **XX, rendered anew** from the French.
5. **XX, in White's translation.**

Read 2 to 4 straight through as the book. 1 and 5 are there to compare.
