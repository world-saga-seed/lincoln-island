YOUR QUESTION, for each render, paragraph by paragraph: where does the render say something other than the French?
- **added:** the render says what the French does not (an explanation, a figure, a cause, a feeling, a modern idea);
- **lost:** the French says what the render leaves out (a clause, a figure, a name, a qualification, a note);
- **changed:** a fact, figure, date, name or relation differs;
- **softened:** the render lowers (or raises) the force of the French on politics, war, nation, race, religion, violence or feeling. Passages A4 (Nemo's life: England, Dakkar, the sepoy revolt, the Southerners), P1 (the civil war) and P2 (Nemo's death) are checked with special care here: Verne's politics stay Verne's, his words and their force.
For each finding, say also whether White does the same thing at that point (yes, no, or partly), quoting White.

Ordinary choices of English that keep the sense are not findings. Give a finding only where you can quote both the render and the French that show it.
