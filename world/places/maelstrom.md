# The Maelstrom: whirlpool off the coast of Norway

*The whirlpool where the Nautilus was caught and from which the three prisoners fled in its boat.*

Keywords: whirlpool, Norway, Loffoden, Lofoten, fugitives, escape

- **What it is.**
  - The Nautilus was drawn along the coasts of Norway into the eddies of the Maelstrom (III.16 ¶58). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: entraîné sur les côtes de Norwége, dans les tourbillons du Maëlstrom
  - Nemo believed the fugitives had drowned in its whirlpools (III.16 ¶58). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: noyés dans ces effroyables remous
- **On the page.**
  - The escape took place on 22 June 1867 (III.16 ¶58). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: le 22 juin 1867
  - The fugitives were cast ashore and taken in by fishermen of the Loffoden islands (III.16 ¶58). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: que des pêcheurs des îles Loffoden les avaient recueillis
- **Appears in:** III.16
- **Open:**
  - The chapter refers to the earlier book by its title. The editor warns that its dates disagree with the tale told here. No earlier book is drawn on.
    - quote: Vingt mille Lieues sous les mers
    - quote: sur la discordance de quelques dates
