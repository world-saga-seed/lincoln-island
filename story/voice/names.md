# White's names: every proper name of the French, in White's form

*Every proper name the French uses in the whole book, in White's form as he prints it in `texts/white-1876/`, with his count in Part I (his chapters I to XXII) and in his whole text, and the first French chapter that names it. The cards keep the French. Where a card's name and this list differ, the page takes this list. Extends `story/voice/names.md` (Settled R1121-20, R1121-107); its rows are kept and recounted here.*

*How the counts were made: each form is searched in the 62 chapter files of `texts/white-1876/` (summary lines left out). A count is the number of times the string stands in the text, case as written (a form in lowercase, such as “the engineer”, is counted with its capital at a sentence's start). A shorter form's count includes the longer forms that contain it (Smith holds Cyrus Smith and Mr. Smith) unless the cell says otherwise. Where a row has several forms, the Part I and Whole cells give the counts in the same order, joined by semicolons. White's chapters are cited by his number in Roman figures (I to LXII); the First column is the French chapter (I.1 to III.20). “none in White” marks a name White prints nowhere: the plain English is a *(Proposal.)*. The count of French names is of rows, not of strings.*

*Corrections to the earlier list: “Safety Island in Part I, Safety Islet later” was wrong, White prints Safety Island in XI and XXII and Safety Islet in XIV and LXII, and “the islet” most often; “the Chimneys” is 83 and 112 and “Jacamar Wood” and “Woods” are counted apart; the missing “Whole” cells for Cyrus Smith's “the engineer”, Herbert, Neb, Pencroff and “the warren” are filled. The cards' names that the French text never uses (Aronnax, Conseil, Ned Land, Nicolas Pesce, the Candiotes, the Aleutians, Candie) are not in this list: no French chapter names them.*
