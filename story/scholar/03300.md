## a

After: they could see the winter of 1866-67 coming on without fear.

The winter meant is the one coming on “with this month of June”, at the head of this chapter, and on this island the winter falls in June, July and August, inside the one year. The year is 1866, which chapter XXX begins. Chapter XXXIV calls this the colonists’ “second winter”, their first having been that of 1865. A winter of 1866-67 would be the one after it. I take the winter of 1866.
