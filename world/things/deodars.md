# The deodars: Himalayan cedars among the riverside conifers

*Trees Harbert names in the evergreen forest, common in the Himalayan zone and aromatic.*

Keywords: déodars, cedar, Himalayan, conifer, aroma, evergreen

- **What it is.**
  - Harbert recognised among the conifers more particularly "déodars" (I.4 ¶29). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Le jeune naturaliste reconnut plus particulièrement des « déodars »
  - The kind is very numerous in the Himalayan zone (I.4 ¶29). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: essences très-nombreuses dans la zone himalayenne
  - They give off an agreeable aroma (I.4 ¶29). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: qui répandaient un agréable arôme
  - The forest's conifers include deodars, already recognised by Harbert (I.6 ¶27). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: tels que les déodars, déjà reconnus par Harbert
  - It is among the trees of the forest cut through on 31 October, most of which were already known round the lake and the Grande-Vue plateau, generally of medium size because their number has hindered their growth (II.4 ¶5). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des déodars
    - quote: généralement de taille médiocre, car leur nombre avait nui à leur développement
  - In October, at the edge of the wood, the new foliage of the nettle trees, banksias and deodars appears among the persistent foliage of the conifers (II.20 ¶80). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: au milieu du feuillage persistant des conifères qui formaient la lisière du bois, apparaissait déjà le feuillage nouveau des micocouliers, des banksias et des deodars
- **Appears in:** I.4, I.6, II.4, II.20
