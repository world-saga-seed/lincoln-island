# Parrots: the many-coloured birds of the creek trees

*Parrots and parakeets of every shade, with the green "rois" and the blue lories, in the boughs along Red Creek.*

Keywords: perroquet, perruche, parrot, parakeet, loris, lory, roi, blues-mountains, plumage, Creek-Rouge

- **What it is.**
  - Parrots and parakeets, with plumage shaded in every colour, fill the boughs along Red Creek (I.12 ¶43). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: perroquets et perruches, au plumage nuancé de toutes les couleurs
  - Among them are "rois", of brilliant green crowned with red, blue lories, and "blues-mountains" (I.12 ¶43). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: « rois », d’un vert éclatant et couronnés de rouge, loris bleus, « blues-mountains »
  - They seem to be seen only through a prism (I.12 ¶43). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: semblaient ne se laisser voir qu’à travers un prisme
  - Parrots ("perroquets") are among the birds that swarm on the forest strip of the west coast, where there is no tree without a nest (II.4 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: tétras, loris, perroquets, kakatoès
    - quote: Pas un arbre qui n’eût un nid
- **Appears in:** I.12, II.4
