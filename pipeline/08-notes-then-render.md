# 08 Notes, then render

*Basic.*

**When:** once the horizon passes the gate and the writer says "draft".

**Uses:** **[workflows/drafting/](../workflows/drafting/README.md)**: [the gate](../workflows/drafting/README.md#the-gate), [rulings.md](../workflows/drafting/rulings.md) (fill in the house style), [drafter.md](../workflows/drafting/drafter.md) and [its prompt](../workflows/drafting/prompts/drafter.md), [principles.md](../workflows/drafting/principles.md#5-calibrated-before-it-is-trusted) (calibrate the critics); and [character-sheet](../workflows/character-sheet.md), [habits](../workflows/habits.md).

**What happens:** the drafter's notes are made ready, the critics calibrated, and the chapters drafted in batches ([drafter.md](../workflows/drafting/drafter.md#batches)).

**Outputs:** drafted chapters; each drafter's report in the batch paper.

**Done when:** each chapter in the batch is at **drafted** in `plan/state.md`, its banned-word hits fixed.
