# Lava: the lava of Mont Franklin, in streaks and flows

*The island's lava: striped traces on the slopes, ashes and pozzolana before the flows, flows poured chiefly to the north.*

Keywords: laves, coulées, pozzolane, cendres, scories, volcanic ash, flows

- **What it is.**
  - On certain slopes traces of lava, capriciously striped, are often seen (I.10 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des traces de laves, très-capricieusement striées
  - Before the flows lie pozzolana of irregular, strongly roasted grains and whitish ashes made of countless little feldspar crystals (I.10 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: pouzzolanes à grains irréguliers et fortement torréfiés, cendres blanchâtres faites d’une infinité de petits cristaux feldspathiques
  - The air-worn stripes sometimes offered too little hold, so that one slid on the flows (I.10 ¶44). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: l’on glissait sur les coulées de laves
  - Lava debris is mixed with the sand and shells of the north coast (I.14 ¶38). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: mélangés de débris de laves
  - The old zebra-stripes of lava on the causeway that frames the Shark gulf show that the last eruption dates from a very remote time (III.18 ¶75). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: les anciennes zébrures des laves
    - quote: la dernière éruption ne remontât à une époque très-éloignée
  - The lava vaporises the creeks in its path and sets the trees on fire, their sap turning to steam and bursting them like fireworks, though some less moist trees stay whole amid the flood (III.19 ¶37, ¶38). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: en vaporisant ces deux cours d’eau sur leur passage
    - quote: les fit éclater comme des boîtes d’artifice
    - quote: d’autres, moins humides, restèrent intacts au milieu de l’inondation
  - As the lower layers harden, other boiling sheets cover them at once (III.19 ¶39). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dès que les couches inférieures des laves s’étaient durcies, d’autres nappes bouillonnantes les recouvraient aussitôt
  - Falling into the lake, the first lavas solidify at once and pile up until they emerge, the lava sliding over them turning to stone in its turn; a jetty of rock forms and fills the lake (III.19 ¶54). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: se solidifièrent immédiatement et s’accumulèrent de manière à émerger bientôt
    - quote: Une jetée se forma de la sorte
  - In the first week of March thousands of threads of glass, made of fluid lava, fall like rain on the ground (III.19 ¶78). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Des milliers de fils de verre, faits de laves fluides, tombèrent comme une pluie sur le sol
