# Timeline fragment D (I.8 to I.11, chapters 00800 to 01100)

The year 1865 rests on I.11 ¶92. The day numbers of I.8 and I.9 rest on I.9 ¶30 ("28 mars"). Where two lines disagree both are given and flagged.

## I.8 (00800)

- 1865-03-26, dawn: Nab leaves the Chimneys to search the coast (I.8 ¶17). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: après avoir quitté les Cheminées dès l’aube
- 1865-03-26, about 5 p.m.: Nab sees footprints on the sand (I.8 ¶21). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: hier, vers cinq heures du soir, je remarquai sur le sable des empreintes de pas
- 1865-03-26, nightfall, about five minutes after Nab starts following the prints: Nab hears Top barking and is led to Cyrus (I.8 ¶27). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: Cinq minutes après, comme la nuit se faisait
- 1865-03-26, the day of the storm, the previous day to the chapter's action (I.8 ¶90). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: l’effroyable tempête de la veille
- 1865-03-27, about 9:20 a.m. onward: the litter is made from the time they leave for the dune; it is ten o'clock on their return, forty minutes later (I.8 ¶50). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: Ce fut l’affaire de quarante minutes environ, et il était dix heures
- 1865-03-27, about noon: Cyrus stands and the party sets out (I.8 ¶83). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: Vers midi
- 1865-03-27, about 2 p.m.: after two hours of road Cyrus falls asleep on the litter (I.8 ¶87). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: après deux heures de route, la fatigue l’emporta
- 1865-03-27, 5:30 p.m.: the party reaches the "pan coupé" and the Chimneys, and finds the fire out (I.8 ¶88, ¶94). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: À cinq heures et demie, la petite troupe arrivait au pan coupé

## I.9 (00900)

  - quote: vers dix heures du matin
- 1865-03-28, about 11 a.m.: after an hour's march, no game yet (I.9 ¶88). *(My reading, to confirm.)*

- 1865-03-28, before noon: Harbert finds the pine nuts (I.9 ¶97). *(My reading, to confirm.)*

- 1865-03-28, about 2 p.m.: Pencroff, carrying the capybara, judges the hour by the sun (I.9 ¶116). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: il devait être environ deux heures
- 1865-03-28, about 2:30 p.m.: the hunters reach the river bend, then see smoke (I.9 ¶117, ¶121). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: Une demi-heure après, ils arrivaient au coude de la rivière.

  - quote: Quelques instants après, les trois chasseurs se trouvaient devant un foyer pétillant
- 1865-03-28, evening and night: supper, then sleep (I.10 ¶15, ¶18). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: Aucun incident ne troubla cette nuit paisible
- 1865-03-29, morning: they wake (I.10 ¶18). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: le lendemain, — 29 mars, — frais et dispos
- 1865-03-29, 7:30 a.m.: the party leaves the Chimneys (I.10 ¶20). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Il était sept heures et demie du matin
- 1865-03-29, 9 a.m.: the western edge of the forest (I.10 ¶20). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: à neuf heures, Cyrus Smith et ses compagnons atteignaient la lisière occidentale de la forêt
- 1865-03-29, 10 a.m.: halt at the forest's edge; the mountain seen (I.10 ¶22). *(Drafted.)*

- 1865-03-29, 1 p.m.: the climb resumes (I.10 ¶32). *(Drafted.)*

- 1865-03-29, about 6 p.m. to 6:30 p.m., almost night: arrival on the first plateau after "sept heures" of ascent (I.10 ¶44). The text gives no clock time; the hour is the line's reading of "presque nuit" with ¶46. *(My reading, to confirm.)*

- 1865-03-29, before 6:30 p.m.: supper over (I.10 ¶46). *(Drafted.)*

- 1865-03-29, about 7 p.m. to 7:40 p.m.: Cyrus and Harbert walk twenty minutes along the plateau, then climb the crater (I.10 ¶49, ¶53). The text gives no start time. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: après une marche de vingt minutes
- 1865-03-29, nearly 8 p.m.: Cyrus and Harbert reach the summit (I.10 ¶56). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Il était près de huit heures
- 1865-03-29, a little after 8 p.m.: the crescent moon sets in the west and Cyrus says "Une île !" (I.10 ¶59, ¶61). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: C’était le croissant délié de la lune, déjà près de disparaître.

## I.11 (01100)

- 1865-03-29, about 8:30 p.m.: Cyrus and Harbert return to camp half an hour later (I.11 ¶1). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: Une demi-heure plus tard, Cyrus Smith et Harbert étaient de retour au campement.
- 1865-03-30, morning: breakfast, and the plan to climb again (I.11 ¶2). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Le lendemain, 30 mars
- 1865-03-30, about 7 a.m.: the five leave camp (I.11 ¶3). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Il devait être sept heures du matin environ
- 1865-03-30, before 8 a.m.: all are at the crater's top (I.11 ¶9). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Avant huit heures, Cyrus Smith et ses compagnons étaient réunis au sommet du cratère
- 1865-03-30, from about 8 a.m. to about 9 a.m.: an hour on the summit (I.11 ¶34). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: demeurèrent une heure ainsi au sommet de la montagne
- 1865-03-30, evening: the colonists talk of their country and the war; the island is named Lincoln earlier in the day (I.11 ¶89, ¶91). *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Et ce soir-là, avant de s’endormir
  - quote: Or, ceci se passait le 30 mars 1865
- 1865-03-31, the next day: Cyrus plans to fix the true north by the sunrise and sunset times (I.11 ¶83). Planned, not yet done. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
  - quote: Mais le lendemain, en prenant l’heure exacte du lever et du coucher du soleil
- 1865-04-15, "seize jours après": the narrator's date for the crime at Washington, with the text's "vendredi saint" (I.11 ¶92). *(My reading, to confirm.)* 30 March plus sixteen days is 15 April; Good Friday of 1865 was 14 April outside the text. Flagged, not reconciled; see the report.
  - quote: seize jours après, un crime effroyable serait commis à Washington
  - quote: le vendredi saint, Abraham Lincoln tomberait sous la balle d’un fanatique
