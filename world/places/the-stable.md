# The stable: the onaggas' shelter beside the poultry-yard

*A stable built near the poultry-yard, where the two onaggas find litter and a night refuge.*

Keywords: écurie, stable, onaggas, litière, basse-cour

- **What it is.**
  - The engineer has it built near the poultry-yard, so that the onaggas find a good litter and a refuge for the night (see [the poultry-yard](basse-cour.md)) (II.7 ¶59). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: l’ingénieur fit construire près de la basse-cour une écurie, dans laquelle les onaggas devaient trouver, avec une bonne litière, un refuge pendant la nuit
  - The onaggas, unharnessed, are led back to it after the day’s work (II.7 ¶64). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Les onaggas étaient dételés, puis ramenés à leur écurie
  - The onaggas’ stable is burnt by the convicts on 28 to 29 November, and is not rebuilt while the convicts are at large (III.10 ¶29; III.11 ¶20). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: à l’étable des onaggas, le feu avait tout détruit
    - quote: les écuries, Cyrus Smith préféra attendre
  - The "étables" built on the poultry-yard site now hold five onaggas, four vigorous and well trained, and a young one just born (III.14 ¶18). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: Les étables contenaient maintenant cinq onaggas, dont quatre vigoureux, bien dressés, se laissant atteler ou monter, et un petit qui venait de naître
  - The colonists run to the onaggas’ stable and harness the cart on the night of 23 to 24 January; its "étables" are destroyed by the lava in March (III.19 ¶30, ¶78). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: vers l’étable des onaggas
    - quote: des étables, il ne resta plus rien
- **Appears in:** II.7, II.15, II.17, III.10, III.11, III.14, III.19
- **Open:**
  - The pigs brought from Tabor are taken to "les étables" to acclimatise easily; whether these are the same shelter as the "écurie" of II.7 ¶59 is not said (II.15 ¶45). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ces animaux furent conduits aux étables, où ils devaient s’acclimater facilement
  - Whether the "étables" of the basse-cour (II.17 ¶3), of the corral (II.17 ¶21, ¶28) and the "écurie" of II.7 ¶59 are one shelter or several is not said (II.17 ¶3, ¶21, ¶28). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: un jour aux étables ou à la basse-cour
    - quote: le corral n’a que des étables
    - quote: à une vingtaine de pieds des étables
