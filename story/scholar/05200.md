## a

After: the engineer and the reporter passed that day, the 7th of December,

The first fit was on the 6th. The fever did not come back that night, “nor did it return the following day,” and was looked for on the morrow after; by that count the second fit fell on the 8th, and a fever that came back every second day would bring the third on the 10th. The dates here give the second fit on the 7th, the next day as the 8th, and the third awaited on the 9th. The dates agree with one another and the count with itself, and I find nothing that decides between them. I leave it open.
