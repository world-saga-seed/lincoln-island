## Questions for the author, each with a recommendation
1. The date discord (II.4 ends 31 Oct; II.5 ¶4 says "2 novembre"; II.7 ¶12-¶14 and the notes treat the bridge as begun "le lendemain, 3 novembre"). Recommendation: keep it on the Open line of the pirogue card and in the outlines; do not carry a date to any card from II.5 ¶4 without the ledger.
2. The balloon: the text calls its envelope cotton (II.5 ¶87) and Smith speaks of "un ballon à air chaud" (II.7 ¶47) against the gas of Part I. Recommendation: leave as the text gives it, both on Open.
3. The ladder in II.6 ¶1 ("premier palier, à mi-paroi") and ¶24 ("jusqu'au seuil"): II.6 ¶1 is a guess made in the dark, so it is not carded as a disagreement. Recommendation: keep only ¶24's reading on the rope-ladder card.
4. The Creek-Glycérine's ditch and the "coupée faite à la rive est du lac" of II.7 ¶18 against the "nouvelle saignée" of ¶39: the text may mean the first cut (the new fall of I.17) and the second; the cards read them so (the new fall; Creek-Glycérine). Recommendation: confirm.
5. The poultry-yard is placed at the lake bank "qui confinait à l'ancien déversoir" (II.7 ¶8) and then on the "rive sud-est" (¶44); not set side by side as a disagreement because the old outlet's place on the shore is not given. Recommendation: leave.
