# The elms: the francs-ormes of the Mercy’s right bank

*Fine elms on the right bank of the Mercy, prized by builders for lasting long in water.*

Keywords: ormes, francs-ormes, ulmacées, elm, builders, water, Mercy

- **What it is.**
  - Fine elms, the franc-ormes of the ulmaceae, stand on the Mercy’s right bank, much sought by builders for keeping long in water (II.3 ¶12). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des ulmacées, ces précieux francs-ormes, si recherchés des constructeurs
    - quote: qui ont la propriété de se conserver longtemps dans l’eau
  - Elms abound on the island, and the elm or the fir was weighed for the boat’s planking; fir was chosen, which stands water as well as elm (II.10 ¶4). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: L’orme ou le sapin, qui abondaient dans l’île
    - quote: qui supporte aussi bien que l’orme l’immersion dans l’eau
- **Appears in:** II.3, II.10
