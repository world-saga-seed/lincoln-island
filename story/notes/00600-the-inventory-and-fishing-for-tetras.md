## Open and flags

- **No knife.** ¶2: "pas même un couteau de poche"; ¶18 repeats "Ah ! que n’eût-il donné pour avoir un couteau !". No discord. *(Drafted.)*
- **Distance of the fall from the shore.** ¶8: "à quelques centaines de pas d’un rivage". I.7 ¶10 gives twelve hundred feet. Quoted both ways; card neither. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: noyé dans les flots, à quelques centaines de pas d’un rivage
- **"Fusil" and "munitions".** ¶12: "Nous trouverons des munitions sur notre route, et nous couperons notre fusil dans la forêt." No gun is made in the chapter. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: nous couperons notre fusil dans la forêt
- **Wind and the hunt.** ¶17: "la brise soufflait du sud-est"; the storm of I.7 is also from the south-east. For the weather's continuity. *(Drafted.)*
  - quote: Le temps menaçait, et la brise soufflait du sud-est.
- **Latitude.** ¶27's palm limits (the fortieth parallel north, the thirty-fifth south) are given as the limits of palms generally. *(Drafted.)* The Wikisource correction at I.12 turns on the island's latitude and concerns a later chapter; nothing here depends on it. *(Drafted.)*
- **Hunting ground.** The hunters follow the left bank up the river for a mile in the first hour (¶21), but at ¶37 and after the text does not say how far they have gone. *(Drafted.)*
