## Discords (neither carded)

1. The date at the promontory: II.3 ¶72 has the party at the falls on 31 October; II.4 is one day; II.5 names the next day 2 November. Counting days gives 1 November.
   - quote: le lendemain, 31 octobre, à cinq heures du matin, tous étaient sur pied, prêts à partir
   - quote: cette journée du 2 novembre
2. How long the canoe was left: left on 31 October, it was alone two days by 2 November; the line fits 1 November.
   - quote: La pirogue est restée seule pendant un jour aux sources de la Mercy
3. The moon, said on 30 October, gives 2 November, which agrees with the "2 novembre" reading.
   - quote: la lune devait être pleine trois jours après
4. The distance from Flotson-point: about three miles to the Mercy (II.2 ¶13, ¶42), four to Granite-house (II.5 ¶98).
   - quote: De la pointe de l’Épave à Granite-house, il restait encore quatre milles
5. Cyrus Smith's "deux heures" to the west coast (II.3 ¶66, II.4 ¶1) became a 10:30 arrival after a 6 a.m. start; II.4 ¶29 explains it.
6. The shot in the guns: small shot in II.4 ¶61, yet Spilett fires a carbine ball at the jaguar, and II.5 ¶25 says the guns are loaded with ball.
   - quote: Nous n’avons que du petit plomb dans nos fusils
7. The chest's books: the dictionaries and Bible are English; the atlas's names are French (II.2 ¶65 to ¶67).
8. The inventory in II.2 ¶53 prints "Instruments" twice and puts "1 jumelle" under the first; kept as printed. (The scan check, page 223, saw the start of this list.)
9. Who killed the jacamar: II.3 ¶24 says Pencroff's shot went off almost in spite of him; ¶28 credits Harbert.
