# S12: III.13 ¶3

¶3

The colonists, a prey to a thousand confused thoughts, and under the influence of a violent excitement, waited throughout the night, without leaving Ayrton's house, without returning to the spot where the bodies of the convicts lay. As to the circumstances in which these had met their death, it was probable that Ayrton could tell them nothing, since he did not himself know that he was in the corral house. But at least he would be in a position to relate the facts which had preceded this terrible execution.
