# The hibiscus heterophyllus: a mallow whose fibres make the bowstring

*A mallow giving fibres as tough as animal sinews, used for bowstrings.*

Keywords: hibiscus, mallow, cord, fibre, string, malvaceae

- **What it is.**
  - It is a plant of the mallow family (I.13 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: appartenant à la famille des malvacées
  - It gives fibres of remarkable toughness, comparable to animal tendons (I.13 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: qui fournit des fibres d’une ténacité remarquable, qu’on eût pu comparer à des tendons d’animaux
  - Nab made a net of hibiscus fibres (see [the net](fibre-net.md)) (I.14 ¶36). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une sorte de filet en fibres d’hibiscus, que Nab avait confectionné
  - It is among the trees of the forest cut through on 31 October, most of which were already known round the lake and the Grande-Vue plateau, generally of medium size because their number has hindered their growth (II.4 ¶5). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: des hibiscus
    - quote: généralement de taille médiocre, car leur nombre avait nui à leur développement
  - A few score fathoms of its light cord are kept in a Chimneys passage with the bows and arrows (II.6 ¶33). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: quelques vingtaines de brasses d’une légère corde d’hibiscus
- **On the page.**
  - It was the plant sought to make the string of the bow (I.13 ¶30). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une plante propre à former la corde de l’arc
- **Appears in:** I.13, I.14, II.4, II.6
