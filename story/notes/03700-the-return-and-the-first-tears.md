## Open and flags

- **The count of days.** ¶1 reads:
  - quote: Le lendemain, — 20 octobre, — à sept heures du matin, après quatre jours de voyage
  The boat left on 15 October at five in the morning (II.14 ¶93–¶94) and the fire was seen about two on the night of 18 to 19 (II.14 ¶102–¶105). "Le lendemain" of the 19th would be the 20th, but the fire was twenty miles off at two on the 19th. Card neither. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- **"Neuf jours".** ¶1 puts the landing on 20 October; ¶77 reads:
  - quote: Ce jour-là était le 30 octobre, et, par conséquent, il y avait neuf jours que le naufragé de l’île Tabor était prisonnier à Granite-house
  Counted from the 20th to the 30th is ten days. Card neither. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
- **The chapter summary.** The ¶0 summary of this chapter lists "La troisième récolte. Un moulin à vent. La première farine et le premier pain. Le dévouement de l’ingénieur." The harvest, the mill and the bread are in II.16 (03800), not here; the jaguar is in II.16 and the devotion is of the unknown, not "l’ingénieur". *(My reading, to confirm: the summary is the printer's argument for a chapter that was later divided. It is a fact of the text, not for the outline.)*
- **"Quelques mois à peine".** ¶24:
  - quote: il y a quelques mois à peine, ce malheureux était un homme comme vous et moi
  II.16 ¶40 has him twelve years on the islet. Card neither. *(Drafted.)*
- **"Anglais ou américain".** ¶38 takes him as English or American from the name Britannia; II.16 ¶33 he says he is English; II.17 ¶54 he is a Scot. Card neither. *(Drafted.)*
- **The "ne parle que d’un seul".** ¶30:
  - quote: il ne parle que d’un seul
  II.17 ¶44 has the Britannia document naming three survivors, but that is another document. *(My reading, to confirm.)*
