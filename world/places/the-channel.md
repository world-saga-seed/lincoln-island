# The channel: the half-mile strait between the islet and the coast

*A half-mile channel with a violent ebb current, forded at low tide at about five feet deep.*

Keywords: canal, chenal, strait, current, jusant, ford, swim, tide, low water

- **What it is.**
  - It is half a mile wide and separates the islet from the coast (I.3 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: séparés par un canal large d’un demi-mille
  - A very rapid current runs through it with a loud noise (I.3 ¶42). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: un courant extrêmement rapide se propageait avec bruit
  - Pencroff takes it for an ebb current, since the tide is falling on the sand (I.3 ¶47). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: c’est un courant de jusant
  - Pencroff judges that a swimmer could be carried out to sea by it (I.3 ¶47). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: entraînés au large par le courant
  - At low water most of the sands forming its bed are uncovered and only a narrow channel remains (I.3 ¶59). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Il ne restait entre l’îlot et la côte qu’un chenal étroit
  - The remaining channel is no deeper than five feet (I.3 ¶60). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dont la profondeur ne dépassait pas cinq pieds
  - The canal that the party followed on 16 April is read as this channel: the islet of Safety lies on its other side (I.14 ¶28). *(My reading, to confirm.)*
    - quote: De l’autre côté, sur l’îlot du Salut
  - At the lowest ebb it leaves a sort of fordable passage, crossed without wetting above the knee (I.15 ¶15). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: sans se mouiller plus haut que le genou
  - It can be crossed back only when the sea has ebbed again (I.15 ¶29). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Les colons durent attendre que la mer eût rebaissé
- **Appears in:** I.3, I.14, I.15
