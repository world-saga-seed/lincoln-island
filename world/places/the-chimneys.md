# The Chimneys: the colonists' first shelter, with sand corridors

*The first lodging of the five colonists: corridors floored with sand, a central chamber, a hearth.*

Keywords: Cheminées, camp, shelter, lodging, cave, bedding, moss, hearth

- **What it is.**
  - It is a heap of enormous rocks fallen at the river mouth, above the reach of high tide, not a cave (I.4 ¶23). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: non point une grotte, mais un entassement d’énormes rochers
  - Its corridors are sanded, and light reaches them through gaps left between the granite blocks, some of which stand by a miracle of balance (I.4 ¶24). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: dans ces couloirs sablés, auxquels la lumière ne manquait pas
  - Wind and cold enter with the light, a true corridor draught (I.4 ¶24). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: une vraie bise de corridors
  - Its plan is shaped like the sign & (I.4 ¶24). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Leur plan géométrique représentait ce signe typographique &
  - Pencroff kept the name "Chimneys" for this temporary dwelling (I.4 ¶27). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: ce fut le nom que Pencroff conserva à cette demeure provisoire
  - It was closed up with sand, stones, interlaced branches and wet earth, shutting the galleries open to the south wind and cutting off the upper loop of the & (I.5 ¶1). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Du sable, des pierres, des branches entrelacées, de la terre mouillée bouchèrent hermétiquement les galeries
  - One narrow winding gut opening on the side was left to carry the smoke out and draw the hearth (I.5 ¶1). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Un seul boyau, étroit et sinueux
  - It was divided thus into three or four chambers, dark lairs hardly fit for a wild beast (I.5 ¶1). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: divisées en trois ou quatre chambres
  - It is dry, and one can stand upright in the principal chamber, which occupies the centre (I.5 ¶1). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: la principale de ces chambres, qui occupait le centre
  - Fine sand covers its floor (I.5 ¶1). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: Un sable fin en couvrait le sol
  - Its first hearth is at the end of the first left corridor (see [the hearth](../things/hearth.md)) (I.5 ¶11). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: au fond du premier couloir de gauche
  - Its corridors have a sand floor (I.13 ¶40). *(Drafted.)*
    - quote: étendre sur le sable des couloirs une fraîche litière de mousses et de feuilles sèches
  - It has a central chamber in which the colonists eat together (I.13 ¶57). *(Drafted.)*

  - It has a hearth, by whose light Smith carved his instrument (I.13 ¶69). *(Drafted.)*
